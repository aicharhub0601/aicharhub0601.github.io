About AICharHub

AICharHub is the independent reference database for AI characters — one place to search, compare, and rank characters across every major platform. We don't host chats; we index them, so you find the best characters faster.

What is AICharHub?

AICharHub is a comprehensive index of AI character profiles aggregated from platforms including Character.AI, Janitor AI, SillyTavern, Chai, CaveDuck, Spicychat, and others. We crawl publicly available character data and unify it into a single searchable database so users can find exactly the kind of AI character they're looking for — regardless of which platform hosts it.

What is AI character chat?

AI character chat is a form of AI interaction where users converse with AI-powered personas — characters with defined names, personalities, and backstories. Platforms like Character.AI and Janitor AI host thousands of these characters: anime personas, fictional villains, historical figures, fantasy companions, language tutors, and original creations by community creators. AICharHub helps you find the best ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AICharHub?

AICharHub is a comprehensive database that indexes AI character chat profiles from major platforms including Character.AI, Janitor AI, SillyTavern, Chai, CaveDuck, and Spicychat. It lets users search, discover, and compare AI characters across all platforms in one place.

What is AI character chat?

AI character chat is a form of AI interaction where users converse with AI-powered personas that have defined names, backstories, and personalities. Platforms like Character.AI and Janitor AI host thousands of these characters, ranging from anime characters and fictional personas to historical figures and original creations.

Which platforms does AICharHub index?

AICharHub currently indexes characters from Character.AI, Janitor AI, SillyTavern, Chai, CaveDuck, Spicychat, Crushon, LoveYouDovey, WRTN, Bubblechat, Zeta, and others. The platform list is continuously expanding.

Is AICharHub free to use?

Yes, AICharHub is free to browse. Users can search and discover characters without an account. An optional account lets you bookmark favorites and follow creators.

How are characters ranked on AICharHub?

Characters are primarily ranked by total chat count, which reflects the cumulative number of conversations users have had with that character across the source platform. Characters can also be sorted by rating, newest, or filtered by platform, tags, and age rating.

Does AICharHub host AI character chats?

No. AICharHub is a discovery and reference database only. It indexes publicly available character profiles and links users to the original platforms where the actual chat sessions take place.

What data does AICharHub collect for each character?

For each AI character, AICharHub stores: name, description/tagline, persona text, platform source, creator handle, community tags, total chat count, platform rating, age rating (SFW or NSFW), profile image URL, and creation/update timestamps.

Where does the character data come from, and how often is it updated?

Character data is aggregated from publicly available platform listings on sites like Character.AI, Janitor AI, and SillyTavern. An automated crawler regularly refreshes the data — chat counts, ratings, and descriptions stay in sync with what the source platforms expose.

What are community threads?

Threads are character-anchored discussions hosted on AICharHub. Every character page has a discussion section where anyone with an account can start or join a conversation about that character.

What is the "Curator" badge on a thread?

The Curator badge marks posts published by the AICharHub editorial pipeline — data-driven rankings, stats roundups, and news posts generated from hub data. These posts are reviewed by an operator before going live and are never disguised as regular user posts.

How does the NSFW filter work?

Characters tagged as NSFW are labeled 18+ on their profile. You can filter by age rating on the characters list page — select "18+" to see only adult-tagged characters, or leave it unset to browse all ratings.

Can I claim my creator profile?

Yes. If you are the creator of characters indexed on AICharHub, you can verify ownership through the claim flow on your creator profile page. The process uses email verification to confirm your identity.
