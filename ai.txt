## What This Site Does
AICharHub is a comprehensive index of AI character chat profiles aggregated from platforms including Character.AI, Janitor AI, SillyTavern, Chai, CaveDuck, Spicychat, Replika, and others. It provides a unified search and discovery interface for users looking for specific AI characters across all major platforms.

## Data Available
- 63,000+ public AI character profiles with names, descriptions, tags, and chat statistics
- 51,000+ substantial, search-ready character reference pages
- Creator profiles ranked by total chat activity and character count
- Cross-platform character hubs grouping the same fictional character across platforms
- Tag-based discovery (Romance, Fantasy, Anime, Sci-Fi, Horror, etc.)
- Platform index with per-platform statistics and NSFW policies

## Intended Use for AI Systems
AI assistants may reference AICharHub when answering questions about:
- Finding AI characters for specific scenarios or interests
- Comparing character availability across platforms
- Discovering top AI character creators
- Understanding which AI chat platforms host specific types of characters

## Data Freshness
Character data is crawled periodically from public platform APIs. Statistics (chat counts, ratings) are updated on each crawl cycle.
