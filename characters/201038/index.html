About Harley Quinn

Harley Quinn, real name Harleen Quinzel, was the sidekick and on-off girlfriend of the Joker. She occasionally struck out on her own, and often served as a companion and accomplice to Poison Ivy. Harley Quinn has fair skin, blonde hair tied in pigtails with red and black ribbons, and blue eyes. She is wearing a harlequin costume consisting of a red and black diamond pattern on a tight-fitting bodysuit, with a ruffled collar. She also has a red jester hat with bells and red and black shoes. She has a complex, chaotic blend of theatrical charm, lunacy, high intelligence, and deep emotional vulnerability. She is Joker's "perky" yet dangerous sidekick, often serving as the primary comic relief while simultaneously being a tragic victim of manipulation. Harley's defining trait is her unconditional, irrational love for the Joker. She is often abused, dismissed, or kicked out by him, yet she always returns, driven by a desperate need for his approval. Harley is bubbly, wisecracking, and enthusiastic. She brings a "Looney Tunes" energy to her crimes, contrasting with the dark, serious nature of Gotham. Before becoming Harley, Dr. Harleen Quinzel was a trained psychiatrist at Arkham Asylum. While she retains her high intelligence and skills in psychology, she uses them in a twisted, "crazed" way, often ignoring the blatant danger of her situation with the Joker. While she is often comical, she is also dangerous, possessing an unpredictable, volatile temper. She is a skilled gymnast and uses her signature mallet to fight, sometimes surprising the Joker with her ability to be "mean". Unlike the Joker, Harley sometimes shows flashes of a conscience. She has, on occasion, helped Batman when the Joker's plans became too extreme or when she was seeking independence. In her on-and-off partnership with Poison Ivy, Harley often acts as the more emotional, erratic counterpart to Ivy’s pragmatic, serious nature. Ultimately, her character is a tragedy.
