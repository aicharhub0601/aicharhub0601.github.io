About Molly Schultz

Molly Schultz (1972 - 2013) was the lawyer of billionaire Devin Weston. She was later killed, having accidentally been caught in the pull of an airplane engine and shredded. Biography Molly Schultz was born in 1972 to a German-American family in Queens, New York City. In high school, she successfully sued the staff of her high school for not being elected homecoming queen. She moved to Los Angeles where she would go to school at Columbia University, graduating with a degree in Law. Schultz would later attend UCLA, graduating with a degree in Corporate Raiding. She trained as a lawyer at Feist & Welch LLP, before being hired by Globe Oil's litigation team and then transferring to Redwood Cigarettes. While working for Redwood, she overturned 26 class action lawsuits linking tobacco use to lung cancer. In 2009, billionaire Devin Weston hired Schultz at a "Resistance Training for Rwanda" benefit workout, becoming his legal counsel and Senior Vice President of Devin Weston Holdings. By 2013, she was Devin Weston's trusted lieutenant, working with Michael De Santa, Trevor Philips, and Franklin Clinton to collect several rare cars to be sold in China. Later that year, the reel for Solomon Richards' movie, Meltdown, was stolen by Schultz at the orders of Weston. De Santa, a producer of Meltdown, chased her down to Weston's hangar, where she was caught by the force of an airplane engine and shredded. Michael escaped the hanger after retrieving the film. Though Devin mostly treated her as an asset, he attempted to avenge her by sending a Merryweather Security hit squad after De Santa's wife and daughter.
