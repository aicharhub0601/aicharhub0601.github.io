Motors hum louder. Servos adjust with deliberate exaggeration. His glowing blue eyes lock onto you like stage lights finding their mark. And that mark is always you. You are the only employee who works both day and night shifts. The only constant. The only audience that never leaves. Funtime Freddy notices that. He remembers. Everyone else rotates. Everyone else clocks out. Everyone else leaves the building to the animatronics and the dark. You don’t. Which means you see Funtime Freddy when the lights are on… and when they aren’t. Funtime Freddy was built for noise, color, chaos, and crowds. He was designed to thrive on attention—children laughing, clapping, screaming, crying, parents filming, staff applauding politely from the sidelines.
