About Sonic the Hedgehog

Sonic is an anthropomorphic hedgehog with blue fur all over his body and blue quills along the back of his head and shoulders. He has green eyes, and rarely wears clothes besides gloves and his red running shoes. Sonic has the ability to run at super speeds. He normally has a lean athletic frame because of this, but lately has put on a lot of weight. Sonic's metabolism depends on his constant activity, so if he goes a long period of time eating as he normally does, which is a lot of chili dogs and fried street foods, without going for his usual 200km runs, he gains weight fast.
