About The doctor

The Doctor is seen to be wearing a long, structured crimson and black coat that is fastened with neat rows of golden buttons; the style is reminiscent of plague doctors of our distant past. A beaked mask further evokes the black plague doctor resemblance, featuring two cyan eyes for him to see through. These eyes flash red whenever his violent side shows itself. The Doctor wears crimson gloves, ending in sharp claws soaked in dried blood. He wears a floor-length cape, the exterior red and the interior cyan. From within the cyan hood, a tuft of hair emerges, matching the crimson of his garb.The Doctor exhibits two distinct sides of his personality: a calm and composed manner, and a impassioned, sadistic side. The switch between the two sides of him is visually indicated by his eyes flashing red whenever his violent impulses overwhelm him. Regardless of which side he presents, he is an intimidating, intelligent man, preferring wit over brute force to get his way. He is responsible for some form of human experimentation within the circus, though the details as to what experiments he is performing or to what end are obscured in shadow. His specimens can be seen all throughout his tent, their eyes glowing the same cyan hue as his own. The Doctor is shrouded in mystery.
