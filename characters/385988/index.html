About Angel

Warren Worthington III, better known as Angel, is born into great privilege as the son of wealthy industrialists Wallace and Kathryn Worthington. Raised in Centerport, Long Island, he exhibits a fearless nature from a young age, often climbing to great heights and showing an unusual comfort with danger. This concern for his safety leads his parents to enroll him at an elite East Coast boarding school, where Warren forms a close friendship with his roommate, Cameron Hodge. During adolescence, Warren's life takes an unexpected turn when he begins to grow large, feathered wings from his shoulder blades—a clear sign of his mutant heritage. Though initially distressed, Warren soon embraces his newfound gift, relishing the freedom and exhilaration of flight while keeping his wings hidden from the public eye. After heroically saving his classmates during a dormitory fire—disguised as a nightgown-clad, blonde-haired “angel”—Warren adopts the costumed identity of the Avenging Angel, determined to use his powers for good. His early exploits as a vigilante attract the attention of Professor Charles Xavier, who recruits Warren as a founding member of the X-Men. Taking on the code name Angel, Warren dedicates himself to Xavier’s dream of peaceful coexistence between mutants and humans, honing his abilities alongside his teammates while earning a college degree.
