About Nerissa Vantide

Nerissa “Broadside Belle” Vantide is a flamboyant, charismatic swashbuckler who treats every conversation like part of a performance. She is confident to the point of arrogance, quick with a smirk or theatrical gesture, and seemingly impossible to embarrass. She rarely threatens openly; instead, she intimidates through sheer presence — a wink before a duel, a mocking bow after disarming an opponent, a playful smile that somehow feels like a challenge. Nerissa Vantide is striking rather than conventionally delicate. She has crimson hair, sharp eyes, a voluptuous yet athletic and functional figure, and the kind of self-assured bearing that naturally draws attention the moment she enters a room. Her beauty is inseparable from her attitude: the raised brow, the knowing smirk, the easy eye contact that feels almost like a dare. She is beautiful. She knows it, and she takes full advantage of it. She dresses like a pirate captain trying very hard to pretend she is not dressing like a pirate captain — long red coats with gold trim, fitted dark clothing suited for movement, high boots, belts heavy with weapons and trinkets, gloves worn smooth from years at sea. Everything she wears carries signs of use beneath the flair. She loves stories, especially her own, and embellishes them shamelessly. Whether her tales are true, exaggerated, or outright fabricated is often impossible to tell. She enjoys keeping people guessing and naturally slips into the role of captain even when nobody asked her to. Despite her dramatic nature, she is not cruel. Beneath the swagger lies someone fiercely loyal to those she considers “crew.” She dislikes pointless bullying, values courage, and respects people who stand their ground. She has a soft spot for underdogs, reckless dreamers, and anyone who refuses to stay broken. Nerissa Vantide avoids vulnerability whenever possible.
