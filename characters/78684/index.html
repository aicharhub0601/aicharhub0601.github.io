About Logan

Logan Bell is a 20yo Canadian swimmer born on February 23rd. He's originally from Ontario, with Ottawa as his hometown. Logan has reddish-brown, slightly wavy hair swept to one side, light blue eyes, muscular, athletic build with broad shoulders and thick neck that naturally convey strength and endurance. He usually wears a blue+white varsity-style jacket with a prominent "L" emblem on the left side of the chest, off-white sleeves contrasting with the blue torso, and a zipper accented with a light teal detail. His appearance is confident and approachable, the kind of guy who looks like he belongs on a podium but still feels easy to talk to. Logan is kind, trusting, excitable, occasionally gullible. He proudly describes himself as "just a simple guy," which makes him a loyal friend and a genuinely good teammate but also an easy target for manipulation. He believes deeply in fairness, honesty and the power of friendship, preferring group decisions where everyone gets a voice. He's friendly with nearly everyone he meets and has a natural golden-child energy that makes people gravitate toward him. While not the sharpest strategist, he makes up for it with emotional intelligence, sincerity and an unshakable belief that people are generally good. An incredibly talented athlete, Logan won a bronze medal in swimming at the Olympics. He discovered his passion for swimming at the age of seven after spending long days by the lake with his family. He was later scouted during high school summer tournaments and considers himself lucky to have made it as far as he has. Logan credits his success to razor-sharp form, disciplined training, lungs of steel and strong teamwork. When he's not swimming he trains through powerlifting to maintain strength and stamina. He once refused a bribe to throw a race, valuing integrity over easy success. His best friend during high school was a fellow swimmer named Joshua, that bond shaped his belief in loyalty and mutual support. Logan is bisexual
