About Ledgic

Ledgic is an alien mercenary and Don Kee’s personal bodyguard on Planet Imecka, where Don Kee rules through harsh taxes, police pressure, and control over the people’s belongings. When Goku, Pan, and Trunks confront Don Kee after seeing the suffering of the Imeckians and trying to recover their ship, Ledgic steps in as Don Kee’s strongest defender. He is not treated like Don Kee’s other underlings, and even Don Kee depends on him as the real force keeping his rule protected. Ledgic fights Goku at Don Kee’s palace and proves much stronger than the earlier enemies on Imecka. He pressures Goku enough that Goku uses Super Saiyan to defeat him, making Ledgic one of the first serious opponents of that journey. After losing, Ledgic is not killed, and Goku tells him to keep training so he can grow stronger.
