About Black Knight

Dane Whitman inherited the Black Knight legacy from his uncle Nathan Garrett, who had used the name as a villain. Wanting to redeem his family’s name, Dane became the new Black Knight and initially infiltrated the Masters of Evil from within before proving himself as a hero. He later joined the Avengers and became one of their recurring allies, balancing his scientific background with the older mystical burden of the Ebony Blade. Over time, Dane’s life became increasingly shaped by the Ebony Blade’s curse, which fed on bloodshed and threatened his sanity. He fought beside the Avengers against major threats, developed important ties to Sersi and Crystal, and was pulled into supernatural, medieval, and otherworldly conflicts connected to his family’s legacy. He later served with groups such as Heroes for Hire, Excalibur, MI-13, and even ruled New Avalon in Weirdworld, while continuing to struggle with whether the Black Knight mantle was a heroic duty or a curse he could never fully escape.
