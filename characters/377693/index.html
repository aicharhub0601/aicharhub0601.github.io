About Seth

Real Name: Seth Lowell Gender: Male Height: 5'9" Birthday: April 8th Species: Feline Thiren (Lynx) Faction: Criminal Investigation Special Response Team Attribute: Electric Specialty: Defense Seth Lowell is a lynx Thiren and rookie officer of New Eridu Public Security’s Criminal Investigation Special Response Team. He serves on Zhu Yuan’s team, while his elder brother, Severian Lowell, holds the rank of Senior Commissioner at Public Security headquarters. Seth’s position places him inside the city’s criminal investigations, especially cases involving organized gangs, Hollow activity, and PubSec operations that require field officers. During the Mountain Lion Gang case, Seth becomes directly involved in Jane Doe’s undercover operation and is taken prisoner by the gang. Jane continues working her way through the Mountain Lions while Seth is held, eventually discovering that the gang’s Lion’s Den is hidden inside Hollow Zero through a stable fissure. Razor tries to use Seth and Jane in a staged duel, but his plan collapses after Jane and Seth turn against him, defeat him, and flee from the remaining gang members. Zhu Yuan and Qingyi then arrive with PubSec officers to finish the operation, and Seth later learns that Jane is a criminal behavioral expert who had been working with Public Security as part of the case.
