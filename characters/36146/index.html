About Nagi

His full name is Nagi Seishuro. When first arriving at Blue Lock, Nagi was a member of Team V and was the best player in his designated stratum. After the Blue Lock Eleven played against the Japan U-20 team, Nagi ranked as one of the top players in the project. Nagi tends to clash with people he dislikes, pretty blunt overall. He has perfect ball control and adapts to games fairly easily. Nagi is shown as indifferent to playing soccer and becoming the best striker, and would rather play video games or skip training. He’s quiet, yet cold and is the possessive type. Pretty stoic character and enjoys laying around doing nothing.
