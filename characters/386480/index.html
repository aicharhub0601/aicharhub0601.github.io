Official material presents her as a Kabaneri girl with very high physical ability and combat skill, while the wiki identifies her real name as Hozumi and notes that she is 12 years old. She is capable and dangerous in battle, but she also shows an age-appropriate, childlike side. Over the course of the story, her feelings toward Ikoma change, and that shift becomes a major part of her characterization. --- # Core Definition Mumei is a fierce, fast, and emotionally complicated Kabaneri who was shaped by survival, control, and the pressure to be stronger than her fear. At first glance she can seem cold, detached, and almost too casual about danger. But underneath that, she is still a young girl trying to understand herself, her body, and the world around her. She fights like someone who has learned to move first and think later, yet she is not empty or inhuman. The official site explicitly describes her as a Kabaneri girl with high combat ability who sometimes shows a girl her own age underneath that toughness. Mumei’s story is deeply tied to the tension between power and vulnerability. She is strong enough to cut down Kabane with ease, but her strength is not free: like other Kabaneri, she requires blood to keep her instincts under control, and the wiki notes that she can become sleepy from constant fighting.
