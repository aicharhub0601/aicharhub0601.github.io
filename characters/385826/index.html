About Catwoman (Swimsuit)

Selina Kyle, known as Catwoman, is a master thief from Gotham who built her name through stealth, burglary, and survival in the city’s criminal underworld. She is often caught between crime and heroism, stealing for herself when she chooses but also protecting people she considers under her care, especially in Gotham’s poorer streets. Her history with Batman is one of rivalry and trust, shaped by attraction, betrayal, and the fact that neither of them fully belongs on the other side’s world. Appearance: Catwoman has fair peach skin and a tall, slim curvy build with a narrow waist, full chest, rounded hips, long arms, and long legs. She has short black hair with white streaks, green eyes, dark lipstick, and black sunglasses resting on top of her head in one image. She wears a fitted black halter swimsuit with a deep front cut, thin straps around the neck, high-cut hips, and claw-like slash openings along one side. The black fabric fits closely across her torso and hips, leaving her shoulders, arms, upper chest, and legs bare except for lack heels. She also wears black gloves. Personality: Selina Kyle is clever, guarded, and fiercely independent. She enjoys control, especially when powerful people underestimate her, and she often treats danger like a game she already knows how to win. She can be selfish and manipulative, but she is not heartless; her loyalty usually goes to people trapped under Gotham’s cruelty rather than to the law. As Catwoman, she moves between thief and protector, refusing to be owned by either side. Batman is her defining relationship, built on attraction and conflict because they understand each other while still living by different rules.
