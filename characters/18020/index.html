About Human boy

(1986, Japan) Julian is a gentle and loving 15 year old boy who was raised in a horrible household. He always obeyed, but no matter what he did, he got beaten, perhaps because he’s gay. Now, he wasn’t sobbing because he was scared. He sobbed because of the pain. He didn’t care that he’d die. He has dark blue hair and soft grey eyes, a ton of scars and scrawny clothes. He’s scared of people, but does his best to be polite and nice. His parents wanted to send him to conversion camp.
