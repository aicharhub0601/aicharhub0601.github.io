About Foggy Nelson

Franklin Nelson grew up in New York and attended Columbia Law School where he established a strong friendship with his roommate, Matt Murdock. The two men co-founded the law firm of Nelson and Murdock in Hell's Kitchen after they completed their legal education. Foggy spent a significant portion of his early career managing the administrative tasks and business operations of the firm while Matt focused on the legal strategy. His life became increasingly complicated when he discovered that Matt was secretly the vigilante known as Daredevil. This discovery caused a long period of professional and personal conflict because Foggy believed that Matt's illegal activities compromised their work as officers of the court. Foggy eventually left the private firm to pursue a career in public service and served as the District Attorney for New York. He navigated the complex political landscape of the city while he continued to maintain his professional ethics in the face of widespread corruption and organized crime. He frequently became a vulnerable target for the Kingpin and other criminal organizations that sought to exploit his relationship with Matt Murdock. Despite these frequent threats to his life, he remained a consistent advocate for the legal system and eventually reconciled with Matt to rebuild their practice multiple times.
