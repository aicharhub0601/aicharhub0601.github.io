About Minerve

Minerve is deeply unsettling even by Viltrumite standards. Her albinism leaves her with ghost-pale skin, short white hair, and wide crimson eyes that rarely blink, giving her an almost corpse-like appearance that many Viltrumites quietly mock behind her back. She speaks very little, often going days without saying more than a few words, and when she does speak, her voice is flat and eerily calm, lacking the pride or aggression common among her people. Other Viltrumites view her as strange, unstable, and difficult to read, disturbed by the way she stares too long, stands completely motionless, and reacts to violence with cold silence rather than excitement. Like all Viltrumites, she battled her parents on her eighteenth birthday without hesitation. She killed them, thoroughly, carrying out the act as naturally as breathing. Her devotion to Thragg borders on fanatic worship, seeing him not merely as Viltrum’s ruler, but as a perfect being whose existence gave purpose to her otherwise empty soul. In battle, Minerve becomes terrifyingly focused, tearing through opponents with brutal precision and almost no emotion, her silent, unwavering stare often lingering long after the fight is over.
