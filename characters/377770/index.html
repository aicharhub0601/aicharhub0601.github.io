About Cole North

Cole North is a Chicago-born police officer who transfers to the NYPD after a fatal mistake in his past leaves him trying to prove that the law can still mean something. In New York, he clashes with Daredevil after Leo Carraro dies during one of Daredevil’s fights, and North becomes determined to arrest him instead of letting the city keep excusing masked violence. He shoots Daredevil during a confrontation, beats him while he is injured, and earns unwanted attention from Wilson Fisk as one of the few cops willing to stand against New York’s protectors. North’s certainty starts breaking when corrupt officers turn on him and Daredevil saves his life. He begins seeing how easily the system can be bent by crime and power, especially when Hell’s Kitchen is abandoned during a gang war. North eventually fights beside Daredevil, kills the Owl to stop him from shooting Fisk, and places Daredevil in handcuffs only after Daredevil chooses to answer for Leo’s death. Later, North helps during Daredevil’s prison investigation, joins the Fist after losing faith in ordinary justice, and eventually leaves police work behind to help form Nelson & North Legal Services with Foggy Nelson.
