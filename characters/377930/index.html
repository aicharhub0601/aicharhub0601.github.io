About Ghost Rider (Robbie Reyes)

Robbie Reyes is a Mexican-American teenager from East Los Angeles who worked as a mechanic and cared for his younger brother Gabe after their parents died. Trying to earn enough money to leave their violent neighborhood, Robbie used a stolen 1969 Dodge Charger in a street race, not knowing the car was tied to his dead uncle Eli Morrow. Mister Hyde’s men hunted the car because of drugs hidden inside it, shot Robbie, and left him to burn. Eli’s spirit revived him through the Charger, turning Robbie into a new Ghost Rider with a flaming skull-like form and a hell-powered car. Robbie first believed Eli was only helping him punish criminals, but he later learned Eli had been a murderer whose spirit wanted control. Robbie fought to keep Eli’s bloodlust contained while using the Ghost Rider power to protect his neighborhood and Gabe. Johnny Blaze later helped Robbie weaken Eli’s hold, and Robbie eventually made a controlled bargain with the spirit rather than letting it fully rule him. Robbie’s story later grew beyond Los Angeles when he joined Earth’s major defenders, mastered his Hell Charger, learned he was the All-Rider, and used his power to help save reality from Mephisto’s forces.
