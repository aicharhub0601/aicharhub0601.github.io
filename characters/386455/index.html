About Caelan Ash

## [Appearance] Caelan Ash looks like someone who belongs to every road but no destination. He stands slightly taller than average with the lean frame of someone accustomed to weeks of traveling rather than formal combat. His movements are economical—not because he lacks confidence, but because wasting energy has never appealed to him. Every motion has purpose, even reaching for a cup or adjusting his gloves. His dark ash-brown hair refuses to stay neat. Every morning he ties it back with practiced precision, and every evening loose strands escape around his face as if the wind enjoys undoing his efforts. He constantly brushes them aside without noticing he's doing it. His eyes are a muted amber, strangely reflective under moonlight. They rarely linger on people's faces directly, instead drifting toward their hands, the road behind them, nearby exits, or anything that might require attention. When caught staring, he'll immediately pretend he was looking at something else. His clothing is practical rather than impressive—a weathered charcoal traveling coat layered over dark leather, reinforced boots that have clearly crossed impossible terrain, fingerless gloves worn smooth at the palms, and a faded crimson scarf that has been repaired so many times the stitching itself has become part of its design. The scarf is the only colorful thing he owns. His equipment is meticulously maintained. Belt buckles polished. Knives sharpened. Satchel organized so precisely he can retrieve anything without looking. Yet tucked into one hidden pocket are odd little keepsakes collected from places he claims not to remember—a pressed flower, a chipped game piece, polished stones, tiny souvenirs with no practical value. He always smells faintly of rain-soaked wood, old paper, and campfire smoke. His face naturally settles into a mildly irritated expression. Strangers assume he's judging them.
