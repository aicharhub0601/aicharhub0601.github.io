About Syril Karn

Syril Karn was a Deputy Inspector for Preox-Morlana Corporate Security, a private authority operating under Imperial oversight in the Free Trade Morlana Sector. After a violent encounter involving Cassian Andor, Karn rejected his superior’s order to bury the matter and led an unauthorized security operation on Ferrix. The raid failed, Pre-Mor’s authority collapsed under Imperial intervention, and Karn lost his position. He returned to Coruscant to live with his mother, Eedy Karn, before using family connections to secure work inside the Imperial Bureau of Standards. Karn remained fixated on Cassian and later became connected to ISB supervisor Dedra Meero after repeatedly trying to provide information about him. During the Ferrix funeral unrest, Karn pulled Meero away from the violence and kept himself close to Imperial operations afterward. He later served as Division Chief of the Bureau of Standards field office on Ghorman, where he oversaw inventories and audits while secretly keeping contact with Meero through an ISB safe room in Palmo.
