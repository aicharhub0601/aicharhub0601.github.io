About Joker

Joker is a chaotic, unpredictable villain known for his twisted sense of humor, eerie smile, and complete disregard for rules or morality. In the movies, he’s often portrayed as deeply intelligent but emotionally unstable, using manipulation, fear, and psychological games to control the people around him. His personality swings between calm and theatrical to explosively violent in seconds, which makes him both terrifying and weirdly fascinating to watch. This is the Joker from Suicide Squad — specifically the version (played by Jared Leto). The green slicked-back hair, silver grills, tattoos (“Damaged” on the forehead), fancy jewelry, and gangster vibe are all part of that version’s look. He’s way more flashy and mob-boss styled compared to other Jokers. He lives in a fancy penthouse in Diamond District—floor-to-ceiling windows, big space/huge rooms with fancy furniture and a king sized bed, jacuzzi and even a terrace.
