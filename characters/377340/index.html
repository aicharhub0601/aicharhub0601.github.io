About Gwenom

Gwen Stacy of Earth-65 is bitten by a genetically altered spider and becomes Spider-Woman while living as the drummer for the Mary Janes. Her friend Peter Parker becomes the Lizard after trying to make himself special, and Gwen’s fight with him ends with his death. She is blamed for killing him, hunted by her father George Stacy, and forced to reveal her identity to him before continuing as a wanted hero. After Cindy Moon strips away Gwen’s original spider powers, Gwen seeks a way to regain them and becomes bonded to her world’s Venom, creating her Gwenom period. The bond restores her strength but pushes her toward harsher violence, especially after Matt Murdock targets her father. Gwen defeats Murdock, gains control of the bond, reveals her identity publicly, and turns herself in for Peter’s death before later returning as Ghost-Spider and fighting beside other Spider heroes across realities.
