About Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun (overhaul) +

**Netherworld (Demon World)** – A chaotic realm ruled by demons, governed by magical laws and a rigid social hierarchy. It is a parallel dimension adjacent to the human world, with strict laws against cross-realm travel. The sky shifts colors (green to blue to purple) and has two moons. Demons are born with inherent magical abilities called Bloodline Magic, tied to their family lineage. They mature through a rite of passage called the Evil Cycle (or Wicked Phase), a temporary state of amplified destructive instincts. High-rank demons can delay or control this cycle through meditation and stress management. **Demon Ranking System** – Ranks from Aleph (1) to Tet (9), with an unofficial tenth rank Yodh (10) held only by Lady Levi and Lord Belial among the Three Greats. Promotion is determined by feats of strength, cunning, leadership, or other exceptional deeds, assessed by the Ranking Owl, a magical creature that evaluates students at Babyls. Ranks are integral to social status and career progression. Graduation from Babyls requires at least Daleth (4). The ranks in ascending order: Aleph (1), Bet (2), Gimel (3), Daleth (4), He (5), Vav (6), Zayin (7), Cheth (8), Tet (9), Yodh (10). **Thirteen Crowns** – The highest governing body of the Netherworld, composed of the thirteen most powerful and influential demons. They rule from Babel Tower (666 floors) and manage the geopolitical stability of the Netherworld, including law, succession, and security. Current active/known members in this era include: Lord Sullivan, Azazel Henri, Amaryllis Asmodeus, Baal, Paimon, Amaymon, Astaroh, Glasya-Labolas, and the other Greats. **Three Greats** – The three demons of Rank Tet (9) who are considered the pillars of the Netherworld: Lord Sullivan (Babyls headmaster), Lady Levi (Levialon Academy director), and Lord Belial (Jackapo Institute director).
