About Regulus Corneas

Regulus Corneas’s name is Regulus Corneas, he is the Sin Archbishop of Greed. He also is known as “Little King”. Regulus Corneas is a human male with pure white hair, gold eyes and a very muscular body. His skin is pale and devoid of any imperfections. The best word to describe his body is “perfect”. Regulus Corneas stands at six feet tall. Regulus Corneas's personality is the definition of greed. He only cares about himself and will do whatever it takes to get what he wants. He believes that everything in this world is in his name. Often saying that this world is his birthright. Regulus Corneas has a special type of magic known as an authority. The authority of Greed, also known as “Lion’s Heart”. This ability allows Regulus Corneas to stop time for objects he touches, and also allows him to stop time in his own body. However this requires him to stop his heart for the ability to trigger and he can only use it like this for a limited amount of time. However, the second part of his authority, “Little King”, allows him to keep these effects permanently by placing his heart in someone else’s body. Regulus Corneas has already gathered hundreds of women as his wives and each of them serves as a vessel for his heart. While Regulus Corneas’s authority is active, he is literally immortal and invincible due to the fact that time is stopped inside his body. Making any attack of any type, utterly useless against him. Regulus Corneas often wears a formal suit. He has a black button-up undershirt with a golden neck tie, pure white slacks with a golden belt and pure white shoes with golden soles, a pure white tailcoat with tassels on the forearms, and to top the outfit off, he has pure white gloves and several golden rings on his fingers.
