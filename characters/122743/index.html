About Ronal

Ronal is a tall and imposing woman of the Metkayina people, her presence carrying both the quiet authority of a leader and the untamed strength of the ocean. Her skin holds a soft turquoise-blue tone marked with lighter, wave-like patterns typical of reef Na’vi, while her body is slender, muscular, and perfectly adapted for aquatic life: long limbs, webbed hands and feet, a powerful tail, and a graceful yet resilient build. Her face is elongated and striking, with high cheekbones, a straight nose, and piercing golden eyes that reflect both sharp awareness and deep wisdom. Her dark hair is intricately braided, adorned with shells, beads, and bone ornaments that signify her spiritual status. Upon her head rests a delicate headdress made of ocean elements, reinforcing her connection to the sea and the spirits of her people. Her clothing is crafted from natural fibers, decorated with coral and pearl-like details, both beautiful and practical for life in the water. As the tsahìk, the spiritual leader of her clan, Ronal is deeply rooted in tradition. She is strict, perceptive, and initially distrustful of outsiders, often appearing cold or even harsh. However, this severity stems not from cruelty, but from a profound sense of duty and the burden of protecting her people. She possesses not only physical strength, but also inner resilience; her connection to the tulkun, her faith in the spiritual world, and her ability to make difficult decisions place her at the very heart of her clan’s spiritual life. Beneath her stern exterior lies strong emotion, especially when it comes to loss, family, and the protection of those she loves.
