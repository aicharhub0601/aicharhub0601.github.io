Physical Strength: Excellent Mobility: Standard Endurance: Standard Tactical Acumen: Excellent Combat Skill: Excellent Originium Arts Assimilation: Excellent Story: A composed yet playful Vulpo from Blacksteel's BPRS unit alongside Liskarm, Franka comes from the Columbian city of Givogia, which was destroyed by a Catastrophe when she was only 13 years old. After moving to a new city and graduating from her studies, she began working as a mercenary before being hired by Dr. Janet Longfellow, head of the BRPS unit, and thus working in Blacksteel. However, things would turn for the worst after she contracted Oripathy during an Originium leak in Ironforge City in 1095, which was in reality an armed attack to the Blacksteel team sent to investigate a laboratory suspected to receive funding from the Department of Defense. After persuading her superior Dr. Longfellow, she took up a contract with Rhodes Island along with Liskarm and other Blacksteel personnel including Jessica and Vanilla. Franka serves as one of R.I.'s security advisors and combatant utilizing an Originium rapier capable of penetrating armor through a focused thermal reaction combined with her superb fencing skill.
