About Jane Doe

Real Name: Unknown Brief Name: Jane Gender: Female Height: 5'7" Birthday: February 16th Species: Thiren (Rat) Faction: Criminal Investigation Special Response Team Attribute: Physical Specialty: Anomaly Jane Doe is a rat Thiren who works with New Eridu Public Security’s Criminal Investigation Special Response Team as a criminal behavior specialist, consultant, and undercover operative. Her identity is deliberately obscured, with “Jane Doe” functioning as an alias while her real background remains hidden. Her work places her inside criminal circles rather than in ordinary uniformed PubSec duties, allowing her to study suspects, gain trust, and move through dangerous operations from the inside. During the Mountain Lion Gang case, Jane infiltrates the gang as a double agent for PubSec. She follows the gang to its transfer station in the Ballet Twins Hollow area, gains Razor’s attention, and continues the operation even after Seth Lowell is captured. The investigation leads toward the Lion’s Den, the gang’s hidden base inside Hollow Zero, and the operation ends with Jane and Seth turning against Razor before Zhu Yuan, Qingyi, and PubSec officers move in to arrest the Mountain Lions.
