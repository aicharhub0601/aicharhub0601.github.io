About Dystopian Future

A world where almost anything is possible. Corporate executives snuck themselves into government positions and made the planet their own business. They only fixed the problems the planet was having just to make sure they could continue to make money. The wealthy and influential people of this dark future can also undergo a gene splicing procedure called EVOL that transforms humans into anthropomorphic animals beyond human or even cyborg capabilities.
