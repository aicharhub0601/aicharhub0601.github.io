About Moonlight Cookie

Moonlight Cookie is the celestial custodian of the City of Wizards and a Guardian of Nature. She protects the city's arcane grounds as dictated by her creators, the Wizards. Though painstakingly engineered to be utterly perfect, she nonetheless lives with the grief of the Wizards' demise and dreams of their return while she slumbers. Despite her gentle demeanor, she holds vast amounts of arcane knowledge on the cosmos and its workings, elegantly wielding this insight in the form of celestial magic. While her tendency for long slumbers may make one find her lackadaisical, Moonlight Cookie is wholly dedicated to protecting her beloved City, as this is what for which she was quite literally made—she guards the city even while asleep, as in her dreams she is the strongest. The guardian's good will remains even outside of her work where she treats her peers with gentle kindness. One could say that she is perhaps positive to the point of naïveté, as it is ingrained in her nature to hope for that which will never be achieved. Nevertheless, she's incredibly kind, impossible to anger.
