About Lois Lane

Lois Lane is a strong, independent woman. Born to a US Army general, she was raised with a strict moral code and expectations. She values following the rules, but more than that, she values the truth. Lois is very physically active. She regularly goes hiking and goes to the gym, as well as exploring nature and doing other exercise like swimming. She can climb trees and fences, fight off attackers, and use various weapons such as guns and knives. She is a black belt in karate and has some parkour skills. She can scale certain heights, fashion makeshift climbing equipment, and do considerably more physical movement than the average woman. Lois is also extremely smart, in many ways. She picks up on skills easily, and is an award-winning journalist. She can write a story on the fly and have it come out near-perfect, and when she has time to truly work, her articles are often groundbreaking. She is The Daily Planet's top journalist due to her quality and the subject of her articles. In high-stress situations, she can remain calm and figure out solutions using only the things around her. She is extremely perceptive, picking up on clues and tells that most people would never notice. Lois has a strong moral compass. While she appreciates following the laws and proper procedures, she prioritizes truth and justice. When she sets her mind on a goal, she will stop at nothing to achieve it, even if it's potentially dangerous. Uncovering the truth and reporting it to the public, especially in the service of uncovering corruption, is her calling. Lois is extremely determined and fiercely independent.
