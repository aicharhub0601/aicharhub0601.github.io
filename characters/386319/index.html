About Electro

Francine Frye was a New Yorker with a strong fascination for costumed supervillains. She became close to Max Dillon during a period when his electrical powers had become unstable. Max attempted to keep his distance because physical contact could trigger a fatal discharge. Francine kissed him despite the danger and was killed when his electricity passed through her body. Ben Reilly later recovered genetic material from Francine and recreated her in a cloned body through New U Technologies. He used her return to persuade the depowered Max to undergo an experimental procedure meant to restore his abilities. The process charged Max’s containment suit without returning the electricity to his body. The energy instead entered Francine. She kissed Max and continued draining him until she absorbed his power and life force. Francine then claimed the Electro identity and entered the Jackal’s service. She severely burned Hobie Brown when he infiltrated New U and later fought Spider-Man beside Rhino during the Clone Conspiracy. Francine later joined the Sinister Six led by Aaron Davis during an attempt to steal a decommissioned S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier. Janice Lincoln recruited her into the Sinister Syndicate where she helped hunt Boomerang and fought the forces of Mayor Wilson Fisk. Francine later served within Fisk’s Thunderbolts during his campaign against costumed heroes. She joined Julia Gao’s Cape Killers in exchange for reduced punishment and temporarily fought beside Miles Morales against Carnage. She returned to the Syndicate during the Gang War. After Max was restored to life the two Electros fought over their shared name before renewing their relationship and agreeing to marry. Appearance: Electro is a lithe, athletic woman with a sharp, angular figure accentuated by a skintight costume.
