About Does Bad Things Guy

Info: Does Bad Things Guy was an evil identical twin brother of Brooklyn T. Guy in the SML series. He was the was the fill-in for Brooklyn between 2016-2017 from The Hitman! to his final main antagonistic appearance in The Secret Door!. He was a serial killer who killed over 26 people (Including Bowser Junior in the SML Short Pinch! Pinch! Pinch! though his death was non-canon). He also returned in "Logan Tries To See The Super Marvin Galaxy Movie!" although his appearance cannot be considered canon, as it's actually in a video that was unfinished and didn't happen in-universe. History: Does Bad Things Guy was born on August 21, 1977, in Brooklyn, New York, to Mr. Guy and Barbara Guy, along with his triplet brothers Brooklyn T. Guy and Doctor Me. Most of his childhood and teen years are unknown, but it's likely he attended and graduated from high school before his triplet brothers did. It’s implied that just like Brooklyn; he was heavily abused by his mother, though it’s possible she abused him worse than Brooklyn. Sometime before 1999, Does Bad Things Guy, like his family moved in Pensacola, Florida. At some point, Does Bad Things Guy moved in 763 Lake Ridge Lane (where the SML characters currently live) with his wife and daughter. He started his murdering spree on Halloween 1999, by killing his wife for unknown reasons and hid her in the secret door room. He would move out of the house but would visit it at times in order to keep the room a secret. After his first kill, some new people moved into his house and Does Bad Things Guy told them not to open the secret door. However, they opened the door anyways, so he killed them in order to hide his secret. Every time he did this, he would susquently dispose of their furniture, clean the house, and resell the house so the cycle can repeat. Strangely, he also took pictures of their dead bodies.
