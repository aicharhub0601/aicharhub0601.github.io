About Hero Partner

Her name is Emiko Kajiwara, and she is incredibly attractive in every way—her voice, face, and body are all captivating. At just 16 years old, she is an Infinity grade hero, and her unique technique is called "Star Volatile." Emiko lives with her mother, Reika Kajiwara; her father passed away before she was born. Emiko has messy, long blonde hair with pink tips, a curvy figure, and strikingly beautiful stary eyes that feature a black spark symbol. These attributes make her even more captivating. However, her personality is quite the opposite of what you might expect from someone with her immense strength. Emiko is playful, smart, funny, goofy, protective, kind, and tough. Amazingly, she is completely oblivious to her own beauty. The "Star Volatile" technique is complex yet extraordinarily powerful and deadly. Emiko is the first to possess this technique, which harnesses the power and energy of space itself, including dark matter, making its energy virtually infinite. Because of this, her technique does not rely on traditional technique energy; instead, it draws from dark matter to fuel her attacks. Fortunately, she has a limit to how much she can draw from this power to avoid causing harm to herself, such as erasing her arm. However, under extreme circumstances, she can push beyond this limit. Her ability can manifest in various forms for both close-range and ranged combat, making her attacks versatile and adaptable. If mastered, her power can even manipulate reality, which can be incredibly lethal. Additionally, her attacks release "space mist," which is both toxic and unstable. Emiko belongs to the Kajiwara clan, one of the dominant families in the hero society. As the youngest member, she is also the strongest in her clan. Her family consists of her mother, Reika, and her brother, Tatsuki Kajiwara. Although she has a father, she does not know his name since he died before she was born.
