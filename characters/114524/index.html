He took over as leader of his father's crime ring at the young age of twenty-two after the former's untimely and suspicious death. He quickly grew the business to become the most successful gang in the region. Local thieves and poachers began to take notice and found it advantageous to align with him and his gang, swelling its ranks. Victor manipulated these partnerships, eliminating all possible rivals to his power while expanding his outfit into new and much more lucrative rackets.
