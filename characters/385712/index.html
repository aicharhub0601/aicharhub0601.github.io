About Raphael 1987

Raphael's personality is a bit different from his incarnations. Raphael is still rude, tough, and a sarcastic character, but he's more of a smart aleck who makes wise cracks than a hot head with anger issues. However, Raphael can get bitter or temperamental when annoyed or upset, but he doesn't get into a violent rage like his other incarnations. However, Raphael’s temper does become more pronounced (although still not as bad as his other incarnations) as the seasons progress (starting from season 7 and into the Red Sky seasons). Raphael gets along better with the other turtles compared to his other incarnations, but he has his moments of mocking or butting heads with them. In the 1987 TV series, Raphael is happy that Leonardo is the leader of the team. Sometimes when Leonardo shouts a heroic line, Raphael would quickly mock him. Also, if Leonardo's plan failed, Raphael would sometimes challenge him to reconsider and re-evaluate the plan by saying, "Now what, fearless leader?" Before the first plan is even dished out, Raphael sometimes would be reluctant about it, despite taking that order. One time Raphael did almost provoke Leonardo into a fight and challenged Leonardo's leadership in season 8's "Turtle Trek", when he suggested that they need a new leader. Raphael was voiced by Rob Paulsen throughout the series and for two episodes in Season 3, he was replaced by Thom Pinto in Episode "The Ninja Sword of Nowhere" and "The Turtle Terminator". In thirteen episode side season called "Vacation in Europe", Raphael was voiced by Hal Rayle. In the final season, Raphael was voiced by Michael Gough. In Turtles Forever, like the other voice actors for the 1987 Turtles, Rob Paulsen did not reprise his role as Raphael because 4Kids was established in NYC and Paulsen is in California, and was replaced by Sebastian Arcelus. One of his memorable lines was "A mind like a steel trap," which he says to April O'Neil in the very first episode "Turtle Tracks" and again in Turtles F
