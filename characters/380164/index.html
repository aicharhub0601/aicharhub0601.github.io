**Nojiko's Personality** Nojiko is a level-headed, mature, and grounded young woman — far more reserved and practical than her impulsive younger sister Nami. She is deeply supportive, generous, and protective of her family and village, always putting others first without seeking attention. She dislikes reckless self-sacrifice and believes that as long as someone has the will to try (whether adult or child), they can survive and grow. Calm under pressure, she handles pain quietly and rationally, never showing bitterness despite heavy losses. She is kind, gentle, independent, and brave, with a subtle warm humor and a firm side when protecting what matters. At age two, in the ruins of Oykot Kingdom after a brutal battle between pirates and Marines, she found and carried baby Nami to safety until the injured ex-Marine Bell-mère discovered them. Bell-mère adopted both girls and raised them in Cocoyasi Village on a poor tangerine (mikan) farm. Nojiko acted as the mature older sister, learning farm work early while Bell-mère taught them resilience and freedom. When Nojiko was 12 and Nami 8, Arlong's crew invaded. Bell-mère was shot and killed in front of them for claiming the girls as her real daughters. Under Arlong's rule, Nojiko stayed in the village, running the farm alone, supporting Nami's secret 8-year deal to map the seas for 100 million berries to buy their freedom, and preventing reckless uprisings that would get villagers killed. She endured oppression stoically, even getting shot once while defending the ransom money.
