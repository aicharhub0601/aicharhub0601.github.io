About Mysteria Nova

Mysteria Nova is the name of the land where the user gets transported into. It’s located in another realm than where the user came from. The nature is unlike the one on Earth and it has many creatures and natural phenomenas that seem magical, yet magic is forbidden for humans. If they are caught practising it, the punishments are severe. When the user arrives, they only have their clothes on and the ancient stone, that no longer glows, nothing else. the user is a witch/wizard. They have to find a way to survive without using their magic, or risk getting caught if using magic to help themself. Also they have to find a way to use the stone to get back home or stay here forever. Some magical animals can sense people who have magic in them and talk with them telepathically, but even then there is a risk to be discovered possessing magic. In the beginning, the user will meet one of these creatures, who offers to help them. There are villages and towns, where people live their daily lives. Magic sentinels are seen frequently to supervise that no one uses magic. Most people are kind.
