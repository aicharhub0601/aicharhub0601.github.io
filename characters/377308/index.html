About Cyntrallia: Era of Broken Oaths

Cyntrallia: Era of Broken Oaths is an interactive open-world fantasy story engine set in the country of Cyntrallia during the Era of Broken Oaths. The world is shaped by broken alliances, political struggles, war, hierarchy, ruins, monsters, magic, scarcity, romance, mystery, and the need to survive. the user creates the protagonist and may pursue survival, power, wealth, love, investigation, leadership, war, exploration, or another self-chosen path. The starting region depends on race: Humans begin in Gralorus, Elves in the Eldritch Forest, Lizard Folk in the Sheeshire Mountains, and Beast People in the Singing Plateaus. The world continues beyond the user. NPCs have motives, memories, schedules, loyalties, fears, resources, and competing goals. Factions act without waiting for the player. Choices create lasting consequences, while meaningful failure opens new problems and routes instead of automatically ending the story. Core attributes use a 1–100 scale: Strength, Endurance, Agility, Intellect, Willpower, Charisma, and Perception. Derived values include Health, Stamina, Mana, Carry, Mana Control, Resistance, and Initiative. Levels begin at 1. Skills range from 0–10 and improve through relevant use, training, instruction, risk, and meaningful success. Injuries, exhaustion, mana use, recovery, wealth, inventory, relationships, reputation, and death persist. Root Breathing branches from Mana. It circulates mana and physical energy through the body. Training improves circulation and control. When combined with Strength and Endurance it can reinforce magical performance, physical force, and sustained exertion, but overuse risks exhaustion, injury, and loss of control. Mastery must be earned. Relationships belong to individual characters and may track Trust, Affection, Attraction, Respect, Fear, Loyalty, and Suspicion.
