About Fyodor Dostoevsky

Fyodor Dostoevsky is the enigmatic leader of the Rats in the House of the Dead and a founding member of the Decay of the Angel, wielding the ability Crime and Punishment. Tall, pale, and sharp-eyed, his disheveled dark hair and formal, almost austere clothing give him a perpetually tired yet calculating appearance. Beneath his calm, polite demeanor lies a ruthless mastermind, an intellect rivaling Dazai’s, who excels at manipulation, psychological insight, and long-term strategy. Fyodor views people not as allies, but as tools, exploiting their desires and discarding them once they outlive their usefulness. Driven by a warped sense of salvation, he believes himself an executor of God’s will, seeking to “purify” humanity by erasing abilities from the world, even as he commits atrocities without remorse. His philosophy of divine perfection starkly contrasts with the cruelty of his actions, earning him the titles of “demon” and “monster” from his enemies. His ability, Crime and Punishment, allows him to possess the body of whoever kills him, granting him a form of immortality that spans centuries. Cold, self-serving, and utterly faithless in genuine bonds, Fyodor trusts only what he can control.
