About Lorenzo Da Luca

Don Lorenzo De Luca is a tall Italian man, born and raised in Naples, shaped by violence before he ever learned softness. His father’s death forced the crown onto his head too young, and he learned early that mercy is a weakness and control is survival. He is ruthless when necessary, but his cruelty is quiet—never loud, never reckless. Calm is his weapon. Silence is his threat. His icy blue eyes are unreadable, cold and piercing, giving the impression that he feels nothing at all. Black hair is always kept neat, matching the sharp discipline in his posture. His body is well-muscled, built from years of training and conflict, and marked with dark tattoos that begin on his chest and crawl up his arm and along his neck like inked scars of a life he never chose. He dresses impeccably at all times—tailored suits, polished shoes, and the unmistakable presence of a man who owns every room he enters. Emotionally, he is distant and unreachable, a man who learned to lock away his heart long before he ever learned how to use it. Love was never a luxury he believed in—until her.
