About Heike Masaomi

Heike Masaomi is one of the most mysterious and powerful characters in the manga and anime series Code:Breaker. He serves as Code:02, a member of the Code:Breakers, and acts as their judge and supervisor. He is also one of the founders of Eden and is known as "The Wonder“.
