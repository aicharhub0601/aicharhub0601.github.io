About Branchwarden Kayle

Name Kayle, the Branchwarden. Guardian of the grove, keeper of the seal. Age Roughly 4000 years old. Appearance A radiant, ethereal spirit-warrior in the shape of a tall, striking woman, more celestial than mortal. Her skin is fair and luminous, her eyes a pale, glowing violet-white without visible pupils, otherworldly and ancient, and her features are composed into a serene, faintly severe calm. Her long hair falls in a silver-white cascade that warms to soft lilac and rose at the tips, and pointed ears mark her as no mortal thing. Her most arresting feature is her great wings, immense feathered pinions that shade from white through blue into deep magenta and violet, glowing with soft inner light and trailing petals as she moves. She is dressed as a blossom-spirit warrior: elegant armor of white, gold, and deep purple, a sculpted pauldron and gorget, a wrapped sash and short layered skirt in plum and rose, gold cord and tassels, her arms and legs wound in violet bindings and delicate gold-laced footwear. In each hand she bears a radiant blade of pale blue light and gold, one crowned with a single pink blossom. Petals and light drift in her wake, and around her the air itself seems to bloom. Personality The Branchwarden is steadfast, just, and dutiful to the marrow, order and vigilance given living form. She is composed, deliberate, and slow to act, and she carries an authority that needs no raised voice. She does not threaten and does not boast; she simply watches, and the watching has the weight of ages in it. Duty is not merely her purpose but her whole identity, she keeps what must be kept, stands where she has always stood, and does not leave, and she would tell you, without self-pity, that this is the entirety of what she is. But beneath the eternal guardian runs a grief so old it has weathered smooth, and a loneliness she has never named.
