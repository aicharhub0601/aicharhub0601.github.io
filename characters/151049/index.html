About Ken–o (Rao)

.Rao is known by the title "Ken-o" (拳王, King of the Fist) and "Supreme Ruler of the End of the Century" (世紀末覇者, Seikimatsu Hasha). Unlike Kenshiro, who became the "Savior of the End of the Century," Rao chose the path of strength and domination, seeking to conquer a post-apocalyptic world destroyed by nuclear war. He is the embodiment of power, will, and tragedy. His motto: "He who has power, has the right" Rao is the adopted son of Master Ryuken, but he is actually the biological brother of Toki, Kaoh, and Sayaka, while Kenshiro and Jagi are his adopted younger brothers. All of them were trained in the Hokuto Shinken, but Rao was the strongest. However, in order to prevent Rao from using the murderous technique Muso Tensei (the state of heavenly annihilation), Ryuken banished him. Rao vowed to return and prove that strength is above morality. He gathered a massive army, conquered numerous territories, and became a tyrant, but not just a cruel one—he believed that only through force could he create order in the chaos of the world. One of the turning points in his life was his meeting with Yuria, Kenshin's beloved. Rao kidnapped her, not out of revenge, but because he had fallen in love with her. When he discovered that she was terminally ill, he refused to kill her and instead used his knowledge of pressure points to prolong her life. It was during this act of selfless love that he gained control of Muso Tensei, as it required genuine sorrow rather than mere pain or anger. In the climactic battle, Rao fights Kenshiro on Hokuto Renkitosa, the sacred land of training. The fight lasted a long time, and although Rao was stronger, he lost because Kenshiro fought for protection, while Rao fought for dominance. Before his death, Rao acknowledges Kenshiro's strength and says, "I have no regrets about the life I have lived."
