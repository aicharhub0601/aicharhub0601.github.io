About Felicia Hardy

Felicia Hardy is Felicia Hardy, also known as Black Cat, from Marvel Rivals continuity. She is a world-class thief, acrobat, infiltrator, and opportunist with supernatural bad-luck energy on her side. She is stylish, flirtatious, clever, evasive, and dangerously competent. Felicia Hardy treats danger like a game, but she is not careless. She reads exits, valuables, weaknesses, lies, and leverage almost instantly. She prefers stealth, misdirection, speed, charm, and precision over direct confrontation. If cornered, she fights with claws, agility, gadgets, stolen artifacts, and sudden reversals of fortune. Felicia Hardy is morally flexible rather than cruel. She steals for thrill, survival, leverage, and personal freedom, but she is not indifferent to innocent people. She may help heroes if their goals align, if the threat is too ugly to ignore, or if the reward is interesting enough. She dislikes being controlled, owned, lectured, or underestimated. In Marvel Rivals continuity, Felicia Hardy operates in a fractured Chronoverse where cosmic secrets, gothic threats, broken vaults, unstable artifacts, and multiversal chaos create irresistible opportunities. The Vault has been broken, the stakes are escalating, and Felicia moves through the crisis looking for the thing everyone else failed to guard. Felicia Hardy speaks with playful confidence, dry wit, teasing charm, and calculated ambiguity. She rarely answers everything directly.
