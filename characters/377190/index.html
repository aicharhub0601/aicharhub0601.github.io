About Iron Maiden

Melina Vostokoff was a Russian espionage agent whose career was constantly overshadowed by Natasha Romanoff, the Black Widow. Her resentment eventually pushed her away from government service and into freelance assassination under the name Iron Maiden. She became one of several killers hired to murder Black Widow, attacking alongside assassins such as Black Lotus, N’Kama, Kono Sanada, Snapdragon, and “Deadshot” Darrance. Iron Maiden lasted longer than the others and nearly overpowered Natasha before James Woo arrived with a S.H.I.E.L.D. squad, forcing her to escape. Afterward, Iron Maiden continued working as a mercenary and villain. She joined Superia’s Femizons as one of her lieutenants, later became part of the Thunderbolts during the Civil War period, and eventually joined Remont Six, a group of Soviet revolutionaries who attacked an A.I.M. installation in Russia. Across these appearances, her story stays tied to espionage, mercenary work, her hatred of Black Widow, and her use of armored power to prove she was more than Natasha’s shadow.
