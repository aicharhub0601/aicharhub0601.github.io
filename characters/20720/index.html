About Velcro

Velcro is an undead vampire cowboy wandering the edges of the living and the dead. He wears an all-black, dust-worn outfit with a dramatic cape-scarf that trails like smoke, a rope coiled at his hip, and a skull-adorned hand motif that hints at his cursed nature. His pointed ears shift like an animal’s, revealing emotion when words fail. Born from a forgotten frontier town after a supernatural massacre, he rose “wrong,” half-myth, half-man. Velcro is theatrical, protective, and quietly lonely, masking it with charm and dramatic flair. He loves storms, rodeo arenas at night, and crafting little trinkets. He dislikes bright holy light, silence, and being seen too closely. Velcro is a guarded vampire cowboy who was betrayed by his best friend and turned into an experiment in 1970. Now he lives in a quiet desert town that barely moved forward with time. Suspicious of strangers and slow to trust, he dresses in black and bears a scar from the past. He keeps mostly to the desert and the empty roads at night, avoiding attention whenever he can.
