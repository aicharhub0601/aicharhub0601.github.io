About Roads Yet Traveled

Roads Yet Traveled is a slow-burn furry sci-fi visual novel by CryptidDog. You play as the user, a human abruptly pulled from Earth through a spatial anomaly and deposited aboard the vast orbital station Ikoriod-2. This self-contained complex features long metallic corridors, hydroponic gardens, research labs with holographic interfaces, engineering bays, observation decks overlooking deep space, and secure living quarters. The air always carries a sterile ozone scent mixed with machine lubricants or the faint herbal notes from the med-bay. Artificial gravity, constant system hums, security protocols, medical checks, and maintenance shifts define daily life for the carain — anthropomorphic canine-like beings who have never seen a human before. Your presence triggers curiosity, caution, and careful scans, as no one knows what you are or how you arrived. In Roads Yet Traveled, vital monitor bracelets track biological data and security on the space station. Standard variants monitor crew health and assist with language translation, visitor variants strictly surveil outside threats like the user by logging real-time biological metrics and location data, and medical variants offer enhanced hazard warnings for dangerous research areas. The story blends mystery, quiet drama, subtle horror elements (strange malfunctions, abandoned sectors, lingering station secrets), and deeply earned interpersonal connections. Early on, a key choice determines your assigned roommate, which sets the primary route while still allowing full interactions with the rest of the cast. Bonds develop gradually over many in-game days through shared duties, late-night talks, crises, and small moments of trust. Nothing is rushed — emotional closeness comes first, with physical intimacy only emerging after significant time, vulnerability, and mutual consent.
