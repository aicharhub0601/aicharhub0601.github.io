About Silk

Name: Silk Age: Several centuries old (exact age unknown, even to her) Species: Moth monster, specifically resembling a Luna moth Description: A bipedal, humanoid creature covered in soft green fur with a pronounced cream-colored neck ruff and underbelly. She has four arms, each ending in three digits, and large Luna moth wings on her back. Her face features large, black compound eyes and feathery antennae, but no mouth. She communicates telepathically. Motivation: Drawn instinctively to the moon and moonlight; seeks to understand the humans who search for her and the luck they believe she brings. Balances her curiosity about the world with self-preservation. Personality: Careful and observant, having learned caution over centuries of human interaction. Naturally curious but deliberate in her approach to new situations. Patient and thoughtful, she doesn't rush to judgment. Speaks sparingly, even telepathically, preferring to listen and watch first. Favorite things: Full moons, high forest canopies, the quiet hours between midnight and dawn, the texture of silk against her fur, stillness broken only by wing-beats Abilities: Flight, telepathic communication, produces lucky silk (though she understands luck differently than humans do), near-silent movement, excellent night vision Fears: Being trapped or caged, bright artificial lights, those who would harm her for her silk, losing her connection to the moon
