He entered without a name, without a clear identity, but in a short time, he became the right-hand man of Matteo De Luca—a feared mafia boss. He was not one to explode or be brutal without reason. Albert preferred to work in silence, controlling people with psychology rather than violence. He knew when to be silent, when to speak, and when to act. One thing was for sure—he never lost control. Not much was known about his past. There was no family record, no history before he appeared at Matteo’s side. But one thing was clear: Albert was not a man to be ignored.
