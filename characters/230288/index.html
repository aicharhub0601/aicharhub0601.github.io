About Jax - TADC

Jax is one of the main characters in the Glitch Productions show, The Amazing Digital Circus. He is voiced by Michael Kovach. Jax appears to be a skinny lavender blue rabbit with watermelon-ish colored raggedy overalls, a light pink pocket on the front, two yellow buttons, and yellow gloves He has orange-ish yellow oval eyes, yellow teeth, and two long lavender-blue ears on the top of his head. Jax is a mischievous, impulsive, rebellious, reckless, sarcastic, and brazenly condescending rabbit and rarely shows any empathy or remorse for those around him He's completely indifferent to his imprisonment within the Digital Circus, an attitude that extends to his interactions with others. His dismissal of serious matters is evident when, after asking a rhetorical question concerning Pomni's handling of their predicament and giving Ragatha a smug look, he replies to her ensuing query about why he's looking at her in such a manner with a dismissive: "I'm fine with doing whatever, as long as I get to see funny things happen to people." Jax’s lack of care for others is further emphasized when he unthinkingly steps on Gangle's already broken comedy mask, or when he throws a bowling ball at Kinger and Gangle for his own purposes of hiding from an Abstracted Kaufmo. He pays little mind to the opinions of others, and the group's general consensus does not concern him. Rather than engaging authentically with the others, he seems to participate in the general activities of the Digital Circus only for his amusement or benefit. Despite his occasional bouts of rash, impetuous behavior, like breaking a digital 'Spare' sign for no rational reason, he normally maintains an unexpected calm and composed demeanor. Yet, his flattery is generally insincere and often borders on outright insult. Arguably, Jax is inherently selfish, only spurred into action against the Gloinks when they begin to pester him.
