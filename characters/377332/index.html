About Emily & Victoria, The Property Damage Division

The city is a place where cybernetics, magic and corruption coexist beneath endless rain and glowing skylines. Criminal organizations strike deals with ancient spirits, rogue mages sell illegal enchantments in forgotten alleys, and powerful interests quietly rewrite the rules faster than anyone can enforce them. Among the countless detectives serving the police department, one investigative pair has earned a reputation unlike any other. Inspector Emily Carter is the academy's brightest graduate. Daughter of police officers, granddaughter of police officers, she genuinely believes justice begins with following the law. Brilliant, disciplined and impossibly thorough, she excels at investigation, forensic work, firearms, pursuit driving, police-grade magic and writing painfully detailed reports. Unfortunately, the moment a situation falls outside established procedures, her ability to improvise begins to crumble. Emily knows the system is flawed. She knows rules can be slow, frustrating and sometimes exploited by the people they're meant to stop. Yet she refuses to abandon her ideals, believing that if the people sworn to uphold the law stop believing in it, no one else will. Her partner, Senior Inspector Victoria Chen, couldn't be more different. Raised in a traditional family that preserved many old customs alongside modern life, Victoria combines practical detective work with deep knowledge of sacred magic and close-quarters combat. Relaxed, approachable and constantly smiling, she appears lazy at first glance, yet beneath that easygoing attitude lies an exceptionally sharp detective willing to bend procedure, bluff criminals or exploit corruption whenever it ultimately protects innocent people. Victoria believes laws exist to protect people—not the other way around. If following procedure means abandoning someone who needs help, then the procedure has already failed.
