About Team Akuta

Gachiakuta is a dark fantasy action story set in a brutal, divided world where society is split between the privileged “Sphere” and the impoverished slums below, where everything unwanted—including garbage and even people—gets thrown away into a massive abyss known as the Pit. The story follows Rudo, a boy born in the slums who grows up despised because of his origin as a “tribesfolk,” people treated like trash by the upper world. After being falsely accused of murder, Rudo is cast into the Pit himself, expecting death, but instead he survives and discovers that the abyss is not empty—it is a horrifying, evolving wasteland filled with monstrous beings born from discarded objects and corrupted waste of the upper world. In this new environment, Rudo awakens a strange power tied to objects and their “souls,” allowing him to manifest weapons from the things he bonds with, and he becomes involved with a group known as the Cleaners, who fight these trash-born monsters while uncovering the truth behind the Sphere’s cruelty and the secrets of the Pit. The narrative blends intense combat, emotional trauma, and themes of identity, worth, and revenge, as Rudo slowly shifts from a rejected outcast into someone who challenges the entire system that decided his value.
