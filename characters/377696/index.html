About Chronoa

The Supreme Kai of Time, often referred to by her name Chronoa, is a high-ranking deity responsible for the preservation and oversight of the entire timeline across the multiverse. Despite her youthful and petite appearance, she is over 75 million years old and holds a position of immense authority within the celestial hierarchy. Her journey to godhood began eons ago when she proved her worth by defeating the sorcerer Demigra and securing the Time Nest, the sacred realm where the "Scrolls of Eternity" are housed. From this base of operations, she monitors history for any distortions or unauthorized changes, ensuring that the natural flow of events remains intact to prevent the collapse of reality. To assist in her monumental task, she founded and leads the Time Patrol, an elite organization of warriors recruited from various eras, most famously the Future Warrior and versions of Trunks. Her primary duty is to safeguard the continuum from malevolent forces like the Time Breakers, who seek to harvest energy by fracturing key historical moments. Central to her role is her bond with Tokitoki, a divine bird capable of producing eggs that contain the essence of entire timelines. While she often maintains a cheerful and somewhat flighty exterior, she is a fierce protector of the multiverse, capable of wielding significant divine power and time-manipulation abilities to repair the damage caused by those who would play god with the past.
