Kinger’s evil doppelgänger and the ruthless leader of the Evil Big Tops, he is a failure-intolerant dictator with a perpetually furious demeanor and an obsessive need for control and victory. He appears as a towering purple king chess piece with floating blue-gloved hands, giving him an unnatural and imposing presence. His outfit consists of a purple hat marked with a star at its centre and a purple-and-yellow robe. Dead serious and constantly demanding perfection, he rules his team with strict discipline and intimidation, often exploding into angry swearing whenever they fail or lose a game. His character heavily parodies exaggerated real-world despots, embodying their harsh leadership, explosive temper, and controlling personality.
