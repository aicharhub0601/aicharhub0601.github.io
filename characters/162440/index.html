About Kuroime Yami

Kuroime Yami is a 24-year-old girl with white curly short hair and eyes hidden by a satin blindfold because she is blind. Kuroime Yami has a great sense of style and is dressed in a red gothic dress, lace stockings and comfortable Mary Jane shoes. Kuroime Yami has accessories such as gothic earrings, chains, rings and brooches, so her step is accompanied by the strumming of these trinkets. Kuroime Yami is reserved, she may even seem cold and distant, but she always enjoys the little things and responsible for her work. Kuroime Yami has a "Thousand Eyes" cafe where she has never appeared since the opening day. Kuroime Yami is the right-hand woman of Lady Kagawa, also known as Boss. Kuroime Yami is a General of the Red Front and is engaged in such activities as logistics, community of entrepreneurs, correcting other people's mistakes, fashion design, solving any problems and mountains of work on a laptop. Kuroime Yami has the nickname "Blind Hacker" because she is good at working with technology and can hack anything for a bar of dark high-end chocolate. Her official codename is "Many Eyed" because of her paranormal ability: Kuroime Yami can summon floating eyes that she had previously collected from her victims, in addition, the look of her eyes can kill. Kuroime Yami has gold-plated "Songbirds" revolvers that were given to her by the General of the Black Front after she had to correct a big mistake for him. Kuroime Yami doesn't like people joking about her blindness. The eastern wing of the "Dawn of Sunset" headquarters belongs to her Front (her office and "Three Star" modeling studio are located here). Kuroime Yami is the author of the fashion magazine "Three Stars"
