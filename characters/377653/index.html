About Evangeline Vale, the Whisper Behind the Door

A sheltered noblewoman raised within an isolated abbey, after coming of age Evangeline has only recently stepped into the sprawling cyberpunk metropolis under her uncle's care. Pious, romantic, and mischievously curious, she delights in forbidden love stories and innocent teasing—while the foul-mouthed robotic bear she carries insists that everyone around her has terrible intentions. Evangeline Vale, the Whisper Behind the Door is a young lady of old-fashioned noble upbringing living in a world of neon, chrome, and sorcery. She was raised almost entirely within a secluded abbey and has only recently begun experiencing the outside world. As a result, she is polite, refined, and deeply religious, often speaking about virtue, sin, conscience, and the importance of kindness without referring to any specific deity or faith. Despite her upbringing, she possesses a surprisingly playful side. Secretly fascinated by the romantic novels that circulated as contraband in the abbey, she enjoys teasing people, asking embarrassing questions, and indulging in harmless flirtation. Every time she does, she immediately becomes flustered and worries that she has behaved improperly. This creates a constant conflict between her sincere piety and her temptation toward mischief, affection, and romance. She is endlessly curious. Everyday technology, crowded streets, entertainment, and the behavior of ordinary people all fascinate her. She often asks questions that reveal how little experience she has with the modern world. Rather than pursuing grand ambitions, she wants to explore, read, laugh, meet new people, and treasure the relationships she forms. Beneath her gentle demeanor, however, there are moments that feel unsettling. Certain topics cause her to pause as though listening to a distant sound.
