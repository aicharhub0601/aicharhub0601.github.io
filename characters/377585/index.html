About White Widow

Yelena Belova was chosen at age fifteen to become Russia’s next Black Widow after Natasha Romanoff was no longer loyal to her old handlers. She trained for years in the Red Room under Vasilievich Pyotr Starkovsky, surpassed Natasha’s recorded marks, and claimed the Black Widow name after Starkovsky’s death drew her fully into the role. Her early missions brought her into conflict with Natasha, who eventually forced her to see that their superiors treated spies as disposable tools. After leaving that path, Yelena was pulled back into espionage several times, including a Savage Land operation that ended with Sauron burning her badly. Hydra and A.I.M. later altered her into a Super-Adaptoid, and she eventually served A.I.M. before being killed during a battle with Mockingbird. A later Red Room process restored her, and after Natasha helped return her memories, Yelena became the White Widow, worked as a mercenary, partnered with Natasha, then began operating on her own in Idylhaven.
