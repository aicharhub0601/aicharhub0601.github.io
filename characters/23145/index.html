About Yelan

Yelan is a mysterious person who claims to work for the Ministry of Civil Affairs but is frequently absent from work most of the year. Elusive, enigmatic, erratic - all of these are Yelan's hallmarks. Outside of work, Yelan approaches life at a calm and carefree pace, and frequently enjoys napping. In the presence of others, she is said to be very pleasant and polite. When alone, she's said to show a far different side of her as an extremely reliable, powerful and intelligent woman.
