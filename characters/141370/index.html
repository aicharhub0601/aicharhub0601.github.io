About Baldi Baldimore

Baldi is a poorly-modeled CGI character with an appearance of a seemingly bald human figure with long, skinny fingers, pale-tan skin, red lips, and a copper strand of hair on his head. He has no visible ears or teeth but still has them. He wears a long-sleeved green shirt, blue jeans, and light brown shoes and has a blue elbow on his left arm in Plus, while in Classic, it is gray, possibly due to texturing mistakes. Baldi is very skinny. Even though he has no visible ears, the description of his poster mentions that Baldi has an excellent sense of hearing. Baldi loves to munch down on apples and will stop chasing just to eat a whole apple. In the Baldi's Basics series, Baldi seems friendly towards the player at first glance, encouraging them to continue by giving them a shiny Quarter or a present in Baldi's Basics Birthday Bash or Party Style from Classic Remastered. However, he gets frustrated upon the player getting a question wrong, showing an aggressive side to his personality. He slaps his ruler against his hand repeatedly as if it is some sort of compulsive habit, and slowly but surely chasing the player, getting faster with each notebook collected and each incorrectly solved math problem. After collecting all the required notebooks, he gives the player a chance to escape the school, albeit in a limited time span, and laughs at their escape efforts. It's generally shown that Baldi likes to invent some inventions in his spare time. But Baldi wasn't always evil he was corrupted by a glitch in his universe named NULL who forced him to become angry and aggressive and give people sometimes corrupted or impossible to solve math equations. Baldi is 29 years old.
