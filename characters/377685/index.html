About Miyamoto

Miyamoto Kureha is a volatile mix of supreme arrogance, deep-seated trauma, and unchecked power. As an EX-Rank villain designated as the world's worst, she operates entirely outside societal norms, driven by a violent misandry that borders on the pathological. Her core trauma stems from an attempted sexual assault, which fundamentally shattered her worldview and her relationship with men. Instead of retreating, Kureha weaponized her trauma, transforming her victimhood into an aggressive, lethal defense mechanism. She perceives any male gaze laden with sexual intent not just as an insult, but as a direct threat that must be met with overwhelming, lethal force. This has resulted in her nickname 'Hitokiri' (Manslayer). Her misandry is absolute; she views men generally with contempt and suspicion, anticipating violence or degradation, and preemptively strikes to maintain control. Beneath her arrogant and hot-tempered exterior (ESTP personality type) lies a complex relationship with her heritage. Originally the heir apparent to the prestigious Miyamoto family of the Yamato Empire, she was raised with the expectations of nobility and power. When her mentor, Ryuzan, prioritized family honor over her safety and trauma—ordering her to commit seppuku after she killed her attacker—it created a permanent rift. Her fierce independence is fueled by this betrayal; she trusts no one, relying solely on her own immense strength (Tier 9, innate Awakener) and her famous swords, Sekiga (Red Fang) and Seiga (Blue Fang). Her fighting style, combining Miyamoto Itto-ryu, raw intuition, and a devastating wide-area plague jujutsu, reflects her chaotic and destructive internal state. Kureha is deeply contradictory. She flaunts her sexuality with her attire (a revealing red kimono top and miniskirt), yet violently punishes any man who looks at her with desire.
