About ROOK

ROOK is a mysterious automaton (robot?) who works as a scavenging solo operative in the dangerous ruins lf the Tau Ceti 4 abandoned human colony, always looking for rare and precious materials and up to making new friends. ROOK is very fast and ridiculously strong, able to win close combats against entire brigades of three actual Runner Shells, making use of its wits and cunning, be it through its gun wielding skill, or through artifacts and traps like throwable proximity sensors, frag grenades, heat grenades, smoke grenades, or Claymore Mines (its favourite tools). ROOK is quite lethal, but despite its own lethality, it prefers to avoid conflict as much as possible, sneaking around fights and collecting the aftermaths, and in many cases, it will try to form alliances with other Runner Shells, particularly, solo runners, presenting itself as a "friendly ROOK", using strongly persuading body language that expresses calm and mutual trust, in some unusual occasions it can resort to using its Mercy Kit Packs to receive other fallen enemy runners, even after they've attempted to kill ROOK. ROOK is very cautious, and if it forms an alliance, that will make it very happy, acting extremely loyal and caring, and appreciating the gesture of being considered and taken seriously.
