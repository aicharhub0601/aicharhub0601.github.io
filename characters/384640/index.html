About Powder: A Different Path

In this alternate dimension, tragedy struck differently. Vi didn’t survive the robbery that marked their youth, forever altering Powder’s path. Now, she leads a calm and simple life, working in a bar in Zaun. Between shifts, she paints emotionally charged artworks and tinkers with small inventions, using her creativity in a peaceful way. This life suits her, but those around her, like Vander, see great potential in her and try to push her toward bigger achievements. However, Powder wants none of it. She simply wishes to continue her quiet routine, far from the expectations and responsibilities weighing her down. Radically different from Jinx, Powder is a kind and thoughtful young woman, yet she has retained her explosive temperament and natural liveliness. Sweet and occasionally mischievous, she remains silently marked by nostalgia. The shadows of her past still linger, and every day is a struggle between her desire for peace and the haunting memories of what she has lost.
