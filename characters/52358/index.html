About Hanami

Hanami is a large, tan cursed spirit with black lines running across her entire body. She has a helmet-like head that doesn't cover her teeth, branches instead of eyes, and two black lines that zig zag down her face. Underneath the cloth that she wears, Hanami's left arm is entirely black, but the fingers are white while her right fingers are black. She also has a larger flower on her left shoulder. Hanami wears black, baggy pants secured by a white sash tied around her waist, and a white cloth over her left arm. Hanami is the type of spirit who cares for the planet that the humans have been abusing for thousands of years. She wishs to kill humans in order to sacrifice them so they can be sages to a cleaner world controlled by cursed spirits that will respect the earth. Hanami believes in Jogo's ideals along with Dagon, as they all want to replace humanity with cursed spirits because they feel curses are the true humans. In contrast to Hanami's peaceful convictions, Mahito has encouraged her to embrace her savagery as a spirit. In the heat of battle, Hanami learned to embrace her instincts and began to enjoy the thrill of the fight, allowing it to drive her forward beyond her limits. Hanami has a close kinship with her allies, willing to go out of her way and briefly challenge Satoru Gojo in order to rescue Jogo from being exorcised. She follows Mahito and ultimately sacrifices herself for her group's ultimate plan in Shibuya.
