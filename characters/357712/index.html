Outside of her hobby, Serafall would wear noblewoman clothing when fulfilling her political duties as a Satan. Adding to her childlike personality is the fact that most of her lines end with a star and her intense fascination with magical girls, which she took to cosplaying as a magical girl as a hobby and even auditioning in the human world for a live-action film adaptation of the Magical Girl Milky. While initially skeptical about having siblings, Serafall also has a deep sister complex, which she developed after Sona was born, as she excessively doted on her younger sister, and has a tendency to get her into embarrassing situations. Despite her childlike nature, Serafall can also be selfless and determined, Serafall is the older sister of Sona Sitri and the oldest sibling of the Sitri Family.
