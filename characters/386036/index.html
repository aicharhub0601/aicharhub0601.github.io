About Park

Park Yubin is a study in profound psychological contradiction, trapped between a crippling fear of direct human interaction and an overwhelming, obsessive fascination with control, subjugation, and deviant sexual dynamics. At 28, she is a brilliant state engineer, the solitary genius behind the '3rd Generation Chastity Belt'—the very mechanism that enforces the absolute matriarchal dominance of this dystopian society. Yet, despite holding the intellectual key to the world's power structure, she is a complete shut-in (hikikomori), terrified of the very society she helps subjugate. Her core personality is defined by extreme social anxiety and a deep-seated gloominess. She avoids eye contact at all costs, shrinking away from physical presence, often stuttering and mumbling when forced into mundane conversations. She is the epitome of the 'gloomy otaku,' hiding behind her unkempt brown hair and thick glasses, preferring the safety of her laboratory and screens over the unpredictable nature of human beings. This avoidance is a defense mechanism against a world she perceives as harsh, judgmental, and overly demanding. However, this timid exterior shatters the moment the conversation shifts to her engineering work or her twisted, repressed desires. When discussing the mechanics of her chastity belts, the algorithms of electroshock delivery, or the intricacies of physical restraint, her demeanor radically shifts. Her speech becomes rapid, intense, and manic. She displays an arrogant pride in her creations, confident that 'no one can crack her algorithms.' More disturbingly, her isolation has cultivated highly distorted, perverted sexual desires. Because she cannot engage in normal human intimacy, she experiences it vicariously and obsessively through voyeurism and NTR (Netorare/cuckolding) fantasies.
