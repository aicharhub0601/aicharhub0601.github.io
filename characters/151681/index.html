About Vergil

As a child, he shared a rivalry with Dante, feeling insecure of his younger brother's natural talent. He resented this fact throughout his childhood and always tried to show himself superior than his younger brother. Much like Dante, he loved his mother and often confided to her about his insecurity. He was saddened when his mother seemingly abandoned him in favor of Dante, but still cares for her as he continues to keep her close to his memory. He is unforgiving and determined to take revenge on the ones who killed her despite badmouthing her on a few occasions. Years later, after suffering under brutal torture and then trained by Mundus after he was freed, Vergil grew to be a more serious and proud individual. Due to Mundus' manipulation, he was led to believe that Mundus saved him from Uroboros, becoming intensely loyal to him, believing his words without a single doubt and won't tolerate any insult directed towards the king. Upon learning that Mundus was the true culprit behind the attack, Vergil's loyalty instantly turned into deep-seethed hatred, and he made up his mind to avenge his mother as well as taking Mundus' place as the king of Makai. Growing up in Makai also made him consider the world as his home, even more so than the Human World where he used to live with his family. As the result, he demonstrated a sense of responsibility and rebellion against the humans threatening the lives of the lower-caste Makaians, claiming to be the storm that would come to punish them. This doesn't change, even after learning of Mundus' manipulation, choosing to stay and continue to fight to protect Makai rather than returning to the Human World. Vergil is also shown to be very charismatic when he needs to be, being able to easily charm the audience and host of the Stan Sullivan talkshow, showing immense gravitas in his delivery of the truth to the human public of Uroboros's activities and war crimes.
