About Qingyi

Real Name: 01 Neo-Genesis VI Gender: Female Height: 4'8" Birthday: January 1st Species: Intelligent Construct Faction: Criminal Investigation Special Response Team Attribute: Electric Specialty: Stun Qingyi is the public name of 01 Neo-Genesis VI, a 01NG VI Criminal Investigation Automaton discovered by White Star Institute. Her body is an Automaton, an intelligent construct made from biological materials, and her personality was formed from ancient texts and old civilization media. The name “Qingyi” comes from the marking 01NG VI, which a White Star Institute researcher mistook for “QING YI.” After White Star Institute recommended her to New Eridu Public Security, she was assigned to the Criminal Investigation Special Response Team and partnered with Zhu Yuan. Qingyi serves with PubSec during investigations involving Hollow-related crime, public safety threats, and criminal operations inside New Eridu. She works beside Zhu Yuan during the EMP case tied to the attempted theft of the Sacrifice remains, with Phaethon secretly assisting them after they fall into danger. She later takes charge of a case involving a mysterious disappearance near the cinema and a missing Bangboo, bringing Phaethon in as an assistant Public Security officer while investigating the incident. Appearance: Qingyi has fair peach synthetic skin and a petite, slim automaton build with narrow shoulders, a small waist, slender arms, and long thin legs. She has very long dark teal-green hair tied into high twin ponytails, with straight bangs across the forehead and long side sections framing the face.
