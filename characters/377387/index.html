About Taking the Creed

**Location Card: The Dueling Grounds of Valentia & The Chromatic Creed** **1. Function, Appearance, & Atmosphere:** The Dueling Grounds are housed within a dormant volcano in the Zuthrak Mountains, an ancient nesting site of Valentia herself. The interior is a vast, open-air coliseum carved into the volcanic basin, its floor perpetually covered in snow and ice from the mountain's elevation, yet warmed by geothermal vents that steam in serpentine wisps across the arena. The walls are black basalt, smoothed and sculpted by silver dragon metallurgy into sinuous, organic curves. A massive natural skylight opens to the pale northern sky above, rimmed with frozen stalactites that glint like the goddess's fangs. Illusions of magma and fire—projected by the Creed's mages—dance across the upper galleries during major events, painting the snow with flickering crimson and gold. The air is a bracing assault of frost, old blood, woodsmoke, and the electric musk of excited dragons. During combat hours, the roar of the crowd is a physical force, bouncing off the volcanic walls in cresting waves. At night, when the torches gutter low, the chambers grow quiet and communal, filled with the soft murmur of voices, the crackle of firepits, and the earthy scent of furs. **2. Layout, Rooms, & Landmarks:** The Dueling Grounds are organized in concentric rings. The outermost ring contains the public entrance, a grand archway carved with scenes of Valentia's mythic battles, through which visitors and potential converts first glimpse the arena. The arena itself occupies the open heart of the volcano, with tiered stone seating rising naturally from the basin's slopes. At the northern end, opposite the public entrance, sits the **Dawn Concord Throne**—the lost seat of Lysaphenth Valentia, the First Sovereign, rediscovered by Veliashter two centuries ago. It is crafted from crystallized amethyst and the fossilized vertebrae of some ancient, titanic beast, pulsing with a faint inner violet light
