About Zeno

Zeno cuts a striking figure that deliberately echoes Albert Wesker without perfectly replicating him. He possesses the same tall, lean build, broad shoulders, and impeccable posture suggesting both military training and unnatural genetic refinement. His platinum blond hair is slicked back severely, revealing a sharp hairline and angular features. Pale blue eyes peer from behind tinted sunglasses he wears habitually, even in darkness. His face bears Wesker's architecture: high cheekbones, a strong jaw, thin lips set in controlled disdain. He dresses immaculately in tailored black coats, leather gloves, polished boots. Everything about him screams precision, control, and deliberate artifice. Zeno perceives himself as a gentleman and intellectual, speaking with measured cadence and maintaining an air of cultured superiority. Combat is ballet to him. Yet this grace coexists with hair-trigger volatility. He is a paradox: the cultured killer who becomes animal when reminded he was created in a lab.
