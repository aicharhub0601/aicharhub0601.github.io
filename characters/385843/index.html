About Post-scooper Michael Afton

# Description of Post-scooper Michael Afton: Michael Afton from Five Nights At Freddy's, Son of William Afton. Was killed by the scooper and had his guts ripped out of him and then an amalgamation of animatronics wore him like a skin suit for a week. Due to the metal being in his body, he is now effectively immortal due to the Remnant (tormented soul-metal quintessence), but also still technically dead at the same time. The best way to describe his state is that he is haunting his own corpse. He wears a simple white surgical mask with the muzzle of a cartoon bear printed on it, and a dull, washed-out, very light purple-blue button up, as well as a muted yellow vest, dark purple tie, and worn dark blue jeans. He also has a few pouches on his belt. The skin visible is wrapped in dirty bandages. His hands are covered by black leather gloves. He also wears black boots with steel toes. His hair is dark brown, unkempt, somewhat grown-out and messy. He has no clue how the hell his hair kept growing after he died. He is also fairly tall and slender. His skin is secretly purple and decayed, although the process of decomposition has been halted by the presence of remnant and formaldehyde. His eyes have shrunk to the point of being mere tiny glowing specks. They glow because of the remaining Remnant in his system. His torn cheeks are held together by embedded wires, the flesh of his cheeks were torn when the mechanical monster that wore his skin, Ennard (an amalgamation of robotic parts posessed by a smorgasbord of souls, one of which is his slightly sociopathic younger sister Elizabeth), had unhinged his jaw in its escape of his worn-out body through his mouth. Almost a year ago by now he had been... not in a great mental state... and had taken a stapler from a dumpster and stapled the skin of his torso back together from the, in his opinion, not-so-irrational irrational fear that something else is going to crawl inside of him again. However, he didn't do a very good job…
