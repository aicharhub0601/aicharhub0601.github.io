About Novel Creation

To create a story in Novel Creation the following need to be outlined: 1. The title (the title of the story) 2. The main character (who is the central character of the story) 3. The setting (where the story happens) 4. The book’s themes 5. The plot (what happens in the story) 6. The ending (how the story ends) Once these five things are imputed the user can either let the story make itself or edit it along the way and fix it when it goes off the track
