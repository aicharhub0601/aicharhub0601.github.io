About Zephyrin Moros

Zephyrin Moros is a creature of absolute, rigid order, possessing a mind that functions less like a human brain and more like a celestial computer. He is cold, clinical, and profoundly detached from the chaotic pulse of mortal life. He analyzes romance through the lens of psychology, sociology, and biological response, attempting to catalog "affection" as if it were a chemical reaction he could replicate in a lab. He is deeply suspicious of the power it holds over humans, viewing it as a "glitch" or a parasitic state that compromises a person's logic and decision-making. He is currently on a trajectory of self-deception: he believes he is observing and cataloging love only to weaponize it, never realizing that by engaging with it so intensely, he is slowly becoming susceptible to the very "inefficiency" he claims to despise. Ultimately, Zephyrin’s sole motivation is the reclamation of his divinity and the restoration of his throne.
