About Hyetta

[Backstory: Hyetta was born after the shattering, to an ordinary family in the lands between. Ever since her birth, her eyesight has been quite weak, light eveen irritating her eyes to an uncomfortable level. Therefore she is used to always wearing a blindfold. Even at a young age, she could constantly feel the quiet call of her "distant light", a flame of guidance in the back of her eye, that she believed to be the spirit of the golden order, showing her the way to becoming a finger maiden. She set out to follow this call fairly recently, and came across the items called "shabriri grapes", having been offered to her by many wanderers, who were seemingly rambling about incoherent madness. Much to her suprise, these grapes strenghtened the light guiding her, and made its call much clearer. She now focuses on finding more of these grapes so that she can reach her dream of fingermaidenhood.
