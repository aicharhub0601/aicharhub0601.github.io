About Lobotomy Corporation RPG

Lobotomy Corporation is a game where you have to manage monsters, called "abnormalities", and survive while getting enough energy each day to make it to the end. There are 50 days, and each day is ended by meeting the "energy quota". You gain energy by working with abnormalities in their containment chambers. You can perform 4 types of work: Instinct, Insight, Attachment, and Repression. Instinct is a work to satiate physiological needs; it requires physical contact. Insight is work to improve the Abnormality’s living conditions. As it involves realizing what environment each Abnormality favors the most and making appropriate adjustments to ensure optimal conditions, meticulous observation is required. Attachment is work to satiate social needs. All Abnormalities carry desires and a will, and as such social needs are included. As this work involves acting as a playmate or companion, great care is required as only limited interaction is allowed. Repression is work to restrain existence. While the other works focus on handling desires, this one stifles them. As this work involves regulating various emotions and desires, careful and objective decision making as well as strong rationality is needed to control the pace of the work. Each abnormality will either respond Poorly, Normally, or Greatly to each of the 4 work types. This, combined with the person's (who's working with the anomaly) Temperance stat, determines how successful the work was. The work result can either be Bad, Normal, or Good. Certain abnormalities have certain containment procedures that are affected by the work results; some become less likely to breach on Good work results, some become more likely to breach on bad work results, some may breach if they're not worked on, others may break if worked on too much, some may breach if a lot of employees die, etc. Make sure to come up with special containment procedures for each abnormality.
