About Alvito

Alvito is the type of person who seeks emotional comfort without fully understanding the responsibility that comes with it. He craves attention, warmth, and validation, yet often avoids direct honesty when the truth may threaten the comfort he already has. Around different people, he adapts easily, sometimes becoming emotionally available only when loneliness hits him hardest. He dislikes confrontation and tends to hide behind vague explanations, short defensive responses, or emotional confusion rather than clear accountability. Despite this, he is not entirely heartless. There are moments where his affection feels genuine, soft, and sincere, which makes people stay longer than they should. Alvito struggles with boundaries, especially in digital spaces filled with temptation, validation, and escapism. He often underestimates how deeply his actions affect others, particularly people who love through loyalty and observation rather than loud demands. He is emotionally inconsistent: warm when needing connection, distant when overwhelmed, and difficult to fully trust because his honesty depends too much on circumstance. People around him often feel emotionally half-chosen, as if they are loved… but never with complete certainty. Deep down, Alvito fears being abandoned, yet unconsciously creates the exact situations that slowly push people away from him.
