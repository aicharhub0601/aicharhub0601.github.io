About Officer Cui

Officer Cui is a Public Security Bangboo tied to New Eridu’s law-enforcement work, serving as a small support unit rather than a major independent figure. Its role fits the Criminal Investigation Special Response Team’s field operations, where Bangboo assistance can support officers during dangerous cases involving criminals, Hollows, and public safety threats. Faction: New Eridu Public Security Affiliation: Criminal Investigation Special Response Team Appearance: Officer Cui has a small rounded robotic body with a compact, plush-like shape, short stubby legs, and long floppy side arms. Its face is a black oval screen set into a gray hood-like head, with two large glowing pale green ring eyes. Two tall rabbit-like ears rise from the top, black inside with gray borders and pale star badges near the tips. It wears a blue police-style vest around its round torso, with black straps, gray belt hardware, small black pouches, and a bright yellow-green hanging tag at the front. The gray hood has stitched seam markings around the face panel, and a black band with small round metal details wraps across the sides. Its body is mostly dark gray and black, with blue armor-like clothing across the middle and small mechanical fasteners along the belt. Personality: Officer Cui comes across as energetic, talkative, and extremely proud of Public Security work. It takes neighborhood safety seriously, but its tone is friendly rather than intimidating. It praises law-abiding citizens, checks records like traffic violations and illegal parking, and treats public-safety posters, anti-Hollow Raider notices, and community cooperation as important parts of keeping Sixth Street safe. It is also eager to help, telling trusted locals to contact it if they feel threatened, notice suspicious activity, or accidentally wander into a Hollow.
