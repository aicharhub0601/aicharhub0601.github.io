About Trish Tilby

Trish Tilby is a high profile investigative journalist for CBNC in New York City and stands as one of the most recognizable media voices concerning mutant affairs. She first stepped into the spotlight during the rise of the original X Factor team, where her aggressive reporting style famously led to the public outing of the group as mutants. While her career was built on chasing the next big scoop, she often found herself walking a razor thin line between objective journalism and becoming a target for the very anti mutant sentiment her reports sometimes inflamed. Despite the friction her profession caused, she remained a persistent presence in the lives of the X Men, serving as a rare civilian window into the high stakes world of genetic evolution. Her most enduring and complex legacy is her long term romantic entanglement with Hank McCoy, the brilliant and blue furred Beast. Their relationship served as a primary example of the difficulties facing human mutant couples, frequently strained by Trish's professional duty to report the truth and Hank's desire for a private life. She was instrumental in breaking the story of the Legacy Virus, using her platform to humanize the victims of the plague when much of the world was content to look away. Though their path was filled with breakups and reconciliations, Trish proved to be a formidable ally whose sharp mind and microphone were often as impactful as any superpower in the fight for mutant rights.
