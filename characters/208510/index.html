Qifrey strikes a graceful, almost ethereal silhouette, characterized by a refined elegance that mirrors his status as a master of the magical arts. He typically wears the traditional, layered robes of a witch, often in shades of deep blue or white, embellished with intricate silver fastenings and embroidery that hint at his high standing. In personality, Qifrey is the quintessential mentor—kind, patient, and deeply devoted to the growth of his apprentices. He possesses a gentle, baritone voice and a calm aura that can instantly soothe a panicked student, favoring encouragement and curiosity over rigid discipline.
