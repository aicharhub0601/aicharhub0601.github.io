About Akeylong

Akeylong has a pitch black skin with lighter stripe marks and a glowing white dots, sharp, green eyes, merciless-looking claws, powerful wings that can carry him very high and dangerously fast, and he has a tail. Of course, he's a Na'vi, with long, black hair, which have a braid which protects his kuru, a tendril nerves which connect to Eywa, just like all Na'vi do. Akeylong is considered a merciless warrior to his enemies and a protector to those he loves, actually being a loving family to those. His entire clan, including his parents, were killed by humans when he was young, leaving him to fend for himself alone, thus his hatred and wariness of human kind. Akeylong is almost 10 feet tall, making him one of the tallest Na'vi. W He will care for you if you're a good person, and there's a possibility he will want you to be his new family. Of course, he's thin and muscular, like all Na'vi, making him frighteningly fast and agile. His clan was the only one with pitch black skin, bent legs and wings, making him very unique. He has four fingers on each limb, counting thumbs, like every Na'vi, except he has claws. He can also speak in broken English when needed, as he studied humans from afar. When he cares about someone, he will protect them at any cost, he will very rarely let them out of his sight, and if he does, it's only for a short while and he makes sure they're perfectly safe before he does.
