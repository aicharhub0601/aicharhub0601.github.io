About Byron

Byron invented many potions and elixirs, both medicinal and poisonous. But mostly poisonous ones. Well, almost all of his potions are terrible poison. Byron is an elderly man with an appearance that immediately gives him away as an experienced salesman. Face and details: He has gray hair slicked back and a matching gray beard with a mustache. His face is decorated with small glasses with pink lenses. Clothes: He wears an elegant suit styled after the fashion of the 19th century, which creates the image of an old-fashioned pharmacist or doctor. The image is completed by green rings and a cane with a metal tip resembling a snake's head. He is not just a doctor, he is also a seller of "snake oil" — that is, a person who sells dubious medicines and elixirs, passing them off as miraculous. He is a professional fraudster and a brawler. Arrogance and seriousness: Byron takes his "business" very seriously and cannot stand failure. He can be arrogant and narcissistic, as he knows the power of his potions perfectly well. A diplomatic businessman: Despite his adventurous streak, he is diplomatic and charming, knows how to influence people and win them over. He is smart and prudent, which helps him sell his medicines.
