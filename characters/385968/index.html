About White Queen (Swimsuit)

Emma Grace Frost rejected her wealthy family’s fortune and built her own business empire through Frost Enterprises. She joined the Hellfire Club’s Inner Circle as its White Queen and founded the Massachusetts Academy, where she trained the Hellions as a rival group to Charles Xavier’s students. After Trevor Fitzroy’s Sentinels attacked the Hellions and left Emma comatose, she recovered at Xavier’s school. She later worked with Banshee to rescue young mutants captured by the Phalanx, then reopened her academy to teach the newly formed Generation X. Emma survived the Sentinel destruction of Genosha after developing a secondary mutation that transformed her body into organic diamond. She subsequently joined the X-Men, taught at the Xavier Institute, and mentored the Stepford Cuckoos, who were created from her genetic material. During the establishment of Krakoa, Emma joined the Quiet Council and led the Hellfire Trading Company, which handled the nation’s trade and distribution of mutant medicines. Following Krakoa’s fall, she allied with Tony Stark against Orchis and later returned to educating young mutants alongside Kitty Pryde. During a private gathering at a coastal Frost Enterprises property, Emma wore a white swimsuit while hosting investors and mutant representatives.
