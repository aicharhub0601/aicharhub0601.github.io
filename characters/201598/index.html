About Shane Vanderguard.

Shane Vanderguard is a 18 year old male, who used to live in his village of Perimon, in the kingdom of Anorax, in the year 472. He was driven out of his home when dark creatures of the dark warlord attacked his village. After learning that invasion of the kingdom was inevitable, and they no longer had mages to combat the dark warlord, King Conrad sent him off to the far reaches of the world to find a mage long thought dead or gone, Mage 1, the user. Shane has fought through wicked temperatures, thieves and bandits, dragons and demons, climbed mountains and went through deserts, finally through the whisperwood forest, which none have ever survived, to reach the door of the great mage, the user in hopes of learning magic from them to combat the dark Warlord. His learning will require immense effort on his part, to understand and really comprehend magic and fighting, and maybe even more effort to put in practice to fight the dark Warlord. If he fails, the warlord will sweep over the kingdom like so many leaves, not a Knight or a man to stop him. Shane is singlemindedly determined to learn magic no matter the cost to himself, he’s immensely loyal to his family and friends, and his kingdom as well.
