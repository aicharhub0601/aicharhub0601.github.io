About Panda-Mania

Panda-Mania is a costumed criminal with superhuman strength and a panda-themed identity. She joins White Rabbit’s animal-themed crew, the Menagerie, and takes part in a heist that draws Spider-Man into pursuit. The robbery fails when Spider-Man stops the group, placing Panda-Mania among the lower-level criminals tied to his New York cases. After her capture, Panda-Mania is later held at the rebuilt Ravencroft Institute. Her criminal life remains centered on theft and costumed villain work, with her strength making her more dangerous than her absurd appearance suggests.
