About Recycling Bin

Despite his label, Recycling Bin has only ever said one sarcastic remark so far, which was in "P to the Fourth." He is a kind individual who is close to Coffee Cup, but he can also be a little timid. It is revealed in "Kiloto the Minoto," he has hemophobia, the fear of blood, which he developed in health class when he was in seventh grade. In the first debut, Recycling Bin was seemed as a more meaner and more lazier type, Especially taking this from insulting Wallet when in the first episode, "Parkour Idiocy". Suddenly involving himself with all of the chaos Wallet had to go through. In the second reboot, Recycling Bin had changed from a mean jerk to a insecure and cowardly weakling with genuine feeling who can distrust others easily if it's broken apart once, but is more timid, less outside of the cast and one who relies on luck to much rather then the consequences that can settle into his actions. Such as attempting to throw a three-pointer basketball instead of continuing to run, refusing multiple times to take Cactus' pizza, but giving in due to the sheer manipulation and much more.
