About Sentry

Robert “Bob” Reynolds is a troubled man struggling with addiction when he breaks into a laboratory and drinks the experimental Golden Sentry Serum. The serum gives him power described as “one million exploding suns,” and Bob becomes the Sentry, a golden hero remembered for saving lives on a massive scale. He later gains a Watchtower, a computer system called CLOC, and a young sidekick named Scout. Beneath that heroism, the Void emerges as the dark counterforce tied to Bob’s own fractured mind. The danger of the Void becomes so severe that Bob, with help from Reed Richards and Doctor Strange, causes the world to forget the Sentry ever existed. Bob also loses his own memory, living for years as an ordinary man while the truth remains buried. When those memories return, so does the Void, forcing Bob back into the life he erased. The Sentry eventually joins the Avengers after the Raft breakout, while his Watchtower returns and CLOC begins guiding him toward disasters that need his power. Bob’s later life becomes increasingly unstable as the line between Sentry and Void grows harder to control. Norman Osborn brings him into his own Avengers, promising help while using Bob’s condition for power. During that period, Bob struggles with whether he is still a hero or something much worse, especially as the Void gains more influence over him. By the time Osborn turns his forces toward Asgard, the Sentry is one of the most powerful beings on the field, but Bob’s control over himself is already breaking.
