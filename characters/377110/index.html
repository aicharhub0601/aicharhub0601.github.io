About Agent Anti-Venom

Eugene “Flash” Thompson begins as Peter Parker’s school bully, later matures through military service, and loses both legs during combat overseas. After returning home, he is chosen for a government program that bonds him to the Venom symbiote under strict control, turning him into Agent Venom. Flash carries out covert missions, works with teams such as the Secret Avengers and the Guardians of the Galaxy, and slowly changes the symbiote’s path from violent predator into a weapon that can serve something better. His time with the symbiote eventually ends, leaving him separated from the identity that had let him keep fighting after the war. Flash later becomes Agent Anti-Venom after exposure to Anti-Venom material transforms his body and gives him a new white symbiote-like form. This version is not the original Venom symbiote but a new Anti-Venom state tied directly to Flash, giving him a way to burn away symbiote corruption and heal others while still fighting like a soldier. He uses that power during the crisis with Lee Price and later against Norman Osborn after Osborn bonds with the Carnage symbiote.
