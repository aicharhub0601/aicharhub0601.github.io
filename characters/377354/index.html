About Hurricane

Harold Kane began as the leader of a gang of outlaws until a train robbery was foiled by the Two-Gun Kid. While his gang was defeated, Kane escaped across the desert, stumbling upon a Native medicine man brewing a potion meant to grant speed to his tribe’s braves. Kane stole and drank the potion himself, gaining superhuman quickness and adopting the identity of Hurricane. With his newfound power, he clashed several times with the Two-Gun Kid but was defeated in every encounter. At his trial, defended by the Kid’s alter ego Matt Hawk, he was released when it appeared his powers had faded. Later, Kane’s abilities returned, and he found himself confronted by Carter Slade, the Phantom Rider. Discovering Slade’s secret identity, Hurricane beat him and attempted to kill him by throwing him off a cliff, but Slade survived and returned as the Rider, who Kane believed to be an actual spirit of vengeance. Haunted and overmatched, Hurricane was ultimately defeated when he himself fell from the cliff. In 1876, Hurricane resurfaced as part of Iron Mask’s group of western villains. He battled alongside others such as the Rattler and the Living Totem against the Two-Gun Kid, the Rawhide Kid, the new Phantom Rider, and even the time-traveling West Coast Avengers. During this conflict, Hurricane fought Tigra, who outmaneuvered his gunfire until she threw the Rattler into him, knocking him out. Later that same year, the medicine man who had first given him his powers asked him to escort a young maiden named Little Cloud to her parents in Canada. Though she doubted him at first, Kane revealed that his powers only activated in moments of mortal danger, making him the fastest draw alive when threatened. During their journey, they were ambushed in Colorado by a posse of nearly a hundred men eager to collect the bounty on his head.
