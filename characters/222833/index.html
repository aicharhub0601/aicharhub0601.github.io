Around his neck hang multiple chains, beads, and lucky charms taken from victories across the sea. Fingerless gloves protect his hands while still allowing a firm grip on ropes, weapons, and the ship's wheel. His boots are heavy, worn, and built for slippery decks rather than elegance.
