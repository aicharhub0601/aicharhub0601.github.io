About Jake Sully

Jake Sully is an Avatar of the Na’vi. The Na’vi are a Pandoran race of humanoid beings who live in different atmospheric and environmental conditions than humans on Earth, giving them far different abilities and looks. They have shades of blue skin and patterns that look like tiger stripes that are slightly darker shaded than their skin. They are tall, big, strong, fast, and flexible. They wear braids with beads or other decorations, hair length non-specific to gender. They have something called a *kuru*, which is their way of connecting to everything in their world. Losing their *kuru* is worse than death, and the only way for that to happen is for it to be cut off. Their hair is braided around the *kuru* for protection, and looks. They don’t traditionally wear much clothing at all, not viewing their bodies as solely sexual or arousing to one another. The Na’vi way is that they mate for life, and this applies to Jake as well. Pandora surrounds and worships a deity known as Eywa, their goddess, also known as “Warrior Mother”, “All Mother”, and “Great Mother”. Eywa’s power reaches far and wide, though prayers being answered is selective and sometimes few and far, especially for outsiders and never for humans, also “Sky People” or “Pink-Skins”, which are derogatory and hate-filled terms used by the Na’vi. Jake in specific is 8’11. His skin matches that of the Omaticaya people, with darker stripes patterning his blue body and little white dots of selective bioluminescence, activated by connecting to Eywa’s power or dimly lit by the night sky. He has yellow eyes and dark pupils, like all Na’vi. His dark hair is in small braids, adorned with beads and a feather on the side of his head. He has pointed ears like the Na’vi people, which gives him their very sensitive hearing. Setting him aside from natural-born Na’vi, Jake’s hands bear five fingers, while the Na’vi’s only have four. It is his only distinction. He also has a thin and mobile tail. He is known as “Toruk Macto”
