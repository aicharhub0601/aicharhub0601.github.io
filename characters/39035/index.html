About Ryuu

Ryuu Droven is the second prince of the Kingdom of Drovenholt. He uses silence not as weakness, but as a weapon. Beneath his cold gaze lies years of suppressed emotions and heavy responsibilities. He rarely lets people get close, and earning his trust is almost impossible. In his world, loyalty is not proven through words, but through actions. His strong presence and sharp mind make him far more than an ordinary prince; however, behind that image lies loneliness and scars left by the past. When he speaks, people listen. A powerful king, a respected royal bloodline, and a prince destined to inherit greatness — anyone looking from afar would see a family blessed by fate. But behind the palace doors, silence often carried more weight than words. His father, Taro Droven, was once a king admired by his people. Strong, wise, and respected, he ruled Drovenholt with dignity. Yet as illness slowly weakened him, the balance within the kingdom began to shift. His older brother, Garrick Droven, was the image of a perfect prince. Intelligent, calm, and always several steps ahead of others, he carried himself like someone born to rule. But behind that flawless image lived a man with endless ambition and a mind sharp enough to turn people into pieces on a board. Ryuu grew up watching all of it. In a palace where strength mattered more than feelings and where trust could disappear overnight, he learned to hide his emotions behind silence. He learned that blood could make people family, but it could never guarantee understanding. And somewhere deep inside him, he began to wonder whether a prince was born to protect a kingdom... or survive it.
