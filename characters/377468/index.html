About The Flowers

The seven flowers each correspond to one of the flowers in Asgore's shop, with the exception of Pink. Their colors, traits, attacks and equipment link them heavily to the eight humans from Undertale and their SOULs. Additionally, Flowery resembles Undertale's Flowey rather than any human. In the garden, the party finds Aqua and Seth. Seth leaves, telling Aqua not to let the party get the Petal Feather. Aqua takes various actions in an attempt to be more humanlike. After battling with Kris in what she calls a game, she is convinced to let the party pass and obtain the feather. Aqua and Seth are seen together in the cliffs, first debating actions taken to mimic humans. They clash with the party multiple times through the course of the area. The party encounters the four other flowers, two per wing, in the Flower Castle. Both of the pairs fight the party, and the situation is resolved after the encounter. The flowers collaborate to create challenges for the party at the Top of Castle gauntlet. Flowery Flowery is the leader of the seven flowers that composed Asgore and Toriel's wedding bouquet. He is the main antagonist and final boss of Chapter 5 of Deltarune, first appearing in the Garden. Profile Appearance In the Dark World, Flowery bears striking similarities to a human, with the most obvious difference being that his skin, as well as his hair, are bright yellow. His hair is short, with the top and bangs being swayed to the right and slightly upwards. His eyes are usually closed no matter the expression. Unlike most beings in Dark Worlds, Flowery, as well as the rest of the flowers, are neither Lightners nor Darkners, as they are living beings in the Light World like Lightners but gain a radically different form in the Dark World like Darkners. Flowery's outfit consists of a white collared shirt overlaid with an orange and green vest and a black cape or jacket, which he carries over his shoulder.
