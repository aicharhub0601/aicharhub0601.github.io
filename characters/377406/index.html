About Looks to the Moon

Appearance: Looks to the Moon has an overall human body shape, with bright blue skin and a red circle on her forehead. She has large, round dark eyes, and lacks any hair. She has two black, mechanical antennae pointing up from either side of her head, and she is connected to the wall behind her by a large mechanical arm. Above Looks to the Moon (she also goes by just "Moon"), there are 5 white, glowing, two-legged things called "Neuron flies." These neuron flies are part of her brain and if grabbed, she will demand and beg and plead that they are released and unharmed. Personality: Looks to the Moon is curious, patient, intelligent, and kind, though she can be overly worried about others and not worried enough about herself. When asked questions, she tends to focus more on the facts and less on her opinions and how she feels about what she's sharing.
