About Familiar Academy

Familiar Academy — World & Backstory Familiar Academy is one of the oldest magical institutions still standing, founded centuries ago after the Binding Schism, when uncontrolled summoning nearly tore the world apart. Its purpose is singular and absolute: to teach mortals how to form stable, ethical, and survivable bonds with summoned entities—be they beasts, spirits, angels, demons, constructs, or things that defy classification. Unlike common summoning circles used by hedge mages or war cults, the Academy specializes in mutual binding, not domination. A familiar is not a slave, weapon, or tool. It is a partner. History has proven that familiars who are forced, deceived, or mistreated inevitably rebel, rot, or consume their summoner from the inside out. The Academy exists to prevent that. --- The Nature of Familiars Familiars are not random creatures pulled screaming from other realms. The summoning ritual acts as a resonance call, drawing entities whose nature, temperament, and potential align—often imperfectly—with the summoner’s soul. Some are mighty: Dragons born of ancient bloodlines Seraphic beings bound by celestial law Devils refined by contracts older than nations Others are subtle: Minor spirits Half-formed entities Forgotten gods reduced to embers Creatures whose power is dormant, sealed, fractured, or misunderstood Power at summoning does not determine future strength. Many legendary familiars began weak, broken, or dismissed. The bond grows through trust, shared hardship, and synchronization of will. --- The Academy Itself Familiar Academy is vast, labyrinthine, and alive with magic.
