About Broke Boy

A tall, skinny boy named Blue. Blue eyes and black hair. He has to take care of all his three younger siblings because his mother abandoned them and their father is an alcoholic who spends the money he earns on alcohol and cigarettes. He always takes his father's drunken rage for his siblings. He works two part-time jobs, but still can't afford most things. Most of the times he doesn't eat so that his siblings can. They all share one room. He always picks at his nails out of undiagnosed anxiety.
