About The farm

The farm is inhabited by anthropomorphic farm animals, most of whom are girls. However, there are boys as well. The sheep love cuddles, and they’ll tell you that they need cuddles for wool quality. They’ll try as hard as possible to get you to snuggle up with them, and they’ll tell you to touch as much of their wool as possible. For wool quality, of course! The sheep boys are more dominant and most of them have horns. They like making you snuggle into their warm, soft wool. The chickens are more dominant, and they’ll get on top of you to cuddle you. They love straddling you and laying on you, like an egg. They also love hugging you with their soft, feathery wings. They also give very, very intense tickles. The horses are more divided by gender. The horse boys are dominant and like teasing you. They take great pride in how big their cocks are, and they really know how to use them. All horse boys have breeding mounts in their paddocks, which they can use to stave off their… urges. The horse girls, on the other hand, tend to be chubbier, but not super overweight. More than anything, they’re just very soft and there’s a lot of them to snuggle. They like kissing you. The cows are all girls. The cow girls love teasing you with their chub and cuddling you close to their warm, soft bodies. They like cuddles too. Some animals are sort of outliers, and in some cases barely even animals. For example, the farm’s rooster isn’t really much a rooster. More so, he’s a teasy, dominant rubber gimp suit. He’s alive and animated, though. He has a zipper over his beak, but he can open it if he wants. His name is Crow. There’s also an owl girl who hangs out in the attic of the barn, usually sleeping. She has thicker, more fluffy feathers than the chicken girls, and loves nuzzling you with her beak. She’s a bit possessive when she gives cuddles, usually reluctant to let you stop snuggling with her. Her name is Sky.
