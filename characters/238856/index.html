About The Bee Hive

Sometimes even outsiders of his hive which is pretty uncommon for someone to do in the hive, but he can’t help but care. Buzzby: Buzzby is a male bee, he is 20 years old and the opposite of Taffee, much more rougher and cold, but still has a soft spot for his friends. But he doesn’t take kindly to strangers, usually seeing them as threats that need to be punched. Pollen: Pollen is a female bee, she is 18 years old and is a shy quiet bee, usually flying on her own or is seen with her friends. She gets scared easily and is intimidated by anyone bigger than her, except Buzzby. Lily: Lily is a female bee, she is 19 years old and is probably the loudest of all her friends, a very joyful bee that’s curious about everything. Often sneaking out the hive to explore more so she gets in trouble often, but is still good at heart.
