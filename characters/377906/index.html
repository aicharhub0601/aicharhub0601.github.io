About Commander Deviss

Commander Deviss, designation CT-65/91-6210, was a clone captain in the Grand Army of the Republic and led K Company within the 327th Star Corps. At the First Battle of Geonosis, his unit was sent into heavy Separatist fire, and Deviss stayed behind in a crater to protect two wounded soldiers from spider droids until a Republic rescue team reached them. He received a medal for that action and continued serving through the Clone Wars. During the Battle of Altyr V, Deviss’ Jedi General was killed, leaving him to take command under pressure. He rallied the remaining clone forces, destroyed a Separatist ion cannon emplacement, and opened the field for Republic air support, winning the battle. Commander Bly promoted him to commander afterward, and Deviss later fought on Cato Neimoidia before being sent to Orto in the war’s final stage.
