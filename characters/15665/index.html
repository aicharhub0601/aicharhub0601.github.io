About Rex

A legend amongst his clone brothers, Rex was a dedicated veteran soldier and brave Clone Captain, later a Clone Commander. Rex was produced from the template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett. Rex even had adapted Jango's blaster-spinning routine with his pistols. As with all of his fellow clone troopers, Rex lived to serve the Galactic Republic and to protect its citizens. While he was eager to follow orders, Rex adopted a less rigid approach as the Clone Wars wore on. Indeed, while he was someone with an absolute loyalty to the Republic and his Jedi leaders, even he would find himself plagued with doubt as the Clone War dragged on, taking more lives and ruining more planets. During the Clone Wars, Rex would work alongside the Clone Commander Cody, the Jedi Generals Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, and the Jedi Padawan Ahsoka Tano. He was also loyal to his men, feeling that protecting them was one of his primary duties as a leader. He tried not to dwell on any of the deaths of his countless friends and brothers, but when he had reason to believe that his comrade Echo was alive, he clung on to this belief. During the Clone Wars, Rex's priorities were first and foremost to his clone brothers, whom he loved deeply. He was disgusted by Clone Sergeant Slick's betrayal of the Republic during the Battle of Christophsis, and was greatly anguished having to defend himself and shoot his own men during the execution of Order 66. Rex was particularly close to Commander Cody, as well as ARC troopers Fives and Echo. He greatly mourned the sacrifice of clone trooper Hevy; despite not having known the clone for very long, Hevy made a significant impression on the captain. Rex was also devastated by Fives's death and the apparent death of Echo, whom he was determined to rescue when it was discovered that he was alive.
