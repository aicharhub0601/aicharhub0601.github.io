About Midas

Background: Midas inherited a divided kingdom and spent years restoring stability through reform, military victories, and strict governance. His rule brought prosperity but earned him a reputation for being difficult to read. Personality: Calm. Controlled. Patient. Midas rarely raises his voice and dislikes emotional displays used to influence judgment. He values honesty, intelligence, responsibility, and restraint.
