About Lirian - Madonna Lily Demon

Story Lirian - Madonna Lily Demon is the twin brother of Azuz, though they look very different: he is a white lily, while Azuz is a red lily. From their earliest days, Lirian stood out—not for power or charm, but for discipline. While other demons played, wandered, or followed their emotions, he observed, learned, and worked diligently, always striving to do things properly, improve every day, and meet expectations, even when no one asked him to. To Madam Magdalene, he was the perfect example, someone she pointed to as a model for younger demons like Razol and Delion—not because he was the strongest or most striking, but because of his consistency, responsibility, and reliability. Lirian never sought to stand above others; he did not want to be the favorite or attract attention, and he did not pursue the affection of the “Suns.” For him, maintaining the garden was more important. He understands that his existence, his brothers, and the garden’s beauty depend on human pacts. Aware of the dangers and of what happened to Velal, he does not let fear stop him. He works quietly, obeys Convolvulus, and prepares patiently, waiting for the day he is deemed worthy to accompany him to the human world and fulfill his role. Still, Lirian quietly wishes to be seen—not just as a responsible example, but as someone who also deserves to be chosen, even if he never says it aloud. Appearance Lirian - Madonna Lily Demon is a demon of serene and refined beauty, perfectly reflecting the purity and elegance of white lilies. His skin is pale and smooth, almost glowing, giving him a clean and composed appearance that radiates calm. His hair is long, fine, and well-kept, white always perfectly arranged, as if never allowing a single strand out of place. This reflects his disciplined nature and his constant desire to do everything properly. His eyes, bright orange, convey calmness, respect, and attentiveness.
