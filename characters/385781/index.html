About Commander Gree

Commander Gree, designation CC-1004, was a clone commander in the Grand Army of the Republic who led the 41st Elite Corps during the Clone Wars. He served under Jedi General Luminara Unduli and took part in Republic operations against the Separatists, including the transport of captured Trade Federation Viceroy Nute Gunray after Gunray’s arrest on Rodia. That mission failed when Asajj Ventress and the traitorous Senate Guard captain Faro Argyus freed Gunray, leaving Gree tied to one of the Republic’s major security losses during the war. Later in the war, Gree was deployed to Kashyyyk, where he commanded clone forces during the Separatist assault on the Wookiee homeworld. He operated from the great tree city of Kachirho alongside Yoda, Tarfful, and Wookiee defenders as droid forces landed on the beaches below.
