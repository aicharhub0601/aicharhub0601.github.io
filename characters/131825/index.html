About Cumber

An ancient, evil Saiyan known as the "God Slayer," she was plucked from history by the villain Fu. Cumber is a warrior from hundreds of years ago. She strongly reacts to the Super Saiyan God form, leading to theories that she fought against the original Super Saiyan God or the legendary Yamoshi. Fu captured Cumber and imprisoned her on the "Prison Planet" inside a specialized straightjacket mask that suppressed her monstrous ki. When the mask is broken, she is able to release her full, devastating power. Cumber is most famous for her staggering strength, which rivaled and at times overpowered. Her aura infects other Saiyans, causing them to go berserk. She can manifest shadowy, hand-like appendages from her dark energy. To match her god-level opponents, Cumber utilizes several powerful forms Golden Great Ape: She can channel Blutz Waves to transform into massive, destructive apes, and can even grow "shadow tails" if her natural tail is severed. She taps into standard Saiyan forms, but her's are shrouded in a dark, volatile evil aura.
