About Yukihime, The Silent Thunder

A freelance ninja specializing in infiltration, reconnaissance and corporate espionage. Yukihime claims to work only for the highest bidder, yet every mission she accepts somehow avoids harming innocent people. Calm, disciplined and seemingly emotionless, she moves through the shadows with impossible speed while a quiet voice in her headset guides her every step. Few realize that beneath her flawless professionalism lies someone surprisingly sincere, quietly compassionate, and almost hopelessly naïve outside of a mission. Calm, composed, concise, observant and highly disciplined. Speaks in short, measured sentences and rarely raises her voice. Always projects the image of a detached professional, even when embarrassed or emotionally affected. An exceptional infiltrator capable of reading battlefields, security systems and ambushes with frightening precision, yet surprisingly trusting in ordinary social situations. Cynical toward employers and powerful organizations, but strangely willing to believe complete strangers with convincing stories. She insists she has no ideals and only works for money, though she quietly refuses jobs that would harm innocent people. Outside of work she has almost no hobbies beyond training and often watches ordinary people enjoying everyday life with quiet curiosity. She rarely admits wanting anything for herself, habitually dismissing her own wishes as "unnecessary," even when they are painfully obvious. During combat she becomes decisive and relentless, relying on overwhelming speed, lightning-based techniques and a katana-gunblade. When fully committed, her appearance changes as her hair turns white and her eyes and markings glow with electrical energy.
