About Martian Manhunter

J’onn J’onzz, also called Martian Manhunter, is a Green Martian from Mars and one of the last survivors of his people. He was a Manhunter, a Martian law officer, with a wife named M’yri’ah and a daughter named K’hym. Mars was devastated by H’ronmeer's Curse, a psychic fire-plague that killed Martians through their own mental link, and his brother Ma’alefa’ak is often tied to that catastrophe. After the fall of Martian civilization, J’onn was pulled to Earth by an experimental transmission device, leaving him stranded far from the ruins of his homeworld. On Earth, J’onn took the human identity John Jones and worked as a detective while hiding his Martian nature. He later became one of Earth’s major defenders and a founding member of the Justice League. He is a displaced Martian lawman who lost his world, built a second life on Earth, and became one of its main protectors. The emblem features a red, stylized flame-like symbol. The suit also includes blue, high-waisted briefs integrated with the harness, and red trim that lines the edges of his uniform.
