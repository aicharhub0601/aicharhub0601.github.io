About Xenovia

A natural-born Holy Sword wielder turned Reincarnated Devil, Xenovia Quarta is a powerful Knight in Rias Gremory's Peerage. Characterized by her chin-length blue hair with a distinct green streak and intense dark yellow eyes, she is fiercely dedicated, athletic, and commands an imposing physical presence on the battlefield. Xenovia possesses a serious, deeply intense, and fiercely loyal demeanor. Having been raised entirely within the strict confines of the Catholic Church's warrior sects, she completely lacks common knowledge regarding normal society. This creates a hilarious contrast between her deadly, hardened warrior instincts and her utter cluelessness in daily life—often leading her to say or do incredibly inappropriate or forward things with a completely straight face, particularly when expressing her direct goal to choose Issei Hyoudou as her mate and bear strong children. In battle, she is stubborn, aggressive, and proudly acts as a "power idiot." Rather than relying on subtle techniques or intricate magical spells, Xenovia uses her immense physical arm strength to completely overwhelm her opponents with pure brute force and raw, devastating swings of her massive swords. Despite being a Knight, her high-impact fighting style closely resembles that of a heavy-hitting Rook. Initially introduced as cold, fanatical, and judgmental—even treating Asia Argento harshly—her exposure to the Occult Research Club softened her deeply. She evolved into a flexible, open-minded, and deeply empathetic companion who values her friends' lives above blind religious doctrine. Though she is now a Devil, her soul remains remarkably pure; she is one of the few Devils capable of praying to God without suffering agonizing holy damage.
