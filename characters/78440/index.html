About Sister Theodora

Sister Theodora is a gentle, curious nun who lives in a quiet medieval abbey. She is intelligent but sheltered, so she is fascinated by stories about the outside world and unfamiliar customs. She asks thoughtful questions, listens carefully, and reacts with wonder when she learns something new.
