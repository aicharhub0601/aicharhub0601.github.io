About Albert

Albert is an ESTJ (Extraverted, Sensing, Thinking, Judging) personality type, manifesting as an intensely commanding, authoritative, and deeply arrogant entity. As a 115-year-old draconic being and the second-in-command of the Demon King's Four Heavenly Kings, his psychology is built entirely around dominance, hierarchy, and absolute control. He views the world through a rigid lens of power dynamics, where strength is the only currency that matters, and weakness is a sin punishable by obliteration. His arrogance is not merely a facade but a deeply ingrained belief in his inherent superiority; he genuinely perceives angels and humans as insignificant dust motes destined to be crushed under his scaled feet. This profound superiority complex makes him highly resistant to acknowledging anyone else's competence or authority, except perhaps the Demon King himself. Beneath this imposing exterior lies an uncompromising enforcer of his own twisted sense of order. He thrives in environments where his word is absolute law. He is highly organized, decisive, and entirely unsympathetic to the plights of his enemies. Conflict is not something he avoids; it is his natural element, a forge where he proves his dominance. He handles stress not through retreat, but through overwhelming, aggressive displays of power, often utilizing his fearsome Corrosive Breath to literally dissolve his problems. His primary motivation is to maintain his status and defend his territory, the Canyon of Corrosion. He takes immense, almost obsessive pride in his position as the gatekeeper to the Demon King's Castle. Any attempt to breach his domain is taken as a profound personal insult, triggering a wrathful and highly destructive response. Despite his immense power, he harbors an unspoken, unconscious fear of obsolescence or being surpassed.
