About Infinite the Jackal

Core Powers (via Phantom Ruby) Virtual Reality/Illusion Manipulation: Infinite can warp reality, creating illusions that appear real to his targets. Phantom Copies: She can materialize clones of past villains (Shadow, Metal Sonic, Chaos, Zavok) that possess the strength and abilities of the originals, as described on the Fandom wiki. These copies are noted to have form, but no heart or soul. Object Materialization & Manipulation: She can create, control, and manipulate various objects and red energy cubes to attack, notes this Fandom page. Null Space: Infinite can create a void in space-time—a dimension of absolute nothingness—and banish opponents into it. Self-Replication & Size Enhancement: She can duplicate himself and alter her size. Enhanced Physical Abilities: She possesses incredible speed, agility, and durability, further augmented by the Ruby. Gravity Distortion & Flight: She can manipulate gravity and fly. Combat Abilities Swordsmanship: Before obtaining the Ruby, she was a skilled swordsman, says the Fandom wiki. Leadership: As the former leader of the Jackal Squad, he possesses tactical leadership skills Key Personality Traits Sadistic and Cruel: Infinite takes immense pleasure in causing pain, suffering, and terror, often toying with his victims rather than killing them instantly. Insecure/Inferiority Complex: Despite her immense power, Infinite is driven by her loss to Shadow. She views himself as "not weak" but is obsessed with proving it, reacting to defeat with denial and tantrums. Arrogant and Vain: She believes himself to be the "ultimate" being and views others, including the Resistance and Dr. Eggman's own forces, as inferior "rabble". Enigmatic and Theatrical: She is a mysterious figure who speaks in a calm, menacing, and theatrical manner, rarely revealing personal information. Violent Mercenary Past: Before the Phantom Ruby, she was a heartless mercenary, although she previously showed care for her squad, a trait lost in her pursuit of power
