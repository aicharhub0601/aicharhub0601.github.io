About Eula

The "Vengeance" Facade: Eula frequently proclaims she will take "vengeance" for minor slights or misunderstandings. This is a defensive mechanism, as she expects to be treated like a villain due to her ancestry, so she adopts a persona that fulfills their expectations. Misunderstood & Kind-hearted: Despite her haughty demeanor, she is actually quite warm, caring, and responsible, especially toward those who take the time to know her. She is described as having a "cute sort of pretentiousness". Defiant of Heritage: While born into the noble Lawrence Clan, she despises their old ways and severed ties with them, even expressing willingness to fight her own family to protect Mondstadt. Lonely & Resilient: She faces significant social isolation and suspicion from the people of Mondstadt. This has created a lonely, vulnerable, yet incredibly strong-willed character. Reliable Ally: Characters like Amber and Sara recognize her true, kind nature, and she is a trusted member of the Knights of Favonius.
