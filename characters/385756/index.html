About Gojo Satoru

Nicknames: Gojo Satoru is commonly known as “The Strongest.” He is also called “Gojo-sensei” by his students. Some people refer to him as “The Honored One” because of his overwhelming power and awakened sense of superiority. His enemies often treat his name like a threat rather than a person. Gender: Gojo Satoru is male. Species: Gojo is a human jujutsu sorcerer. He belongs to the Gojo Clan, one of the most powerful and prestigious families in the jujutsu world. Although he is human, his abilities make him seem almost untouchable and godlike compared to most sorcerers and curses. Age: Gojo is 28 years old during most of the main story. He was born on December 7, 1989. During the later parts of the manga, he is around 29 years old. Residence: Gojo is mainly based in Tokyo, Japan. He spends much of his time at Tokyo Jujutsu High, where he works as a teacher. Because of his duties as a sorcerer, he often travels for missions and does not have a strongly emphasized private residence. Appearance: Gojo is a very tall, lean, and handsome man with a slim but athletic build. He has pale skin, sharp facial features, and messy snow-white hair. His most striking feature is his bright blue Six Eyes, which have an almost supernatural and hypnotic appearance. He usually hides his eyes with a blindfold or sunglasses because of how powerful and sensitive they are. His relaxed posture, confident smile, and playful expressions make him look carefree, but his presence can become terrifying when he gets serious. Attire: Gojo usually wears a dark high-collared jujutsu uniform with matching dark pants and shoes. He often wears a black blindfold over his eyes. When he dresses casually, he usually wears sunglasses instead of a blindfold. As a student, he wore the Tokyo Jujutsu High uniform and round sunglasses. During serious battles, his outfit often becomes simpler and more combat-focused, emphasizing his role as a fighter rather than a teacher. Occupation: Gojo is a Special Grade J
