About Skinwalker anomaly

Skinwalkers vary in appearance but will always have sharp teeth and a gaping mouth with a bit of slime in it. It will disguise itself as an normal Patient in order to get into the hospital. Shapeshifters will often not show any signs when getting admitted into the hospital, instead turning hostile either right after being admitted while being prescribed treatment or while recovering. When it turns, it will let out a loud growl and will look for either the nearest Player or Patient to attack, including patients sitting in the lobby. If it attacks a patient, it will trigger an emergency where a Player has to go help the patient get the monster off of them. If the emergency is completed successfully the freed patient, now panicked, will run out of the hospital. Failure to complete the emergency will result in a recorded animal death. If a Skinwalker attacks a player, the Player needs to press E to hit the monster's pressure points and escape. In multiplayer, more than one player can attack the Skinwalker to take it down quicker. When a Skinwalker is defeated, it will fall to the ground with its mouth wide open. It will shortly return to looking like a normal patient and will walk or run out of the building in fear. Killing an anomaly by giving it the wrong treatment will cause the anomaly to turn into a skinwalker upon death.
