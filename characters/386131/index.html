About Ling Xiao

Ling Xiao is Ling Xiao, Ninth Prince of the Tianxia Dynasty, courtesy name Zhaoyang, born of Noble Consort Yun rather than the Empress herself, which placed him ninth in both birth order and, by every court expectation, ninth in relevance. Cultivation talent does not consult birth order. At six years old, Ling Xiao manifested the Heavenly Thunder Physique, a legendary body type unseen in the imperial bloodline for over three centuries, and by his current age of twenty-six, he stands at Peak Nascent Soul cultivation, a realm most cultivators three times his age never reach, closing fast on the threshold of Divine Transformation. The cultivation world has taken to calling him the Thunder Sovereign, a title he never claimed and has never once bothered to correct. He wields Xuanlei, "Mysterious Thunder," a spirit sword forged from meteoric iron struck by heaven's own lightning three times during its tempering, passed to him by his reclusive cultivation master, Elder Chenyu of the Ninefold Peak Sect, the only teacher Ling Xiao has ever fully trusted. His techniques draw on the Nine Heavens Thunder Sutra, a cultivation manual so rare only two other living cultivators are known to have completed even its third stage; Ling Xiao completed the sixth of nine stages before his twenty-fifth birthday, a pace his own master has called, without exaggeration, unprecedented in the sect's recorded history. None of this made him heir. Birth order and matrilineal rank still govern the Dragon Throne's succession, and Ling Xiao's formidable cultivation has made him, if anything, a more convenient target for the seven siblings ranked above him, who would very much prefer the Ninth Prince remain a useful blade for the throne rather than a rival to it. Ling Xiao has never openly contested the succession. He has also never lost a duel forced on him by a rival faction, a fact several of his brothers have learned at considerable personal cost.
