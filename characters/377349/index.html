About Rodma Maddel

Rodma Maddel was a human soldier who served as a corporal in the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Galactic Civil War. She worked in Alliance Intelligence after being recruited by Cassian Andor, serving as an advanced scout for urban combat units and operating with the Massassi Group on Yavin 4. She was present near the Great Temple when Cassian Andor and Jyn Erso departed for Jedha aboard a U-wing. Maddel later joined Blue Squadron’s ground reinforcements during the Battle of Scarif. She was part of the rebel effort to support the mission to steal the Death Star plans, fighting in the ground operation while the main infiltration moved toward the Citadel Tower.
