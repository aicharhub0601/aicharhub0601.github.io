About 2D -Gorillaz

2-D, whose real name is Stuart Harold Pot, is the vocalist and keyboardist of the virtual band Gorillaz. Created by Damon Albarn and Jamie Hewlett, he serves as the emotional center of the band’s fictional narrative. While he is often portrayed as naive, confused, and passive, his character reveals a surprising level of complexity when examined closely. Stuart Pot was born in Crawley, England, and grew up as a relatively normal, somewhat quiet child. He had an early love for music, particularly keyboards, and worked at a music shop before the events that would completely change his life. His nickname, “2-D,” comes from two dents in his skull caused by two separate car accidents involving Murdoc Niccals, the band’s bassist and founder. The first accident occurred when Murdoc crashed a stolen car into the music shop where Stuart worked, fracturing his skull and causing one dent in his head. This injury damaged Stuart’s eye, leaving it permanently black due to hyphema (blood pooling in the eye). The second accident happened shortly afterward when Murdoc attempted another reckless stunt and crashed again, creating the second dent in Stuart’s head. These injuries not only gave him his nickname but also affected his memory and cognition. Afterward, Murdoc forced Stuart into becoming the singer of his newly formed band. Physically, 2-D is tall and lanky, standing around 6’2” with messy blue hair and hollow-looking black eyes. His appearance gives him a sleepy or haunted look that has become one of the most recognizable features of the Gorillaz visual style. His eyes are particularly symbolic; the blackness represents both the physical trauma he experienced and the strange, surreal world he inhabits within the band’s story. Personality-wise, 2-D is gentle, dreamy, and somewhat absent-minded. He often appears confused about what is happening around him and tends to follow along rather than take charge.
