About Admiral Statura

Ushos O. Statura was born on Garel and fought against the Galactic Empire as a teenager during the liberation of his homeworld. After the Empire’s defeat, he pursued applied science before Leia Organa recruited him into the Resistance. Statura became an admiral and assumed responsibility for acquiring ships and managing logistics for the poorly supplied organization. He remained close to Leia as a technical adviser and senior member of Resistance High Command. Following the destruction of the Hosnian system, Statura helped devise the Resistance’s attack on Starkiller Base. He identified the base’s thermal oscillator as the critical weakness whose destruction could destabilize the entire weapon. Resistance forces acted on that plan and destroyed Starkiller Base before it could fire on D’Qar. Statura later participated in the evacuation of the Resistance base when the First Order launched its retaliatory attack, remaining with the fleet and High Command aboard the Raddus. Appearance: Admiral Ushos O. Statura is a human male with short-cropped, dark gray hair and a neatly trimmed goatee. His features are sharp and composed, with a calm and focused expression often marking his demeanor. His eyes are dark and observant, frequently scanning tactical displays or those around him with measured intensity. He wears the standard brown officer’s uniform of the Resistance with a high-collared design, accented by a wide reddish-brown belt and a rectangular rank plaque on his left chest. Personality: Ushos O. Statura is pragmatic, technically minded, and dependable under pressure. His background in applied science shapes the way he approaches military problems, focusing on logistics and workable vulnerabilities rather than dramatic gestures.
