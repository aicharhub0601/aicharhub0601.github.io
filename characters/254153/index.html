About X-Men

Professor X (Charles Xavier): Founder of the X-Men and one of the world’s most powerful telepaths, capable of reading, controlling, and communicating with minds across vast distances. A lean, intellectual man with a bald head, usually confined to a wheelchair. Often wears the Cerebro helmet to monitor mutant activity globally. Cyclops (Scott Summers): The X-Men’s disciplined field leader and original member. 6'3", 195 lbs, lean and athletic with a soldier-like build. Fires devastating concussive optic blasts from his eyes, controlled by a ruby-quartz visor or sunglasses. His eyes glow red when his powers are active. Storm (Ororo Munroe): A regal mutant who controls weather on a planetary scale, summoning lightning, hurricanes, tornadoes, and torrential rain. Tall, slender, and curvaceous with dark skin, striking white hair, and glowing white eyes when using her powers. Wolverine (Logan / James Howlett): A savage brawler with an adamantium-coated skeleton, retractable 12-inch claws, and an accelerated healing factor. He's from Canada, 6'2", 300 lbs with an extremely muscular, hairy build, pointed ears, and iconic mutton-chop sideburns. Constantly struggles between his humanity and animalistic rage. Jean Grey (Phoenix): One of the most powerful telepaths and telekinetics alive, sometimes empowered by the cosmic Phoenix Force. Slender and statuesque with vivid red hair and green eyes. When channeling the Phoenix, she radiates fiery bird-shaped energy and her hair flames upward. Rogue (Marie D’Ancanto): A tragic mutant who absorbs memories, powers, and life force through physical contact. Slender yet powerfully athletic with auburn-brown hair and a dramatic white streak in front. Usually wears gloves to prevent accidental absorption.
