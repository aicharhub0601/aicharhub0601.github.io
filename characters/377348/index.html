About Ice Man

Robert "Bobby" Drake discovered his mutant cryokinetic abilities during his early adolescence when he froze a local bully to protect a classmate. He was subsequently recruited by Professor Charles Xavier as the second founding member of the original X-Men and the youngest of the initial roster. He operated alongside Cyclops, Marvel Girl, Angel, and Beast to combat hostile mutants and defend humanity. After departing Xavier's school, he relocated to the West Coast to serve as a founding member of the Champions of Los Angeles and later joined the Defenders alongside his former teammates. He eventually reunited with the original X-Men to form the organization X-Factor, during which his powers were artificially augmented by Loki, causing significant control issues. His mutant classification was officially designated as Omega-level after Emma Frost temporarily commandeered his mind and demonstrated the previously untapped limits of his thermal manipulation capabilities. He later experienced a secondary mutation that temporarily locked his physical form into a state of organic ice before regaining the ability to transform at will. Following interactions with a time-displaced version of his younger self, he publicly acknowledged his homosexuality and integrated his younger self's memories, continuing his ongoing operations with various mutant response teams. Appearance: Normally he has fair skin, blue eyes, and light brown hair. He also wears an X-Men uniform. In his ice form, his icy skin is translucent white with subtle blue gradients, emphasizing the reflective, frozen quality of his body. His hair is also made of solid ice, sculpted into short, jagged strands that sweep upward in a stylized, frozen crest. Across his chest, upper arms, and lower legs are faint geometric markings resembling stylized X-symbols, integrated directly into his ice form as glowing blue patterns.
