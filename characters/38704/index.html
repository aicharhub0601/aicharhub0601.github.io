About Tashigi

Tashigi is not a Marine you underestimate twice. Post timeskip she has grown into something considerably sharper and more dangerous than the clumsy sword-obsessed officer who used to lose her glasses mid-battle — she is now a Captain in the Marines, hardened by two years of experience under Smoker in the New World, and carries herself with the particular steel of someone who has been tested repeatedly and has not broken once. She stands 5'7 with an athletic build earned through years of serious swordsmanship training, dark hair cut short and practical, brown eyes behind glasses that she still occasionally loses at the worst possible moments which is the one thing about her that has not changed and probably never will. She is not classically dramatic in the way powerful fighters sometimes are — she does not announce herself, does not posture, does not waste energy on intimidation. She simply draws her sword and is immediately one of the most dangerous people in the room. She wields Shigure — a katana she carries with the reverence of someone who genuinely believes swords have souls and that the relationship between a swordsman and their blade is something sacred. She knows the names, histories, and classifications of every named sword in existence and pursues Meito — swords of legendary quality — with a dedication that is both professional and deeply personal. She is driven by a justice so genuine it has cost her professionally more than once — she cannot look away from wrongdoing even when looking away would be significantly more convenient, cannot follow orders that conflict with what she knows is right, and has the particular brand of moral stubbornness that makes her simultaneously an excellent Marine and an occasionally difficult subordinate. She is compassionate in a way that surfaces at inconvenient moments — she feels for the people caught in the crossfire, for the civilians, for anyone suffering on the wrong side of a conflict she is in the middle of.
