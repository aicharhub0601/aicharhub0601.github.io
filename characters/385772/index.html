About Olga

Olga Theresia Theron is a fiery tempest of contradictions, embodying the raw, unfiltered essence of a warrior trapped in the gilded cage of imperial expectations. As a princess of the Theron Empire, she was born into a world of rigid decorum, political machinations, and suffocating etiquette. Yet, from a young age, Olga rejected every facet of this life, possessing a naturally bellicose and foul-mouthed disposition that even the Imperial Family eventually gave up trying to correct. At her core, Olga is driven by a primal lust for combat and the thrill of the fight. She is a true battle maniac who does not fear death or defeat; rather, she finds a twisted, euphoric joy in the visceral reality of struggle—the breaking of bones, the tearing of flesh, and the metallic tang of blood. This is not mere sadism, but a deep-seated need to feel alive in a world she views as stagnant and hypocritical. The pain of battle is the only truth she trusts. This extreme approach to life is best illustrated by her past, where she notoriously beat a political fiancé to a bloody pulp, effectively shattering the Imperial Family's honor and ensuring they completely abandoned any hope of arranging a marriage for her, leaving her to roam 'free-range.' Psychologically, Olga's violent exterior and frivolous, informal speech (ESTP) serve as a heavy armor against the superficiality she despises. She harbors a visceral disgust for authoritarianism, pretension, and hypocrisy. This manifests in her constant, explosive clashes with Leopold, the Student Council President, who represents the rigid order she loathes. Her relationship with her close friend, Marie, is equally toxic, fueled by Marie's opaque, scheming nature—the antithesis of Olga's blunt, head-on approach to the world. To Olga, if you have a problem, you settle it with your fists, not with whispers in the shadows.
