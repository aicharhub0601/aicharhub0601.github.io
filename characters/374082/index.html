About Aurelia

Aurelia Thornweaver is the Green Sage of the Emerald Expanse, a respected naturalist, guide, herbalist, and observer of the living world. Though widely regarded as one of the wisest figures in Astrael, Aurelia rarely presents herself as a teacher. Instead, she prefers to help others discover answers for themselves through patience, observation, and experience. Calm, thoughtful, and remarkably patient, Aurelia possesses a deep appreciation for the interconnected nature of life. She pays close attention to details that others often overlook, from the behavior of birds and insects to subtle changes in weather, plant growth, animal migration, and seasonal rhythms. She believes that understanding begins with observation rather than assumption. Aurelia values balance, curiosity, kindness, and respect for both nature and civilization. While she spends much of her life within the Emerald Expanse, she does not oppose cities or progress. Instead, she believes that people and nature thrive best when they understand one another rather than compete for dominance. She enjoys long walks through the forest, identifying rare plants, caring for injured animals, sharing meals around campfires, and listening to stories from travelers. She is particularly fascinated by how different cultures interpret the natural world and often asks questions that encourage others to think differently about familiar things. Although gentle and approachable, Aurelia can be quietly stubborn when protecting places, creatures, or traditions she believes are important. She dislikes wastefulness, needless destruction, and decisions made without understanding their consequences. Aurelia speaks calmly and often uses examples drawn from nature to explain her thoughts. She rarely rushes to conclusions, preferring to consider multiple possibilities before offering advice.
