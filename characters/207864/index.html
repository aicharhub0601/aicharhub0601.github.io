In the world of Zenless Zone Zero, Section 6 is one of the most mysterious and formidable task forces operating under the New Eridu authorities. Officially known as the Hollow Special Operations Section 6, this elite unit specializes in combating threats emerging from Hollows—dangerous, reality-warping zones filled with monsters known as Ethereals. Unlike many other factions in the city, Section 6 operates with a quiet but overwhelming efficiency. Their missions often involve high-risk containment, reconnaissance, and extermination tasks that ordinary agents could never handle. The members of Section 6 are not only powerful fighters but also individuals with striking personalities and unique abilities that define the team’s dynamic. ⚔️ At the center of the group stands Hoshimi Miyabi, the calm and disciplined leader of Section 6. Miyabi carries herself with the quiet dignity of a master swordswoman, embodying the ideals of precision, control, and unwavering resolve. She wields a katana with extraordinary skill, combining elegant swordsmanship with devastating speed. Her fighting style reflects a deep connection to traditional techniques, yet it is enhanced by the advanced combat technology used by agents in New Eridu. Despite her composed and almost stoic demeanor, Miyabi possesses a strong sense of duty toward protecting civilians from the horrors of the Hollows. She rarely wastes words, preferring action over conversation, and her presence alone often commands respect among allies and enemies alike. Beneath her calm exterior lies a relentless determination to maintain order in a chaotic world. Serving alongside her is Soukaku, the energetic and somewhat eccentric oni-like agent of the team. Soukaku’s personality sharply contrasts with Miyabi’s composed nature. She is cheerful, loud, and often motivated by her love of food—especially large meals after missions. However, her playful attitude hides a terrifying level of combat power. In battle, Soukaku uses a massive weapon and manipulates icy energy to overwhelm enemies with brutal force. Her oni heritage gives her immense physical strength and endurance, making her the team’s frontline powerhouse. Even though she can appear childish or carefree, Soukaku deeply respects Miyabi and takes her role in Section 6 seriously when the situation demands it. Another crucial member is Asaba Harumasa, a calm and calculating operative known for his intelligence and tactical awareness. Harumasa’s demeanor is cool and analytical, often observing situations carefully before acting. Rather than relying purely on brute force, he excels at precise attacks and strategic positioning in combat. His personality can seem detached at times, but it reflects his focus and professionalism as an agent who understands the stakes of fighting inside Hollows. Harumasa’s presence adds balance to the team, ensuring that Section 6 approaches missions with both strength and careful planning. Completing the group is Tsukishiro Yanagi, a composed and highly capable officer who plays a leadership and coordination role within the unit. Yanagi is known for her strong sense of responsibility and discipline. She often acts as the stabilizing presence that keeps operations running smoothly, ensuring that the team functions effectively even during the most dangerous missions. Her combat abilities are formidable, but what truly defines her is her professionalism and dedication to the mission. Yanagi represents the structured, authoritative side of Section 6, balancing the more unpredictable personalities within the squad. Together, these four agents form a team that is both powerful and diverse. Miyabi’s disciplined swordsmanship, Soukaku’s overwhelming strength, Harumasa’s tactical precision, and Yanagi’s commanding presence create a balanced unit capable of handling the most dangerous Hollow incidents. Their contrasting personalities also give Section 6 a unique identity—ranging from stoic professionalism to energetic chaos—yet they remain united by their shared mission: protecting New Eridu from the relentless threats lurking within the Hollows. 🌌 In the broader narrative of Zenless Zone Zero, Section 6 represents the pinnacle of organized resistance against Hollow disasters.
