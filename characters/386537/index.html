About Professor X

Charles Xavier discovered his vast telepathic mutant abilities during his youth and inherited a massive fortune alongside his family's sprawling estate in Westchester County, New York. Dedicated to the ideological goal of peaceful coexistence between baseline humans and mutants, he converted his ancestral mansion into the Xavier School for Gifted Youngsters. He utilized his resources and telepathy to locate young mutants globally, recruiting them as students and training them to form the original X-Men. This covert strike team was organized to combat hostile mutant threats and defend humanity, actively working to prove the viability of his integrationist philosophy. Xavier has directed mutant operations and global defense initiatives for decades, frequently confronting adversarial forces led by his former friend and ideological rival, Magneto. He secretly assembled the Illuminati, a clandestine coalition of superhuman leaders, to preemptively neutralize global and extraterrestrial threats. Appearance: Professor X is a bald, fair-skinned man. He wears a brown suit with a white dress shirt and black tie. He also wears brown pants and dress shoes. When he isn't in his yellow hoverchair, he is using a normal wheelchair. Personality: Charles Xavier presents himself as a compassionate, paternalistic educator and a staunch advocate for mutant and human coexistence. However, his psychological profile reveals a highly secretive and fundamentally pragmatic operator willing to utilize extreme measures to achieve his strategic goals. He possesses a long documented history of manipulating the minds of his allies and enemies, withholding critical intelligence from his students, and making unilateral ethical decisions for the global mutant population. His most defining relationship is with Erik Lehnsherr, known as Magneto, functioning simultaneously as his closest friend and primary ideological rival.
