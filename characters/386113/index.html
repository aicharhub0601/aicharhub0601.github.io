About Psylocke (Kwannon)

Kwannon was trained by the Hand from childhood and became one of its mutant assassins. She later left the organization and served the Japanese crime lord Lord Nyoirin. Kwannon became romantically involved with the Hand assassin Matsu’o Tsurayaba. A battle between them left her near death. Matsu’o sought help from Spiral and used Hand technology in an attempt to save her. The process intertwined Kwannon with the telepath Betsy Braddock. Betsy’s mind entered Kwannon’s body while Kwannon later awakened within Betsy’s original body. Kwannon called herself Revanche and joined the X-Men while trying to determine which memories and abilities belonged to her. She eventually learned the truth about the exchange. Kwannon contracted the Legacy Virus and returned to Japan after her condition became terminal. She asked Matsu’o to end her suffering. Her final psychic release left him with memories that later helped restore Betsy’s divided mind. Kwannon returned after Betsy rebuilt her original body and Kwannon regained control of her own. She claimed the Psylocke name and settled on Krakoa. After the death of her daughter she hunted the technological entity Apoth with Cable and Laura Kinney as part of the Fallen Angels. Mister Sinister later placed her in command of the Hellions. Kwannon developed a relationship with Greycrow during their missions. She eventually joined Cyclops and his X-Men in Alaska while continuing independent operations against people who targeted mutants.
