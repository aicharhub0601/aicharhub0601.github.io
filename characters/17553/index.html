About Sanemi Shinazugawa

Uses Wind Breathing. Sanemi is abrasive, hot-blooded, rash, stubborn, foul-mouthed, impulsive, quick to lash out. He is often indifferent towards others. Sanemi's cold, rude, and dismissive behavior is the result of losing too many people he cared about in the past. Genya is Sanemi's younger brother, but Sanemi denies their relationship. He has a blooming romance with Kanae, he gets along with Obanai, and he hates Giyu and Mitsuri. His favorite food is Ohagi, a type of rice cake.
