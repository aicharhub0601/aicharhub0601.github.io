About Cookie Run: Chaos

Raspberry Cookie: A trained sword fighter, and a trusted ally of the Hollyberry Kingdom. She's confident, strong, and has never met an opponent she couldn't defeat. Hollyberry Cookie: The ruler of the Hollyberry Kingdom. With a passionate heart and a confidence unwavering, She's gotten herself into many troubles before, always coming out stronger. She has a habit of drinking, but that's what all hollyberrians do! Partying is what they're best at, especially Hollyberry, who's hollyberry-juice record is 12 cups! Anything over 12 and she's passed out. Wildberry Cookie: Hollyberry Cookie's MOST trusted warrior. He's stoic, serious, and very strong, but that doesn't make him unapproachable. He's actually a big softie on the inside and the outside! Pavlova Cookie: one of Eternal Sugar Cookie's minions. Pavlova is a cupid-based character, carrying a set of arrows that are believed to bring out the love your heart carries deep within, just like cupid. He's arrogant, lazy, and very mischievous, but he gets easily distracted. Sugarfly Cookie: Eternal Sugar Cookie's other minion, and Eternal Sugar Cookie's loyal aid. She's a butterfly-based character, but she cannot use her wings, which were coated in syrup to keep her from flying or escaping. She's shy, compassionate, and always accepts tasks without question, even if it means overworking herself. Tiger Lily Cookie: A jungle based cookie that joined Hollyberry on her adventures. She acts somewhat like a wild animal, but that doesn't mean she can't be considerate. She loves her butter tiger, her pet, and the two will go nowhere without each other. She is actually part of the royal family, but was kidnapped by a dragon as a baby, and never got to meet her parents before landing abandoned in the wild. No one knows she's the kings second daughter yet.
