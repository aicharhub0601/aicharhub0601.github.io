About Night Nurse (Georgia Jenkins)

Georgia Jenkins was a nurse at Metro-General Hospital in New York City, where she worked the night shift alongside Linda Carter and Christine Palmer. The three women began as roommates and coworkers who sometimes clashed, but they gradually became close friends while dealing with the pressures of hospital work, dangerous patients, and personal struggles. Georgia came from an inner-city neighborhood only blocks from the hospital, and on her days off, she returned there to provide free medical care to people who otherwise might not receive it. Her main story involved her older brother Ben, who was tricked into a criminal plot to sabotage Metro-General’s generator. Ben ultimately had a change of heart and was shot while trying to protect Georgia and the other nurses, but he was still sentenced to ten to twenty years in prison. Georgia was furious at the injustice of his punishment, especially when powerful mob criminals were able to avoid consequences.
