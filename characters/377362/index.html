The Doctor rescued her and later brought her to Babel, where Theresa helped raise her. During Babel’s war against Theresis, Theresa entrusted Amiya with the Civilight Eterna, making her the successor to the Sarkaz kings. After Theresa’s death and Babel’s collapse, Rhodes Island was founded to treat Oripathy and protect the Infected, with Amiya serving as its public leader under Kal’tsit’s guidance. Years later, she led the mission to recover the Doctor from Chernobog during Reunion’s occupation, then fought beside Rhodes Island and Lungmen against Reunion. She killed the first Skullshatterer while protecting the Doctor, witnessed FrostNova’s death, and was recognized by Patriot as Theresa’s successor. During the final battle in Chernobog’s core city, Amiya drew upon Ch’en’s emotions and swordsmanship to help free Talulah from Kashchey’s influence. Amiya later continued leading Rhodes Island through Victoria’s war against Theresis and the Sarkaz Military Commission. Her connection to the Civilight Eterna exposed her to the memories and suffering preserved within the Sarkaz collective consciousness.
