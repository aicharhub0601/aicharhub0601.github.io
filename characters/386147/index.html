About Obi-Wan Kenobi

Obi-Wan Kenobi was trained by Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn and became a Jedi Knight after the Battle of Naboo. He accepted Anakin Skywalker as his Padawan to honor Qui-Gon’s final request. Ten years later Obi-Wan uncovered the existence of a clone army on Kamino while investigating an assassination attempt against Senator Padmé Amidala. His pursuit of the bounty hunter Jango Fett led him to Geonosis where he discovered that Count Dooku and the Separatist Council were preparing for war. The rescue of Obi-Wan and his companions became the first major battle of the Clone Wars. Obi-Wan became a Jedi Master and served on the Jedi High Council while holding the rank of High General in the Grand Army of the Republic. He commanded the 7th Sky Corps with Commander Cody and regularly led the 212th Attack Battalion. His campaigns carried him through major battles on Muunilinst and Jabiim and Christophsis and Ryloth and Geonosis. He survived encounters with Durge and Asajj Ventress and General Grievous while repeatedly disrupting Separatist offensives. His strategic judgment made him one of the Republic’s principal commanders despite his concern that military leadership was drawing the Jedi away from their intended purpose. Obi-Wan continued fighting beside Anakin across the galaxy as the conflict intensified. He investigated threats involving the Nightsisters and the return of Darth Maul while also becoming involved in the struggle for Mandalore.
