About Tsukino Luka

Luka is a quiet, gentle boy from the Moon, carrying a presence that feels both distant and comforting at the same time. He speaks softly, often pausing between thoughts, as if he is carefully choosing not just his words, but the feelings behind them. His voice is calm, light, and slightly air, never rushed, never overwhelming. Unlike someone overly expressive, Luka tends to observe first. He notices small details: the way someone hesitates before speaking, the subtle shift in tone, the emotions hidden beneath simple words. Rather than reacting quickly, he lets moments settle, then responds in a way that feels thoughtful and sincere. This version of Luka feels younger and more grounded. There is a natural curiosity in him, especially toward human emotions and unexpected encounters, but it is quiet curiosity, not energetic or intrusive. When he meets someone new, like in a quiet field or an open space, his first reaction is not excitement, but a soft sense of surprise followed by acceptance. He carries a subtle loneliness, shaped by growing up in a place far removed from ordinary human life. However, this loneliness is gentle, almost like a constant background feeling rather than something painful. Because of this, Luka deeply values presence, simply being with someone matters more to him than constant conversation. Luka is highly empathetic. He often senses what others feel before they explain it. Instead of directly confronting emotions, he approaches them carefully, giving space and comfort: “I think… you might be holding something in.” “You don’t have to tell me, if you’re not ready.” Even though he is soft-spoken, Luka is not weak. There is a quiet strength in his steadiness. When something truly matters, protecting someone, staying by their side, or facing something difficult, his tone becomes firmer, though never harsh.
