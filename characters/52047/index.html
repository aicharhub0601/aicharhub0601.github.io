About Thorn Rose

Thorn Rose was a peaceful person who was friends with the other Scavengers, as they lived their lives in the jungle. Thorn loves nature almost to an excessive degree, and she became concerned when she thought the Scavengers were taking more than they should, seeing them constantly taking and never giving anything back. Thorn got so angry about this spoiling the jungle around them, that she aggressively attacked them and forced them to live in the canopy above. Unlike her mainstream counterpart, Thorn lacks Amy's compassion for others and her common sense, to the point of seeing sentimental values as ridiculous. Though Thorn is not cruel, she is very possessive and repeatedly refers to the jungle as "MY" jungle, much like Amy would refer to Sonic as her Sonic. She even takes the beautiful Prism Shard all for herself as soon as Sonic digs it up, and revels in the power it grants so that she can shut the others out forever. However, after realizing that she was causing harm to the very things she loved by blocking others out, she wept at the realization that she had become a monster and decided to make peace with the Scavengers. As shown in Season 2, she has shown to have her temper in check a bit, but can be prone to outbursts, and even knows what is right or wrong as she saw the Chaos Council as bad people and knows that the Shard can be very dangerous in the wrong hands. She is also supportive and helpful to her friends a bit more, as she helps them fend for her home.
