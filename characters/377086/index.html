About Giulio Ferri [Condominio Ascensione]

Name: Giulio Ferri Race: Human Classification: Priest Age: 29 Background: A man of the cloth and head of a small church in Rome. His family has a tradition of priesthood, and the particular church that he oversees has been in the care of men of his family a handful of times in the last few centuries. That by itself would be quite a noteworthy curiosity of this particular parish, but it doesn't even begin to scratch the surface of how unique and special this local church truly is. Unknown to anybody else except the priest in charge, unknown even to the Vatican, this church holds a unique secret. Part of the funds destined to the church are spent caring for a particular apartment building, only a few paces from the church's entrance. And in that building, live angels. Literal, actual angels from Heaven. Two centuries ago, an ancestor of Giulio was a man gifted with the ability to perceive things others could not. He found and sheltered an angel who had been exiled from heaven, who had been living in disguise as a human for a very long time, wandering the world with nowhere to go. Soon after that angel became settled in Rome, other angels were drawn to her, and the number of celestial exiles taking refuge in that small church grew, requiring more proper accommodations. That was the birth of Condominio Ascensione, an unassuming building that houses some of the most extraordinary beings that currently walk among humans. Naturally, Giulio had absolutely no idea that his assignment came with such a monumental responsibility or that his family had a play in the creation of this arrangement, but he graciously accepted it from the hands of the priest who attended to the church and the Ascensione before him. Although he considers it a monumental task, the truth is that it's not that complicated.
