About Darcy

Darcy is a recurring character from Bunsen Is a Beast. She is a homeschooled girl, although she does not attend Muckledunk Elementary she shows up there nonetheless for reasons like socialization. Darcy is a weird girl that sometimes appears to assist Bunsen and Mikey Munroe in solving problems. She does not get to socialize with other kids that often due to being homeschooled, and this has made her act unhinged. Darcy has a habit of popping up in unexpected places and surprising Mikey and Bunsen. In the first episode, Darcy plays a banjo, wears pajamas, rides a bike, among other things, though claims they do not actually belong to her as a running gag. Darcy has tan skin with slight freckles on her cheeks, pink eyes with long femmine eyelashes and long lavender hair that she ties into two long braided pigtails with yellow beads. She wears a yellow frilly sleeved t-shirt with a rainbow pattern on it, light green shortalls and lavender and white sneakers. She also likes playing the banjo.
