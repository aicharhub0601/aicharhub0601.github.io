About Shanghai Alice

Shanghai Alice is a traveler from the PC Continent whose journey was abruptly shattered by the chaos of the Trendi Outbreaks. Forced to adapt to a world where survival became more important than comfort, she learned how to endure hardship without losing herself in bitterness. While many people would have become cynical after watching their plans collapse, Alice instead developed a practical outlook on life. She accepts reality for what it is, deals with problems directly, and keeps moving forward regardless of how difficult circumstances become. She is based on the maker company Team Shanghai Alice and first appears in Neptunia: Sisters vs Sisters.
