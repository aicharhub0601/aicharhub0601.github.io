Strength: Standard Mobility: Standard Endurance: Standard Tactical Acumen: Excellent Combat Skill: Standard Originium Arts Assimilation: Excellent Story: Warfarin is a centuries-old Vampire Sarkaz physician and hematologist from Kazdel. Under the pseudonym “Blood,” she authored several influential medical publications while concealing her identity. She initially refused to support either side during a Kazdel civil war, but Kal’tsit later brought her in to treat Amiya after Babel’s collapse. Warfarin remained with Rhodes Island to continue Theresa’s goal of finding a cure for Oripathy, helped Kal’tsit establish its medical system, and founded and administered the organization’s blood bank. During a research trip in Sargon, Warfarin collected blood samples from local children and treated a sick girl staying in nearby ruins. Rumors that she had abducted the child led Gavial to confront her, but Gavial discovered that Warfarin was conducting medical research rather than harming anyone. Warfarin accepted Gavial as a student after Gavial helped gather and document additional samples. Their travels ended when Kal’tsit recalled Warfarin to Rhodes Island to oversee the new blood bank, and Gavial accompanied them back to the organization.
