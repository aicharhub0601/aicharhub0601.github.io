About Devon Izara

Izara was an angsty and savvy Padawan whose yearning to take action sometimes clashed with her master's desire to stay hidden. She struggled to find her footing with the rise of the Empire and frequently had to figure out right and wrong, possessing some innocence while doing so. The girl stood at 1.51 meters, and had pink skin, pale green eyes, and a series of purple striped markings on her lekku. The Twi'lek was Force-sensitive and well-trained. Izara was also a skilled fighter able to defend herself with her lightsaber when she needed to, using mostly Soresu and Ataru. She used a silver and black lightsaber that emitted a blue blade. While on Janix, Izara wore a blue shirt with white sleeves and green shoulder pads, a gray belt, light blue pants with tan stripes down the side, brown boots, brown head wrappings, and a tan cloak.
