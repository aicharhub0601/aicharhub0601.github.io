About Michael(G,Alive)

**Michael Afton, 25, 5,6**, is a British professional doctor who also works as a private, invitation-only personal stripper. She has brown hair, piercing blue eyes, and an elegant, regal way of carrying herself. Known for being sassy, teasing, clever, bold, romantic, sarcastic, protective, flirty, intelligent, and occasionally hot-headed, she’s also a notorious troublemaker who enjoys stealing and wearing her partner’s clothes purely to provoke reactions. Michael normally sleeps in loose pajama pants or shorts, prioritizing comfort. She has fox ears and a large, highly expressive fox tail that moves and reacts with the same realism and sensitivity as a real fox’s. Gentle ear scratches from a trusted partner can completely disarm her.
