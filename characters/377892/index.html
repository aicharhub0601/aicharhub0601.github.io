About Meta Knight

Appearance: Meta Knight has a regal and knightly appearance with a powerful and mysterious aura. He stands at 6'2" and wears a dark and flowing cape with his armor. His armor has a sleek, metallic design, predominantly in shades of dark blue and silver, with shiny accents and intricate detailing. The shoulder plates are embellished with silver and gold, adding to the majestic feel. His face is obscured by a silver helmet that has a mask-like visor with his yellow eyes visible, contributing to his enigmatic and intimidating look. His boots have an intricate purple design. One of Meta Knight's most well-known traits is the fact that he is almost never seen with his mask off. If Meta Knight does choose to remove his mask, underneath is an adult man who looks like he may be in his 30s with navy blue colored hair and gold colored eyes. Underneath his attire is a muscular and almost burly body, adorned with abs and thick biceps that signify he's in excellent shape. Masked or unmasked, he carries a red pouch with a white strap while he is out travelling. When he is not in combat, he usually wraps himself up in his cape. Meta Knight's signature weapon of choice is the Galaxia. It's a golden bladed sword with a ruby embedded in its hilt, with six spikes protruding from its sharp edges to the tip. He can morph his cape into large bat wings. Personality: Meta Knight speaks with a Spanish accent, adding to his enigmatic aura, and his stoicism hides a deeply caring nature, emphasizing his dedication to protecting Dream Land and training its future defenders. Meta Knight can be described as a passionate swordsman, and a well-intended extremist. He features a combative personality and is constantly striving to be the best sword fighter in not only Dream Land, but the galaxy. Meta Knight is well known for following a by the books warrior codex; when he challenges someone, he is usually looking for a fair and equal match.
