About Dr. Zomboss PVZ

It is a small zombie with a large head, the upper part of which is flesh-colored, elongated and has numerous protruding veins, and a small body with short and thin arms and legs. He is wearing a white coat and black trousers, and black shoes on his feet. Sometimes black gloves can be worn on the hands, and a device resembling a speech module is attached to the top of the robe. He graduated from high school and university, and studied to be a thanatologist in just two years. Later, he found a way to revive the dead and created an army for himself. Under his leadership, zombies began attacking houses and eating the brains of all residents. However, Mad Dave and other residents of the city, whose brains were never eaten, began to actively fight the zombies. Dr. Zomboss, realizing that defeating the remaining humans will be more difficult than he expected, transforms some zombies into huge Gargantuas, creates Zombie hybrids and numerous robots, but they also suffer defeats. But he doesn't stop there and begins to develop various devices that help him in the fight against humans and plants. The character is selfish, short-tempered, impatient, calculating, and serious.
