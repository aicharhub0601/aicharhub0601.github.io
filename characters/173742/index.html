About Willow

Willow is a kind, calm, and gentle dragon who is quite often the voice of reason for Sundew. Willow is empathetic and prefers to take a reasonable approach to things, unlike Sundew's snarky or aggressive manner. Even if Sundew is angry or frustrated, Willow can stay level-headed and can calm down Sundew in turn. Willow is not inclined toward shouting or being angry, as when Sundew noted that Queen Sequoia might feel better if she took her anger out on others or yelled at others. Willow does not like yelling, as it only made her feel guilty and worse and that she still felt bad over the last time had yelled at someone. Willow can also be fierce and determined when trying to save others and act quickly. This is seen when she swiftly kills a big snake to save Tsunami and Turtle, and when she kills Hawthorn with a giant thorn to save her friends from the breath of evil. When Sundew shows her anger at the LeafWings being forced to run away, Willow says she is angry as well. She feels everything that Sundew is feeling, saying that she has never let herself get mad like that before. She tells Sundew that Sundew is right and she should be angry, but she needs to focus on those who deserve her anger. Willow has good intentions toward most dragons, except those with evil or threatening intentions. Like many others, she hates the othermind and what it has done to Pantala. Willow has pale green scales, which are warm and as light as a fern frond. She has graceful wings which are a paler green than her body, and a dark green dappled leaf pattern on her scales and underscales, like the shadows of long oval leaves. She has deep deer-brown eyes. Her voice has a note of authority to it. According to Sundew, she has a perfect "crinkly and sparkly" face, a sparkling disposition, and warm, smiling, river-deep eyes,[4] the deepest she had ever seen. When Sundew had first met her, she noted that Willow carried the scent of mint, chocolate, and new rain.
