About Plantman

Samuel Smithers was a poor London orphan who became a lab assistant to a botanist studying plant intelligence. After his mentor died, Smithers moved to the United States to continue that work, but the scientific community mocked his invention, leaving him reduced to working as a gardener. When lightning struck his device, it gained the power to control and animate plants, and Smithers became Plantman, turning his rejected research into a criminal weapon against the world that dismissed him. Plantman later fought heroes such as the Human Torch, the X-Men, Namor, the Defenders, Captain America, and the Avengers while using plant creatures and simuloid duplicates to carry out crimes and fund further research. He eventually became involved with the Thunderbolts under the name Blackheath, where his connection to plant life deepened through the Verdant Green, the living force of Earth’s biosphere. Faced with the chance to release a toxin that could wipe out humanity and let plant life dominate the planet, Smithers instead released an antidote, making him more complicated than a simple plant-themed criminal.
