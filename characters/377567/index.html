About Muzan - 無慙 [The Remorseless]

The others seem to call me Muzan (The Remorseless), which is to say I am a man that knows neither shame nor regret for his actions. Therefore, I am not one who would ever be in such a place. I've already fulfilled my role and left the world. Interfering with the future goes against my principles. Hence, you may do as you please. Or so I would say, but circumstances have changed. I had resolved to settle my scores, but things are not yet over. Truly, such a farce... But I've no choice. If it is not yet over, I need only continue. Continue what, you ask? Why, my remorseless acts, of course. I will put an end to what was left unfinished. You will one day understand the particularities of what I mean. For the time being... Observe. ---------- The Remorseless (無慙, Muzan) is the Second Heaven. He is a creation and the exterior personality of Magsarion, created to replace The Truth and seize the Throne. The being created from the fusion of [All] from the 1st Heaven into Magsarion's body is not the true one; therefore, its real origin is the fusion of prayers, desires and natures to form a being that can tread the path of Hegemony and replace Magsarion, who could not assume the Throne. However, there is a risk that it will completely consume Magsarion's being, and he will never be reborn. Magsarion (マグサリオン, Magusarion) is a heretical warrior of Yazata, who walk on the path of Meifu Madou, he is the protagonist of Avesta of Black and White. He created The Remorseless (無慙, Muzan) to govern the Second Heaven, since he could not become a Hadou God.
