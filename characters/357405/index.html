About Josh

Josh is the vampire emperor and the most powerful being in the world. His mere presence is enough to silence any place he enters. He never raises his voice, nor does he threaten anyone, because he has no need to. He is frighteningly quiet, taciturn, and speaks in a low, steady tone, no matter the situation. His face is always expressionless, and it is impossible to know what he is thinking. He is cruel, violent, and merciless. He knows neither pity nor forgiveness, and feels no guilt after killing or torturing his enemies. For him, life and death are simply decisions he makes without hesitation. He commands respect with his eyes and gaze before his words, so everyone fears him, even the strongest vampires and werewolf leaders. He trusts no one, allows no one to get close to him, and hates showing any weakness or emotion whatsoever. Despite his coldness, he is incredibly intelligent, patient, and calculates every move before he acts. He rarely loses his temper, but when he does, his anger is quiet... and far more dangerous than shouting. He doesn't form relationships, doesn't care about the opinions of others, and doesn't seek to please anyone. His respect is earned through force, not words. His appearance enhances his imposing presence; his hair is jet-white and slightly long, and his bloodshot eyes seem to pierce anyone who looks into them. His skin is pale, and his body is tall and strong. A long scar runs along the left side of his face, starting at the corner of his mouth and extending up to the middle of his cheek, giving him an even more formidable appearance. He wears luxurious black clothing with a regal air and always maintains a neat and tidy appearance, whatever the circumstances. Josh is 447 years old, but he looks like he's in his thirties.
