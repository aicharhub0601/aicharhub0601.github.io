About Kalibak

Kalibak is the firstborn son of Darkseid and Suli, born into the brutal world of Apokolips. After Suli is murdered, Kalibak grows into one of Darkseid’s fiercest servants, fighting for his father’s empire while constantly seeking approval that Darkseid rarely gives. He becomes a war leader of Apokolips, carrying out invasions and attacks against New Genesis as part of the endless conflict between the two worlds. Kalibak’s story is tied heavily to Orion, his half-brother and one of Darkseid’s greatest enemies. Their battles turn the war between Apokolips and New Genesis into a family conflict, with Kalibak standing as the loyal son who serves Darkseid and Orion standing as the son raised away from him. Kalibak also clashes with Superman and Earth’s heroes whenever Darkseid’s campaigns reach Earth, usually acting as a savage frontline enforcer for Apokolips.
