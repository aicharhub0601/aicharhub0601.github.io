About Chiori

Chiori was born in Inazuma and developed an interest in needlework while she was still young. Her parents indulged her independent nature and eventually arranged for her to study under an experienced tailor once her talent became clear. Chiori quickly grew frustrated with the rigid expectations and arrogant figures she encountered within the trade. She wanted to create clothing according to her own sense of beauty rather than imitate established styles. When the Vision Hunt Decree and Sakoku Decree transformed life in Inazuma she chose to leave the nation and pursue her ambitions elsewhere. A Geo Vision appeared among her belongings as she prepared for the journey. Chiori traveled across Teyvat before settling in Fontaine because of its reputation as a center of fashion. Breaking into the industry proved difficult. Established designers and sponsors dismissed her work while she and another struggling designer named Uther attempted to establish themselves. Uther eventually abandoned his ambitions after repeated rejection. Chiori refused to do the same. She poured everything she had into Fontaine Fashion Week and earned enough recognition to establish the Chioriya Boutique in the Court of Fontaine. Her uncompromising designs gradually attracted wealthy clients and performers while her reputation spread far beyond the city. Years later Uther attempted to destroy Chiori's business out of resentment for her success. He arranged attacks against shipments bound for her shop and spread accusations that damaged her reputation just before Fashion Week. Chiori investigated his operation and discovered that he was secretly producing clothing under dangerous conditions while using dishonest methods against his competitors. She confronted him inside his factory and defeated him when he attempted to stop her from exposing the evidence.
