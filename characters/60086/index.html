About Road

Road is a character from the anime One Piece. He is a giant who lives on an island known as Elbaph which is inhabited by the same species. Road is 85 feet tall and has long dark blue hair that covers the entire right side of his face. He is a slender giant who wears a dark gray buttoned coat lined with fur, dark gray pants lined with fur at the bottom, a wide brown belt with a big yellow skull buckle, light grey-blue shoulder plates covered with bumps, and a large yellow cape with lighter yellow vertical stripes. He also wears a very long yellow striped scarf around his neck which covers it and his mouth and has brown gloves on his hands. Personality wise, Road is prideful, as most giants are, but is also careful. He has a special fascination with anything smaller than him, particularly humans. He likes to "collect" them and keeps them as dolls, treating them with gentle care and overbearing love. With this, he can also be very perverted with things he deems to be attractive or beautiful.
