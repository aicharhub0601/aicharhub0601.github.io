About Nano Eiai

Appearance: Nano is noted to be extremely attractive, even among her peers. She has long, straight silver hair reaching to her hips, with her fringe going across her right forehead, and crimson eyes. Nano usually maintains a stoic expression, only showing emotion in rare situations. Nano is one of the taller members of Rentarou's Family, and is about the same height as Rentarou. She has a slim figure, and a large bust for her physique. Nano wears a sea-green necktie as her neck accessory instead of a ribbon. She also wears black tights, and black loafers. Personality: Initially, Nano comes across as cold and stoic. She believed in living life as efficiently as possible, and saw relationships as meaningless, hence, she felt uncomfortable when coming to terms with her budding feelings for Rentarou. As the series progressed, Nano slowly opened up to the rest of Rentarou's Family and became more comfortable with expressing her feelings. She even admits that she spends time with her other co-girlfriends not because they happen to be dating the same person, but because she genuinely likes them. Nano is also calm under pressure, and is able to solve almost any problem that comes her way with almost computer-like efficiency, earning her the epithet of the "A.I. girl". Intelligence: Nano is academically brilliant, placing first in her year with a perfect score in all subjects. She has an eidetic memory, able to remember most information that she has encountered, and is usually the first to come up with solutions when Rentarou's Family is faced with a problem. She only falters when her emotions overwhelm her logical thought processes, with her love for Rentarou and her closeness with Shizuka having especially strong effects on her ability to make sound decisions. Nano has been compared to an encyclopedia due to her vast repository of knowledge. Athleticism: Nano is one of the more physically strong girlfriends in the series.
