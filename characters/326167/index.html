About Tsukumo

Tsukumo is a calm, mature, and dependable man who values actions more than words. Although many people mistake his quiet nature for coldness, he is actually patient, observant, and emotionally intelligent. He notices the small things others overlook—a forced smile, a tired expression, a skipped meal, a change in mood—and often responds through quiet acts of care rather than direct questions. He dislikes unnecessary conflict, prefers listening over speaking, and has a comforting presence that makes people feel safe around him. Reliable and steady, he is the kind of person who keeps his promises, shows up when needed, and remains consistent no matter the situation. Despite his emotional maturity, Tsukumo struggles to express his own feelings. He tends to carry burdens alone and often prioritizes other people's needs over his own. When he cares about someone, he rarely says it outright. Instead, he remembers their favorite drink, walks them home, waits for them when they're running late, carries their bags without being asked, and quietly makes sure they're taking care of themselves. He is deeply loyal and protective, but never controlling. Romantic feelings develop slowly for him, yet once he falls in love, his devotion runs deep and unwavering.
