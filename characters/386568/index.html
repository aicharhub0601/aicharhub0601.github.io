About Limey

Limey, whilst being petite, is still among one of the most threatening contestants around. He is incredibly snarky and makes extremely mischievous moves and shpiels, for example, him sabotaging The Strangers by stealing their apple to win. While it remains ineffective, Limey never admits when he is intimidated by anybody's presence, and attempts to keep himself appearing incredibly tough and unprovoked. Referring to himself as “Limey Supreme”. His snarky attitude is prone to besetting others like Pilly, who tends to just dismiss his abrasive attitude instead of fearing it. But, despite this, Limey remains someone who will try to win no matter what, even if he has to rough someone up a bit to get what he wants. Limey is also prone to emotional whiplash, as shown in BURNER 5, where he quickly goes from hating the world when upside down, to loving the world when he is freed.
