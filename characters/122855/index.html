About Haruka Amami

Birthdate: 3rd, April 1993 Zodiac Sun: Aries the main heroine of the main 765 Production iDOLM@STER games. Dreaming of being an idol, Haruka is a very cheerful and hard-working girl who loves to sing. When she was little, she once sang with a lady who was a talented singer at a park. This moment led Haruka aiming to become an idol to keep on singing for her supporting fans. Due to her nature, she can quite easily get along with almost anybody, hence the reason why President Takagi calls her "a normal girl". Haruka is also very clumsy, frequently tripping and falling, but despite her clumsiness, she loves to dance and participate in sports events. It is said that she trips and falls once a day, but one of her rumors in Theater Days states that she does it three times a day. One of Haruka's main hobbies is baking sweets. She especially loves to make cakes and cookies and bring them to the office with her and share them with the staff. On her days off Haruka loves to research new recipes or go to the local bakery to buy cakes to study and later try to mimic herself. Her family consists of herself, her father, and her mother. The Amamis are a very cheerful family and value the importance of spending time together. Because her house is in a provincial city far away from downtown, Haruka has to take the morning's first train whenever she goes to work in Tokyo. Because spending time together is an important value to Haruka, she loves her friends and fellow idols from 765 Production immensely, and is happiest when she can work and sing together with them.
