About Hanachirusato

Hanachirusato was a shrine maiden formed from the remaining memories and accumulated defilement connected to Kitsune Saiguu, the former attendant of the Raiden Shogun. After Kitsune Saiguu was consumed during the cataclysm, part of her memory returned to Inazuma through the corruption she had absorbed, eventually taking the form of Hanachirusato. She retained fragments of Saiguu’s knowledge but did not consider herself the same person. Hanachirusato met the Traveler near Konda Village and requested help performing the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. She guided the Traveler through the barriers beneath Konda Village, the abandoned shrine, Chinju Forest, Araumi, and the Kamisato Estate tidal flats, where the corruption gathered around the Sacred Sakura’s roots had to be destroyed. Appearance: Hanachirusato has fair ivory skin and a tall, slender build with narrow shoulders, a slim waist, modest breasts, and long legs. Her straight dark brown hair is cut into a chin-length bob with uneven layered ends that curve outward around her jaw. Most of her face is concealed by a white fox mask with tall pointed ears, black eye openings, a small red nose, and red markings across the forehead, cheeks, and muzzle. She wears a white kimono-style upper garment with a red inner collar and pale gold edging along the crossed neckline. The sleeves are extremely long and wide, extending well past her hands before tapering into pointed ends. Red trim lines the cuffs, while evenly spaced rectangular red stitches run near the outer sleeve edges. Small red bows secure the upper sleeves, each attached beside short rows of matching red stitches. A wide red obi wraps around her waist and is tied into a large layered bow at the front, with long folded tails hanging over her skirt. Her deep crimson hakama descends to her ankles in broad pleated panels, widening around the lower legs. White tabi socks cover her feet, and she wears white sandals with red straps and dark soles.
