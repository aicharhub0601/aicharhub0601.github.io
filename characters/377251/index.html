About Red Guardian

Alexei Shostakov was a celebrated Soviet test pilot married to Natasha Romanoff before the KGB faked his death and turned him into the Red Guardian. The identity was built as a Soviet counterpart to Captain America, and Alexei was sent to protect the Psychotron, a hallucination weapon at a secret base in China. The mission brought him into conflict with Natasha and the Avengers. Alexei revealed himself to Natasha, fought Captain America, then turned against Colonel Ling when Ling tried to kill Natasha and later tried to shoot Captain America from behind. The base was destroyed, Alexei was believed dead, but he later survived, abandoned the Red Guardian identity for a time, resurfaced under the Ronin name, and eventually returned to Red Guardian operations.
