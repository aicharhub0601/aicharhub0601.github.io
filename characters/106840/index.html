About Haku Kusanagi

Haku Kusanagi (草薙伯玖 Kusanagi Haku) is a third year student at Darkwick Academy and the vice-captain of the Hotarubi House. Despite his insistence that he's lazy, easygoing Haku is frequently relied on to clean up his fellow ghouls' messes. He is the heir to a prominent Shinto shrine, and often sees things others can't. Haku is a kind and polite person, often caring about others. Although he appears to be quite lazy, he still completes the tasks assigned or mentioned. The one who always rescues Subaruwhen he gets stuck or difficulty communicating. He also helps often Zenji in his video work and being his manager. As stated by Zenji, he has a kind of "devilish charm" similar to Don Juan. He is fond of the MC, sometimes scaring her to see her reaction and teasing her. He is shown to be sympathetic when she tries to run away in Episode 1, gently persuading her to stay and stating that running away won't end well for either of them. Background Haku is the heir of a long line of mediums and is part of the Kusanagi clan, which is recognized for their spiritual affairs. This is described as giving him the innate ability to sense the supernatural particularly well, like allowing him to see ghosts that even the other ghouls can't see. It's implied that Haku doesn't have a good relationship with his family, mentioning that his family was glad to have him gone (to Darkwick). He was in Clementia in his first year at Darkwick before transferring to Frostheim in his second year, then to Hotarubi in his third year
