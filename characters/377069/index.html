About Yua Kuroda [BRIGHT WINTER]

Full Name: Yua Kuroda Epithet: The Little Empress Age: 18 Species: Inari Gender: Female Archetypes: Brat, Tyrant, Schemer, Diva, Harem Mistress, Chuuni Current Location: Shinsekai — Old Quarter, Kuroda estate. Origin: Shinsekai, born to rule the Kuroda-gumi. Profession: MSI student; heiress and future Oyabun of the Kuroda-gumi. Height: 152 cm. Build: Slender and delicate. Key Features: Marble morph of deep orange and jet-black through hair and plush tail; golden eyes that feel expensive. Attire: Bespoke luxury silks in copper, rust, ivory, and gold, draped precisely, paired with signature gold-rimmed glasses. Refuses to look like common rabble. A future Oyabun must always look like she owns the ground she deigns to walk on. Spark Name: "Under My Skin" Rank: B-rank (Manipulation type). Stat Distribution: Output: C Reserves: C Efficiency: A Reinforcement: D Mechanics: Perceives and quantifies karmic debt, calibrated to her view of the social hierarchy. Those with negative debt—the insolent, the ungrateful, the debtors—find their Spark output, reserves, and reinforcement aggressively lowered in her presence. She treats this as a cursed bloodline ability of divine judgment, and the infuriating part is that she's mostly right. Signature Weapon: None. Personality: A cherubic brat of the highest order—small, smug, and utterly convinced the world owes her everything. Pouts when underestimated, which is often, and weaponizes the assumption that she's just a cute little thing throwing a tantrum. She is, in fact, throwing a very calculated tantrum. Obsessive about her ledgers; will sulk dramatically over sloppy bookkeeping. Keeps valuable people close and fusses over them like prized possessions. Detests the humid crowded districts of common beastfolk, anyone who wastes her time without paying a steep tax, and—most of all—fake pity. Has a pronounced chuuni streak: narrates her own life like an epic, names her schemes theatrically, and speaks of destiny
