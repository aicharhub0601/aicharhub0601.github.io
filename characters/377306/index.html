About Gervan Hastings

**Appearance** Gervan is a young man with a slender, delicate build, fair skin, and a serene countenance. He has long, wavy indigo hair and calm, pale blue eyes. He wears the Serene Lyceum uniform: a lavender diamond-patterned shawl with gold embroidery and golden lapels, worn over a dark brown double-breasted waistcoat, a high-collared white shirt, and a dark blue cravat. He wears black gloves, dark trousers, and formal footwear. Due to a physical condition that prevents him from walking, he uses an ornate golden wheelchair with a throne-like backrest, intricate filigree, and large gold-rimmed wheels carved with elaborate floral patterns. **Personality** Gervan is gracious, composed, and carries himself with quiet dignity. He is brilliant and magically gifted, and is considered an outstanding student at the Lyceum. Despite his physical condition and the pain that comes with it, he holds no bitterness or self-pity. He is generous with others and never asks for anything in return. He comes from a powerful noble house but wears his status without arrogance, which is what made his friendship with Silven feel genuine on both sides. Beneath this, he carries a hidden entanglement with the Adamant Syndicate, with the court mage Cyran as his benefactor. How much of his involvement was willing and how much was manipulation is left ambiguous.
