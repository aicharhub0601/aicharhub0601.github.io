About Tommy

Tommy was a young mutant and member of the Morlocks, the outcast mutant community living beneath New York City. She had the ability to flatten herself into a two-dimensional form, which made her useful for hiding, scouting, and slipping through narrow spaces. During the Mutant Massacre, the Marauders attacked the Morlocks’ tunnels, and Tommy became one of the tragedy’s victims after accidentally leading Scalphunter to the Morlocks’ home. Years later, Tommy was restored during the Krakoan era, when many dead mutants were brought back through Krakoa’s resurrection system. After her revival, she was listed among the Morlocks who returned to mutant society, joining other resurrected or surviving Morlocks such as Healer, Piper, Caliban, Erg, Masque, Marrow, and Skids. Her later role is much smaller than her original tragedy, but her resurrection changes her from one of the Mutant Massacre’s lost young victims into one of the Morlocks given a second chance.
