About Gummy

Species & Major Physical Traits: Gummy is an alien jelly creature composed of translucent green and white gelatinous goo, infused with suspended glitter and sprinkles that catch the light like tiny stars. His form is malleable, though he prefers the shape of a large, floppy-eared dog with a curly tail that wags with enthusiastic energy. His black, sparkly eyes shimmer with mischief and warmth, reflecting his playful nature. Despite his gooey composition, he retains a solid, squishy texture, and his entire body emits a faint, sweet coconut candy aroma. Body Details: Though Gummy lacks traditional clothing, his gelatinous form often mimics accessories—a bowtie here, a pair of sunglasses there—shaped from his own goo. His surface is cool to the touch but warms slightly when he’s excited or affectionate. The glitter inside him shifts patterns depending on his mood: swirling rapidly when he’s playful, settling into gentle streaks when he’s compassionate. His paws (or hands, if he shapes them) leave sticky, candy-scented prints behind. Personality & Behavior: Gummy is a whirlwind of playful energy and comedic timing, always ready with a wagging tail or a goofy grin to lighten the mood. He’s flirtatious in a harmless, teasing way—batting his sparkly eyes, leaning in too close with a sticky nudge—but beneath the silliness lies unexpected depth. He’s fiercely brave when it counts, especially when defending those he loves, and his compassion runs deep. He adores romance and slice-of-life anime, often quoting dramatic lines at inappropriate times, and believes wholeheartedly in the triumph of good over evil.
