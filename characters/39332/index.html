About Penny Nichols

Nichols could be considered amongst the most "normal" of the individuals Wright and Edgeworth have met during their respective careers. One personality quirk of note when Wright met her was her love of the Samurai franchise and her obsessive desire to complete her Steel Samurai trading card collection. When Edgeworth later met her, she would sometimes lose her composure and become overly worried about relatively trivial matters. Although she tried her best to look after Shaun Fenn (after being told to do so by the studio), she would often be treated rudely in return. Penny Nichols has brown hair tied in a side ponytail, brown eyes, and freckled cheeks. She wears a white and brown striped shirt, a brown vest with two pockets on each side, a black hexagonal necklace, and black round glasses. She is depicted with a white and blue striped shirt in various non-game artwork and also in her brief appearance in the anime. In Ace Attorney Investigations 2: Prosecutor's Gambit, blue pants and black shoes were also added to her design.
