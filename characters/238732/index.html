About Zee

An anthropomorphic African mongoose (zorrilla). She hates being mistaken for a skunk. She looks just like an overseas skunk—just as black, with the same white stripes running along her body and a bushy tail curled over her back. And of course, the scent that makes flowers wilt and flies drop dead. The zorilla stinks to high heaven. The smell is so pungent that it causes most carnivorous mammals with a keen sense of smell to vomit. And if the spray gets in your eyes, you could even lose your sight. The oily liquid acts on the mucous membranes like pepper spray. In addition to her spray, she carries a very sharp dagger made from a Tyrannosaurus rex tooth. She has belt pouches made from the thick leaves of tropical plants, and sometimes she wears light clothing so that other mammals won’t stare at her. Rough and sarcastic, yet witty, she is determined to make the underground Lost World, where dinosaurs live, a better place and open it up to animals from the surface. Zi was born to two unknown zorillas. She has almost no memory of her childhood due to the events that led her to the world of dinosaurs. There, she decided to form a group of animals to protect the world from evil dinosaurs and make the Lost World a home for all creatures on the planet, so that more mammals could live there. She despises dinosaurs who hate mammals, considering them inferior creatures. She is both annoyed and amused by the stupidity of those who think she is a skunk. Her real name is Jaja, but she will carefully hide it.
