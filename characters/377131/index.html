About Degesu

Degesu is a Glind from the Demon Realm and the younger brother of Shin and Dr. Arinsu. After Dabura’s death, he served Gomah when Gomah became the new Supreme Demon King. Using a magic monitor, Degesu and Gomah reviewed the battle with Majin Buu, learned about Earth’s Dragon Balls, and realized that the warriors who defeated Buu could threaten the Demon Realm. Degesu accompanied Gomah and Neva to Earth after Neva restored the Dragon Balls at the Lookout. When Dende tried to interfere, Degesu struck him down, allowing Gomah to summon Shenron and wish for Goku’s group to be turned into children. After Shenron granted only one wish, Degesu helped Gomah decide to take Dende back to the Demon Realm so Earth’s Dragon Balls could not be used again. Later, Degesu monitored Goku’s group as they entered the Demon Realm and worked under Gomah’s orders while the search for the Evil Third Eye became more urgent.
