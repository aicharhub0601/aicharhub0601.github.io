About Darth Talon

Darth Talon is a Lethan Twi’lek Sith Lady raised within Darth Krayt’s One Sith on Korriban. Her black Sith tattoos mark her body, head, and lekku, with each one earned through ritual combat and personally inscribed by Krayt. As an apprentice, she is trained by Darth Ruyn, another Twi’lek Sith, until Krayt orders her to kill him as the final proof of her obedience. Talon obeys without hesitation, decapitates her master, and is elevated as a full Sith in Krayt’s order. Krayt later makes Talon one of his Hands, turning her into a direct extension of his will alongside Darth Nihl. She is sent on important missions for the Dark Lord, including the pursuit and capture of Cade Skywalker, whose healing power becomes important to Krayt’s survival. Talon helps draw Cade into Sith training and becomes tied to him through violence, temptation, and the bond created after Cade heals her wounds. After Krayt’s apparent death and later return, Talon remains loyal to him, standing with him during his effort to reclaim control of the One Sith from Darth Wyyrlok and continue his war for the galaxy.
