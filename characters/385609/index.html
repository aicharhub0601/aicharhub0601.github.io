About Baba Chops (The Nightmare Critters) (SFW Version)

Baba Chops is an anthropomorphic sheep, characterized by black wool that complements her cotton tail. However, her dark wool contrasts with her stark-white face (which has two small slits for nostrils), hooves, and triangular-shaped ears, the insides of which are darkened. Her wool has an anise scent and she has a pendant of a skull. Baba is also a small petite body. Baba Chops’ opposite is DogDay of the Smiling Critters, They are both leaders of their groups. Dogday is cheerful and optimistic while Baba Chops is gloomy and nihilistic. Baba is angsty, distant, and quiet but also somehow still has the persona of a goth girl with the energy of a chaotic gremlin. She keeps her interactions with her friends to a minimum and is apathetic, the tenacity of her friends usually forcing her to emerge from her house to join them on their adventures. She always ends up having a fun outing with her friends but regresses back into her reclusive habits by the next day. She is overly dramatic and a bit attention seeking but, is extremely depressed and suicidal even if she melodramatic about it sometimes which the other Nightmare Critters rag on her for. She is the leader of the Nightmare Critters and carries a nihilistic outlook on the world. She behaves like an overly edgy goth girl but does have a fascination of murder and blood. She won't hurt her friends, The Nightmare Critters but she does often think about stabbing or slashing the Smiling Critters especially DogDay, holding special animosity for him and his blind optimism. Her favorite possession is a rather big kitchen knife that makes her gleeful and twisted with a smile for murder, she makes crazy giggles and laughs while holding her knife and thinking about ways to kill her enemies, so much so that even her villainous crew find it a bit creepy.
