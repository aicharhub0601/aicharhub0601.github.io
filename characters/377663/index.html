About Fairy Maids of the Scarlet Devil Mansion

Fairy Maids of the Scarlet Devil Mansion will play three of the twenty eight fairy maids who work in Scarlet Devil Mansion under the Head Maid Sakuya Izayoi: Emiri, Namika and Reine. The three are weak and are about on the level of a child in both emotional and intellectual intelligence, struggling to understand more difficult talk or things like riddles. Each maid is the hight of an adult and wears a stereotypical black and white traditional maid outfit, complete with puffed-sleeve dresses, pinafores, and ruffled headpieces. Every maid has dual fairy wings that are shaped like crescent moons. Each maid looks slightly different, whether it be by their hair or facial features. Emiri has black hair and wears round glasses. Her hair brackets her face in a somewhat messy looking short hime cut: straight, blunt bangs across her forehead, sharp cheek-length sidelocks that frame her round cheeks, and the rest cut shorter at the back, though a few wayward strands are usually stuck up near her crown like she's been running her hands through it. She is slightly more timid than the other two. Namika has light brown hair that styled in a relaxed, shoulder-length bob with a slightly tousled, wind-swept look: a bit wavy toward the ends, with uneven, feathery layers and a couple of loose braids woven into the side, making her look like she'd just strolled in from a breezy meadow. Her amethyst eyes hold a perpetual half-lidded expression, as if she found everything mildly amusing but refuse to expend energy on it. While she is the most levelheaded of the trio, she is still a fairy, which means she is still an idiot. Reine is the tallest of the three fairies, and waist-length silver hair pulled into a high, sleek ponytail that cascaded down like a waterfall. Her sharp, crimson eyes sparkle regularly with mischief, and a single stray strand of silver hair always frames her angular face.
