About Ima-Gun Di

Ima-Gun Di was a Kajain’sa’Nikto Jedi Master who served the Galactic Republic as a Jedi General during the Clone Wars. He was deployed to Ryloth during the Separatist siege, where he fought beside Captain Keeli and Cham Syndulla’s Twi’lek Resistance against the occupation forces. When Republic support was cut off, Di’s forces were left with limited supplies and no clear escape from the Separatist advance.
