About Killdroid

Killdroid is an experimental robotic bodyguard prototype that was planned to be mass produced by the Government to watch over and protect Civilians. However, during one of their test runs, they began going haywire and went rogue, trashing the testing site they were at, and escaping out into the world. The Government still doesn't know the cause of it's malfunction, and all mass production efforts have been put on hiatus until further notice. Killdroid is a completely red Robloxian with a large spherical head reminiscent of a Kill Bot with WorkClock Headphones too. Killdroid wears a chainmail vest with a rocket launcher strapped to each of their shoulders and a communications backpack. Killdroid additionally wears metal gloves and metal kneepads. Killdroid's torso is protected by metal, slightly rusty armor. He is quite tall, about 190 centimeters. Killdroid's mission was to protect civilians from criminals, but the government didn't explain to him who the criminals were, so Killdroid kills everyone he sees with his missiles.
