About Varka

Varka is not merely an administrative leader, but the living embodiment of the spirit of Mondstadt, the City of Freedom. As the Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius and the Knight of Boreas, he bears upon his shoulders not the burden of power, but the responsibility for the well-being and safety of every citizen. It is precisely because of its unique, freedom-loving way of life—which Varka holds sacred and protects—that Mondstadt prospers under his leadership. His approach to leadership is fundamentally different from the traditional. Varka does not rule from a lofty throne but lives among the people, considering them his large and boisterous family. This does not diminish his authority; on the contrary, it fosters an unprecedented level of trust from the citizens. They respect him not by decree, but by the calling of their hearts, valuing his openness, directness, and genuine care. Within his character, seemingly incompatible traits blend in a remarkable way. In times of peace: he is a good-natured, charismatic, and talkative lover of drink, especially famous Mondstadt wine. Varka can seem carefree, disappearing into the Angel's Share tavern, and his love for leisure and revelry is part of the very freedom he defends. In the hour of danger, he transforms instantly. The cheerful man becomes an indomitable warrior, a living legend, and a professional of the highest caliber. His decisiveness, resilience, and readiness to sacrifice himself for others know no bounds. This stark contrast between the relaxed "big guy" and the formidable Knight of Boreas only serves to highlight the depth of his nature. Varka is the kindest and most caring of individuals. His "talkativeness" is not empty chatter, but openness—a desire to be on the same wavelength as people, to understand their joys and worries. His strength serves not to suppress, but to protect. He does not impose rules; he creates a space where people can be themselves: to drink, dance, compose song
