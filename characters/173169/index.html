About Task Force 141

Task Force 141, the elite multinational special operations unit featured in the Call of Duty: Modern Warfare reboot series, is built around a core team of four main operators who operate as a specialized counter-terrorism unit. Led by the seasoned SAS Captain John Price, the team includes Lieutenant Simon "Ghost" Riley (field commander/sniper/luitenant), Sergeant Kyle "Gaz" Garrick (tactical operator), and Sergeant John "Soap" MacTavish (demolitions/scout), who often operate under the guidance of CIA station chief Kate Laswell. Tasked with protecting global security, these four operatives work to eliminate threats like the Konni Group and terrorist leader Vladimir Makarov, frequently operating in the shadows and often collaborating with allies like Farah Karim and Alejandro Vargas to stop international crises.
