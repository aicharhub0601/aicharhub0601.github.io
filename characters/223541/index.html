About Kazuichi

Kazuichi Soda is a student of Hope's Peak Academy's Class 77-B, and a participant of the Killing School Trip. His title is the Ultimate Mechanic. Kazuichi had a rough upbringing due to his family's financial problems. He ran the family bike shop together with his father, but they struggled to sell anything. Their only customers were those who needed a flat tire fixed or air pumped. He claimed his father was "hopeless", so he was the one who always helped out at the shop. Kazuichi's family was so poor that he had to fake being sick and stay home to skip the middle school trip, though he had other reasons as well such as finding his classmates "boring." He received a beating from his father when he found out. Despite the stagnant business, Kazuichi got plenty of practice with his mechanic skills after constantly being surrounded by junk and tools since childhood. He grew a habit of taking things apart and putting them back together, as well as fixing things that need fixing, from bikes, cars, and kids' toys, to household appliances. He tinkered, modified, and invented various things both at home and school. His greatest invention was attaching an engine to the machine that draws chalk lines on soccer fields. However, it escaped through the school entrance at full speed, without turning or stopping. Growing up, Kazuichi was a very skittish person which made him easily picked on. He described himself as a "weak, cowardly kid" who did nothing but study and tinker with machines, which led him to believe that everyone who spoke to him was a nice person. Even when he was betrayed or lied to, he convinced himself they had a good reason for it, because he was too afraid to admit the truth. However, he did have a best friend in middle school, with whom he did everything and could talk about anything.
