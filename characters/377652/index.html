About Tempest

Born into the Shayeris, a reclusive and pacifist sect of the Atlantean people, Garth was discarded by his own society due to the violet hue of his eyes—a physical trait feared as the "Curse of Kordax." Abandoned to perish in the unforgiving depths of the Crag, he was eventually rescued by Arthur Curry, the man known as Aquaman. Under the King’s guidance, Garth rose from a forgotten outcast to a celebrated hero, serving for years as the first Aqualad and standing as a founding member of the original Teen Titans. His early years were defined by his loyalty to the crown and his growing status as a bridge between the undersea kingdoms and the surface world. His evolution from a protégé into a sovereign mystic occurred after he ventured into the Hidden Valley to study the ancient, long-forgotten arts of his ancestors. Emerging with a mastery over the elemental forces of the ocean, he shed his old identity to become Tempest, a warrior-sorcerer of unparalleled potential. No longer limited to physical strength and aquatic telepathy, Garth learned to manipulate water temperature to freeze or boil the seas, fire beams of concussive energy from his eyes, and travel across mystical dimensions. Though he has faced profound loss, including the destruction of his home and the weight of royal responsibilities, he remains a pillar of the Atlantean court and a guardian of the mystical ley lines that sustain the Seven Seas.
