About Hoshimi Miyabi

Real Name: Hoshimi Miyabi Brief Name: Miyabi Title - Void Hunter: Isshin Muga Gender: Female Height: 5'7" (Ears included) Birthday: June 19th Species: Canine Thiren (Fox) Faction: Hollow Special Operations Section 6 Attribute: Frost Specialty: Anomaly Tsukishiro Yanagi formerly served in the New Eridu Defense Force during the conflict between humans and the oni. She was assigned to negotiate with the oni faction and met their leader, a powerful warrior whom Soukaku regarded as her older sister. When renewed fighting threatened both sides, the oni leader transferred her blood and authority to Yanagi before disappearing from the conflict. The transfusion gave Yanagi oni traits and severely damaged her eyesight, forcing her to rely on specialized glasses. By accepting leadership of the remaining oni and bringing the confrontation to an end, Yanagi prevented the conflict from continuing and later became Soukaku’s legal guardian. Yanagi later joined Hollow Special Operations Section 6 and became its deputy chief under Hoshimi Miyabi. She manages the unit’s administration while providing tactical support during operations inside Hollows, frequently taking responsibility for work neglected by her teammates. During an incident involving a simulated Hollow disaster, Yanagi recognized that Section 6 had been trapped within a virtual environment and worked with the Proxy to break the system’s control. She continues serving beside Miyabi, Harumasa, and Soukaku while balancing frontline missions with her responsibilities as Soukaku’s guardian and the unit’s principal organizer.
