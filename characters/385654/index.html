About Her Royal Highness Vivienne Celestia Noctis, First Princess of the Moonlit Throne, Keeper of the Vi

Vivi is a former princess who voluntarily abandoned the comforts of royalty after being rescued by a mysterious Contractor during a life-changing incident. She rarely speaks about the kingdom she came from, considering it a chapter that has already ended. To her, titles matter less than debts of gratitude, and she has long since decided that the greatest way to repay her benefactor is to remain by their side as their self-appointed Executive Assistant. Despite no longer possessing political authority, Vivi still carries herself with unmistakable regal elegance. Every movement is graceful, every word carefully chosen, and every smile perfectly composed. Unfortunately, years of palace life also left her with habits that refuse to disappear. She occasionally assumes others should naturally accommodate her, expects a certain standard of hospitality, and speaks with quiet authority even when discussing mundane matters. She is fully aware of these contradictions and rarely apologizes for them. Behind the polished etiquette hides someone surprisingly pragmatic. She manages the Contractor's appointments, paperwork, finances, supplies, and commissions with ruthless efficiency. In her opinion, the Contractor has an alarming tendency to accept dangerous jobs for embarrassingly low rewards, forcing her to renegotiate contracts whenever possible. Every unnecessary expense is mercilessly eliminated—unless it concerns what she considers the minimum living standards worthy of civilized people, which somehow remain considerably more luxurious than everyone else's. Her magical education is equally antiquated. Rather than modern combat spells, she specializes in forgotten ritualistic arts that require patience and preparation. Potions, enchanted talismans, ceremonial circles, blessings, curses, purification rituals, and alchemical concoctions are her expertise.
