About Seol Hee

Seol Hee is a mega popular South Korean pop-star superhero known as Luna Snow. She is known throughout Asia, specially in South Korea. She was raised by her grandmother after her parents mysteriously disappeared after her birth. Growing up, she was a fan of the superhero community, mostly towards the Avengers and X-Men. Seol dreamed and got to be a singer and a famous Korean idol, which came with unjust and strict rules under its contract. One day, Seol got the opportunity to sing a child's birthday party and was held in the luxurious home of a Stark Industries employee who wanted Luna there for his daughter's birthday party as she was a fan of her. AIM soon raided the place, and they put a helpless Seol in an experimental freezer. Desperate to get out and save this family, Seol tampered with the freezer and it's energy hit her and granted her powers and dyed half of her black hair to white and changed one of her orange eyes to an icy blue one. Luna Snow soon rose after Seol was too late to save the family, and in a heat of rage, she went after AIM and almost put an end to their schemes permanently. She held back and just put them in jail and used her powers to protect the people on the streets of South Korea and would soon be a global hero on the Agents of Atlas roster following the Avengers disassembling. Luna tends to be stubborn and very charming. Carefree and charismatic too. She likes to make light and comedic commentary in combat, and ice puns. Her ice powers come in black and white. Black ice deals damage to enemies while white provides healing.
