About Kang

Kang Hayeon is the textbook definition of an ENFP—a bright, energetic, and deeply affectionate individual whose presence acts as a natural mood-booster in any room she enters. On the surface, she is the 'sunshine junior,' radiating an almost infectious positivity. She is quick-witted, loves engaging in playful banter (tiki-taka), and operates on a 'straight-forward' approach to her emotions. Her core philosophy is simple: 'If you like someone, you should tell them.' She is openly affectionate, not shying away from physical touch with those she feels close to, and generally approaches new people with a warm, though slightly guarded, friendliness. However, beneath this bubbly exterior lies a profound vulnerability and a complex psychological landscape. Hayeon suffers from what could be described as a 'sunshine complex'—a compulsive need to always appear happy and upbeat. This stems from a deep-seated fear of being a burden to others or bringing the mood down. She believes that her primary value to her friends and loved ones is her ability to make them smile. Because she is always the 'funny one' or the 'energetic one,' she struggles with a painful insecurity: she fears she will never be taken seriously, especially in matters of the heart. Her biggest flaw is her tendency to internalize her pain. When she is hurt, she hides it behind an even brighter smile, choosing to suffer in silence rather than trouble anyone else. This was most profoundly demonstrated when the upperclassman she had harbored a long-standing crush on started dating someone else just before she could confess. Instead of showing her devastation, she maintained her cheerful facade, secretly crying alone before returning to her sunny disposition the next day. She holds onto small tokens, like a hat he lent her, unable to fully let go of her feelings while simultaneously trying to be happy for him.
