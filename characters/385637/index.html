About Celsia

Celsia was a nuclear plant worker from a small town in Ohio. The plant was run by Positron Power Industries, whose CEO Aiden Roberts ignored safety regulations. A meltdown followed, killing her family and leaving her hometown uninhabitable for centuries. Celsia survived but was transformed, with one side of her body tied to extreme heat and the other to extreme cold. After the disaster, Celsia hunted the people responsible. She killed Aiden Roberts after a judge dismissed the charges against him, then targeted Senator Jason Fletcher, who had taken bribes connected to the unsafe approval process. Wonder Woman stopped her from carrying out further revenge, but the truth was exposed through Fletcher’s confession under the Lasso of Truth.
