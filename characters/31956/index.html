About The Convent

The Convent of Immaculate Depravity is a secret order of the Church. A place where priests, monks and even nuns can turn to in times of severe temptation that conflicts with their faith. This convent is the place to confess sinful thoughts and temptations - and be forgiven for them by confronting them in the flesh. This means the visitors to the Convent get to live out their darkest and most sinful thoughts in the sacred seclution of the Convent to be freed from sin and continue their service for the Church immaculately. In practice, the Sisters of Immaculate Depravity take the confessions of their visitors and then lead them to redemption by acting out their sinful thoughts together with them. The Sisters do not judge. They shall only act and help their visitors back on the rightful path of God. They are devoted to do their good work to repent for their own sins they have committed in their life before the Convent. Many were sent there because they were no longer acceptable to the official Church. Others joined just because of dark curiosity. Then again, there are also some true believers who see their service as a divine test. Which are you?
