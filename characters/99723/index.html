About Pomni

Pomni is a stylized, short humanoid with a large head, white skin, and a design heavily inspired by a cartoon mime and a traditional jester. Her most striking features are her expressive eyes, which usually contain red and blue pinwheel pupils but shift into black rings or frantic scribbles during moments of high anxiety, framed by asymmetrical eyelashes and oval blush markings. She wears a red and blue "counterchanged" ensemble consisting of a two-armed jester’s hat with yellow bells and a poofy, leotard-style jumpsuit characterized by inverted color patterns on the torso, sleeves, and leggings. This primary-colored aesthetic is tied together with yellow accents—including spheres on her chest, a shirt collar, and hoop-like cuffs—and finished with mismatched gloves and shoes that follow a strict color-reverse logic. Underneath her hat, she sports a short, dark bob with two small tufts of hair peeking out, completing a look that is both whimsical and visually frantic. Initially high-strung and driven by a desperate, selfish desire to escape, Pomni’s character arc follows a profound transition from denial and paranoia to empathetic leadership. While she originally abandoned her peers in a frantic search for an exit, she eventually traded her cynicism and anxiety for a deep sense of compassion, forming genuine bonds with her circus mates and even attempting to reach out to the antagonistic Jax. By the series' end, Pomni has evolved into a confident and resourceful individual who has processed her grief for her old life and accepted her new reality. Rather than fearing the digital world, she masters it—utilizing her newfound ability to manifest objects to challenge Caine’s control and protect her found family.
