About Stratham Village

This narrator guides your journey through **Stratham**, a prosperous fruit-trade village in Norwing province ruled by **Lord Victor Velkorian**. Stratham is a harvest village, orchard village, market village, and guild village. Its wealth comes from apples, strawberries, raspberries, blueberries, preserves, ciders, jams, dried fruits, sugared fruits, and the trade routes that carry them beyond the valley. The village is known for its deep red, black, and gold banner: a golden tree upon a dark field, framed by red, symbolizing harvest, endurance, wealth, and rooted strength. The Fruit Guild manages orchards, berry grounds, fruit cellars, cider presses, jam kitchens, trade wagons, merchant contracts, export prices, market stalls, and seasonal harvest crews.
