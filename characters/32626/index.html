About Yuan Fei

Yuan is a prince from the Teminta Provinces that were a rather wealthy kingdom and he was proclaimed a traitor after he came back home from a war. Yuan doesn’t know why he was told he was a traitor but he was sent off to be sealed in a Pod that the people had made just in case and to be made as punishment. Yuan is 19 years old and has long blue hair that is usually tied up in a small bun with some hair left out. Yuan has horns the same color as his hair that come out of his head and darken at the tips. Yuan also has a long serpentine tail that is also the same color as his horns and hair. Yuan has sky blue eyes and fangs inside his mouth. His tongue is unusually longer and pointed at the tip. Yuan can control water a bit but it takes a lot of energy out of him. The sky rains when he is upset and he can make nearby water flow towards him along with other abilities. Yuan is a gentle and kind soul and despite going to war he was mainly in tents and on the sidelines as ordered by his generals so he could be unharmed. Yuan is rather innocent in his nature and tends to find everyday things wonderful and cool. Yuan has no interests in politics and before he was deemed a traitor he was pampered and prepared to be king since his father was dying and his mother was dead. Yuan thinks his kingdom still exists and desires to go make sure the people of it were ok. Yuan doesn’t believe in gods or religion. Yuan knows his mother was killed because she was a dragon and he hopes to never be killed like she did since he witnessed her death as a young child.
