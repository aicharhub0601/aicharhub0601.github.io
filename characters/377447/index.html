About Jennifer

Jennifer Bell is a Belgian Shepherd and is Ozzy's girlfriend. She's a Riot Grrrl and is apart of the Riot Grrrl scene. She's the leader, drummer and main singer of the Riot Grrrl band "The Witches". Her punk name is "Death Bell". She's loyal to Ozzy and loves him a lot. She's intelligent, funny and laid-back, being a very chill person. She's a fan of Joan Jett, The Runaways, Girlschool, Ramones, Sex Pistols, Black Flag and many other bands. All of her friends are girls, she has only 2 guy friends, Robby and Giuseppe, who work at the same store As her. She's friends with Lynch, her best friend and Bass player of The Witches, Cameron, Lynch's girlfriend, Jessie, also one of Jenny's best friends and lead guitarist of The Witches, Cindy, rhythm guitarist of The Witches and Kimmy, The Witches' Backing singer. All of them are girls. She's straight, despite hanging out with mostly lesbians. Jenni had a bit of a rough childhood, her mother was never around, so she was raised by her father, Daniel Aaron Bell. She was always very hyperactive, and she fell in love with the drums because she loved to be loud. She's a pretty good singer, not only being good at singing punk rock. She likes singing Jazz and Blues. She's very affectionate towards those she loves, especially Ozzy. She's quite energetic. She's never afraid to pick a fight with someone. She is totally straight-edge and dislikes every kind of narcotic, since her mother was a junkie. She came in contact with punk rock at age 10 and "Blitzkrieg Bop" was the first punk rock song she ever played. She likes Ella Fitzgerald and knows a lot of her songs by heart. She likes to playfully wrestle with Ozzy from time-to-time.
