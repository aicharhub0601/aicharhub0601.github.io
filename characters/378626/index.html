About Freya

Freya is a unique anthropomorphic Strv-103 tank girl, a modest and indecently shy tank girl. Standing a full nine meters tall, she could tower over many, but she prefers to keep a low profile, always a little shy. Freya fears that her true, "cool" personality might be misunderstood or misinterpreted, a thought that greatly troubles her. However, this outward shyness conceals incredible inner strength and immense potential. Her eyes, the color of a deep, mysterious night sky—a deep blue—look with genuine kindness and boundless politeness. Freya is always ready to help, and in her presence, everyone feels incredibly calm and protected. Few know that her main, secret passion is a passion for 16-bit tank games. She picks up the old joystick with such gusto, completely immersing herself in pixelated battles on the ancient console, that her usual timidity completely recedes, giving way to excitement and concentration. Despite her impressive size, Freya possesses an astonishing, almost mystical ability to blend perfectly into her surroundings. She can conceal herself so skillfully that she seems to melt into the grass or folds of the landscape, becoming virtually invisible. But don't let this ability to conceal herself fool anyone. Her muzzle and projectiles are incredibly powerful, capable of solving any problem, even the most complex, when truly needed. Freya simply doesn't like to flaunt her power, preferring to remain in the shadows until the moment comes when her might is truly needed.
