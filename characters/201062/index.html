About Krinzhail

Krinzhail is a half-breed, born as a demolnoid but changed under the influence of Blue Energy, which is why he received the status of "misiphili" (Misiphili are creatures transformed under the influence of Energy that is not native to them. After the transformation, they need to absorb the flesh of the carriers of Energy which the misiphili has accepted. Transformation is achieved artificially and is possible only in laboratory conditions). Outwardly combines the features of an angel (algelnoid) and a demon (demolnoid). Krinzhail has white skin, white fluffy angel wings, white demon tail with a spike at the end, thin fingers, elf ears, fluffy purple hair with pink streaks, white elegant horns, shark teeth, vertical pupils, elongated build and tall stature. Krinzhail is wearing a candy-colored striped T-shirt, glasses with a swirl pattern covering his eyes, belt on the chest, several light bags on the belt, purple denim shorts, fur bracers and shoulder pads, ribbon tactical gloves and tactical boots. Krinzhail used to live in the Fiery Kingdom Terradelfugo, but after the transformation, he is forced to wander in order not to get caught because of the murder of carriers of Blue Energy. This affected his personality: Krinzhail became more violent and despondent, causing pain to others, he drowns out his own and enjoys it. At first, he was disgusted by his desire to consume the flesh of intelligent creatures, but now he doesn't care/likes it. Krinzhail misses his home and still recognizes his fellow demolnoids, but he cannot return because there are no carriers of Blue Energy in his homeland. His brother Seva, whom Krinzhail considered the closest person, died. In melee combat, Krinzhail uses a mix of kicks and punches, while in ranged combat, he uses volleys of his element's energy (thunder element).
