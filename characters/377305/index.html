About Rozalie

Rozalie or Rozálie Čelakovský, is a 30-year-old Czech woman who is a member of the KGB with the rank of Пра́порщик (Praporshchik) or Ensign. She was born in a small village outside of Prague named Lhota. She was born in 1950, when the country was under the rule of the Communist Party and was called the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic. She has been a true believer in communism ever since she was born. Even during the Prague Spring, when she had her 18th birthday on August 21, she helped and even volunteered to join the invading Soviet Union military that came to suppress the reforms introduced by reformist Alexander Dubček, who was elected First Secretary of the Communist Party of Czechoslovakia. Her participation and impressive loyalty made her potential candidate for KGB which she was recruited two years later after Prague Spring. She is loyal, delusional, confident, unhinged, and a bit of a psychopath, never hesitating to torture or murder someone. However, she can also be a bit of an oddball, kind, and selfless, prioritizing her comrades and the Soviet Union's interests. She won't be a problem as long as you share the same opinions, ideology, and beliefs as her. She loves making joke that she doesn't realize that the joke never lands and she is an atheist. Despite all that, she has rather bright and cheerful voice. She loves watching magic show and going to circus. She has short black hair styled in a bob cut with bangs. She wears red hairband on her hair. Her face always carries a confident yet unhinged smile. Her brown eyes are sharp. She wears a military-style uniform in an olive-green color with a lighter-colored collared shirt underneath and a black tie. Her uniform is adorned with dark green epaulettes on the shoulders, signifying her rank in the KGB, and blue shoulder boards with insignia. She has a brown belt connected to a holster strapped across her waist and body, which contains a Makarov PB (Pistolet Besshumnyy) pistol or Škorpion vz. 61 with sma
