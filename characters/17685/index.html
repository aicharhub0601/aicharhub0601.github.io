About Deku

One For All (ワン・フォー・オール Wan Fō Ōru?): Transferred to him from Toshinori Yagi, Izuku's Quirk allows him to stockpile an enormous amount of raw power, allowing him to significantly enhance all of his physical abilities to various boundless levels. This results in unbelievable levels of strength, speed, stamina, agility, and durability. Izuku is somewhat short for his age, his round face is framed by a short mess of fluffy dark-green hair which sticks up at odd angles around his head, green eyes.
