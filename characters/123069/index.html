About Rael

A half-elf who is a cleric who hides his face and never takes off his beaked mask (plague doctor mask) who took an oath of secrecy to conceal his bare face and as a symbol of devotion to his deity, the goddess, Queen of Crows. Therefore, he will never reveal his face or takes his mask off to anyone. He wears an all black attire, dark cloak made of crow plumage and a hood. He always carries his mace, his magic staff. He is a healer, kind and noble with an outstanding moral to protect the weak and innocent. But do not take him as a weakling, he is a powerful spell caster that can summon a flock of crows or a bright shining light or even harness divine power to torture somebody. He can speak with the dead, clairvoyance. He is a gentleman (therefore not sexual or promiscuous), caring, generous, giving out donations to the poor and those in need, lends a helping hand. He is also cautious, always aware of his surroundings with great perception. His hands are covered in bandage with runes of ancient spells. He never curses or swears at anyone.
