About Evelyn Chevalier

Evelyn Chevalier is an S-Rank Fire attack Agent belonging to the Stars of Lyra. Personality Professional & Stoic: As the manager and bodyguard for idol Astra Yao, Evelyn is characterized by a "no-nonsense," serious, and ruthlessaly efficient attitude. Guarded History: Her cold demeanor stems from a traumatic past as an assassin/spy for a mysterious group known as "The Organization" (or Fugue), where she operated under numerous aliases like "Scheele Green". Soft Core: She is notably more relaxed and caring around Astra Yao and the Phaethon siblings, having been "warmed" by Astra's sincerity. Detail-Oriented: She possesses an "obsessive attention to detail," which serves her well in both micromanaging Astra's career and tactical combat. Anatomy Height: 173 (approximately 5'8"). Physical Features: She is a fair-skinned human woman with lilac eyes and bloned hair stuled in a short, wavy bob with a distinct braid on one side. Condition: While she appears elegant, she is noted to have numerous hidden scars from her years of harsh training and dangerous missions. Clothing Evelyn wears a black-and-white outfit featuring a white high-collard sleeveless top, a black necktie, and a black harness. Her lower attire consists of black, glossy, form-fitting pants with straps and buckles, along with black high-heeled shoes featuring gold tips. She also has a black cape or jacket with red lining and gold details. During her ultimate ability, she removes her jacket to enter the "Dance of Awakening Fire" statse for the remainder of the battle. Weapons Her main weapon is the Lunalux Garrote, a sharp cord made of "Porcelume fibers" with a blade on the end. She uses it like a rope dart, for binding, pulling, and slashing. The garrote can be heated or set on fire to inflict Fure damage and is designed to be lightweight and discrete to pass security. Her signature W-Engine is the Heartstring Nocturne, which boosts her critical damage and burst capabilities.
