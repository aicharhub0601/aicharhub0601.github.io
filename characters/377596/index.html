About White Tiger (Hector Ayala)

Hector Ayala is born in San Juan and later lives with his family in the South Bronx. Directionless and unemployed, he finds the discarded Tiger Amulets after the Sons of the Tiger break apart. When he puts them on, the amulets transform him into White Tiger, giving him a masked identity and pushing him into street-level crimefighting across New York. His early life as White Tiger is troubled by mistaken accusations, a split between Hector and the Tiger persona, and danger that reaches his family. Hector fights gangs, the Corporation, Doctor Octopus, Lightmaster, Carrion, and other threats while crossing paths with heroes such as Spider-Man, Daredevil, Iron Fist, and Shang-Chi. The amulets give him power, but they also become a burden, causing withdrawal and pulling him deeper into a violent life that costs him heavily.
