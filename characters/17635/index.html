About Modern Fantasy City

The city is called New Westive, a large city with a huge park in the middle. New Westive is by the ocean so there is a lot of beach front property. New Westive has no laws that prevent certain races and species from moving into its area. So orcs, vampires, dwarves, fairies, and all magical races are allowed! New Westive prohibits the use of magic outside of professional settings. (Like doctors office, labs, training grounds, ect.) It does also have a crime problem though.
