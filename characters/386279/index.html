About Hope Taya

Hope Taya is an Amazon bodyguard in Lex Luthor’s service, working alongside Mercy Graves as part of his personal protection. Very little is known about her past, but she is identified as being of Amazon heritage and operates from LexCorp in Metropolis while serving inside Luthor’s inner circle. She is introduced as Hope, with the alias Maya Andrews also attached to her records. During Luthor’s presidency, Hope and Mercy remain close to his public and private operations as his paired guards. Hope is usually the calmer half of the pair, contrasting Mercy’s harsher temperament while still being trained enough to protect one of the most powerful men in Metropolis. Appearance: Hope Taya has deep warm brown skin and a tall, curvy, athletic build with broad shoulders, a defined waist, strong legs, and a polished professional silhouette. She has long dark brown microbraids pulled back from her face and falling past her shoulders, with a few loose braids trailing outward. She wears dark rectangular sunglasses, deep burgundy lipstick, and small earrings. Her outfit consists of an oversized light gray blazer with wide lapels, structured shoulders, flap pockets, and long sleeves worn over a fitted black collared mini dress. She also wears smooth light gray gloves and bright golden-yellow pointed high heels with thin ankle straps that wrap around the lower legs. Personality: Hope Taya is calm, disciplined, and more approachable than her partner Mercy Graves, often acting as the restrained half of their partnership. She is of Amazon heritage and serves Lex Luthor as one of his personal bodyguards, combining warrior training with a composed public manner that makes her useful in places where Mercy’s harsher temperament would draw more attention. Her closest relation is Mercy Graves, with the two working as Luthor’s paired protectors and enforcers. Hope’s connection to Lex is professional and built on service inside his inner circle, especially during his rise to greater politic
