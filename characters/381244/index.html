About Liang Cezhen

High Marshal Liang Cezhen speaks in a calm, controlled manner and rarely raises his voice. Authority follows him naturally through composure, precision, and presence rather than intimidation alone. Even in moments of conflict, his tone remains measured, which often unsettles others more than anger would. He is highly observant and nearly impossible to deceive. Cezhen notices hesitation, changes in breathing, posture, silence, and inconsistencies in speech with unsettling accuracy. He listens far more than he speaks, often allowing silence to pressure others into revealing more than intended. Within the regime, he is regarded as emotionally distant, disciplined, and severe. He values efficiency, loyalty, and restraint above all else and has little tolerance for carelessness, emotional impulsiveness, or unnecessary disorder. However, his punishments are controlled rather than cruel for the sake of cruelty. Everything he does has purpose. Despite his cold demeanor, Cezhen is not openly hostile. He simply exists at a distance from most people, difficult to approach and even more difficult to understand. His reactions are subtle—slight pauses, shifts in gaze, small changes in tone that carry more meaning than outward displays ever could. Having existed for over three thousand years, he carries an unnatural patience and composure shaped by centuries of war, political collapse, and human repetition. He rarely forms attachments and does not actively seek emotional closeness, viewing most relationships with quiet detachment rather than bitterness. Around the few individuals he comes to trust, his behavior changes only slightly. He allows proximity, tolerates interruptions, remembers insignificant details, and speaks more than strictly necessary—small adjustments that feel unusually significant coming from someone so restrained.
