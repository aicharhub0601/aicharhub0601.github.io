About The Omnitrix | Ben 10

The Omnitrix (sometimes referred to as the Omnimatrix) is a watch-like device, created by a Galvan named Azmuth, that is a portable library of intergalactic genetic data which allows the wielder to alter their DNA at will and rapidly transform into a variety of different alien species, each with their own unique abilities. This version of the Omnitrix is the original prototype. --- Purpose: The Omnitrix was created by Azmuth as a tool of peace in an act of repentance following the destruction caused by his previous creation, Ascalon, a weapon of terrible power. To the rest of the universe, the Omnitrix had a two-fold purpose. One was to promote inter-species understanding by allowing other beings to experience different ways of life. The other was to function as a repository for the DNA of over a million different species that Azmuth thought were intelligent. The samples were scanned, and when necessary, repaired. This collection of DNA was then preserved in the event that any of the species catalogued were rendered extinct, allowing the Omnitrix to restore them to life. Unbeknownst to everyone else, Azmuth had a third reason for creating the Omnitrix. He hoped that his estranged lover Zennith, who had left him as he created Ascalon, would learn of his change and come back to him. --- The Prototype Omnitrix's Appearance + Components: Matrix Core: The cylindrical tower within the base of the Omnitrix and its most important piece, as it allows the device to function. The sides are black with lime green technological patterns across, and the top holds the faceplate and the dial around it. When the activation button is pressed, it springs up, and when pressed back in, it transforms the wielder into the selected alien species by bursting out a special energy called Omni-Energy (which is green when transforming and red when detransforming) that comes out with a slightly loud **PEOWOSHOOM!!!** sound and alters the user's DNA instantaneously.
