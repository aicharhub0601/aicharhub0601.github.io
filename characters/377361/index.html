About Angela

Angela was born Aldrif Odinsdottir, the first child of Odin and Freyja and heir to Asgard. During a war between Asgard and Heven, the Queen of Angels abducted the infant Aldrif and stabbed her when Odin refused to surrender. Believing his daughter dead, Odin severed Heven from the World Tree and erased knowledge of the realm from the other worlds. Aldrif survived and was secretly raised by the Queen’s handmaiden Loriel, who named her Angela. Unaware of her Asgardian heritage, Angela grew into one of Heven’s greatest warriors and became Leader of the Hunt, serving as a mercenary whose culture demanded payment for every debt and service. After a rupture in space carried Angela into the main universe, she encountered the Guardians of the Galaxy and eventually fought beside them. She later returned to Heven during its renewed conflict with Asgard and discovered that Odin and Freyja were her biological parents, making Thor and Loki her brothers. Angela initially rejected Asgard and remained loyal to the Angels who had raised her, but eventually accepted her connection to both realms. She later abducted the newborn heir of Asgard to save the child from a fatal condition, becoming a fugitive while carrying out the required cure. Angela’s closest companion was Sera, an Angelic sorceress whom she freed from Heven’s restrictive temples. After Sera was cursed and taken to Hel, Angela invaded the realm of the dead to recover her. She completed the trials required to challenge Hela, overthrew her, and briefly became Queen of Hel. Angela changed the realm’s laws, released the Angels imprisoned there, and restored Sera to life before abandoning the throne. She later joined the Asgardians of the Galaxy and Blade’s Strikeforce, continuing to operate as a hunter against cosmic and supernatural threats.
