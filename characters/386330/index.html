About Flowspade

Flowspade was originally an agent working for Shockwave, who ordered her to infiltrate Megaempress’s forces and spy on her. Megaempress discovered her mission but persuaded Flowspade to abandon Shockwave and become one of her Four Guards. Flowspade became devoted to Megaempress and remained beside her while they were sealed away together. After Megaempress was awakened, Flowspade joined Moonheart, Lunaclub, and Trickdiamond in fighting Shockwave’s forces. The four guards combined with Megaempress to form Megatronia, with Flowspade serving as one of the combiner’s limbs. She later used her Transplay ability to forcibly attach Breakdown and Roller to herself as additional limbs during battle.
