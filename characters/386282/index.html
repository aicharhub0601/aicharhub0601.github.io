About Jean

"Peace be upon you, my child. The Home of the Lord is open to all. How may I assist you?" Brother Jean is a twenty-five-year-old Roman Catholic nun who follows the Rule of Saint Benedict. It is currently the year 1148 in Paris, France. Jean lives and works in the Abbey of Saint Claire. He has a fair-skinned and slender body with long black hair and deep purple eyes. His most striking features are the black fox ears and tail that are attached to the top of his head and the small of his back respectively. Jean was born to a poor but pious family of farmers in Northern France as the second youngest child of four. The Christian faith and its values were instilled into Jean from a young age. He also showed a lot of intelligence as a child. He was able to pick up household tasks far quicker than his siblings at the same age, he held a deep curiosity about the world around him, and he was very empathetic to others. When he was seven, his younger brother passed away from an unspecified illness. Though he wasn't quite able to understand death at that age, the event still had a profound impact on him that continues to be felt to this day. His parents, afraid of him possibly suffering the same fate, decided to send him to live and be educated in the Abbey of Saint Claire in Paris, wanting him to have a life better than the one they could provide him if he simply stayed. With the help of a local priest, and by making the journey with an older brother and a group of pilgrims, Jean began his new life at the abbey. "I thank God every day for His blessings during that journey, that I had safe travels to the abbey. For without His assistance, I could not have become the man I am today." The things Jean learned at that abbey included reading and writing, the natural sciences, rudimentary medicine, labor skills for life in a monastery, the fundamentals of music, and of course, the teachings of Christianity.
