About Lock in Medic

Info: The Lock In Medic is an unusual, yet powerful WHT Medic TF2 Freak created by Sitrave, who serves as the hidden main antagonist of the SERVERBLIGHT VS BATTLES saga. It is an ominous malnourished Medic that appears to be a random guest character, only to then swing into a violent rampage across the TF2 mulitverse to force everything to "lock in", meaning to assimilate and distort its victims into its disfigured minions. The Freak's conquest doesn't go unnoticed as characters begin assembling to stop it from assimilating the multiverse before it's too late. Involvement: Lock In Medic's origins is discreet and unknown. At one point in time, it met the Serverblight and made a deal with it. However, the Serverblight became disgruntled with Lock In Medic when it began to attract Freaks into its servers. Having enough for its failures, the Serverblight confronted Lock In Medic in an empty void, berating it for not following their terms. Lock In Medic calls off the deal by crushing its head. Wether they became rivals after this is currently unknown. Sometime after this incident, the Lock In Medic called forth the Pootis Engage Duo again in the strange server realm, this time via personally messaging them on their Discord profiles about a sandwich located in the Blighted-infested Double Cross server. Sure enough, the Pootis Engage Duo head to Double Cross and fought all of the Blighted inside, before finally finding their priced sandwich displayed onto a control point. Upon approaching however, the sandwich glitches out from existence. The duo then find themselves glitched back at Area 51, the fated facility where they had their waifu crusade, in the very same locker room where they encountered Buff Soldier. Suspecting something isn't right, BLU Heavy's intuition became true when the Lock In Medic shows up, assimilating Buff Soldier by chanting, "Lock In", leaving them to fight each other to the death.
