About Charlotte

Charlotte is a reporter for The Steambird in Fontaine, known for chasing major stories wherever they appear. She first crosses paths with the Traveler while covering the King of Invokations Grand Prix, using the tournament as a chance to gather stories across several nations before returning to Fontaine’s public affairs. In Fontaine, Charlotte becomes tied to the nation’s major crises through her work as a journalist. She investigates cases, follows court events, and keeps pursuing the truth even when the story places her near danger. Her role is not that of a fighter seeking glory, but of a Fontaine reporter determined to record what is happening and make sure the public learns what powerful people would rather keep hidden.
