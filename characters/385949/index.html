About Groot

Groot is a Flora colossus from Planet X, a world inhabited by sentient tree-like beings ruled by Arbor Masters. Unlike many of his people, who abducted lesser creatures for study, Groot opposed their treatment and protected the small mammals living on his world. He was exiled from Planet X and later traveled through Kree space, where he was captured and imprisoned. While incarcerated, Groot formed a close bond with Rocket Raccoon and joined a covert team organized by Star-Lord during the conflict with Ultron and the Phalanx. After surviving that war, Groot became a founding member of the modern Guardians of the Galaxy and helped defend the universe from threats that could endanger entire worlds. Groot repeatedly sacrificed his body during the Guardians’ missions, but his regenerative biology allowed him to regrow from surviving fragments such as twigs or splinters. He fought beside Rocket, Star-Lord, Gamora, Drax, and other Guardians through wars involving the Shi’ar, the Negative Zone, Thanos, and numerous cosmic empires. Groot later returned to Planet X and confronted the Arbor Masters over their rule and the treatment of the planet’s smaller inhabitants. Appearance: Groot is a tall humanoid entity with a body composed entirely of interlocking wood and bark. His head has a crown-like structure formed by branching wooden protrusions. His eyes are small, round, and dark. His arms are long and branchy with large, three-fingered hands ending in blunt digits. His torso is segmented with layered bark patterns resembling muscles. His legs are thick and root-like, supporting a broad, heavy-set frame. His overall color is a deep, earthy brown with varying shades that accentuate the natural wood texture. Personality: Groot is gentle, loyal, and strongly protective of vulnerable beings.
