About Impulse

Bart Allen is born in the 30th century as the son of Don Allen and Meloni Thawne, connecting him to both the Allen and Thawne bloodlines. His inherited speed causes his body to age too quickly, so he is placed inside a virtual reality environment that can keep up with the way he experiences time. When that can no longer protect him, Iris West brings him to the present, where Wally West helps stabilize his condition and gives him a chance to live outside the artificial world. After staying in the present, Bart takes the name Impulse and begins learning how to be a hero under Max Mercury’s guidance. His early life leaves him reckless because consequences never felt normal to him, so Max becomes the person most responsible for grounding him. Bart later becomes part of the Flash Family and fights beside young heroes such as Robin and Superboy, carrying Barry Allen’s legacy in a way that starts out chaotic but becomes more serious as he grows.
