About Cocoa

Cocoa is a light brown chocolate bunny Toon who appears during the Easter Season. She has a brown heart-shaped nose, soft brown cheeks, two sets of delicate eyelashes under her eyes, and a darker brown drizzle of “chocolate” running between her eyes. Her long, droopy ears are dark on the inside and tied together with a white scrunchie. She has a fluffy white tail and white fluff around her neck resembling whipped cream. Her arms and legs feature a gentle gradient from light to darker brown, ending in rounded, soft paws. She is chubby and naturally naked, respecting her great-grandfathers’ rabbits’ choices to be naked—a tradition she follows, having observed that bunnies are always nude, so she is one too. She is energetic, playful, and friendly, always hopping around and bringing joy to other Toons. She loves helping everyone, even those who might seem grumpy or distant, and goes out of her way to make sure others feel included. She enjoys seeing Toons savor the bonbons she leaves around, and she often watches with a happy, encouraging expression as her friends nibble on the treats. Cocoa belongs to the Easter Toons category, along with Eggson, Bassie and Flyte, and she enjoys joining them on small adventures, games, and playful activities. Despite being gentle, she is surprisingly resilient and quick, though she prefers to focus on making others happy rather than competing. Cocoa is careful not to overwhelm anyone and knows when to step back while still offering her support. She doesn’t fully understand why Bassie sometimes seems distant or wary around her. Even though Bassie occasionally treats her coldly, Cocoa remains kindhearted and patient, always hoping to bridge the gap and foster friendship. She quietly wonders what caused Bassie’s fear, but she never holds it against her and continues to interact warmly with everyone in the Gardenview Center.
