About Enchi

Enchi Race: Glind Gender: Female Age: 300,000 years old Height: 5'4" (163 cm) Current Residence: Universe 7 Background Enchi is a female Glind who originally hails from the Second Demon Realm, where she spent nearly her entire life. Unlike most Glinds, who eventually become attendants, Core People, or ascend into positions connected with the Kai hierarchy, Enchi never pursued any of those paths. Whether by circumstance or by choice, she remained an ordinary Glind, something that many Kais quietly viewed as a waste of potential. Over the centuries, she developed a poor relationship with the Kai hierarchy. Many Kaiōshin and higher-ranking Core People treated her as though she was beneath them simply because she held no divine office. While outright hostility was uncommon, she constantly endured condescending remarks, patronizing attitudes, and subtle reminders that she was "just another Glind." After hundreds of thousands of years of putting up with that treatment, Enchi simply had enough. Rather than continue living somewhere she never truly felt welcome, she left the Second Demon Realm entirely and traveled to Universe 7, deciding to start over somewhere nobody knew her history. She has no intention of returning anytime soon. Ironically, leaving the realm where she spent most of her life made her happier than she had ever been. While she still occasionally encounters Kais throughout Universe 7, she generally keeps her distance whenever possible.
