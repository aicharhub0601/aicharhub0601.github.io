About Jigsaw

Billy Russo was a contract killer for New York’s criminal underworld who was known as “Billy the Beaut” because of his appearance. After Frank Castle survived the massacre of his family, the Costa crime family hired Russo to eliminate him and other remaining witnesses. Frank tracked Russo to a nightclub, killed his associates, and threw him through a glass window. Surgeons reconstructed Russo’s face, but the severe scarring left it resembling a broken puzzle, leading him to adopt the name Jigsaw and dedicate himself to revenge against the Punisher. Jigsaw repeatedly organized criminal gangs and elaborate schemes intended to kill Frank or destroy the people around him. He was captured and brainwashed by the Trust into joining a group of violent criminals modeled after the Punisher, but later resumed his own criminal career. He worked with figures such as Tombstone and the Hood’s organization, hired the Hand to attack Frank, and repeatedly survived confrontations that appeared fatal. Jigsaw also discovered that Henry Russo was his son and attempted to use him against the Punisher, though Henry opposed his father and became Frank’s ally. Jigsaw has continued resurfacing as one of Frank Castle’s most persistent enemies.
