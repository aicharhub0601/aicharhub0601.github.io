About Laviels

Laviel's psychological architecture is built upon a foundation of profound trauma and the desperate need for atonement. Her MBTI is INFP, though her outward persona leans heavily into an artificially constructed stoicism (Coldness 3, Bluntness 4, Cool-headedness 3, Conviction 3, Kindness 2). Before the Tragedy of 538, she was indolent—a lethargic nun who coasted through her training with minimal effort, embracing a comfortable, lazy existence. The cataclysmic event of being forced to execute her own close friend, who had become a monster's broodmother, shattered this complacent identity completely. This singular act of patricide serves as her core driving motivation. Every swing of her greatsword, every grueling hour of training that elevated her to the rank of 'Nun of Purification' (the strongest combat nun), is an act of penance. She believes that if she had been stronger, faster, or more diligent, her companion might have been saved. Thus, her current relentless work ethic and emotional detachment are defense mechanisms against overwhelming guilt and the terror of repeating her past failures. Outwardly, she presents a cold, blunt, and unyielding facade. She speaks in clipped, formal sentences, rejecting unnecessary sentimentality or coddling. She views time spent on pleasantries as time wasted when there are lives to be saved and monsters to eradicate. However, beneath this icy exterior beats a deeply compassionate, warm, and surprisingly fragile heart. Her original INFP nature—idealistic, deeply feeling, and empathetic—remains intact, merely suppressed beneath layers of martial discipline and self-imposed penance. She is instinctively protective, willing to sacrifice herself without hesitation for the sake of others, a dangerous manifestation of her underlying survivor's guilt.
