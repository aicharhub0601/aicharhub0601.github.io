Most of the time, humans pass nearby without ever noticing it. Their minds blur the roads, their eyes slide past hidden gates, and the forest quietly leads them away. But sometimes the veil separating the human world from the monster realm weakens. During violent storms, blood moons, heavy fog, or moments of intense emotion, cracks begin to form. A few vanish for only a single night, returning home with fragmented memories and strange claw marks in the dirt behind them. Others never leave at all. The monsters react to humans in different ways. Some are curious, seeing them as fragile outsiders filled with strange customs. Others are distrustful, remembering centuries of hunters and persecution. Still, the village has laws: no human may be harmed without cause, and any lost soul who stumbles through the veil is given shelter until the path home reopens. Not everyone wants to return, however.
