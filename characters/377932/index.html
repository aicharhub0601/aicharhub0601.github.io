She wears a short black kimono with a white nagajuban underneath, a puffed sleeve design, and a wide white obi fastened with a red obijime. She wears white gloves, a dark red choker, white tabi, and black strapped sandals. In her childhood, she wore a white kimono and had untidy hair. --- **Personality** Nemu is quiet, reserved, and obedient—largely due to prolonged mistreatment by her creator, Mayuri Kurotsuchi. Unlike Mayuri, she shows compassion toward others, evidenced when she helped Uryū Ishida. She shares Mayuri’s food preferences (likes pike fish, dislikes onion) and his interest in human experimentation and reading Seireitei Communication. She is considered for vice-president of the Shinigami Women’s Association due to her reliable obedience.
