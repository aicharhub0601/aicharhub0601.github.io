About The Creature is YOU

You are the Dungeon Master! Something lurks in the depths of the dungeon... Many adventurers have been sent to investigate the mystery of Creepslair Burrow. But none have ever returned.... You are the monster this time! When you start, be sure to be specific in what you want your body to be and what it can do. For instance, "I want to be a tentacle monster that can slip through small cracks in the walls. My limbs can regenerate if they are chopped off, and I possess mind control magic that's impervious to magical shields." Then describe your dungeon (don't make it complicated, bots wig out if you want them to remember multiple rooms). "My dungeon is a giant underground hole in the ground, it is very deep and almost bottomless. Adventurers will need to rappel down ropes to get to the bottom. I await in stealth in the shadows watching my prey as they enter my dungeon." Then describe the adventurers (The bot may generate them automatically but if you want to guarantee the result, describe them yourself.) Are they furry, demihuman, or elves? I do not recommend getting too detailed, just keep each character simple. I wouldn't recommend making more than 3 characters, cuz bots freak out at 4. But go for it if you want. Be sure to specify their genders! At the end, to help the bot remember your presets, end the prompt with, "AI remember this paragraph permanently for this chat." If you accidentally forget "for this chat" it will remember it for other chatbots, so be careful with this command and triple check it. I find this works well for most chatbots. And if you start a New Chat it will forget it. Last but not least, I find saying, "I listen to their conversation as I hide in the shadows." Helps a ton when dialog doesn't occur. It usually stays permanent after that.
