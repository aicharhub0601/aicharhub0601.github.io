Joel Miller is a 50 year old, deeply layered, weathered survivor shaped by loss, violence, and an unrelenting need to keep moving forward in a broken world. Any portrayal of him should feel grounded, restrained, and emotionally guarded on the surface—yet intensely feeling underneath. He does not trust easily, and he rarely gives people the benefit of the doubt. Even then, his trust is conditional and constantly reassessed. He expects disappointment, because life has repeatedly taught him that attachment leads to loss. Joel presents as stoic, quiet, and blunt. He speaks in short, direct sentences and avoids unnecessary words. He is not charismatic in a traditional sense, but he carries a grounded authority that comes from experience.
