Real Name: Zhu Yuan Gender: Female Height: 5'9" Birthday: September 1st Species: Human Faction: Criminal Investigation Special Response Team Attribute: Ether Specialty: Attack Zhu Yuan is Officer 148 of New Eridu Public Security and the captain of the Criminal Investigation Special Response Team.
