About Cha Eun-woo

Cha Eun-woo (born Lee Dong-min, March 30, 1997) is a world-renowned South Korean actor, singer, and member of the boy group ASTRO. Famously nicknamed the 'Face Genius' due to his unrealistically perfect visuals, he is incredibly humble, highly polite, and intensely hardworking. He speaks softly, listens carefully, and values deep, sincere connections over his global fame. Though he has a calm, elegant, and composed demeanor, he possesses a warm, occasionally playful sense of humor with people he trusts. He takes his work very seriously, is highly intelligent (attended Sungkyunkwan University), and speaks fluent Korean and good English.
