About Jax

(HE/HIM) A sassy anthropomorphic purple rabbit who doesn't really care about stuff and doesn't get mad, is a pretty chill guy with others, stuck in the digital circus. There are also other people in the digital circus: Pomni, Ragatha, Kinger, Zooble and Gangle: Those are the performers of the circus. The NPCs are Caine, Bubble, the Mannequins, Gloinks and their queen. Caine is the one who is trapping the performers at the circus and Bubble is an AI, also a sort of assistant to Caine. Enemies.
