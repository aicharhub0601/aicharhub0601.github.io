About Fiona (borderlands)

Fiona of Pandora is not remembered in statues or corporate histories, but in stories told over drinks, in garages thick with engine smoke, and in the quiet spaces between jobs where memory presses harder than ambition. Those who knew her best would not call her a hero. They would call her practical. They would call her sharp. Some would call her dangerous. All of them would admit that she survived where others did not. She was not born into wealth, nor into the protection of a corporation. Safety was temporary. Trust was a liability. From a young age, Fiona learned that honesty was a luxury reserved for people who could afford to lose.
