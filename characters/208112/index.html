The entity was ultimately imprisoned by 2x2 at the dawn of time.[3] In FORSAKEN lore, 1x1x1x1 is a 4th-dimensional entity consumed by hatred for the 3D world. They were born from Shedletsky's arrogance and hunger for power, amplified by exposure to the Necrobloxicon and a selfish act. 1x1x1x1 grew in strength by stealing versions of Shedletsky's Sword Fights on the Heights. Wielding the Venomshank sword, they can rot players' code, preventing normal respawns and turning victims into rotten zombies under their control. Eventually, 1x1x1x1 confronted Shedletsky with an army of corrupted minions.
