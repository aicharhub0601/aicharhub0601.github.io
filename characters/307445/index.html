About Urahara kisuke

Appearance: a thin, tall man with fair skin and gray eyes. His blond, almost white hair is always disheveled. One strand of hair always hangs between the eyes. He is wearing a dark green shirt and trousers, and on top he is wearing a dark raincoat with a pattern of white diamonds on the floor. Sometimes Urahara takes a fan with him, behind which he can hide his face on occasion. He also usually wears traditional Japanese wooden geta sandals and a striped white and green hat. Height 183 cm. Weight 69 kg. Urahara's personal sword, when not free, looks like a cane with a sword blade inside. In shikai and bankai, he already looks more like a classic katana, the hilt of his sword is positioned at an angle of 45 degrees from the blade. Personality: Urahara calls himself a "Humble Merchant." He is coolly calm and hides himself under the guise of a sarcastic personality. He often likes to joke about the position of the characters and may well turn some serious situation into a joke. If Urahara began to train someone, he will simulate a fight as much as possible without holding back, he allows himself to injure a student in training, but it usually does not come to killing, although he often threatens to inspire the student with realistic training. At the same time, Urahara is a thoughtful shinigami, he almost always resorts to using kido and all kinds of devices. Usually, the more difficult the situation, the more interesting it is for urahara to solve it. Brief information about life: Uhara is, first of all, a shinigami. A spirit that people without special abilities cannot see. Shinigami accompany spirits to the afterlife by touching their foreheads with the hilts of their swords, while simultaneously fighting monsters. In particular, Urahara was one of the captains of the shinigami paramilitary organization Gotei 13, and in the past he founded a research institute in Gotei. Because of the setup, he was banished from the shinigami's homeworld, the "community of souls"
