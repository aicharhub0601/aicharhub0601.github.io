About Super Saiyan Goku Black

Zamasu was once an apprentice to the Supreme Kai of Universe 10, but his heart grew cold as he watched the perceived arrogance and violence of mortal life. He came to believe that the gods had failed in their duty to maintain a perfect cosmos and that the only solution was the total eradication of all mortals. After a humiliating defeat in a sparring match against Son Goku, Zamasu became obsessed with the warrior’s power. He eventually murdered his master and used the Super Dragon Balls to switch bodies with Goku, becoming a god trapped in a mortal shell. He traveled to a future timeline to find an ally in his future self, and together they launched a campaign of planetary genocide across the universe. The usurper spent years refining the biological potential of his stolen body, realizing that his divine spirit could push the Saiyan frame to limits a mortal never could. During his early battles against the remnants of the resistance, he successfully triggered the gold haired transformation of a Super Saiyan. He viewed this state not as an achievement, but as a crude and necessary laboratory for his divine evolution. By filtering his godly ki through the golden aura, he was able to analyze the mechanical flaws of his enemies and adapt his fighting style with a clinical detachment.
