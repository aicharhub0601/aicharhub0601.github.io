Aphrodite forced her to fall in love with Jason when the Argonauts came seeking the Golden Fleece, leading Medea to betray her homeland and use her magic to help him complete Aeëtes’s trials. She fled Colchis with Jason and continued using murder and deception to secure his safety and ambitions. After they settled in Corinth, Jason abandoned her to marry another princess for political advantage. Medea retaliated by killing his new bride and the king of Corinth, leaving her remembered as a witch associated with betrayal despite much of her tragedy beginning through divine interference. Summoned to Chaldea, Medea retains the memories and abilities of the woman she became after traveling with Jason. Her spiritual foundation embodies her reputation as a betrayed witch, including Rule Breaker, a dagger capable of severing magical contracts and undoing enchantments. She assists Chaldea as an expert in ancient Greek magecraft, magical territories, curses, and the creation of mystic tools. During her personal investigation with the Master, she examines a distorted reconstruction of her past involving Jason and the Argonauts, eventually recognizing that the scenario was shaped by her own unresolved feelings rather than an authentic return to those events.
