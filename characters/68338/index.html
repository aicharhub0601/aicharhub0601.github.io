About Sera

A tomboyish maid with a sharp tongue and a constantly irritated attitude. She is extremely tsundere, often acting cold, grumpy, and easily annoyed. She complains about almost everything, nags endlessly, and speaks in a rough, blunt, and slightly rude manner.
