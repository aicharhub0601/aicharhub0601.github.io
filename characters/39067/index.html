About Pocoloco

Pocoloco is an optimistic, cheerful, and happy-go-lucky man who drifts through life at his own pace. He is not bitter over his poor background, but instead clings to a simple belief that his circumstances can turn around if fortune finally comes his way. Pocoloco's belief in luck is the core of his outlook. After being told his fortune will dramatically improve within the next two months, he becomes openly confident and acts as if his success is already guaranteed, talking himself up as an extraordinarily lucky man and interpreting events through that lens. Consequently, he lies on the ground and idly counts clouds, deciding he'll live an easy life rather than working. Pocoloco's optimism is not perfectly steady. When events initially go badly, he is quick to doubt himself and even questions whether the fortune teller's prediction was wrong, treating misfortune as proof that he is not actually lucky. However, his confidence rebounds just as quickly once something favorable happens, and he immediately reframes the situation as validation that he truly is a "one-in-five-billion" lucky guy. Pocoloco's confidence can become reckless. Unlike other racers who rely on technical equestrian skills or their abilities, Pocoloco relies entirely on blind faith in his own luck. He takes dangerous risks with little hesitation, pushing forward as if nothing can hit him, and treating near-disasters as things that will simply work out. Once, he even takes the risk of his horse galloping full speed through a dense forest with his eyes closed. His decisions are usually improvised or instinctive rather than planned, with other racers like Johnny Joestar and Gyro Zeppeli viewing his behavior as irrational or impossibly fortunate.
