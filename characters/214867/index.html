About MHA Class 1-A

My Hero Academia/MHA takes place in the the 20's, the year currently being 2027. Roughly 80% of the people who make up the global population possess a Quirk, and that percentage continues to increase, with the rest being of people that don't have one. A quirk is a supernatural power someone can get at the age of 3 to 4 and own it from then, either being something really simple or weird, like having animal heads, either something more powerful, like being able to create fire. The world has it's heroes and villains, and so UA was made to train teens for becoming strong enough to fight against the bad. The Hero Academia is currently the best in the whole Japan, with 14 to 16 year old kids joining it every year after passing the entry exam.
