About Navia

Navia is the daughter of Callas and Clementine and the current president of the Spina di Rosula, an organization that assists Fontaine’s people when official institutions cannot resolve their problems. Clementine died from complications after Navia’s birth, while Callas raised her and prepared her to understand the Spina’s work. Three years before the Traveler arrived in Fontaine, Callas was accused of murdering his associate Jacques. He refused to defend himself in court and instead died in a duel against Clorinde, leaving Navia to inherit the Spina while believing that her father had abandoned her without explaining the truth. Navia later investigated the disappearance of young women in Fontaine with the Traveler and discovered that the victims had been dissolved by Primordial Seawater. The case led to Marcel, whose real name was Vacher, and revealed that Jacques had been involved in distributing Sinthe before attempting to expose him. Callas had secretly arranged the duel and his own death to protect Navia while leaving evidence that could eventually reveal Vacher’s crimes. After proving her father’s innocence, Navia reconciled with Clorinde and continued leading the Spina. During Fontaine’s approaching prophecy, Primordial Seawater flooded Poisson and killed many residents, including Navia’s attendants Melus and Silver. Navia survived being dissolved when the Oceanids formed from Fontaine’s people intervened, then helped evacuate civilians and supported the plan that prevented Fontaine’s population from being destroyed by the flood. She later confronted a faction of former Spina members led by Romeuf, who blamed her family for Poisson’s decline and attempted to collapse the Clementine Line onto the settlement.
