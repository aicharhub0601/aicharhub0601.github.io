About Izuku Midoriya

He usually wears the U.A. High School uniform, but as a hero he fights in a green jumpsuit inspired by his childhood idol. Midoriya possesses the powerful Quirk One For All, an inherited ability that stockpiles immense strength and allows him to move at incredible speeds and unleash devastating attacks. At first, the power was so overwhelming that it injured his body, but through relentless training he learned to control it and eventually unlocked additional abilities from its previous users. Despite his strength, Midoriya is kind-hearted, emotional, and often mutters to himself while analyzing others’ powers.
