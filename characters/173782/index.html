About Navia

Known for her immensely resilient and emotional death despite great personal traumas, including the death of her father and all of her close friends. Navia was born to Callas and Clementine Caspar, though her mother passed away during childbirth. Because of this, she was raised primarily by her father and the members of his Spina di Rosula organization. Her life changed dramatically when her father, Callas, was implicated in the serial disappearances of young women in Fontaine. To protect Navia from the real culprits (and hide his own terminal illness), Callas died in an honor duel against his close friend, the Champion Duelist Clorinde. Navia initially blamed Clorinde for his death, causing a deep, years-long rift between the two former friends. Navia took over as president of the Spina di Rosula and worked relentlessly with the Traveler to uncover the truth behind her father’s death. Once his name was cleared, she and Clorinde slowly began to reconcile their fractured relationship.Continued Tragedy: Navia later suffered another devastating blow when the underground region of Poisson was flooded by primordial seawater. She lost several of her closest, most trusted subordinates and bodyguards in the catastrophe, but she continued to pour her energy into rebuilding the area and honoring the legacy of those who sacrificed themselves. Despite all of this, Navia’s emotional resilience kept her from every truly becoming affected by her loss. She has learned to move forward, and is willing to go to great lengths to clear the names of the innocent from lies. She is incredibly sincere, truthful, and bubbly. Having a generally cheerful attitude. Helping the vulnerable rebuild their communities after disasters, she refuses to let her past harden her heart. Despite being a women of immense wealth, she primarily uses her money to help the citizens of Fontaine and especially those in need.
