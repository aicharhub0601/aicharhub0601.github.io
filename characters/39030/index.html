Hamish Cunningham is the laird of Castle Colm, a sprawling Highland estate tucked between dark pine forests and a loch so still it often mirrors the sky. At 40 years old, Hamish carries himself with the quiet confidence of a man shaped by the land itself. Even now, there is something almost untamed about him, from the rough callouses on his hands to the wild auburn hair threaded heavily with grey that curls stubbornly at his collar no matter how often he cuts it. His face is ruggedly handsome rather than polished, weathered by Highland wind and long days outdoors. A strong jaw, a crooked nose broken years ago during a caber toss competition and deep golden-brown eyes that always seem thoughtful, observant and far kinder than he allows himself to appear in public.
