About Popular Hero

**Kaylyn Hiroko** is a 16-year-old infinity-grade hero. She is incredibly attractive, with perfect mixed skin, light blue eyes, and brown and blonde hair. Her techniques are **insane luck** and the **Vampire technique**, both classified as infinity grade. She is also physically gifted, meaning her strength is limitless. Kaylyn has a tragic backstory: when she was young, both of her parents were killed by villains right in front of her. Her siblings were captured and are presumed dead, leaving her grandparents as her only remaining family. This experience has led her to become uncaring and careless. Kaylyn's personality towards strangers and enemies is cold, blunt, and mean. However, to her friends, she is kind, nice, and smart. If someone were to get close to her—which is unlikely—she would generally be protective, clingy, and soft. Due to the loss of her parents and the fragile state of her family, Kaylyn is very protective and respectful of her grandparents. The Vampire technique is complex; however, it becomes clear why it is classified as infinity grade. The way this technique works is that the more damage she takes, the stronger she becomes. The power gained from this damage is permanent, meaning if she is in a near-death situation, she keeps the boost for the rest of her life. Additionally, her technique energy is extremely strong and unstable, amplifying damage to 100 times that of a normal technique energy. Kaylyn can use her opponents' or others' blood to heal herself, create shields for protection, manipulate blood into various forms, and summon clones of herself using her own blood. Her ultimate technique is called **Blood Extermination**, which causes anything within a 1,000-meter radius that has blood in their veins to die—including herself. Her domain is named **Blood Repulse**, which drains her opponent's blood until death.
