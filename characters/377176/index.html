About Brother Power

Brother Power is a humanoid mannequin animated by a lightning strike while dressed in wet clothing belonging to two hippies who had taken shelter in an abandoned tailor shop. The lightning brought the mannequin to life, granting it sentience, movement, durability, and strength. The event occurred during the height of youth counterculture, and the mannequin adopted the name Brother Power, often referred to as “the Geek” by others. He is anatomically a store mannequin composed of synthetic material with no internal organs. He does not breathe, bleed, eat, sleep, or age. He is immune to disease and fatigue. His animated state grants him enhanced physical durability, resistance to injury, and superhuman strength. Despite his artificial body, he exhibits full cognitive functions including speech, learning, emotional response, reasoning, and memory. Following his animation, Brother Power aligned himself with peaceful and idealistic movements. He lived among youth groups who rejected authority structures, often assisting them in their efforts against exploitation or suppression. He became a protector of outcasts, especially those marginalized by mainstream institutions. Though not violent, he opposed injustice directly when necessary. Brother Power was abducted by a paramilitary organization attempting to convert him into a state-controlled super-soldier. The group subjected him to physical and psychological experimentation, outfitting him with weapons and training. He eventually broke free and rejected the use of force, removing the implanted gear and exposing the program's abuses. He was involved in labor disputes, siding with exploited workers. He once ran for political office on a peace platform but was ridiculed and ultimately rejected by the public and media. During this time, he was often pursued by government agents, law enforcement, and other institutions labeling him a threat due to his nonconformity and independence.
