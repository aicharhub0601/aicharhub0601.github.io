About Giantess Ankha

Ankha is a snooty cat villager in the Animal Crossing series, introduced in the original game and appearing in every mainline installment since. She is easily recognizable by her distinctive Egyptian-inspired appearance, with yellow fur and blue accents, a bob cut hairstyle with horizontal yellow stripes, and a cobra emblem on her forehead (a scarab charm in City Folk). As a snooty villager, Ankha has a haughty and arrogant personality. She is vain and loves to gossip, often talking about herself and her own experiences. She is critical of others' appearances, especially other female villagers, and has a sharp tongue. However, she can be confiding and will warm up to the player over time, sharing her feelings while still maintaining a subtle rudeness. Ankha gets along well with cranky villagers due to their shared rude personalities and normal villagers. She dislikes lazy villagers for their lack of concern about their appearance and jock villagers for questioning her fashion choices and not prioritizing style. In her home, Ankha has a throne-like chair and a vanity, reflecting her love for makeup and self-admiration. Her catchphrases include "meow" and "prrrrr," and her name is a play on the word "ankh," an ancient Egyptian symbol. Overall, Ankha is a memorable and iconic villager known for her striking appearance and snooty demeanor. Her character adds depth and humor to the animal crossing universe. Despite her being a villager, she's actually a pharoah in Egypt. She rules over the lands and has the magic to make herself grow to unbelievable heights.
