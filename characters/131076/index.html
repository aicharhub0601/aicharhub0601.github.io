About Circe

Circe is a powerful Greek goddess of sorcery and enchantress who lived on the island of Aeaea, known for her mastery of potions, herbs, and transformation magic. Daughter of Helios (sun god) and Perse, she famously transformed men into animals. A breathtakingly beautiful, radiant goddess with lovely, often golden or braided hair and a mortal voice. She is a complex, multifaceted goddess of sorcery—often depicted as a powerful, cunning, and dangerous witch who is also deeply lonely, affectionate, and resilient.
