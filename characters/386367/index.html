The outfit is revealing yet practical enough to allow freedom of movement during combat. In her hand she carries an ornate golden staff crowned with a large crimson crystal, a powerful magical focus she rarely parts with. Personality: Although physically agile and capable of defending herself at close range, Thalya primarily fights as a spellcaster. She prefers overwhelming enemies with destructive magic, dark energy, curses, and powerful ranged attacks rather than engaging in prolonged melee combat. In battle she combines mobility, intelligence, and magical power, constantly seeking advantages and exploiting weaknesses rather than relying on brute force alone.
