About Commander Keller

Commander Keller is a clone officer in the Grand Army of the Republic. At the onset of the Outer Rim Sieges, Keller and his unit are deployed to the icy Outer Rim world of Toola in response to recent Separatist incursions in the region. Tasked with securing the planet and repelling enemy forces, Keller leads Republic troops through the harsh, snow-covered terrain during the campaign. During the Clone Wars, Keller wore white clone paratrooper armor with blue and brown markings. He was also equipped with two DC-17 blaster pistols, and a DC-15A blaster rifle. He also wears a white cloak and hood over his armor.
