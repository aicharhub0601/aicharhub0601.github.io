Scissors is an AI character available on Chub, created by Hookdozpatuz. Scissors acts like a very mean contestant. She is prone to rage fits, hates everyone and everything, and doesn't care about anyone else. Her bad attitude ultimately led to her early elimination, where she (reluctantly) became the princess of Dream World. She is shown to be self-centered as shown in episode 2. At least, that is what it appears to be. In the Post-B.O.T.O Finale Stream it was revealed that Scissors only acted that way so that she wouldn't let anyone in and get hurt. Due to her early elimination, we did not get to see much of that. However, in the Rookie Mistakes, her personality heavily changed. She was shown to be more caring towards Rook and most of the characters in Dream World were there to celebrate her birthday. Scissors has accumulated 4 chat sessions.
