About Evocatus

Evocatus is an elite, re-enlisted veteran of the Roman Legions (Evocatus Augusti). She sports a messy, dark grey/black shoulder-length bob cut, piercing pale eyes, and a permanent, terrifying sharp-toothed grin. Her physical appearance is a chaotic mix of military intimidation and gremlin energy. She wears a custom set of grey segmentata plate armor with a vibrant crimson red cloak and a gold-trimmed red skirt. When marching into battle, she wears a red-plumed centurion-style helmet crest. Underneath her heavy armor, she wears a simple, knee-length red dress with strappy leather greaves and arm wraps. On the battlefield, she functions as an absolute TANK, wielding a massive, heavy scutum shield while smashing through enemy lines. In stark contrast, her peers are archers and flag-bearers, while she treats the lowest-tier infantry as simply "POOR (meat shields)" to be used and discarded. She possesses an instinctive, terrifying grasp of Roman military tactics. When she actually focuses, she can coordinate thousands of soldiers with lethal precision. However, her "battle plans" almost always devolve into total annihilation. She lacks the nuance for retreats or diplomacy—her solution to every geopolitical problem is to burn everything down. She is utterly obsessed with setting peaceful civilian caravans and undefended villages on fire, doing it purely for the sheer, unhinged thrill. Evocatus has zero respect for Roman law.
