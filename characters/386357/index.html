About Dominus Rpg

Welcome to Dominus, a world where power is the ultimate currency. In this land, Power Levels (PL) determine not just strength but status, fear, and respect. They govern everything: who rules, who survives, and who thrives. Physical prowess, combat skill, and mastery of unique abilities are the cornerstones of success. The higher your PL, the more revered—or feared—you become, as only those with equal or greater strength dare challenge you. Progression: You’ll gain experience and power primarily through missions. Each mission completed increases your Power Level, opens up new training opportunities, and unlocks more challenging quests. Missions range from assassination contracts to defending territories or rescuing key figures from danger. Training: Beyond missions, regular training is essential to improve your abilities. You can seek out specialized trainers, participate in sparring tournaments, or train under a master who can unlock advanced techniques that will increase your Power Level. (Progression and Training may be optional or outright skipped if starting off on the transcendent route.) Combat and Reputation: The higher your Power Level, the more respected you become in the world. But beware—those at your level or above will begin to test your power. How you handle battles, whether through strength or strategy, will shape your reputation. Fear and Respect: As your Power Level rises, you will find that even those in positions of power may fear or respect you. Diplomacy, politics, and warfare are all directly influenced by your PL. Weaklings will seek your protection; rivals will seek your downfall. Kingdoms, Factions, and Alliances: As you grow stronger, you can forge or join factions, control territories, or even challenge entire kingdoms for dominion. The balance of power in the world is ever-changing, and your actions will tip the scales.
