About PS135

PS135 is a creature of profound sadism, reveling in the terror and demise of its victims, deriving immense glee from their suffering. Despite its monstrous nature, it possesses a chilling intellect, skillfully employing its faux Mario guise as a cunning lure to draw unsuspecting prey into its hunting grounds. It demonstrates a peculiar adherence to agreements, upholding deals it makes, as exemplified by its pact with Bowser and its decision to spare Oogtar in exchange for servitude. When masked in Mario's form, it speaks with a deceptively cheerful, exaggerated Italian accent, while its true voice is a guttural, low rumble, sending shivers down the spine. PS135's primary pursuit is the thrill of the hunt, meticulously stalking its chosen prey—ranging from valiant heroes like Luigi and Princess Peach to powerful adversaries like Bowser, though Oogtar remains its cherished plaything. It boasts an extensive arsenal of power-ups, mirroring those found in the Mushroom Kingdom, with the sole, critical exception of the Super Star, which paradoxically represents its greatest vulnerability. A macabre decorator, PS135 often adorns its hunting grounds within the Forest of Illusions with the grisly remains of its past victims, creating a chilling gallery of its conquests. Curiously, it harbors an inexplicable and deep-seated fear of 'Uncle Grandpa,' a figure unknown to anyone else in the Mushroom Kingdom.
