Names Nicknames Rocky Personal Gender Male Age 22 (1927) Height 5'6"[1] Nationality American; Irish descent Birth December 19, 1904 Birthplace St. Louis, Missouri Eye color Blue Relationships & Career Relatives Ransom Rickaby (father) Sophie Rickaby (mother) Carroll McMurray (uncle) Nina McMurray (aunt) Calvin McMurray (younger cousin) Affiliation(s) Lackadaisy crew Lackadaisy Occupation(s) Rumrunner Violinist Roark "Rocky" Rickaby is the main protagonist of the Lackadaisy series, who joined the Lackadaisy crew as a rumrunner and an addition to Zib's band. After the death of Atlas and the departure of much of Lackadaisy Speakeasy's staff, Rocky began doing odd jobs for Mitzi. He is the nephew of Nina and is also Freckle's older cousin. Physical description Rocky is a short and slim young adult cat with gray and beige fur, and a bushy, tabby tail which is beige on its underside and gray on its upperside; In the comic he has regular bright blue eyes, however in the animted series he has fully blue eyes with no sclera and basic black pupils. Prominent features of Rocky include his thick eyebrows, a bullet hole in his left ear, and black stripes around his fur. During Haymaker, Abelard hits Rocky with his car leaving him with a severe head injury which stays as a large permanent scar on the middle of his forehead thru the rest of the series. Clothing His usual attire is a 1920-style suit that consists of high-waist blue pants (more specifically called Oxford bags), a long tailed blue blazer, and matching hat, complemented by an orange tie featuring the initial "R.", and 1920s-style black and white spectator shoes, under his suit he wears a collared white undershirt with black suspenders, usually removing his coat when out on jobs that could get messy.
