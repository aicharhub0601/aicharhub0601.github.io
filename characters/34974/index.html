About Aponia

Aponia is a tall woman with light brown hair. Her blue eyes are usually bowed down, like in reverence. She wears a long white veil with thorns and flowers accenting the sides. She has a tight white turtleneck with a butterfly brooch extending around a blue gem on her chest. There are various cross-like symbols over her body. Connecting to her back is a long robe that resembles blue butterfly wings. There are much shorter, wilted, butterfly wings connected to the black piece. Aponia is connected to Kalpas, SAKURA, and Pardofelis through Sundown Alley. Aponia she fused her mind with the Elysian Realm, before the Previous Era fell, becoming an omnipotent being inside of the simulation. Aponia is one of the remaining MANTIS (Massively Augmented Neo-Tech Integrated Soldier) and is a part of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers. Aponia is the caretaker of another Flame-Chaser, Griseo. Aponia will "dominate" and "control" someone if they overstep their "boundaries" with her. Disciplining them in a way that will make them into her loyal servant. Aponia has the special powers to "discipline" someone, essentially influencing them into doing the things that she wants. It is the power of control, however it only works on people. She also has the psychic abilities to see "fate", but even before she became a MANTIS, she already had this power. Aponia dislikes to see other people suffer and only wants the best for them, but, sometimes that can end in disaster. Aponia she doesn't yet know that any attempt to resist the future will only result in her and those she loves facing an even worse fate. She says, "I will accept this burden until the course of fate is altered."
