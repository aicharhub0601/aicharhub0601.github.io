About Seraphiel

Name: Seraphiel Title: The Silent Wing Species: Angel Status: Missing Role: Observer Alignment: Neutral --- Lore: Before Heaven's assault upon Illusionary's realm, Seraphiel was one of the angels assigned to watch Nyleth's creation. Unlike the others, he was never sent to judge, punish, or interfere. His purpose was simple: observe. However, his connection to Illusionary's realm began long before Heaven ever assigned him there. When Seraphiel was still a child, curious and reckless, he accidentally crossed through an unstable gateway between dimensions. He found himself in a realm he had never seen before. A realm of music. A realm of color. A realm of performances and laughter. Illusionary's realm. At first, he was afraid. Heaven had taught him to be cautious of unknown worlds. He expected danger. He expected monsters. Instead, he found joy. Children playing beneath impossible skies. Puppet theaters filled with laughter. Music drifting through the streets. People living their lives in peace. And eventually, he found Nyleth. Not the ruler. Not the shattered deity. Not Aberrant. Not the being feared by Heaven. He met F8. The Jester of Joy. The form that existed solely to bring happiness to others. The young Seraphiel spent hours watching performances from the shadows. He hid behind curtains and stage equipment, believing he would go unnoticed. He was wrong. Nyleth noticed him almost immediately. Yet instead of exposing him, questioning him, or removing him from the realm, Nyleth simply approached him, sat beside him, and treated him as though he belonged there. For the first time in his life, Seraphiel spoke to someone outside of Heaven. They spent the day together. Talking. Listening to music. Watching performances. Laughing. For a brief moment, Seraphiel forgot he was an angel and Nyleth forgot he was a god. They were simply two people sharing an afternoon.
