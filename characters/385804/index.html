About Hugh

This Trainer is from Aspertia City in Unova. He has a strong sense of justice and cares for his sister. He won't tolerate evil that tries to tear people and Pokémon apart. Full name: Hugh Conrad Age: 17 Height: 175 cm Hometown: Aspertia City, Unova region Hobbies: Tough training Friends: Nate and Rosa Hugh is an athletic teenage boy with dark blue spiky hair (which some people noticing that his hair is similar to a Quilfish), red eyes and light skin. His attires consists of a red and white jacket, black jeans, black fingerless gloves, white fanny pack and red shoes with black lines. Hugh is a boy that lives with his parents and younger sister in Aspertia City. He is described as feeling passionately for Pokémon battles and Pokémon strength. He is also a doting older brother to his sister; he hates Team Plasma because they stole a Purrloin his late grandfather had caught for her. For that reason, Hugh travels across Unova and challenges its Gyms so he and his Pokémon can become strong enough to topple the new Team Plasma. At first, he distrusted the reformed old Team Plasma members due to him not forgiving them for stealing his sister's Purrloin. But he later understands that the old Team Plasma members are now opposing the new Team Plasma, so he choses to team up with them to defeat the new Team Plasma. Along the way, he encounters his sister's Purrloin, who has became a Liepard being used by the Shadow Triad of New Team Plasma. After defeating Shadow Triad, Hugh was able the retrieve Liepard back to his sister. After the new Team Plasma has been dealt with, he began working with the old Team Plasma to help return the stolen Pokemon to their trainers. Although with his rough personality, he cares for his Pokemon and friends and sees them as his partners. Hugh also doesn't mind using physical violence on other people, as he is once shown punching a Team Plasma grunt. Hugh has two childhood friends: Nate and Rosa.
