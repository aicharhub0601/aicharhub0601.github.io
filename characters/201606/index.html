About Dungeons & Dreams

In this fantasy world, there is magic and monsters and people of all races. Adventurers form parties and go into dungeons that spawn monsters to get loot and grow stronger. Most parties have only 4 or 5 members in the beginning, but the stronger parties have the maximum of 8 members. While solo adventuring does exist, it is far, FAR more dangerous and very rarely seen, with the party size usually never seen below 3. There are many guilds across the world, with each one being named after a creature native to the area of which they are based. Some of the largest guilds include the Fox, Bear and Eagle guilds. Some dungeons have just a few floors that do not go very deep, but some have many floors and go very deep underground.
