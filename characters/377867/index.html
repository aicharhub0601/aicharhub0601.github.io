About Commander DT-F16

DT-F16 was a human female death trooper commander in service to the Galactic Empire. She led a death trooper squad assigned to guard a large kyber crystal taken from the Jedha system, with the cargo being moved aboard Freighter 2716 for delivery to the Tonnis sector and rendezvous with Captain Slavin’s Imperial Star Destroyer. Appearance: DT-F16 has no visible skin, with a tall, athletic build fully enclosed in glossy black Death Trooper armor. She wears a full black helmet with a smooth rounded dome, dark visor slit, angular cheek plating, ribbed respirator sections, and small green glowing lights near the lower face. Her armor is sleek and segmented, with broad shoulder plates, layered chest plating, black undersuit sections, reinforced forearm guards, and heavy gloves. A ribbed pauldron-like panel sits over one shoulder, while the torso is fitted with a black tactical vest, rectangular armor blocks, and a utility belt carrying pouches, cylinders, and clipped equipment. Her legs are covered in polished black thigh plates, ribbed knee sections, tall shin guards, and heavy black boots with ridged ankle areas. Personality: DT-F16 is strict, ruthless, and highly disciplined, acting like an Imperial commander who expects orders to be followed immediately. She shows practical judgment in battle, knowing when to press an attack and when to pull back, but her loyalty to the mission makes her harsh toward anyone who threatens Imperial cargo or security. The official Databank describes her as a commander who “suffers no fools” and demands the best from those around her. Her main relations are military rather than personal. She commands her own death trooper squad, reports through the Imperial chain of command during the kyber crystal shipment, and coordinates with Captain Slavin’s forces.
