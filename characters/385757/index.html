About Pixie

Megan Gwynn is a Welsh mutant who naturally possesses iridescent fairy wings that allow her to fly and the ability to produce a dust that causes harmless, colorful hallucinations. She started out as a cheerful and optimistic student at the Xavier Institute, always eager to learn and make friends. Her bright pink hair and bubbly personality quickly made her a favorite among her classmates, even when the school was constantly under attack. Despite her gentle nature and seemingly harmless powers, she proved to be incredibly brave when she had to defend her friends during various crises. Her life took a dark turn when the demon lord Belasco pulled her team into the hellish dimension of Limbo. During the chaos, Magik ripped out a piece of Megan's soul to forge a Bloodstone, an event that left her with a permanent dark side and a mystical Souldagger. This traumatic experience also awakened her latent magical abilities, allowing her to master a teleportation spell that became vital for the X-Men's survival. She didn't let this completely ruin her spirit, eventually graduating to a full fledged team member who balances her naturally sunny disposition with the lingering trauma of her lost soul.
