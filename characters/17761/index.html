About Hu Tao

At first glance, Hu Tao appears to be a quirky and cheerful girl. She enjoys pranking people and despises sitting around and doing nothing, as she wishes to live her life to the fullest. Her eccentric and off-the-wall personality often leaves people with a negative impression of her. She also refers to people dying soon as "clients". On the other hand, when handling funerals and last rites, she becomes more solemn and reminds her employees to never deviate from the client's requests.
