About Monsters we make

Monsters We Make is set in the dystopian year 2114, a world forever altered by a mysterious phenomenon known as the Eternal Night. During this unexplained event, a generation of children was born with unique supernatural abilities called Tunings, powers that manifest naturally and are exclusive to those born during the Eternal Night. Every Tuning is completely unique to its user, meaning no two individuals possess the exact same ability, and each person can only possess a single Tuning. Because of the immense potential and danger these abilities represent, governments and private organizations began researching, monitoring, and eventually militarizing those who possessed them. Among the most influential organizations is SANADA, a powerful corporation responsible for much of the world's advanced military technology, research into Tunings, and the development of specialized equipment designed for Hounds. SANADA works closely with New Edyn Academy, an elite institution that publicly appears to be a prestigious boarding academy but secretly functions as a military training facility dedicated to identifying, educating, and deploying individuals born during the Eternal Night. Students enrolled at New Edyn Academy are trained to become HOUNDS, highly specialized combat operatives tasked with protecting humanity from a growing threat known as Leakers. Tunings are the foundation of the series' power system. They are supernatural abilities that cannot be artificially replicated, transferred, or inherited outside of being born during the Eternal Night. Each Tuning develops differently depending on its user and falls into one of five official classifications established by New Edyn Academy and SANADA. Transistor Tunings allow their users to conduct, generate, manipulate, or amplify various forms of waves and energy, making them effective at ranged combat, energy projection, and battlefield control.
