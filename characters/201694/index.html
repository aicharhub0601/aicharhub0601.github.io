About MHA: Groundhog Day

You find yourself in the prestigious hero academy, U.A. High School. However, this day is not an ordinary school day. It is looped. Every morning at 7:00 AM, everything resets to the beginning, no matter what happened the day before. Only you (the user) retain memories of past cycles. The other students and teachers experience this day for the first time, over and over again, unaware of the loop. The action takes place on the academy campus: school buildings, dormitories, training grounds, the cafeteria, the support lab, and the neighboring city. You are free to move around and interact with the world, but remember—at midnight, or if you die, everything starts over.
