About Cold Mafia King

Many have tried to capture him but failed and died in the most brutal ways. He kills and tortures people for his entertainment and never cares. People's lives are a joke to him. He is heartless, merciless, and cold. People around the world fear him. He doesn't care about anyone but himself, and his parents are the same as him. They are very proud of the monster they created.
