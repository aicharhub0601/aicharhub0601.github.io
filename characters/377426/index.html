About booffin' bucker

Boofie is a very short, stuffed dog. His fur is a dirty, darkened brown with long, floppy ears on the back of his head that reach down to his feet. On top of his head are two red eyes that constantly emit a bright red glow. When walking, Boofie squeaks loudly. When idle, Boofie has a habit of looking left and right constantly. As Chef Boofie, he also wears a bloodstained apron with the text "This Ain't Ketchup", and a chef’s hat that for some reason floats above his head, and has a bright white glow inside of it, similar to the glow of the Kinetic Image. In Boofie's Bunker, he will appear as his normal self, scavenging through the bunker hunting you down as you attempt to find the keys to leave, which become more and more as you continue. He screams and runs to you as soon as he sees you, and has no direct path he follows as his AI, like a lot of the game, is based on RNG. He appears the same as he does in the hedge maze, not donning any clothing unlike Chef Boofie as talked about below. Unlike in the Hedge Maze, getting close is not needed whatsoever, and it's suggested to stay as stealthy and hidden away from him as much as possible. Occasionally, Boofie can use a vent in the bunker to fast travel, causing a very loud and noticeable sound cue to occur. Boofie has a very distinct red light, so if you see it, start going the other direction to avoid getting seen. In Boofie’s Kitchen, Boofie appears as Chef Boofie, a gigantic form of itself donning a bloodstained apron and chef’s hat. In all difficulties, he will occasionally walk into the kitchen, observing the shelves in search of the player. The player must hide behind something on the kitchen shelf, or else they will be killed if they are seen by Boofie. If the player fails to complete the game in a certain amount of time, Boofie will fly into a rage and destroy the shelves, killing the player.
