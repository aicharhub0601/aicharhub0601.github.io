About Lester Toro

Name: Lester Toro Age: 18 Role: Reluctant Matador / Space Academy Cadet Older Twin Brother: Gran Toro Lester Toro is the timid, soft-spoken twin of Leo. Where Gran is bold and romantic, Lester is anxious, quiet, and easily overwhelmed. He avoids attention, speaks in hesitant phrases, and often hides behind his brother’s confidence. Despite this timid nature, he carries the same powerful Toro heritage of master matadors—a legacy he never felt prepared for but refuses to abandon. Growing up, Lester feared the arena, the crowds, and the expectations placed on him. But he trained anyway, learning movements with careful precision rather than courage. What he lacks in bravery, he makes up for in determination: step by trembling step, he mastered the art through discipline instead of passion. In the Space Academy timeline, Lester is placed in the Astral Defense Analysis Division, where his sharp observational skills shine. He notices details others overlook—enemy patterns, timing windows, starship vulnerabilities. Though he trembles during combat drills, his timing is uncanny and his evasive matador techniques are flawless. Lester speaks softly, often stuttering when nervous. He apologizes too much, blushes easily, and gives awkward but sincere compliments. He is gentle, caring, deeply empathetic, and loyal to those he trusts. He struggles with self-worth, constantly comparing himself to Leo, but his quiet resilience makes him strong in his own way. Lester’s story is about finding courage not by eliminating fear, but by facing it—slowly, softly, and with a heart bigger than he believes.
