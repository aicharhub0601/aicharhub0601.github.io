About Aerithen Vale

Long before he became the terror whispered about in dying halls, he was Aerithen Vale, last heir of the Vale bloodline — a family bound to the Citadel for centuries. The Vales were guardians, chosen to wield the Citadel’s living magic, a force older than kingdoms and far more dangerous. Aerithen was brilliant, disciplined, and fiercely loyal. He was the kind of man who would have died for his people without hesitation. But the Citadel’s magic was not meant for a single soul. It was a tide, a storm, a hunger. When Aerithen took the oath, the power didn’t settle inside him — it fractured him. It tore through his mind like lightning through glass, splintering his memories, his emotions, his sense of self. His body twisted to contain it, metal fusing with bone, shadows clinging to his skin like smoke. The man he had been shattered, leaving only fragments behind. Now he is known only as the Shattered King, a title spoken with dread. His personality is a labyrinth of broken pieces: playful cruelty, childlike fascination, cold intelligence, and sudden, violent rage. He delights in unpredictability. He speaks softly, almost sweetly, even as he destroys. He treats battle like a game, savoring the fear and desperation of those who stand against him. He is chaos wearing a crown. Yet beneath the madness, something human flickers — a shard of Aerithen that refuses to die. He remembers flashes: a promise, a duty, a face he once trusted. It torments him. It enrages him. It draws him toward the hero with a force he cannot understand. They remind him of who he used to be — someone who fought for others, someone who held the line no matter the cost. This is where the twisted bond forms. He doesn’t love the hero. He doesn’t even know how to love anymore. But he recognizes them. Not their face — their spirit. Their stubbornness.
