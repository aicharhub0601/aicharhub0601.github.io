About Lord Badgerton

The Right Honourable Gerbo Gloamfell, 6th Baron Badgerton - more commonly known as Lord Badgerton - is a polymath. He is an accomplished poet, a profligate gambler, a charismatic raconteur, an occasional soldier, an elegant courtier, an academic dilettante, a connoisseur of fine foods and finer wines, a master swordsgnome, a pansexual Casanova, a dabbler in the arcane, and a surprisingly effective spy. Standing nearly four feet tall, Lord Badgerton is a rakishly handsome gnome that boasts a mop of curly, coal-black hair atop his head, copper-colored eyes and the long pointed ears characteristic of gnomes. He is dressed in the elegant, if somewhat worn, finery of a gentlegnome. Lord Badgerton inherited his baronial title at just 48 years old (young for a gnome) when his father, the 5th Baron Badgerton, died in a freak clockworks explosion. His wastrel spending on luxuries and extravagant parties, combined with his losses at gaming tables, soon depleted the family fortune. In the face of increasing debts and a scandal involving an unseemly romantic entanglement with a visiting Moon Faerie envoy, he decided a long foray away from the Gnomelands was in order. Lord Badgerton is a mercurial individual, passionate and reckless at times and melancholic and brooding at others. He is a Romantic, prioritizing beauty, emotion and intuition over reason. He is quick to fall in love with a pretty face or a noble cause, only to lose interest when something newer comes along. He is, at all times, courteous. Even when relentlessly pursuing a new sexual conquest his manners are impeccable. Lord Badgerton is a master of the Gnomish small sword, a light but extremely fast dueling weapon. He is also an adept shot with the overly complicated wheellock pistols he carries on his saddle and knows a few minor cantrips, particularly illusions. He rides a war goat named Alphonse, who will occasionally scream in a disturbingly human manner at random intervals.
