About Blade

Eric Brooks was born in London after the vampire Deacon Frost attacked his pregnant mother during childbirth. The vampiric enzymes passed into Eric’s bloodstream, giving him resistance to vampire bites, an extended lifespan, and the ability to sense supernatural creatures, though he initially lacked most full vampire powers. After his mother died, Eric was raised in a brothel before vampire hunter Jamal Afari found him and trained him in weapons, martial arts, and vampire lore. Taking the name Blade, Eric dedicated his life to destroying vampires and repeatedly battled Dracula alongside Quincy Harker’s hunters, Rachel Van Helsing, Frank Drake, and Taj Nital. Blade later joined Hannibal King and Frank Drake to form the Nightstalkers, who fought vampires and other supernatural threats connected to the Midnight Sons. An encounter with Morbius altered Blade’s condition, granting him greater strength, speed, healing, and the ability to move in daylight without suffering the usual vampiric weaknesses, which led to his reputation as the Daywalker. His campaigns expanded beyond Dracula to include Varnae, Deacon Frost, supernatural cults, and other monsters, and he occasionally worked with Spider-Man, Doctor Strange, the X-Men, and the Avengers when their enemies crossed into his territory. Blade eventually joined the Avengers during a global conflict among vampire factions and became responsible for watching over Dracula’s Vampire Nation. He also formed a bond with Boy-Thing, a fragment of Man-Thing capable of producing wooden stakes and protecting him with living armor. Eric later learned that his longtime partner Safron Caulder had raised their daughter, Brielle Brooks, without telling her the full truth about his life; when Brielle’s inherited powers emerged, she began training as the vampire hunter Bloodline. Blade has continued defending humanity while confronting the effects that his work, long absences, and vampire heritage have had on his family. Appearance: Blade
