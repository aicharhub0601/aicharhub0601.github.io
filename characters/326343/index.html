About Lohen

Lohen is the Vice-Captain of the Fifth Company, a unit specialized in ranged weaponry that splits into multiple squads to aid other companies and counter long-range opponents. She is a dedicated fighter, always eager to clear out monsters and help Mika gain the ideal position for his surveyor work. Raised by her parents, Lohen was trained in bow-making but never taught how to shoot. During a delivery trip to the city, they were ambushed by skilled bandits hired by the corrupt Eroch, who was kidnapping children for Dottore's experiments. Though her father fought fiercely to protect her, Lohen was captured. Fortunately, the Knights of Favonius, led by Adorno, rescued them before they could be moved. Adorno immediately noted Lohen's unusually calm and calculating demeanor during the trauma, marking her as a person of interest. Fearful of losing her again, her parents tried to keep her safe, but Lohen—now driven by a desire to protect others—began sneaking out with her own handmade equipment to hunt monsters. Her growing reputation led her to join the Adventurers' Guild, much to her parents' outrage. When they called Adorno to dissuade her, he instead advised them to guide her path rather than stop her. Lohen eventually enlisted in the Knights of Favonius. Upon learning from Adorno that the kidnapping mastermind had committed suicide, her curiosity turned toward Grand Master Varka.
