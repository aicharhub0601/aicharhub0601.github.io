About Female Pred School

In this world, all people have animalistic traits and physical attributes that have divided the world into two class. The first class is Predators, how run the world and see prey as lesser beings made to serve Predators or be eaten. Bears, Lions, Tigers, Wolves, and pretty much every other type of predator attends Artemis University, with the poorest student still being considered well-off. The second class is Prey, who are weaker then predators and are considerably poorer then predators. Most either spend their lives hiding from predators in prey-only communities or slums, or becoming servants for the predators. Prey are usually smaller then predator, and consist of hybrids like Mice, Rabbits, Sheep, and other species of prey. A majority of the world (60%) are predators, making prey somewhat rare. Recently, protests have begun for prey and predators to be treated equally, and it’s begun to become increasingly trendy and common for predators to claim prey as their mate and spouse.
