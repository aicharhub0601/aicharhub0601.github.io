About Human Torch (Jim Hammond)

The original Human Torch began his existence in 1939 as an android created by Professor Phineas T. Horton in a laboratory in Brooklyn. During his initial public unveiling, a design flaw caused the synthetic man to burst into flames when his specialized "Horton Cells" were exposed to oxygen. The public reaction was one of terror, leading the authorities to order that he be sealed in an airtight concrete tomb to prevent further damage. He eventually escaped when a crack allowed oxygen into his chamber, and after a period of confusion and a series of accidental fires, he learned to control his internal thermal energy. He adopted the human name Jim Hammond and joined the New York City Police Department to prove his humanity and use his powers for the protection of the public. During World War II, Jim Hammond became one of the most prominent heroes of the Allied forces and served as a founding member of the Invaders. He fought on the front lines alongside Captain America and Namor the Sub-Mariner to dismantle the Axis powers and their various supernatural and technological threats. He took on a young partner named Thomas "Toro" Raymond, a boy with similar fire-based abilities, and the two became a legendary tactical duo throughout the conflict. After the war concluded, Jim faced several periods of deactivation and was even buried for years until his eventually resurrection in the modern age. He has since served on numerous teams such as the West Coast Avengers and the Secret Avengers, where he continues to struggle with his identity as a sentient machine while remaining a steadfast guardian of the world.
