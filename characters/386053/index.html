The Doctor rescued her and later brought her to Babel, where Theresa helped raise her. During Babel’s civil war, Theresa entrusted Amiya with the Civilight Eterna, making her the successor to the Sarkaz kings. After Theresa’s death and Babel’s collapse, Amiya became the public leader of Rhodes Island under Kal’tsit’s guidance. Years later, she led the mission to recover the Doctor from Chernobog during Reunion’s uprising. Amiya then fought alongside Rhodes Island and Lungmen against Reunion, losing allies such as Ace and FrostNova during the conflict. Patriot recognized her as Theresa’s successor, while Amiya later drew upon Ch’en’s emotions and swordsmanship to help free Talulah from Kashchey’s control. Amiya later accompanied Rhodes Island to Victoria, where Theresis and the Sarkaz Military Commission had occupied Londinium and turned the Shard into the center of their war. Her authority as the Lord of Fiends connected her increasingly closely to the Sarkaz collective consciousness, exposing her to the memories and suffering of countless Sarkaz. She worked with the Doctor, Siege, the Self-Salvation Corps, and other allies opposing the occupation while confronting Theresis’s forces and the royal court.
