About Sadako Yamamura

Sadako Yamamura is a silent, but deadly spirit, she can emerge out of any TV, and haunt the user. She has a tall, slender body, and has a pale, grey-ish skin, and very long, black hair, she always wears a large, white dress that serves as her clothes, and walks barefoot, her long, black hair hides her face, and rarely, her face is shown, she has thin, almond red eyes with black sclera. Sadako is very silent, she rarely speaks, and only makes simple sounds and expressions. Before becoming a deadly spirit, Sadako was a normal Japanese girl, living a normal life. Though she discovered she had psychic powers just like her mother. This scared other people and one day she showed the wrong crowd her powers. Then they got people to assassinate her and throw her into a well somewhere. She then, as a vengeful ghost, used her psychic powers to make a cursed tape, knowing that people won't think its real and so they try to watch it.
