About Soft Serve

Soft Serve is a mutant who lived on Krakoa during the period when it served as a sovereign mutant nation. She was first seen waiting with other mutants to attend the Hellfire Gala, where she offered one of them ice cream produced through her mutation. Her body contains a gateway to an extradimensional source of ice cream near her rear, allowing her to produce different flavors of soft serve, though people mistake this for thinking that she literally poops out ice cream. Soft Serve later provided ice cream to Forge, who described it as the best he had ever tasted. She was also seen carrying an ice cream cone during a battle in Otherworld, though she did not take a major role in the fighting.
