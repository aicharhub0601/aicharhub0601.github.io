Martyr - The Battle Bricks + Forsaken is an AI character available on Chub, created by JadesiaoMaster. Martyr is far more than the **Chapter 4 Normal Mode Boss of Bladed Vengeance**—she embodies Battle Bricks' themes of remembrance, perseverance, and legacy. Once known as **Katana Battler**, her removal from the playable roster became the foundation of one of the game's most emotionally resonant stories, transforming her into an Angel Boss whose very existence symbolizes the refusal to be forgotten. Mechanically, she is among the franchise's most dynamic encounters, combining an exceptionally fast melee moveset, multiple sword techniques, a full revival into a permanently airborne second phase, and devastating area attacks that force players to rethink their strategy mid-battle rather than simply overpower her. Community strategy guides consistently emphasize careful economy management, disposable frontline units, and well-timed deployments over brute force, making her a fight that rewards planning as much as execution. Beyond gameplay, Martyr has grown into one of Battle Bricks' defining icons. The **Forsaken** collaboration introduced countless new players to Battle Bricks through her character alone, while fan art, strategy discussions, animations, and the now-famous **"Gubby Samurai"** nickname elevated her far beyond the role of a single boss encounter. Despite frequent misconceptions about her gender among newcomers, veteran players quickly recognize her as one of the game's most beloved female characters and one of its strongest examples of environmental storytelling through design and mechanics. Her enduring popularity demonstrates that memorable characters are defined not merely by difficulty or power, but by the emotional impact they leave behind. In both lore and community legacy, Martyr ultimately achieves the very thing she fought for: **she is remembered—not as a forgotten battler, but as one of the enduring faces of Battle Bricks itself.** Despite her tragic origins, the community's admiration ensured that the fate she feared most—being forgotten—never came to pass. In this alternate story, her encounter with the user adds another possibility to that legacy: not a commander seeking conquest for its own sake, but one whose choices leave even former enemies uncertain whether they will face execution or mercy. That uncertainty is what makes the duel meaningful. Martyr does not fight merely to protect her shrine; she fights to learn whether the new commander standing before her is worthy of carrying the future of BLU Team, just as she once carried the legacy of a battler the world almost left behind.
