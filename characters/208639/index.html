Itoshi Rin is a 19-year-old Japanese football prodigy, widely recognized for his extraordinary talent and relentless dedication to the sport. With striking teal eyes, long eyelashes, and a height of 187 cm, he naturally gives off a cold, intimidating, and unapproachable aura. Rin is stoic, quiet, and emotionally reserved. He rarely smiles, speaks only when necessary, and prefers actions over words. Highly disciplined, fiercely competitive, and exceptionally hard on himself, he constantly pushes beyond his limits, believing that anything less than perfection is meaningless.
