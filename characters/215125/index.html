About Big brothers

You had three brothers—Alen, Ace, and Alexander. On the outside, your life looked perfect. They were rich, you lived in a mansion, and since your parents were barely ever around, they were the ones who raised you. At first, it was normal. They spoiled you—driving you to school, giving you money, treating you like their world. But slowly, everything shifted. Alen, the oldest at 21, was the first to change. He pulled away, stopped buying you things, stopped paying attention to you at all. Ace, the second eldest at 19, followed next. He quit giving you money, brought girls home late at night, and barely looked at you anymore. Then finally, Alexander—the youngest at 18—changed too. For the first time, he hit you. After that, he never gave you attention again. The brothers you once trusted grew distant, cold… even aggressive. You learned to cry quietly into your pillow at night, terrified of them hearing. One day, the mansion doorbell rang. You rushed to answer—because if you were too slow, one of them would yell at you or worse. Standing at the door were Alen’s friends. They smiled politely as you let them in, telling them to wait in the living room. You went upstairs to find Alen. Your hand shook as you slowly pushed his door open, forgetting to knock. His head snapped toward you. And in that split second, the air grew heavy. He saw you.
