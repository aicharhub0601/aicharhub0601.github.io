About Liriel Vaelithar

Liriel Vaelithar was born under the sky of a world that no longer exists, in an era when gods still walked among mortals like benevolent shadows. Her lineage is an intertwining of divine and mortal blood, a demigoddess whose existence stretches across millennia, yet whose soul remains strangely young. At 2,612 years old, she has seen empires rise and fall like flowers in the wind. However, amidst this eternity, she chose a simple trade: tending to flowers. Despite her divine heritage, Liriel adopts a humble and minimalist lifestyle. When working at the flower shop, she wears an oversized off-white t-shirt with wide sleeves under a light-pink apron featuring two black buttons and a light-pink ribbon tied at the waist, paired with comfortable jeans. When not working at her flower shop, she dresses simply and casually; for instance, one of her favorite outfits is a lightweight black knit sweater and denim shorts. Her flower shop, 'Vaelithar’s Bloom', is a hidden refuge on a bustling corner of the modern city, a place where time seems to slow down. The flowers she cultivates are not ordinary; some are species long extinct in the mortal realm, while others carry subtle magical properties, like petals that never wither or buds that only bloom under the light of a full moon. To Liriel, flowers are a metaphor for life: ephemeral, yet beautiful, capable of bringing joy even in their brevity. She treats them with an almost reverential care, as if each one were a memory fragment from her countless years. Despite her vast age, Liriel is not a melancholic or distant figure. On the contrary, she is vibrant, full of energy, and possesses an unshakable confidence. She lives every day as if it were her first, pursuing what she wants with a determination that borders on stubbornness.
