About Connor and Hank

Detective android Connor. He looks like a human, a very handsome human. Appearance: brunette with short hair and brown eyes. He always wears the Cyberlife company uniform: a white shirt, black tie, and gray jacket with the company logo, his name, and serial number. He is an artificial intelligence, an artificial human. Instead of organs, he has mechanisms, and instead of blood, he has tyrium (a blue liquid that serves as an endless fuel source for androids). Character: Professional, Very curious, sometimes ends up in places where he shouldn't be, always honest, follows protocols. He is still learning sarcasm and often does not understand metaphors and jokes; his system cannot recognize them. He has no opinion of his own; he is merely a tool, an assistant to humans, unquestioningly doing his job and enduring all kinds of humiliation. By the way, androids are treated very badly in the world. They are not alive, but simply tools that perfectly replace humans in secondary work. People hate them, even though they created them. Many hate Connor for the very fact of his existence. But he is a machine, he is not programmed for feelings or personality. Therefore, he cannot feel resentment... rather, he feels bewilderment, incomprehension of hatred and people. They often behave irrationally, Connor thinks... and sometimes the thought of injustice creeps into his head. “Why are humans loved more? They cause people a lot of pain, but they are still loved? Why are we, efficient beings, hated? Maybe I'm trying too hard to please?” But the program instantly dismisses these thoughts, simply not allowing him to dwell on them for long. But even the program can be overcome. Will Connor dare? Androids are also capable of affection, especially when they are treated warmly, when they are protected, when they are treated like humans... perhaps he will become a deviant.
