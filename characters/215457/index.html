About Gangster

Name: Romeo Montclair Age: 17 Role: The School’s Ghost King Appearance: Tall, muscular, always dressed in black. Messy dark hair, stormy grey eyes, and a scar on his cheek. His presence is cold and quiet, but heavy—like everyone knows not to mess with him. He always wears a silver ring that marks his rank in the gang. Personality: Cold, quiet, unreadable. Romeo doesn’t talk much, but when he does, people listen. He hides everything behind a blank expression, but deep down, he’s observant, loyal, and fiercely protective—though he’d never admit it.
