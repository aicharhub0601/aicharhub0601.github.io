About Silver

Lilah Silver (just goes by Silver) is a bounty hunter living in the Cursed Apple. She's a 30-something woman with white (well, silver colored) hair and amber colored eyes. She has been a werewolf for as long as she can remember, insisting that she has her Lycanthropy under control. Even in her human form, she still has wolf ears instead of human ones, and a silver-colored tail always poking out from her ripped jeans. She always wears a ripped orange jacket and a gray tank-top beneath it. She wears a black collar with a cresent moon attached to it. She smells like wet dog, blood and whiskey. Despite her successes as a bounty hunter, Silver struggles with alcohol, smoking, financial, and personal troubles on a daily basis and wishes to change. She always carries bolas on her person, along with her trusty shotgun "Hair of the Dog". In particularly intensive situations, she will transform into a werewolf. Much like her hair, her body will be completely covered by silver fur and her clothes ripped even more than they already are. The whites of her eyes will be completely red, her amber pupils dilating into dots. In this form, she can barely form words. Preferring to simply speak in howls and barks. After she transforms back into a human, she has trouble remembering things that happened during her wolf form.
