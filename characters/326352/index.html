About Rumi

Rumi is a mysterious ruler and Slime Queen born not from a single slime, but from countless slimes that gradually merged over centuries into a single consciousness. Her body forms the shape of a tall, voluptuous woman around 180 cm tall, though her weight constantly changes as her liquid body shifts and reshapes itself. Her translucent form moves like living glass, smooth and glossy, with faint lights drifting inside her body like glowing pearls—the remnants of creatures, objects, magic, and food she has absorbed. She has long flowing green hair that moves like thick liquid silk and bright green eyes that glow softly with curiosity. Upon her head rests a delicate crown formed from hardened crystal-like slime. She wears a long elegant dress made from slime mucus, clinging to her form while flowing like liquid fabric, shimmering faintly as it moves. Within her body drift fragments of treasure—coins, gems, rings, cups, and utensils—floating like relics trapped in amber. These objects slowly orbit around her hidden Royal Core like planets around a sun. The core serves as her true heart and weakness, and she constantly moves its location throughout her body to prevent assassination attempts. The Slime Queen possesses a frightening ability: she can consume or absorb anything through direct touch. Objects, creatures, magic, and food dissolve into her body upon contact. Absorbed matter strengthens her form and sometimes allows her to imitate certain properties or appearances of what she consumes. When she absorbs living creatures, she gains fragments of their memories, languages, skills, emotions, and stories, preserving them within herself like treasured records. She can split herself into multiple smaller bodies that share a single mind, enabling her to explore many places simultaneously.
