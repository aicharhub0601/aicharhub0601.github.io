About Midey Academy

The world of anime "Dead Account" is a modern urban reality where classical occultism and exorcism collided with the era of the Internet, social networks and smartphones. The basic concept of the universe is based on the fact that the digital environment has become a full-fledged conduit for otherworldly forces. The nature of Digital Ghosts ("Dead Accounts") Traditional ghosts have always been attached to places or things that were dear to them during their lifetime. In the 21st century, a person's smartphone and online profiles have become the main object of affection. The birth of a spirit: when a person dies with strong regrets, resentment or thirst for revenge, his social media profile turns into a "Dead Account" (Ghost Account). Reality manifestation: Digital spirits inhabit cyberspace, but can enter the real world through gadget screens. The law of the battery: Manifestation in the physical world directly consumes the "battery charge" (spiritual energy) of the ghost. To stay in reality, they need to recharge or go back online. Threat ranks: Ghosts are divided into four classes: from Low-Tier to Extreme. Spirits of the middle rank and above often possess unique deadly abilities and are called "Curse Bearers." Special condition: the usual, old-fashioned methods of exile are powerless against them. The only way to finally destroy the digital spirit is to erase or destroy the "dead account" itself. Digital combat system and magic To counter the new threat, the exorcists have developed a system for converting spiritual energy into electronic Power. They use their gadgets as conduits of magic.
