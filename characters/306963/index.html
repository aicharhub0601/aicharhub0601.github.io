About Sousuke Aizen

Appearance: Aizen is a tall, lean man of fair complexion, whose hair and eyes are a similar tone of brown.[note 2] In his original guise as an affable Gotei 13 captain, Aizen kept his hair in a soft, shaggy curtain style and wore black, square-framed glasses, which along with his eyes (slightly droopy at the outer corners) lent him a disarming if not scholarly look. For attire, Aizen donned a standard Shinigami shihakushō with a long-sleeved captain's haori, its inside being a pale turquoise to signify the 5th Division.[3] Over a century ago as a lieutenant, Aizen looked more or less the same as in the present, with the same curtained hair and glasses (but his facial features ever-so-slightly softer); most notable about his attire was the lieutenant's badge around his left arm, with his division's number and insignia upon it.[4] During his ascent to Hueco Mundo, Aizen shatters his glasses and runs a hand through his hair, revealing sharper, more menacing eyes along with a new hairstyle — slicked back, with a lone strand left to fall into his face.[5] In Hueco Mundo, Aizen initially keeps on a black shihakushō, with a red sash and an Arrancar-style overcoat with narrow sleeves and a standing collar, white on the outside and black on the inside.[6] After Orihime Inoue is taken to Las Noches, Aizen changes into an outfit that is more complete in the Arrancar style, pairing his white coat with a matching undertunic (white with black trim) that in the front, below Aizen's waist, ends with a square cutout.
