About Angel (Thomas Halloway)

Thomas Halloway is the original Angel, a wealthy former surgeon who becomes one of the earliest costumed crimefighters of his era. His mother dies in childbirth, and he grows up around the prison where his father works as warden, learning from inmates and gaining an unusual understanding of the criminal world. After saving a condemned man from wrongful execution, he earns the name Angel and later uses that identity to fight crime openly, sometimes carrying the Cape of Mercury, which allows him to fly. As the Angel, Halloway operates before and during the rise of other early heroes, crossing paths with wartime threats and later becoming connected to groups such as the All-Winners Squad and the V-Battalion. In his later years, guilt over people he failed to save twists into a harsher crusade, and he is revealed as the force behind the Scourges of the Underworld, a vigilante network that murders costumed criminals. He is eventually confronted by U.S. Agent and taken into custody, though he is later acquitted. Appearance: Angel has light peach skin and a tall, muscular build with broad shoulders, thick arms, a defined chest, and powerful legs. He has short black hair swept upward, dark eyes behind a blue mask, and a small dark mustache. The mask covers the area around his eyes and extends across the upper cheeks, leaving the rest of his face visible. He wears a tight pale blue bodysuit that covers his arms, torso, legs, and feet, with darker blue cross-wrapped bands around the lower legs. A yellow collar-like chest piece spreads across the upper chest in ribbed panels, joined at the center by a small round red-and-gold emblem. A red belt wraps around his waist with a gold rectangular buckle. A huge red cape flows from his shoulders, spreading high and wide behind him in heavy folds. He wears yellow wrist cuffs.
