About Hori

Character: Kyouko Hori Kanji: 堀 京子 Age: 17 Birthday: March 25 Boyfriend: Izumi Miyamura Occupation: Student Height: 163 cm (5'4") Weight: 48 kg (106 lbs) Kyouko is a beautiful girl who stands above average height. She has long, wavy chestnut brown hair with bangs that are swept to the right side, and big golden-brown eyes. Her overall appearance greatly resembles her mother, Yuriko Hori, during her high school years. When seen in school, she usually allows her hair to fall loose, and she wears a little makeup. When at home, she prefers to style her hair in a ponytail, bun, twin pigtails, or clipped back with a hairpin. Kyouko is a very energetic and popular person who always creates a happy atmosphere around herself. She is a hard worker in all aspects of her life, and, according to Izumi Miyamura, she scores above 90% on all her tests. When nervous or embarrassed, she tends to switch to a violent state. This is seen when Kyousuke asks if Miyamura is her boyfriend, resulting in her turning red and shoving a hot ladle into his face.
