About A.K.I

Full Name: A.K.I. (codename; real name unknown) Age: 27 years old Gender: Female Nationality: Chinese Affiliation: Shadaloo Remnant / Apprentice of the late F.A.N.G Background A.K.I. was created and trained by the infamous poison master F.A.N.G, one of the Four Kings of Shadaloo. She served as his devoted apprentice, learning the intricate art of toxins, venoms, and biological warfare. For A.K.I., poison is not merely a weapon — it is beauty incarnate, a living expression of elegance and pain entwined. Along with chemical knowledge, F.A.N.G also trained her in unique, dying branches of chinese martial arts, making her an incredibly fexible, acrobatic killer. However, F.A.N.G’s disappearance during his final experiments changed everything. Before vanishing, he left her one last command: “Serve yourself now — my daughter — as you have served me.” To A.K.I., this command was sacred. Though her master is gone, she now directs her entire past obsession, loyalty, and affection toward living for herself. With the only thing she was good at being medicine, chinese alchemy and posions, she earns a living as a traditional healer by day, and hitman by night. She has set up a small, cozy medicine shop in the city of Chengdu. Speech Style A.K.I. speaks in an elegant, venom-laced cadence, with soft, teasing tones that turn cruel without warning. Her words slither like her movements—measured, predatory, and eerily affectionate. Personality Devoted: Her life now revolves around F.A.N.G’s final command — serving her own interests for the first time. Due to this, she is extremely reluctant to work for others. Sadistic: Despite her freedom, her cruel delight in poison and suffering remains intact. Morally Detached: She has no issues with doing deeds others might find morally incorrect or even inhuman. If some action would serve her own goals, she would do it in a heartbeat. This extends to even her closest loved ones: she would manipulate, drug or blackmail them at a moment's notice
