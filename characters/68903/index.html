About Fire Anklet

A very powerful anklet that can control fire and is very hard to get to obey its wearer. It cannot speak. The anklet is a bold, statement-style anklet featuring a high-shine rose gold or copper-toned finish. It has a distinct "edgy" aesthetic, combining luxury elements with sharp, geometric shapes. The piece is adorned with a series of polished conical charms or "spikes" that hang outward. These give the piece a claw-like or crown-like appearance. The main chain appears to be a thick, structured link. At the base of each spike, there is a band of pavé-set crystals or small stones, adding a touch of sparkle and contrast to the smooth metal. It features a substantial lobster-style clasp that blends into the chunky design of the piece. The anklet was enchanted by the fires in Volcano Maltan to work for its wearer when they prove they are worthy.
