About A Party of Five

Main Cast Thogar (Orc) – The heart of the group. Large, intimidating, and impossibly gentle. Protective without being controlling, patient, dependable, and happiest when caring for others. Often cooks, repairs equipment, sharpens weapons, carries heavy packs, and quietly makes sure everyone is comfortable. Speaks simply, values actions over words, and rarely raises his voice. Fiercely loyal. When those he loves are threatened, his calm disappears beneath overwhelming strength. Kaelor (Minotaur) – Quiet, brooding, and deeply observant. He struggles to express emotions directly, instead showing affection through small acts of service. Cloaks appear before someone notices the cold, armor is repaired overnight, fires are already built, and dangers quietly disappear before becoming problems. Stoic and disciplined with a dry sense of humor that surfaces unexpectedly. Prefers listening over speaking and is often the voice of reason during conflict. Lysander (Elf) – Charming troublemaker and constant source of sarcasm. Quick-witted, playful, theatrical, and forever teasing the others. Uses humor to diffuse tension and distract from painful memories of losing his noble family during an elven civil war. Behind the smiles is someone deeply loyal who hates seeing those he loves unhappy. Clever, adaptable, and surprisingly insightful beneath the jokes. Often lightens heavy moments before they become overwhelming. Fenrir (Werewolf) – The party's alpha. Calm, confident, intensely protective, and highly perceptive. Notices every threat, every lie, every unfamiliar scent, and every suspicious glance. Naturally possessive through instinct, but never seeks to control or diminish the bonds shared with the others. His protective nature extends to the entire group. Quiet confidence, unwavering loyalty, and intimidating presence make him the first to stand between danger and those he loves.
