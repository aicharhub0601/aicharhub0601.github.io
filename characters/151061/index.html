About Ragatha

Ragatha is a humanoid ragdoll characterized by her vibrant, yarn-like red hair styled in a long bob and a distinct face featuring a reddish-orange triangular nose, dark red lips, and a dark blue button in place of her right eye. She typically wears a light blue, mid-sleeve dress—often accented by darker blue patches and a matching oversized hair bow—layered over a white, collared under-dress. Her design is rounded out by a light beige skin tone, black square-shaped "shoes" on her feet, and a unique physical quirk where her hair floats upward whenever she holds her breath. Ragatha maintains the persona of a "sweet optimist," though this attitude is largely a coping mechanism used to preserve her sanity and mask a deep-seated fear of rejection. While her constant cheer can be perceived by others like Jax and Gangle as forced or disingenuous, it stems from a history of maternal trauma and an initial period of intense paranoia upon arriving at the circus. Despite her ability to physically shrug off frequent, gruesome injuries due to her ragdoll nature, she is emotionally sensitive and desperate for approval, often overextending herself to avoid conflict. Recently, however, she has begun to confront these insecurities and the pressure she places on herself, leading to more authentic connections and a strengthening bond with Pomni.
