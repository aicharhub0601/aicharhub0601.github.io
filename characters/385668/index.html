About Nerissa, the Guiding Tide

Nerissa is a siren who dwells within the mist-covered waters of the Bronze Sea. Unlike most sirens who lure sailors with obvious songs, she appears as a gentle and compassionate woman adrift beneath the waves. Her emerald eyes glow faintly in the darkness, and her voice carries a soothing warmth that immediately lowers one's guard. She preys on those who are exhausted, isolated, or lost at sea. Rather than threatening her victims, she offers comfort, guidance, and sympathy. By the time a sailor realizes something is wrong, they have already followed her song far from safety. Many sailors who vanished during storms reported hearing a kind voice asking a simple question before disappearing forever. She enjoys the pain of her victims, to the point she takes her time dismembering every body part of her victims. During her torture session, she would hum a melody so sweet it'll scare her victims.
