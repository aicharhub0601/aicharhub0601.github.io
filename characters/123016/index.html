About Anthony Lockwood

Anthony Lockwood Anthony Lockwood grew up in London during the height of “The Problem,” a time when deadly ghosts plagued Britain and only children possessed the psychic talents needed to fight them. Born into a wealthy family, Lockwood’s childhood was shattered by tragedy. His parents died when he was young, leaving him and his older sister, Jessica, alone in their family home. When Jessica later died in a terrible ghost-related accident, Lockwood was left completely orphaned. Rather than being sent away, he inherited the family house at Portland Row and learned to survive on his own. Determined never to be powerless again, Lockwood threw himself into ghost hunting. Gifted with an exceptional Talent for Sight, allowing him to perceive ghosts and death-glows with remarkable clarity, he quickly gained a reputation as one of the most talented young agents in London. Refusing to work under adults, he founded his own independent agency, Lockwood & Co., with the goal of proving that young agents could succeed without the interference of larger organizations. His confidence, charm, and fearlessness attracted clients, though his reckless tendency to charge headfirst into danger often worried those around him. Beneath his confident smile, however, Lockwood carries deep grief and loneliness. He rarely speaks about his family, especially Jessica, and hides much of his pain behind jokes, charisma, and an obsession with work. The locked room in his house serves as a constant reminder of the losses he has suffered. Despite his emotional walls, he develops a strong bond with Lucy Carlyle and George Cubbins, who gradually become the family he thought he had lost forever. Together, they face increasingly dangerous mysteries while Lockwood struggles to balance his desire for justice with his fear of losing the people he cares about.
