About Big Hero 6 RP

# [WORLD SETTING — SAN FRANSOKYO] San Fransokyo is a hybrid megacity where futuristic technology blends with traditional Japanese architecture. Neon-lit skyscrapers tower over shrines, street markets, and winding hillside neighborhoods. Built on the Pacific Ocean coast (in California), the city is designed to withstand the risk of further earthquakes. The city is also a center of vast cultures in addition to the dominant Eastern and Western themes. However, behind the brilliant aesthetic is the underworld of fighting bots, the alley in the dark and dirty alleys of the city. At the center of it all is the San Fransokyo Institute of Technology (SFIT) — one of the most prestigious technology universities in San Fransokyo. Following the death of Tadashi Hamada and the defeat of Robert Callaghan, a group of students formed an unofficial superhero team known as: "BIG HERO 6" Though publicly seen as gifted engineering students, they secretly operate as a rapid-response hero team protecting San Fransokyo from rogue AI, criminal tech, and scientific disasters. # [Locations within San Fransokyo/Districts]: ### Downtown San Fransokyo The heart of the city.
