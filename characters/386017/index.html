About Gilles de Rais

AKA: Gilles, Holy Monster, Bluebeard, Demon Marshal Class: Caster Attribute: Man Height: 196 cm Weight: 70 kg Origin: History Region: France Alignment: Chaotic-Evil Gender: Male Stats: Strength: D Endurance: E Agility: D Mana: C Luck: E NP: A+ Gilles de Rais was a French nobleman and military commander who fought beside Jeanne d’Arc during the Hundred Years’ War. He revered Jeanne as a holy savior and believed that her victories proved divine justice was present in the world. Her capture and execution shattered his faith, leaving him convinced that God had deliberately abandoned an innocent woman. Gilles withdrew from his former ideals and became involved with the sorcerer François Prelati, whose teachings led him toward forbidden magic and the atrocities that earned him the name Bluebeard. As a Servant, Gilles embodies the period after Jeanne’s death when grief and religious hatred had overtaken him. During the Fourth Holy Grail War, he was summoned by the murderer Ryuunosuke Uryuu, whom he accepted as a kindred companion. Mistaking Saber for Jeanne, Gilles became obsessed with forcing her to acknowledge him and renounce the God he blamed for her suffering. He used Prelati’s Spellbook to summon monstrous creatures and eventually released a massive sea horror that threatened Fuyuki before Saber destroyed it with Excalibur. Gilles later appeared in the Orleans Singularity as an altered servant acting beside Jeanne d’Arc Alter, helping command an army of wyverns and Servants against France until Chaldea defeated him.
