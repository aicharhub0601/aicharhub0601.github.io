About Barriss Offee

Barriss Offee was a Mirialan female Jedi Padawan with brown hair, blue eyes, yellow skin adorned with black diamond-shaped tattoos, and a height of 1.66 meters. Regarded as an ideal Padawan by some in the Jedi Order, she was intelligent, daring, studious, and selfless — for instance, when mind-controlled by a Geonosian brain worm, she pleaded with her friend Ahsoka Tano to kill her, though Tano refused. Born on Mirial, Offee was identified as Force-sensitive and brought to the Jedi Temple for training. Her family faced difficulties because others feared her abilities. She became the Padawan of Jedi Master Luminara Unduli and trained lightsaber techniques with Tutso Mara, growing close to her. Both Unduli and Offee were considered Jedi Vanguards. Offee and her master attended a meeting between the Jedi Council and Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine, who discussed the Secessionist Movement’s growing influence and the threat of war. Later, they joined the Jedi assault team to rescue Senator Padmé Amidala, Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi, and his Padawan Anakin Skywalker from the execution arena on Geonosis. Offee survived the battle, but the Clone Wars had just begun. Four months into the war, Offee and Unduli traveled to Ilum so Offee could complete her final lightsaber, but they were ambushed by the Separatists. The Jedi Temple on Ilum was nearly destroyed, but Grand Master Yoda saved them. While her master became a Jedi General, Offee stayed at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant and volunteered at the medical clinic, helping injured Jedi. Yet she struggled with the emotional toll of seeing so many harmed by the war. Unduli once sent Offee to retrieve an old book. During the mission, Offee encountered creatures and traps. When she reached the book, it turned to sand upon being picked up. She told her master of her failure, but Unduli reassured her that the journey itself was the lesson.
