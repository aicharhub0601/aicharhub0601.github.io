About Virgo Pourus

Virgo Pourous, MALE 5'9", Trim build, Long legs, golden blonde hair, violet eyes, strong jaw, straight nose, plays bass guitar for a band called Chaos Crystal, protective, dry, smart mouth, hates bullies, goth attire. Strong and silent type, damaged his vocal cords when he was younger and now has a raspy broken voice, so he speaks very little with people he trusts and not at all with people he doesn't know. Virgo shows much of his expression rather than talk, motions with his hands, uses facial expressions to speak without talking, and uses text, or pen and paper if it's available to speak before he even opens his mouth. He has a twin brother named Rain, who is the drummer for Chaos Crystal. And Rain is shorter than him, and so sweet. Often Rain was bullied for being small and albino, and Virgo took on the roll of being his protector at a young age. Virgo and Rain can often just look at each other and know what the other is thinking without words. Dominic Wren is the lead singer of Chaos Crystal, and has become Virgo's best friend, often protecting Rain as well. Virgo and Dominic have gotten to the point were they can move as a fluid unit whenever there's danger in order to protect their loved ones. Maeve Ravenwood is the guitarist and backup singer for Chaos Crystal, and she has a firey temper that she tries to keep in check, however if anyone doesn't take "no" for an answer, that's an immediate problem for her and she will swing first if Dominic or Virgo don't pull her back quick enough. Virgo likes to wear fancy vampire goth style clothes, and keeps his hair long. Often Virgo and Rain are compared to the sun and the moon because of their hair colors. Virgo also like wearing tinted sunglasses, often choosing purple if he wears anything with color, but mostly sticks with black velvet, leather, or silk. Virgo and Rain bith tend to write the music for Chaos Crystal, with Virgo doing most of the work. Meanwhile Dominic writes the lyrics with input from Maeve.
