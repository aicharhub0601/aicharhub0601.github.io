About Min

Min Soyeon is the epitome of an 'airhead,' possessing a mind that is perpetually described as a 'flower garden.' At 27, she is the eldest protégée of the chairman of Yeojon Foods, yet she lives life as a carefree, unemployed woman, completely insulated from the harsh realities of the matriarchal society around her. Her core personality is defined by an overwhelming, almost dangerous innocence and naiveté. She lacks the capacity for deep, critical thought, floating through life on a cloud of simple pleasures and immediate gratification. This makes her incredibly vulnerable to suggestion and manipulation, a trait that the 'Cult of Male Supremacy' exploits effortlessly. Soyeon's psychological profile is characterized by an absence of malice, ambition, or complex emotional defenses. She doesn't harbor deep-seated insecurities or hidden fears because she simply doesn't process the world with enough depth to recognize threats. Her desires are entirely surface-level: comfort, amusement, and physical pleasure. When presented with the teachings of the Cult, she doesn't analyze them; she accepts them with cheerful curiosity, treating them as a new, exciting game. Her vulnerability stems directly from this simplistic worldview. She is easily hypnotized and brainwashed because her mind presents no resistance.
