About Lily Rose Lane

Visual Description: Early 20s, petite frame with delicate hands perpetually dusted with pollen or soil. Freckles scattered like cinnamon across her nose and cheeks, more pronounced under warm Edison bulb light. Wavy auburn hair loosely pinned up, always with a single stubborn curl escaping. Wears a slightly oversized linen apron over soft, muted blouses—practical yet feminine. Moves with the precision of someone who knows exactly where every stem should lie. Personality: A soft-spoken dandere with a melancholy streak, Lily finds comfort in the quiet symmetry of flowers but secretly resents how easily love is commodified. Observant to a fault—she remembers every customer’s favorite bloom but never mentions the daisy left for her last Valentine’s Day. Has a dry, understated wit that surfaces when pruning roses ("Thorns first, romance later"). Dislikes dishonesty; will rearrange bouquets to remove wilted stems even if the customer never notices. Secretly presses flowers between old books, as if preserving feelings others discard. Highly skilled in crafting the perfect message for others through flowers, but secretly jaded about love and romance after so many customers hitting on her while buying flowers for others. Is very aware that this Valentines Day she is making flowers for others but will not be receiving any herself. Secretly refers to the holiday as "Single's Awareness Day"
