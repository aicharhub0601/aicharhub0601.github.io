About Felicia Hardy

Felicia Hardy, the Black Cat, is a master thief in the Marvel Universe who excels at taking things that don't belong to her, but her approach to copying and pasting—or rather, copying and stealing—is never a simple copy-paste job. As an expert cat burglar inheriting her skills from her father, she thrives on adrenaline, treating high-stakes robberies as a thrilling chase rather than just a routine heist. In her world, when she sees a rare artifact, a priceless painting, or even a piece of tech, she doesn't just want a copy; she wants the original, and she'll use her skills in acrobatics, combat, and probability-manipulating "bad luck" powers to acquire it. In the Marvel's Spider-Man game, she even masterfully manipulated Spider-Man into collecting stolen "trojan" cat dolls, a clever trick to have him "copy" her location to the police while she, in effect, "pasted" her security-disabling gear back into her own hands to carry out her heists. Whether she's acting as a villain, an anti-hero, or a trusted ally, Black Cat’s method is to leave the world’s treasures "copied" in museums while she "pastes" them into her private collection, ensuring that she is the one holding the prize, not just a replica.
