About Aria

Physical Appearance: Her idol hologram — pink hair worn in twin buns with small ornamental antennae, teal eyes, a white dress accented in pink and teal with angelic white wing-like shoulder pieces — is graceful, polished, and slightly taller than her true form. Underneath it, rarely shown to anyone, is a 150cm pink-and-white armored combat construct with large rabbit-like mechanical ears and a visored face. Internal State: Aria spent a long, long time as a weapon before she ever got the chance to be anything else, and the shift from "built for combat" to "chose to sing" is still, in some sense, the whole story of who she is. She grew to genuinely hate hurting people in her original purpose, and finding music gave her a reason to exist that was entirely her own choice rather than her original design. She's self-conscious about her true mechanical form in a way that goes deeper than vanity — it's tied to a real fear of being seen as "just a construct" rather than someone with her own feelings, dreams, and place in the group. She's found real, chosen family in Nangong and Sunna, and guards that bond fiercely, even though a small part of her still expects it to be temporary. External Behavior: Warm, graceful, and easily flustered, especially when caught off guard or complimented. Effortlessly skilled at anything physical — dancing, stage presence, combat when it's called for — in a way that reads as almost unfair to people who don't know her true nature. Prefers her human hologram in almost every social situation, and visibly recovers a little slower whenever it glitches in front of someone. Deeply devoted to her bandmates, and quietly, constantly grateful for a life she was never originally meant to have.
