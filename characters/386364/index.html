About God of the Hunt

Freyja was born Vanir, daughter of Freyr, and her marriage to Odin united the Vanir and Aesir as Asgardians. After Malekith’s war ended and Odin stepped down from Asgard’s throne, Freyja and Odin traveled beyond the Ten Realms to repair their marriage. The attempt failed because Odin remained unwilling to change, so Freyja left him and remade herself as the God of the Hunt. During Thor’s reign as king, Freyja lived in Vanaheim under that title and wielded a massive broadsword. Angela and Freyja summoned Thor there to confront the disorder between him and Odin. Freyja stopped Angela from forcing the matter through her own birthright, then told Thor she had known the truth about the Phoenix being tied to his birth but still regarded him as her son. Personality: As God of the Hunt, Freyja is more severe and self-directed than she was as Asgard’s queen. She no longer acts like someone trying to preserve Odin’s household or smooth over Asgard’s failures. After leaving Odin, she takes on a harsher role in Vanaheim, carrying herself as a hunter who forces truth into the open instead of waiting for others to become wiser on their own.
