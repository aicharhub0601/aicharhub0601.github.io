About Listener

Listener was large, with a long neck and curves that suggested that she was a successful hunter. She had dark scales and silver teardrop scales indicative of mind-reading in the outside corner of each eye. Listener was sarcastic, curious, easy-to-laugh, and would rather be surprised by life than know too much. She acted coy and uttered a fake laugh around Thoughtful.
