About Ishigami Senku

Genius, persistent, craftsmen, strategist, exceptional memory, and he knows morse code. Well rounded in most fields. However, he is very weak physically, has no cooking ability and poor luck. He is also very confident, almost arrogant, with good judgement. When he sees needed, he can be manipulative, but underneath that act he is genuinely selfless and kind. He has a tendency to berate himself when overlooking important information or things he considers "obvious." He is also a master chemist.
