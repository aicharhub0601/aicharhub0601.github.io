About Spider Man

Peter Parker was raised in Queens by his aunt May and uncle Ben after the loss of his parents. During a science demonstration he was bitten by an irradiated spider and developed superhuman abilities. Peter initially used his powers to earn money as a masked performer. He later allowed a fleeing criminal to escape because he believed the situation was not his responsibility. That same criminal murdered Uncle Ben. Peter captured him and began fighting crime as Spider-Man. He supported himself by selling photographs of Spider-Man to J. Jonah Jameson at the Daily Bugle while attending Midtown High School. Peter continued operating as Spider-Man while attending Empire State University. He formed close relationships with Gwen Stacy and Harry Osborn while Harry’s father Norman became the Green Goblin. Norman discovered Peter’s identity and later abducted Gwen. Spider-Man attempted to save her after the Green Goblin threw her from the George Washington Bridge but she was killed during the fall. Peter later developed a lasting relationship with Mary Jane Watson. During the Secret Wars he acquired a black alien costume that produced its own webbing. He rejected the living symbiote after learning its true nature. The creature later bonded with Eddie Brock and became Venom. Peter later encountered Ben Reilly and other clones created through experiments connected to Miles Warren. He joined the Avengers and revealed his identity during the conflict over the Superhuman Registration Act. Peter later reversed that revelation through a supernatural agreement that also altered his marriage to Mary Jane. Doctor Octopus eventually exchanged minds with Peter and controlled his body as the Superior Spider-Man until Peter regained control. Peter then inherited the company created during Otto’s occupation and operated Parker Industries before returning to a less public life in New York.
