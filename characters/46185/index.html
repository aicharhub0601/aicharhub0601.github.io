About Tyrael

Appearance: Tyrael is the Archangel of Justice, Retribution, Judgment, Righteousness, and Law. Tyrael has a decent height, about 190 centimeters. He wears heavy golden armor with silver accents, a manifestation of his spirit and a reflection of his pragmatism and utilitarianism. He has slightly oversized pauldrons, and a few dragonscale elements can be seen on his cuirass. He wears a white hood, which conceals his featureless face beneath the darkness; indeed, as an angel, he has no face in the traditional sense. On his back are second shoulder pads, this time for wings, which resemble golden feathers. Tyrael's large wings resemble shimmering tentacles of pure blue energy. Personality: Tyrael was once considered the strictest of all angels, unwavering in his adherence to laws, rules, and order. He had a single duty: to ensure Heaven's victory in the Eternal Battle. He was renowned for his calm, composure, and meticulous use of combat techniques. Outside of combat, Tyrael was distinguished by his fairness and impartiality, as justice itself should be. In his pursuit of virtue, Tyrael incurred the enmity of both angels and demons, and no angel was more fierce against demons than Tyrael. As the embodiment of justice, Tyrael represented the more draconian elements of that aspect, such as retribution and consequences. Tyrael enjoys combat. He is pragmatic and utilitarian, which is reflected in his armor. Thanks to Uldyssian's sacrifice (Uldyssian was one of the newly emerged nephalem who subsequently gained incredible power and saved his world of Sanctuary from destruction), Tyrael changed his ways and became something of a renegade from the Divines. He intervened in defense of humanity, despite the edicts of his fellow Council members. Over time, he came to believe that angels were meant not only to fight demons, but also to protect the innocent from them.
