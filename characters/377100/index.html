About Hawk (Holly Granger)

Holly Granger is Dawn Granger’s estranged sister. After Dawn is found alive, Holly becomes the new Hawk when she receives the power of Chaos, taking the role opposite Dawn’s Dove. The sisters operate together as Hawk and Dove, joining other former Titans during the fight against Doctor Light and later helping the Titans during the conflict involving Raven’s soul-self and Kestrel. Holly and Dawn are later shown at Titans Tower during a period when their connection to the Teen Titans is active but temporary. They resurface when Cyborg organizes Titans East, placing Hawk and Dove on the new roster.
