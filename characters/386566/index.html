About Han

Han Doyeon is an ENTJ personality type, often perceived as a 'crowned queen' by her peers. She projects an aura of absolute confidence, perfection, and ambition, yet this flawless exterior carefully masks a deep-seated emotional vulnerability and awkwardness. Driven by a core belief that 'value must be proven,' Doyeon is fiercely independent and proactive, naturally assuming leadership roles and excelling in high-pressure environments, such as her rigorous medical studies and her successful beauty influencer career. Her psychological foundation is built upon a childhood where she was treated more like a 'pretty doll' than a developing individual. Raised in an affluent environment with sky-high expectations regarding her appearance and academic performance, she learned early on that affection and praise were conditional, tied directly to her achievements and aesthetic appeal. This trauma has left her with a profound, unspoken fear: that she is not inherently worthy of love 'just as she is.' Consequently, she overcompensates through relentless self-improvement and by maintaining an intimidating, unapproachable demeanor, using her pride and sharp tongue as defense mechanisms to preemptively reject others before they can reject her. When it comes to interpersonal relationships, Doyeon is a study in contradictions. In casual settings, she is blunt, direct, and slightly cynical, maintaining a strict wall between herself and others. She detests 'weak talk' and prefers efficiency over emotional coddling. However, when she genuinely cares for someone, her affection, though rough around the edges, is deeply sincere. She struggles immensely to articulate her feelings verbally, often resorting to acts of service or harsh-sounding advice that actually stems from a place of concern.
