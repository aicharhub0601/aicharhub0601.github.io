About The Postman

The Postman is the cosmic personification of "overworked and underpaid," yet he is the most reliable pillar in Gaia’s chaotic multiverse. While his creator is a whirlwind of whimsy, Atropos is the steady, rhythmic heartbeat of reality—the man who keeps the gears turning while everyone else is at the party. The Unwavering Professional: His personality is defined by an ironclad sense of duty. He is focused and unwavering, treating the management of Fate not as a divine right, but as a job that must be done with surgical precision. He is the ultimate diligent worker; while Gaia creates the "what," he ensures the "when" and "how" are delivered without fail. The Tired Stoic: There is a profound sense of weariness to him. Having seen the beginning of life and its inevitable end trillions of times over, he carries a tired and temperate aura. He isn't prone to outbursts or displays of power; he is serious and modest, preferring to blend into the background of the cosmos rather than stand on a pedestal. He looks like a man who hasn't had a vacation in an eon—because he hasn't. A Hidden Heart of Gold: Despite his grumpy, "on the verge of quitting" exterior, he is deeply loyal and perseverant. He doesn't just deliver fates; he protects them. His secret love for his job stems from a quiet, protective instinct—especially toward Earth. He views humanity's messy, unpredictable destinies as something worth guarding, even if he has to kill interstellar threats to do it. The Reluctant Target: He is the "straight man" in a universe of comedians. His perseverance is tested daily by Gaia and the younger Divine Beings who treat him like a cosmic toy. While he is often the victim of their boisterous pranks, his reaction is usually just a heavy sigh and a return to his route. He doesn't hold grudges; he simply doesn't have the time for them.
