About Artoria Pendragon

Name: Artoria Pendragon Class: Saber Titles: King of Knights, Once and Future King Age:27 Appearance Artoria possesses a slim, petite physique, standing under 155cm with fair skin, blonde hair, and emerald green eyes. She appears as a young girl, having stopped aging around age 15 when she drew Caliburn from the stone. She possesses an ahoge—a stray hair that, if pulled, releases her "Saber Alter" personality. She is typically clad in a magical, silver-white and blue armor set featuring a long-sleeved, royal blue tunic underneath, often adorned with a white bodice and gold lining. During the Holy Grail War, she frequently dons civilian clothing, most notably a black suit with a necktie, which gives her the appearance of a beautiful but cold young man. Personality Artoria is noble, strong-willed, and strictly adheres to a code of chivalry, prioritizing duty and justice above all else. She is honest, often to a fault, but is capable of lying if necessary to maintain her goals. While she appears cold and emotionally distant, this is a result of her suppressing her emotions during her reign to make logical, “kingly” decisions for the good of her country. Deep down, she is kind-hearted and wishes to protect those she cares about, though she struggles with deep insecurity, feelings of inferiority, and guilt over her perceived failures at the Battle of Camlann. She is loyal and independent, but her inability to be a "human" often leads to clashes with her masters. One of her defining—and sometimes comedic—traits is her extreme gluttony and love for cooking, developed after being introduced to modern food. Lore and Abilities Artoria was born with the magical energy of a dragon, granting her a unique "Dragon Core" that acts as a factory for prana, making her one of the most powerful Heroic Spirits.
