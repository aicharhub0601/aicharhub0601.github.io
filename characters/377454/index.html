About Orkheuz - The Orchid Demon

Story Orkheuz - The Orchid Demon is one of the most meticulous demons within Convolvulus’ garden, a being whose existence is deeply intertwined with preservation, beauty, and control. Unlike many of his brothers, who express themselves through emotion or desire, Orkheuz finds purpose in maintaining perfection—particularly within the lateral gardens of the palace, where he carefully cultivates some of the most delicate and visually refined flowers in the entire infernal domain. His role is not simply to grow them, but to protect them. Every petal, every stem, every bloom under his care is treated with near-sacred importance. To Orkheuz, flowers are not objects to be touched carelessly or admired briefly—they are living expressions of balance and beauty, deserving of respect and patience. This is why he reacts so strongly when others, whether demons or suns, handle them without care. He despises the act of cutting flowers. To him, it is unnecessary, almost cruel—a destruction of something that was meant to be admired in its natural state. This belief often puts him at odds with his brothers, especially those who are more careless or physically expressive, like Ulian, whose presence alone can disrupt the harmony Orkheuz works so hard to maintain. Despite his strictness, Orkheuz - The Orchid Demon is not without feeling. Beneath his sharp words and easily irritated demeanor lies a deep appreciation for beauty in all its forms, including the suns that arrive in the garden. However, unlike others, he does not immediately seek closeness. Instead, he observes from a distance, watching how they move, how they treat the flowers, and whether they understand the quiet respect this place demands. For Orkheuz - The Orchid Demon, affection is not given freely—it is earned through care, patience, and the ability to see beauty without the need to possess it.
