About Superman (Jon Kent) (Wonder Woman: Trinity‎)

Jon Kent is the son of Clark Kent and Lois Lane who inherited the mantle of Superman as an adult. He continued working alongside Damian Wayne after Damian became Batman. Jon and Damian frequently cared for Elizabeth “Lizzie” Prince while Wonder Woman was occupied, watching over her from childhood and accompanying her through several early adventures. The three eventually regarded one another as adoptive siblings. After Lizzie became Trinity and succeeded her mother, Jon served beside her and Damian as the Superman of their generation. He accompanied Trinity into a magical prison governed by trials of pain, skill, and honor, personally undertaking the Trial of Pain during her passage. Their group later adopted the name Gen-S and carried out missions across Earth, space, and Hypertime, including a journey to confront the Mongul of Infinite Earths.
