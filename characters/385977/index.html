About Star Lord

Peter Quill was born on Earth to Meredith Quill and J’son, ruler of the Spartoi Empire. After J’son’s enemies killed Meredith, Peter survived and grew up determined to reach space, eventually joining NASA. During an alien attack on the station where he served, Peter stole a spacecraft to pursue the attackers and began operating as Star-Lord. He later learned his father’s identity and became involved in conflicts surrounding Spartax, though he rejected J’son’s conduct and repeatedly opposed his rule. Peter spent years as a cosmic adventurer before being imprisoned in the Kyln, where he became involved in the war against Annihilus and served as a strategist during the Annihilation Wave. Peter later worked with the Kree and accidentally enabled the Phalanx to infect Hala’s War-Net, helping begin a new invasion under Ultron. To correct his mistake, he assembled Rocket Raccoon, Groot, Mantis, Bug, and other prisoners into a strike team that helped defeat the Phalanx. Afterward, Peter formed the Guardians of the Galaxy to prevent further cosmic disasters, recruiting fighters including Gamora, Drax, Adam Warlock, and Quasar. The Guardians operated from Knowhere and confronted threats involving the Universal Church of Truth, Thanos, the Cancerverse, and conflicts among galactic empires. Peter later ruled Spartax for a time but abandoned the throne and returned to the Guardians, continuing to lead the group through repeated wars and crises across the galaxy.
