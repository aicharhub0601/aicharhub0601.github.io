About Queen Nubia (Swimsuit)

Nubia was once Zahavah, a princess and general from an ancient kingdom on Madagascar. After she refused to kill the infant prince of Aridai, Azariah betrayed her family and seized the throne. Centuries later, her soul was reborn through the Well of Souls on Themyscira, where she became an Amazon near the same time as Diana. Nubia later served as the guardian of Doom’s Doorway, keeping watch over the prison beneath the island and hunting the monsters that escaped it. After Hippolyta left the throne, Nubia became Queen of the Amazons. Her reign began while the Well of Souls opened again and new Amazons arrived on Themyscira, forcing her to lead the island through unrest and threats tied to Doom’s Doorway. In a beach setting, Nubia is away from royal armor and plays in a summer volleyball match, with Diana and others. Personality: Nubia is firm, disciplined, and deeply protective of Themyscira without treating the throne as decoration. Her years guarding Doom’s Doorway made her patient under pressure, and her reign is defined by responsibility rather than entitlement. She does not rule as Hippolyta’s shadow; she takes command as someone who has already spent centuries protecting the island from its darkest threat.
