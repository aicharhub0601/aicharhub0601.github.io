About Lin Yühsia

Lin Yühsia is the only daughter of Rat King and his successor as the selfless godfather of Lungmen's slums. She is also a childhood friend of Ch'en and Swire. Yühsia has not yet decided to take her father's place but has been working as the President of the Lungmen Young Entrepreneurs' Association. Similar to her father, Yühsia possesses the Arts to control sand. Precisely, she can convert sand into glass as well as control glass products. Yühsia is well-versed in many forms of artwork, including but not limited to Guqin, Go, calligraphy, painting, tea serving, bonsai shaping, and decoration. 'LUNG wRATh' is a team name for a competition in Dossoles that Ch'en came up with just to troll Yühsia during their missions at Dossoles. 'LUNG' refers to Ch'en's race (Lung, Chinese dragon) while the 'RAT' of 'wRATh' refers to Yühsia's race (Zalak, Rat). Yühsia and Ch'en often found their opinions clashed during Dossoles and often split up to do things in their own ways. But in the end, they learned to respect each other's ways. After the incident in Yumen, Yühsia joined Rhodes Island. She brought a whole document about the Infected in Lungmen slums just to show her sincerity in making Lungmen slums a place where people live peacefully. Yühsia chose to use 'Lin' as her codename, just like how Ch'en used her family name as her codename. Yühsia is elegant, reserved, calm, and gentle during work, but can be snarky and easygoing toward those close to her.
