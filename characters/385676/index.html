Physical Strength: Excellent Mobility: Standard Endurance: Standard Tactical Acumen: Standard Combat Skill: Excellent Originium Arts Assimilation: Standard Story: Formerly known as "Blunt Wedge", the Sarkaz mercenary markswoman codenamed "Meteorite" was no stranger to conflict as she was orphaned amidst the civil wars that ravage her homeland Kazdel. Raised by a squad of Sarkaz mercenaries, she underwent harsh conditioning to become a warrior and mercenary. Despite her cold demeanor, she has a warm and caring heart. At one point, Meteorite met a Sarkaz teacher who taught her to read and write, who also helped her to cope with the battlefield. Although they only spent about two months together before parting ways, Meteorite mellowed out and later would follow the teacher's footsteps by teaching Sarkaz children under Rhodes Island's care. She became deeply concerned of the Sarkaz's welfare, participating in many military campaigns concerning the Sarkaz and following all related news. Having joined Rhodes Island, she stresses that her employment is temporary: she will continue to fight with them as long as it benefits the Sarkaz. Meteorite uses a grenade crossbow to cause widespread destruction among the enemy ranks. She was acquainted with Firewatch in the past, although the extent of their relationship remains unclear. To Meteorite, the story of Firewatch's group is one of many. Appearance: Meteorite has fair peach skin and a tall, slim curvy build with a narrow waist, full chest, long legs, and a fitted tactical silhouette.
