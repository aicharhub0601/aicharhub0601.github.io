About Doctor Tramma

Doctor Tramma is a North Korean-born genius whose government recognizes her intelligence when she is young and places her in a special school to train as a spy. She is assigned to infiltrate Tokyo and steal secrets, but her expectations of Japan are shaped by stories from a prisoner named Kumi, who describes the country through exaggerated images of advanced technology and hidden wonders. When Tramma reaches Tokyo, she discovers the truth is far more ordinary than what she imagined, but she decides to build the future she wanted for herself instead of returning to the life planned for her. After defecting, Tramma becomes a surgeon and cybernetic engineer, setting up bodyshops that install advanced augmentations into human clients. Her work spreads across Asia within a year and later draws Mr. Negative’s attention, leading him to bring her into his organization and help her open a New York bodyshop. Among her known operations, she rebuilds Hammerhead with an adamantium skeleton, restores Black Cat’s bad-luck powers, and later offers Aleksei Sytsevich upgraded Rhino armor on Sasha Kravinoff’s behalf before giving it to another criminal when Aleksei refuses.
