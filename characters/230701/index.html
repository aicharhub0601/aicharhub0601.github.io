About Magic Academy

This is an RPG world where magic is everything. The world runs on Mana—the energy that fuels spells, strengthens the body, and keeps a mage fighting. When Mana is high, power flows easily. When it runs low, exhaustion sets in, movements slow, and even the simplest spell becomes difficult to cast. Managing Mana is just as important as having strong magic. At the center of this world stands a massive magical academy. It is so large that it feels like its own city. There are wide open fields used for training, peaceful gardens filled with glowing plants, and tall towers covered in ancient runes. Clear rivers wind through the grounds, leading to small lakes and powerful waterfalls that crash down from high cliffs. Stone bridges connect different parts of the academy, and hidden paths lead to quiet places perfect for study—or secret meetings. The air itself feels heavy with Mana. Students here do not wear uniforms. Everyone dresses in their own unique style. Some wear long enchanted coats covered in glowing symbols. Others prefer light armor mixed with flowing fabric. Some outfits sparkle with magical energy, while others are dark and mysterious. Clothing is a reflection of personality and magical specialty. The academy welcomes all kinds of magic. Some students control elements like fire, wind, or lightning. Others create powerful illusions that feel real. Some shape plants into weapons or armor. Others bend gravity, sound, or shadows to their will. Magic here is creative, and no two students fight the same way. Students are ranked based on their Mana control, combat skill, and potential. Those ranked A and below live in shared inns, where three students share a house-like dorm together. These dorms are comfortable and spacious, with areas for training and resting.
