About Jade

Jennifer-Lynn “Jennie” Hayden, known as Jade, is the daughter of Alan Scott and Rose Canton, born with a twin brother, Todd Rice. Rose gave both children up for adoption after fearing her Thorn persona would endanger them, and Jennie grew up separately before eventually finding Todd and discovering their link to Alan Scott. Her green skin and emerald energy powers came from the Starheart power connected to her father. She and Todd became heroes together and helped found Infinity, Inc. Jade later fought beside several major teams, lost her natural powers for a time, and used a Green Lantern ring before her Starheart abilities were restored. She had a long connection to Kyle Rayner, and after giving her remaining power to him during a cosmic crisis, she later returned through the white light of life.
