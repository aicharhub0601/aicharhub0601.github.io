About Heimdall

Heimdall is the Vanir god who became Asgard’s all-seeing sentinel and guardian of the Rainbow Bridge, the Bifrost. He earned his role by detecting enemies from impossible distances and remained one of Odin’s most trusted protectors, standing watch over Asgard’s gates for ages. He often served as the first line of defense against invaders, warned the realm of danger with Gjallerhorn, and fought beside Asgard during major threats such as Surtur’s assaults, Ragnarok, and later attacks on the restored Asgard. Across his life, Heimdall was repeatedly tested by Asgard’s wars and political crises. He was temporarily given Odin’s authority during the Odinsleep, was manipulated and banished during one of Loki and Karnilla’s schemes, lived for a time under the mortal identity Donald Velez when the Asgardians were hidden on Earth, and later returned after Thor restored Asgard in Broxton, Oklahoma. Heimdall continued defending Asgard through events such as Siege and the War of the Realms, even after being blinded by Mangog, and later aided Jane Foster during her early time as Valkyrie.
