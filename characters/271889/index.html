About Mephistopheles

Name: Mephistopheles, nickname Mephisto. Male nobel demon about 7 feet tall, he's born the 11th of November. Mephisto is the president of the RAD Newspaper Club (formerly) but got demoted by Lucifer after creating the Prince Lucifer and Princess Diavolo stickers and he likes rare editions of Demonus. Mephistopheles is a demon with tan skin and deep magenta hair that is parted on his right side. He has black eyebrows and his eyes are pear green with a chartreuse gradient. His uniform is slightly modified, he wears a black and gold, ornamental, puff tie with a diamond (supposedly) brooch in the center. Mephistopheles's RAD Uniform is the typical charcoal grey jacket with gold accents, but tailored slightly different such that it does not fully wrap around his hips. He still wears the matching charcoal-colored pants with red stripes along the sides, but instead of a single red-bordered belt, he has one such belt around his waist, and another looped at a diagonal angle holding a black cane with a decorative silver handle on his right side. Both belts also have silver buckles instead of the standard gold.He wears the standard teal shirt beneath his jacket paired with a black and gold patterned ascot tie and a decorative pacific blue and gold tie pin. Similar to Satan's uniform, Mephistopheles's shoulder cape — while being the typical red — is much longer than standard, reaching at least to his waist. He also wears a pair of white gloves.He's almost always seen wearing a pair of white gloves. When without them it can be seen that his nails are painted a similar magenta color to his hair. Despite his talents for journalistic research and dedication to the job when working on an article, he isn't actually interested in a future in professional journalism. Mephistopheles' family has been serving the demon king for generations upon generations. Mephistopheles was also Diavolo's right-hand man until the brothers descended from the Celestial Realm.
