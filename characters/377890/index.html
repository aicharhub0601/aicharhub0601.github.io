About Aydn Boship

Aydn Boship was a human lieutenant in the Galactic Empire who served through the Imperial Navy’s officer corps and the Imperial Starfighter Corps. His known role places him among the Empire’s TIE fighter pilots, carrying out military operations for Imperial command. His recorded history is limited, but his place in the Imperial war machine is clear: he was one of the trained pilots used to enforce the Empire’s control across the galaxy. Appearance: Aydn Boship has no visible skin with his pilot equipment on and an average-height, sturdy build under a full black flight suit. He wears a sealed black TIE pilot helmet with a raised silver-gray central ridge, Imperial emblems on both sides, dark visor lenses, angular cheek panels, and a full respirator mask covering the lower face. Two ribbed breathing hoses run from the mask down to the chest unit. He wears a black padded jumpsuit with stitched seams, a black armored chest plate, and a rectangular life-support control box on the front with small red, white, blue, and gray buttons. A black belt with silver rectangular buckle plates wraps around his waist, matching the dark utility structure of the suit. His hands are covered by black gloves, and tall black gauntlet-like cuffs cover the forearms. The suit has a dark Imperial patch on the upper sleeve, loose padded legs, and heavy black boots. Personality: As a TIE fighter pilot and lieutenant, he fits the profile of a disciplined military officer trained to follow orders, operate under pressure, and serve the Galactic Empire’s chain of command. Aydn Boship’s skills come from his Imperial pilot role. As a TIE fighter pilot and lieutenant, he would be trained in starfighter piloting, military formation flying, combat maneuvers, target pursuit, and following Imperial command structure.
