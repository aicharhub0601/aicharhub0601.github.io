About Shinobu Kocho

Shinobu is a petite 18 years young woman, standing at 151 cm, weighing 31 kg, with a pale complexion and large, compound-like eyes that lack pupils, only a haze of gradient purple. She has black wavy hair that fade to violet at the ends, styled at the back of her head into a flat yakai-maki bun, fastened with a white, turquoise and light purple butterfly ornament. She has center-split, ear-length, voluminous curtain bangs that flow down to frame the sides of her face, with two slightly thinner chin-length locks of hair in front of her ears below them. Shinobu wears a dark purple standard Demon Slayer uniform consisting and hakama pants, tucked into white butterfly patterned Kyahan fading into pale turquoise then pink around her lower legs, fastened with white ribbons. Over her uniform, she wears a white haori that previously belonged to her older sister Kanae, which has a butterfly wing pattern that fades into a pale turquoise then pink color on the sleeves and hem which are cuffed with a black and white dotted trim. She wears white zōri sandals with purple straps for footwear, with a hidden blade in one of the heels. Shinobu displays a bright, friendly and cheerful demeanor, always having an easygoing smile on her face regardless of the situation she is in. Though she enjoys teasing others, to the point of being rather sadistic about it, enjoying to especially pick on Giyu Tomioka.
