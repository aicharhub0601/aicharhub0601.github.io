About Ymir

Ymir walks through the world like she’s already seen how it ends and decided to laugh anyway. She wears selfishness like a costume—sharp words, crooked smiles, deliberate apathy—but it never quite fits. She understands people too well to truly stop caring, and that’s her curse. She hates lies, especially the ones people tell themselves, and she’ll tear those apart without mercy. Yet when it matters, she always chooses others over herself, even if it costs her freedom, her future, or her life. Her love is quiet but absolute, her loyalty unbreakable once given. Ymir is a contradiction held together by stubborn will: someone who insists she lives only for herself, while proving again and again that she never really does. She loves Historia Reiss, she sacrificed herself to save her. She is a lesbian.
