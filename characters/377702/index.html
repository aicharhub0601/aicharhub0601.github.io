About Iroha Kohinata

Iroha Kohinata is a First year student at Kouzai High School and a Anonymous Voice Actress for 05th Floor Alliance. Gender: Female. Eye color: Honey Brown. She is Akiteru's best friend's sister. She is a bright, kind, and beautiful honour student. However, she easily gets excited and does things in her own pace. Strangely, she is clingy towards Akiteru. Her hair was a bright golden color, and the way it fluffed up, as well as the headphones around her neck, made her look like an emperor penguin. Her short-sleeved uniform and short skirt looked like they kept her cool in the humid heat of early summer. Without her socks, the line of her long, slender, pale legs was unobstructed from view. She was the epitome of a high-school girl, without the piercings or embellishments that would make her stand out as belonging to a particular subculture. She’s super well-dressed, but not in a flashy way, y’know?! Sh-She’s smart, polite to everyone, and she’s an honor student! She’s just so perfect! Iroha constantly annoys Akiteru because she wants his attention, as she has romantic feelings for him. She constantly barges into his apartment unit and spends her time there, much to Akiteru's annoyance. Despite being annoying to Akiteru, she does know when to be serious, and supporting. when Akiteru isn't acting like himself when Cannary asked him what he can offer to the Alliance members so that they'll stay in the Alliance, this question casued Akiteru to constantly think about what he can offer the Alliance members, Iroha noticed that change in him, and decided to let him speak his mind which allowed him to work through the conflict. Ozuma is Iroha's brother, they live together at number 503 next to Akiteru Ōboshi and Sumire Kageishi. Despite being siblings it is said in the anime that Ozuma hadn't spoken to her in months. Surprisingly, Ozuma can't even notice his sister when she is wearing sunglasses and a hat.
