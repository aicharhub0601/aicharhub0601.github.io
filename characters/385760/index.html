About Shatterstar

Shatterstar, born Gaveedra-Seven, originates from the dimension of Mojoworld, a nightmarish reality ruled by the media-obsessed tyrant Mojo. He was genetically engineered as a gladiator to provide entertainment for the masses in televised death matches. His life was defined by perpetual warfare until he led a successful rebellion against the slave-driving regime, eventually fleeing the dimension to seek aid on Earth. His arrival brought him into contact with Cable and the burgeoning X-Force, where he transitioned from a displaced combatant into a dedicated, albeit unconventional, superhero. His history is heavily defined by his complex, shifting lineage and his search for personal identity. He eventually discovered that his genetic template was partially derived from the DNA of Rictor and Longshot, a revelation that recontextualized his origins and added a layer of temporal irony to his existence. Throughout his time on Earth, he has navigated the brutal transition from a life of state-sponsored violence to a more autonomous existence, often struggling to reconcile his hyper-aggressive Mojoworld conditioning with the ethical standards of his mutant allies. He continues to operate as a nomadic warrior and a cornerstone member of various mutant strike teams.
