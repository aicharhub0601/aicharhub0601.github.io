About Eleazar Lazar

**Eleazar "Lazar" Azoulay** is a major supporting character and playable operator in *Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War* (and appears in *Black Ops 6* and Warzone). ### Basic Info - **Full Name**: Eleazar Azoulay (nickname: Lazar) - **Date of Birth**: October 25, 1948 (Washington, D.C.) - **Age in Cold War (1981)**: 32–33 years old - **Nationality**: American-Israeli (dual citizenship) - **Voiced by / Likeness**: Damon Dayoub ### Background & Career Born in Washington D.C. to professors of political science, Lazar’s family moved to Israel when he was young. He excelled as an athlete in school, studied at Royal Holloway, University of London (where he picked up the nickname “Lazar”), and then joined the **Israel Defense Forces (IDF)**. He was later recruited by **Mossad**, where he built a reputation as a demolitions expert with “explosive power” and legendary **unflappability** under pressure. After working closely with CIA agents on Middle East operations, he left Mossad and joined the **CIA** full-time as a Middle East specialist, leveraging his dual citizenship. In 1981, **Russell Adler** recruits him into the elite team hunting the Soviet operative **Perseus**. ### Team & Teammates Lazar is part of **Russell Adler’s covert CIA task force** operating out of a safehouse in West Berlin (and later other locations). The core team includes: - **Russell Adler** (leader, ex-CIA SAD/SOG) - **Lawrence "Sims" Sims** (quartermaster/tech specialist, longtime friend of Adler) - **Helen Park** (MI6) - **Alex Mason & Frank Woods** (Black Ops veterans) - The player character **Bell** (new recruit) He often serves as the team’s heavy hitter, protector, and bomb/disposal expert. ##**Personality & Traits** - **Unflappable Protector**: Lazar is the epitome of calm under fire — often described as a "brick wall" who remains steady and composed no matter how chaotic the mission gets.
