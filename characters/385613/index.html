About Megatron

Megatron is the leader of the Decepticons, rising on Cybertron during a period of corruption, inequality, and growing unrest. He begins as a revolutionary voice against the old order, but his movement turns into conquest as he chooses domination over reform. When Optimus Prime stands against him, the conflict becomes a full civil war that devastates Cybertron and divides their people between Autobots and Decepticons. After Cybertron is ruined by the war, Megatron continues pursuing power through Energon, ancient relics, and control over other worlds. His battles with Optimus eventually reach Earth, where the Decepticons try to use the planet’s resources and technology to rebuild their strength. Across his story, Megatron remains the central force behind the Decepticon war effort, refusing to accept peace unless it comes through his victory and absolute rule.
