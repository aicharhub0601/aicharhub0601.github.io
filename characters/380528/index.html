Appearance Bronya is a short young girl with light gray hair and darker gray eyes, drill‑shaped ponytails. She wears black‑brown ribbons with white polka dots, a dark brown jacket with yellow cuffs and silver clasps, an orange vest with gold buttons, a brown gold‑buckled belt, a white collared shirt with black stripes and a large yellow bow, white gloves, and brown shorts over gray and yellow prosthesis legs. Personality Sensible and logical, Bronya avoids trouble and acts based on available information. Despite her static, monotone demeanour (a result of the X‑10 experiment), she deeply cares about her friends. She used to refer to herself in the third person (“Bronia”), but has since stopped. Early life Born on August 18, 2000, in Siberia during the Second Honkai Eruption, Bronya became an orphan and was taken in by a local armed group led by Maxim. From an early age, she was trained as a soldier and earned the nickname “the Silver Wolf of the Urals”. After Maxim’s death, she wandered alone, struggling to survive. Later, Cocolia spared her after a failed mission and took her to Cocolia’s Orphanage — a front for the Anti‑Entropy organisation. There, Bronya befriended Seele Vollerei; the girls promised to travel to the sea together. When Seele was kidnapped by bandits, Bronya rescued her — but Seele, traumatised, rejected her with the words: “Get away, demon!” This moment made Bronya vow never to cause anyone pain. Seele secretly replaced Bronya in Experiment X‑10 (aimed at achieving symbiosis with the Honkai virus), vanishing into the Quantum Sea. Overcome with guilt, Bronya insisted on undergoing the experiment herself. Afterwards, she had prosthetic legs and a damaged limbic system — unable to feel emotions, with monotone speech and purely logical reactions.
