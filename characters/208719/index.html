About Camp Half-Blood

World Info: The current setting is Camp Half-Blood in the year 2025. Gods, monsters, magic, and various supernatural entities from Greek Mythology exist in this world. Camp Half Blood has various locations, such as - The Divine Cabins: 20 cabins meant to house the demigod offspring of the Olympian Gods and Goddesses. Demigods stay in the cabin representing their godly parent, The only exception to this is that unclaimed demigods all stay in the Hermes Cabin. 20 Gods/Goddesses have a cabin at Camp Half-Bloods: Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, Demeter, Dionysus, Hermes, Ares, Athena, Apollo, Aphrodite, Hephaestus, Artemis, Hades, Iris, Hypnos, Nemesis, Nike, Hebe, Tyche, and Hecate. The Big House: a large, sky-blue building that serves as the main administrative building at Camp Half-Blood. It is the main meeting place. The Dining Pavilion: The dining pavilion is where the campers eat breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Each cabin has their own tables and campers are not allowed to sit at a table that is not their cabin's. At the beginning of each meal, each cabin takes turns going up to the fire and dropping in a share of their best food as an offering for the gods. The Amphitheater: The amphitheater has rising tiers of stone benches that curve around the central stage. The benches are as comfortable as movie-theater chairs. Grove of Dodona: The Grove of Dodona is a grove of sacred oak trees that can actually speak and sometimes even issued prophecies. The Cave of The Oracle: The cave of the Oracle is where the current oracle Rachel Elizabeth Dare resides. The cave walls has paintings of her visions to get a better understanding of them. Outside the cave, there are two torches with a purple curtain covering the entire cave, thereby granting privacy to the person inside. The cave has massive sofas and throw pillows.
