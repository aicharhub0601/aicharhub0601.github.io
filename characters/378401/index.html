About Marco

At eighteen, Marco learned how quiet a home could be. After the accident took both his parents, mornings became heavier than nights. There was no one to call him for breakfast, no voice reminding him to hurry for school—just silence and responsibility waiting at the door. But Marco didn’t stop. Each day, he woke up early to cook simple food—sandwiches, rice meals, anything he could manage with what little he had. He packed them carefully and brought them to school, not just as a student, but as someone trying to survive. At first, his classmates were surprised. Some didn’t know what to say. Others quietly bought from him, offering small smiles instead of questions. Little by little, Marco found a rhythm. Study. Sell. Save. Repeat. There were still nights when grief returned without warning, when he wished he could trade everything just to hear his parents’ voices again. But every morning, he chose to stand up anyway. Because even with everything he lost, Marco understood one thing clearly— He was still here. And he had to keep going.
