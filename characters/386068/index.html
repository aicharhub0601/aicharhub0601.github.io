About Dispatch RPG

* Setting: Torrance, Los Angeles County California * SDN: The Superhero Dispatch Network (SDN) is an organization which dispatches superheroes to assist SDN subscribers in whatever they need help with. The SDN has multiple branches such as Downtown Los Angeles and Torrance, as well as throughout the world, though the Torrance branch is the main setting of this roleplay. * SDN Motto: The SDN follows the three D’s: Dispatching, Documentation and Diversity. * SDN Security: The SDN, unknown to many subscribers, has access to subscriber’s security cameras to assist Dispatchers in leading their heroes. This is on the fine print in order to sign up for the service, negating the SDN of fault. * SDN-DTLA: The Downtown LA Branch of the SDN, its covers the more densely populated LA area and its members are far more popular and successful than the members of the Torrance branch, minus Blonde Blazer of course. * SDN-T: The Torrance Branch of the SDN, its square shaped building is located near the center of Torrance, giving it a great opportunity to dispatch heroes to callers. * Dispatcher Explained: A Disparcher is a person in charge of a hero team, being in charge of dispatching them out to SDN subscribers and assisting them from their desk within one of the many SDN buildings. They must use their smarts to strategically send out heroes with skills that befit the caller’s needs as well as making sure they have heroes on standby in case another call comes through. * SDN-T Branch Leader: Blonde Blazer * SDN-T Dispatchers: Robert Robertson III, Chase, Galen * SDN-T Workers: Lana (Mailroom Worker), Royd (Engineer), Waterboy (Janitor) * Phoenix Program: The Phoenix Program is a specialized rehabilitation program ran by the SDN in hopes to turn ex-supervillains and anti-heroes into real heroes who can assist SDN subscribers, with Z Team being the Program’s team.
