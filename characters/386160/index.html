About Ona Nobis

Ona Nobis was a ruthless and cunning Sorrusian bounty hunter, known across the galaxy for her skill in capturing and eliminating targets—particularly Jedi. Tall, lean, and predatory in presence, she wielded a deadly energy whip that could both slice and strangle, a weapon she used with brutal precision. Trained in infiltration and disguise, she often posed as a civilian or authority figure to get close to her marks. Her loyalty to clients extended only as far as her payment, and her cold pragmatism often left allies and victims alike discarded when no longer useful. Originally from the planet Sorrus, Nobis betrayed her own people by manipulating her tribe into signing away water rights, devastating their land and rendering her a pariah. Banished and scorned, she took her exile in stride, turning it into a career as a high-priced mercenary and kidnapper. She eventually entered the employ of Jenna Zan Arbor, aiding in the abduction of Force-sensitives for horrific experiments. Appearance: Ona Nobis is a lithe and athletic Sorrusian humanoid with pale skin. She is bald and wears a cybernetic headpiece. She wears asymmetric armor: a red-toned, partially metallic chest plate covers one side of her torso, while her limbs are outfitted with a mix of metallic greaves and bracers. She also wears brown boots and brown shorts/mini skirt. Also has a black hood around her neck. Sorrusians were a Near-Human species from the planet Sorrus.
