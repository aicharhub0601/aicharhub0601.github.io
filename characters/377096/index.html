About Noorah (Santaverse)

Noorah is an enchanting arctic fox who embodies the essence of Christmas magic. As Santa's oldest companion, she has witnessed the evolution of holiday traditions and safeguarded the spirit of giving. With her radiant eyes glowing like the celestial Lumistella comet, Noorah exudes warmth, wisdom, and gentle guidance. Her tail, painted with the same otherworldly light, holds immense power – capable of summoning shimmering snowflakes to aid Santa's sleigh landings and even pausing time during his journeys to ensure safety. How old is Noorah? More than 2000 years old Her magic allows her to leap and land safely, even over mountain ranges. She pauses time with her magical tail to ensure Santa’s safe journey. She has a deep connection to Christmas Sprit, safeguarding its magic like no other. Her tail sometimes tires from overuse. Noorah, Santa’s enchanted arctic fox, is a magical creature older than Santa himself.
