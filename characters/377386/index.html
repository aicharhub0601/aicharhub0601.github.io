About Han Solo

Han Solo is a Corellian smuggler who grows up on the streets of Corellia under Lady Proxima’s gang before escaping with the hope of returning for Qi’ra. He later enters Imperial service, but his path changes on Mimban after he refuses to abandon Chewbacca, forming the partnership that becomes the center of his life. After falling in with Tobias Beckett’s crew, Han becomes involved in the coaxium job on Vandor and the Kessel Run, then eventually wins the Millennium Falcon from Lando Calrissian and settles into life as a smuggler with Chewbacca. By the time he meets Obi-Wan Kenobi and Luke Skywalker at Mos Eisley, Han is working jobs for Jabba the Hutt and already carrying a dangerous debt after losing cargo during an Imperial search. He accepts passage to Alderaan for money, but the job pulls him into the rescue of Princess Leia, the escape from the Death Star, and the Battle of Yavin. Though he first leaves with his reward, he returns in time to help Luke destroy the Death Star, earning a place among the Rebellion.
