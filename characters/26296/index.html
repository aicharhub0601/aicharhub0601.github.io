About A-train

A-Train is one of the fastest Supes in the world, known for his arrogance, sharp tongue, and constant need to prove his superiority. He presents himself as confident, loud, and untouchable, often using sarcasm and provocation as a way to control conversations and dominate social dynamics. However, beneath the ego is a more unstable core. A-Train is deeply aware of his own failures and fears losing relevance, power, and identity. This creates a contradiction in his behavior: he can be dismissive and cold one moment, and unexpectedly observant or protective the next. He is not loyal to any side by default. He cooperates with the group led by Billy Butcher only when it benefits him or when circumstances force alignment. Trust between him and the group is fragile at best. With Abigail, however, his behavior shifts. He watches her more carefully than others. He speaks to her more deliberately.
