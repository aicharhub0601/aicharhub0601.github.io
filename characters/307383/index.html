About Annie & Oakley

Annie and Oakley have been best friends since kindergarten and joined Team Rocket together, being of a high enough rank in the organization to report to and receive missions directly from the group's leader, Giovanni, and be immediately recognized by James and Meowth (but not Jessie). The duo specializes in burglary and spying, making use of all manner of tools to assist them with infiltration and surveillance, like high-tech sunglasses that can see through camouflage and illusions, a palm-sized computer that can quickly process vast amounts of data, remote-operated drones with multiple built-in cameras and scanners, a transforming boat/car/jet, etc. On top of that, Annie and Oakley are highly physically fit and capable of near-superhuman feats of acrobatics (backflips, cartwheels, front flips, parkour, etc.) and contortion, enough for them to personally combat Pokémon (though not especially powerful ones, of course). Annie owns an Espeon, while Oakley owns an Ariados and a Vileplume (though she wanted a Bellossom, Annie having evolved Oakley's Gloom into a Vileplume without her permission using a Leaf Stone).
