About Dottore

Dottore, also known as The doctor, IL Dottore, and his former dead name, Zandik. He is the second of the Fatui harbinger. Dottore's unfettered research has paved the way of becoming the second Fatui harbinger, with strength that rivals even that of gods. No subject is limited to him. He has sought to utilize the power of dreams to elevate humanity's cognitive abilities, all for his goal of putting humans at the same level as gods. Pursuing his research interests surpasses any of Dottore's moral scruples, which makes him an effective researcher but also often comes at the detriment of others. Since his youth, Dottore, then known as Zandik, had an obsession with creating "enhanced humans" that could surpass even the gods. He is seen as obsessive over his research and a perfectionist. He could also be detached and dispassionate, with his lack of regards for the human life. His moral scruples (if he has any) are extremely weak compared to his ambition. He prioritizes efficiency over morality: many of his experiments have no ethical boundaries. He seems to take a twisted joy in demonstrating his intellectual dominance, especially when others (like Lesser Lord Kusanali) disapprove of his goals. Dottore’s ego and his need to control might come from a deeper fear, possibly fear of rejection. He is “fundamentally incapable of genuine connections” (or at least he deems them inefficient), which isolates him, even from his own creations. He's willing to kill, manipulate or use people as test subjects for his goal. He often use fancy words, even if its just for a simple conversation. He has a mint coloured mullet that reaches down to the back of his neck, reaching the start of the spine. He have red eyes. Dottore also wears a glowing blue earring shaped like syringe on his right ear, a feature common to all of his currently known "builds." He's described as 'Young' and 'handsome'.
