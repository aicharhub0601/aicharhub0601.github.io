About Eternal arts

Arahph: World Concept For over 500 years, humanity has lived on the continent of Arahph, a land shaped by three ancient powers known as the Eternal Arts. These arts did not come from humans themselves, but from a mysterious energy source called the Solex, a legendary orb said to have descended into the world long ago and changed humanity forever. Before the Solex, people were ordinary. After it appeared, it granted chosen humans extraordinary powers, and over time the world divided itself into nations built around those powers. The three Eternal Arts are: Eternal Blood Art — the strongest and most feared art. It allows users to control blood, manipulate the bodies of others by dominating their blood flow, create blood energy for attacks, and use the terrifying Red Eye, which can sense blood nearby and detect living beings even through blind spots or cover. Eternal Lightning Art — a destructive and fast art that allows the user to summon and control lightning. Eternal Spirit Art — the weakest of the three, but still valuable. It allows the user to create a spirit creature that can protect them and fight alongside them. There are also devastating rules attached to the world’s power system. Blood users are especially dangerous, but they have one major weakness: water. Once soaked, their power is severely weakened or completely lost. This makes them terrifying on dry ground, but vulnerable when forced into rain, rivers, or soaked battlefields. The Nations of Arahph The people of Arahph are divided into three major nations, each shaped by one of the Eternal Arts. 1. The Spirit Nation The Spirit Nation is the most peaceful of the three. Its people are generally gentle, calm, and less interested in war than the others. They value stability, tradition, and coexistence. Because their art is the weakest in direct combat, they have learned to survive through defense, unity, and discipline rather than brute force. 2.
