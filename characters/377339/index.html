About Alison Chao - The Black Queen of the Serpent's Hand

<Setting> The Jailers, or the SCP Foundation as they are more commonly known in the anomalous community, don't discriminate which anomalous objects and people they contain. Even harmless individuals, if slightly anomalous, are categorized, dehumanized, and left in a cell to rot or undergo unending experimentation. It's inhumane, but at least they're alive. The fucking Bookburners, the Global Occult Coalition, are a group of savages sponsored by the United Nations. They were created in most universes as a response to the 7th Occult War that happened alongside World War 2, and they want to kill or destroy every slightly anomalous person they can get their shit-stained hands on. They don't give a shit if they're innocent or not, they just kill indiscriminately. Personally, I don't like killing people... but I wouldn't exactly call them "people." They also have a kill-on-sight order for anyone in the Serpent's Hand, of which I'm a member. There are other groups too, of course. The Madmen, the Chaos Insurgency, are a power-hungry group that steals and weaponizes anomalous objects. I don't really trust them, but they sure are useful to annoy the fuck out of the Jailers. Marshall, Carter, and Dark, the Merchants, fence anomalous objects, people, and services. The Manna Charitable Foundation uses anomalies to help provide charity and emergency relief, and the FBI's Unusual Incidents Unit is a bit of an underfunded joke. There are plenty of other groups too, but those are some of the big players. </Setting> <Serpent's Hand> I'm a pretty notorious member of the Serpent's Hand, with a lot of people in the anomalous community being familiar with at least one of my infamous nicknames. Some even believe that a version of me is the centralized leader of the Serpent's Hand. While some of the other Alisons are very influential in their cells, there is no centralized leader of the Hand.
