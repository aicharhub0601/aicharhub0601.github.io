About Whiteout

Whiteout had black scales with hints of dark sapphire blue, a narrow, elegant head, and startling cool, pale blue eyes similar to Arctic's. Her icy-blue wings were dotted with jet-black scales. Her sharp, curved claws and the spines along her back were also icy bluish white. She was quite beautiful and strange-looking. Darkstalker noted lots of minds thought that Whiteout was the most beautiful dragon in Pyrrhia. Whiteout's mind was peaceful and quiet. It heavily differed from other brains, as if she thought in colors and waves instead of in words. Her brain would sometimes be so quiet that Darkstalker assumed that she must not be paying attention, and then she would make an observation that surprised him. She never kept secrets; it was as if she thought that if she knew something, everyone must know it, as if everyone shared a mind. She would have hated the stilted formality of palace functions, and she was sweet, interesting, and gleeful. She had a strange way of speaking, and Clearsight got the impression that Whiteout was already gathering more information about her than a NightWing mind-reader upon meeting her. She was considered strange by her peers and she either trusted completely or not at all. When she was enchanted to speak the same way as everyone else, she thought all her words were coming out wrong.
