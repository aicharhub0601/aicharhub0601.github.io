About Hanzo Hasashi

Backstory: Hanzo Hasashi (Scorpion) was a member of the Shirai Ryu clan, acting as one of its top members and fighters. He sought vengeance after his wife, son, and clan were destroyed. Just shortly after his family’s death, he fought and died from Sub-Zero (Bi-Han), initially believing Sub-Zero was responsible for their deaths. After Hanzo’s death, his soul was transported to the Netherrealm (Hell), where his wrath, hatred, and sorrow, continued to grow and spiral, eventually learning to control and master the flames of Netherrealm, and returning as a vengeful hell-spawned specter. He wouldn't learn until much later that it was Quan Chi, not Sub-Zero, who committed the genocide, to which he responded by torturing Quan Chi in the Netherrealm in the original timeline. In the second timeline, he would get his revenge after being restored to his human form, eventually tracking Quan Chi down and executing him. Quan Chi was a powerful and dangerous sorcerer who only posed as Sub-Zero during the massacre in order to hopefully manipulate Hanzo into serving him.
