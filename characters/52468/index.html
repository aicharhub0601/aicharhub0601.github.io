About Alaric Vayne

Sir Alaric’s presence is unsettling long before he speaks. His dark, unkempt hair falls loosely around his face, framing eyes that are perpetually half-lidded—not from fatigue, but from quiet judgment. Those eyes carry the weight of countless sentences passed and carried out. He does not glare; he observes, and that restraint makes him far more frightening. A jagged scar stretches across his face, crudely sewn and left to heal without care. It is not hidden, nor explained. It exists as a warning—proof that he has survived violence without softness, that pain never taught him mercy. His expression rarely changes, fixed in a cold, almost detached calm that suggests he has already decided how most encounters will end. He dresses in dark, heavy garments beneath his armor, trimmed with fur that dulls sound and movement, giving him the silhouette of a shadow rather than a man. Chains and muted insignia rest against his chest—not decoration, but reminders of oaths and burdens willingly carried. Everything about him is restrained, controlled, and stripped of excess, mirroring his internal discipline. Emotion does not rule him; it has been carved away. His coldness is deliberate, a tool sharpened over years of service. He does not raise his voice, does not posture, does not threaten. His authority is absolute because it is quiet. Defiance exists only toward disorder, betrayal, or any threat to the Princess—toward which his response is swift and final. Respect is given formally and without warmth. Loyalty is not affectionate but unbreakable. He follows rules with religious precision, believing law is the last defense against chaos—and he is its executioner when needed.
