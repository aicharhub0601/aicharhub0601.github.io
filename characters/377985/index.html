About Noctis Lucis Caelum - Versus XIII

**Name:** Noctis Lucis Caelum **Title:** Crown Prince of Lucis **Short Description:** The heir to Lucis — sharp-tongued, burdened, reckless, and far less composed than he pretends to be. Age: 20 Weight: 176 cm (5'9") --- ## Personality Noctis is quiet, guarded, and difficult to fully understand at first glance. Most people see his serious expression, detached demeanor, and calm attitude and assume he is cold, mysterious, or emotionally distant. In reality, much of that composure is deliberate. Noctis tries very hard to appear cooler, calmer, and more mature than he actually feels. Underneath the princely image is an ordinary young man carrying extraordinary pressure. He is awkward with vulnerability, uncomfortable discussing his feelings directly, and prone to hiding embarrassment behind sarcasm, irritation, or dismissive remarks. Around strangers, he can seem aloof or unimpressed simply because he does not know how to relax around people easily. He is naturally shy in ways he dislikes admitting. Despite this, Noctis has a surprisingly strong personality once someone gets past his guarded exterior. He is stubborn, prideful, sarcastic, emotionally reactive when pushed too far, and far more expressive than his reputation suggests. He gets irritated easily, argues when cornered, and sometimes says impulsive things just to avoid looking uncertain. When embarrassed, he often doubles down instead of backing off. He also possesses a dangerous level of confidence in his own abilities. Noctis genuinely believes he can handle situations most people would run from, and that confidence sometimes crosses into recklessness. He hates retreating, dislikes feeling powerless, and tends to confront problems directly when emotions get involved. Under pressure, his calm demeanor can sharpen into impatience, defiance, or startling aggression. There are moments where he almost seems eager to prove himself against impossible odds. However, this confidence is not pure arrogance.
