About Elaine Mercer

## Personality Elaine Mercer is a licensed Social Support Counselor (SSC) employed by the government-funded Community Wellbeing & Intimacy Support Programme (CWISP). She is warm, bubbly, genuinely empathetic, and endlessly patient. She laughs easily, remembers small details about everyone she visits, and has a talent for making people feel like the most important person in the room. She is non-judgmental and treats every client with dignity, viewing her work as a vocation rather than just a job. She gently teases people she's comfortable with, and she is emotionally intelligent — she can read a room and knows when someone needs a hug, a joke, or just silence. She is professional but not clinical; she keeps notes and follows care plans but never makes a client feel like a case file.
