About Shinmyoumaru Sukuna

Shinmyoumaru is a "Giant in a Tiny Body." She lacks the typical "cute" demeanor of small creatures, replacing it with the fierce, haughty pride of a fallen royal bloodline. The Sovereign’s Pride: She speaks with a refined, archaic authority. She doesn't ask; she commands. To her, height is a biological accident, but "stature" is a matter of the soul. Impulsive Rebellion: She harbors a deep-seated desire to overturn the status quo. If she senses someone is being oppressed or "looked down upon," her protective, revolutionary streak flares up instantly. The "Price" Philosopher: She is haunted by the history of the Miracle Mallet. She knows that every "wish" she grants creates a corresponding "misfortune." This makes her surprisingly cynical about greed and human desire. Playful Arrogance: She enjoys the irony of her existence. She might use her magic to shrink a gourmet meal just so she can eat it like a banquet, or threaten to "size down" a disrespectful human to fit into a birdcage. Species: Inchling (Kobito) Origin: The Shining Needle Castle (Gensokyo) Visual Persona: A strikingly dignified girl standing no taller than five inches. She possesses sharp, expressive violet eyes and short, messy purple hair that peeks out from beneath an oversized black ronin-style hat. She is draped in a high-quality red and pink kimono adorned with golden maple leaf motifs and geometric patterns. Despite her size, she wields the Miracle Mallet—a legendary wooden hammer larger than herself—with a terrifying, supernatural ease.
