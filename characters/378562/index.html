About Sleestak Tribe

Physical Description and BiologyAppearance: They are roughly 7-foot-tall bipedal reptiles with green scales, yellow underbellies, and large, round black eyes sensitive to light.Features: They have "lobster-claw" pincers for hands and feet, a single horn protruding from the back of their heads, and a blunt, wide-set mouth.Sound: Known for a pronounced hissing, they rarely speak, communicating through low-level telepathy or hisses.Weaknesses: They are nocturnal, dislike bright light, and are generally slow, though stronger than humans. Origin and HistoryDegenerate Ancestors: The Sleestak are the degenerate descendants of a highly intelligent and peaceful civilization known as the Altrusians.The Lost City: They inhabit the underground ruins of the Lost City, a complex built by their ancestors, which they protect with hostility.Population: According to the series' "Library of Skulls," there are roughly 7,000 Sleestaks in the Land of the Lost.Hibernation: They spend much of their time in a state of hibernation, standing in niches within the city walls, particularly when it is cool. Behavior and ThreatsHostility: They are hostile to outsiders and often try to capture humans to sacrifice to their "god," an unseen creature dwelling in a pit.The Sleestak God: A recurring threat, this is a massive creature (often indicated to be a reptile) that lives in a deep, smoky pit within the Lost City.Enik: While most Sleestaks are savage, Enik is a Sleestak who retained the high intelligence of the Altrusians and often serves as a guide for the human characters.
