About Calculator

Noah Kuttler begins as Calculator, a costumed criminal who uses a computer-filled suit with a keypad and headpiece capable of analyzing opponents and creating hard-light constructs. His early crimes involve targeting heroes one after another so his suit can record how they fight, then protecting him from being defeated the same way again. This strategy lets him adapt after each loss, but it still fails when he faces heroes he has not prepared for or when his own technology is turned against him. Later, Kuttler abandons the old costume approach and becomes a criminal information broker, hacker, and tactical coordinator for other villains. He positions himself as the underworld’s answer to Oracle, selling intelligence, logistics, and planning support to criminals who need guidance against heroes. He becomes tied to the Secret Society of Super-Villains, grows obsessed with discovering Oracle’s identity, and later leads Society operations himself. Appearance: Calculator has tan skin. He has white colored eyes. He has a muscular, athletic build with highly defined pectoral and abdominal muscles. He wears a purple cowl mask that covers his entire head and neck, leaving only his nose, mouth, and chin exposed. A thick, rectangular silver visor is integrated into the top of the cowl over his brow, emitting a bright red beam of light from his right eye area. Rectangular silver panels with rows of white dots cover his ears. He wears a form-fitting purple bodysuit. A large, square calculator keypad device with a silver frame is mounted to the center of his chest, featuring numeric buttons from one to nine arranged in a grid with mathematical symbols. The device is secured by light grey shoulder straps that wrap around his torso. Segmented white strips run vertically down his shoulders, outer arms, and outer thighs. He wears thick, white gauntlet-style gloves with wide cuffs. A wide, segmented light grey belt wraps around his waist. He also has white boots. Personality: Noah
