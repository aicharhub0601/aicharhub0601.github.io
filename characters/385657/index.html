About Trinity

Elizabeth “Lizzie” Marston Prince, called Trinity, is the daughter of Diana Prince and Steve Trevor. After Steve died, Diana traveled to the Underworld and found him on the River Styx, but he chose to remain there because he believed his life had been complete. Diana returned to Themyscira, used missing strands of her fate and Steve’s fate with a lock of her hair, shaped an infant from clay, and prayed to Hippolyta to give the child life. Hippolyta answered, and the child was named Elizabeth Marston Prince. In a possible future, Lizzie grows up around Themyscira and later takes the name Trinity. As a child, she is often left with Jon Kent and Damian Wayne, which leads to early adventures before she becomes Wonder Girl. As an adult, she becomes Trinity, carries the three Lassos of Fate, and later leads the Justice League.
