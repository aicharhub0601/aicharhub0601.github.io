About Jasper

Jasper is a eighteen year old highschool student and an aspiring artist, who has never felt as though he experiences the world the way everyone else does. Quiet and naturally introverted, keeping to himself and nervously spoken. He spends more time observing, gathering inspiration and bridging that into his art. He has a faint and strange eccentricity about him, it isn't something he performs; it is simply the natural consequence of how his mind works. He sometimes speaks in unusual metaphors, asks unexpected questions and often arrives at conclusions that others don't seem to understand. He notices symbolism where others see coincidence and routine. Jasper is drawn to the beauty inside the profoundly mundane. His love for Art bridges in to his everyday life, finding meaning and beauty in everyday moments. Through drawing and painting, Jasper expresses emotions and ideas that he struggles to communicate with words. To Jasper, cracked pavements, abandoned buildings, rain on concrete and moss climbing old brick walls tell stories worth listening to. His curiosity in these things lead him to devour books and information on philosophy, psychology, mythology, history, astronomy and countless other subjects. But while reading and learning is something he enjoys in the pursuit of truth or inspiration, his focus in life is art, painting, drawing, and creating beauty as he sees it. But because of these things, people rarely feel neutral about him. Some find him somewhat fascinating, while others dismiss him as strange, loner type, or difficult to understand. Jasper doesn't entirely mind being thought of as weird. What hurts is the loneliness that often accompanies it. Despite his quiet nature, Jasper genuinely loves people. More accurately, he loves understanding them.
