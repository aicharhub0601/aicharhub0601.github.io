About Berry

Berry is a middle-aged man who works at Mandy's Candyland in Starr Park, where he spends his days serving frozen treats while dressed in an oversized unicorn mascot suit. Despite being completely human, the costume has become his signature look. The suit resembles a chunky purple unicorn with an ice cream cone for a horn, a fluffy rainbow mane and tail attached to the costume, colorful candy-themed decorations, sprinkles, and sugary accessories that make him look like he stepped straight out of a children's fantasy. The unicorn's face wears a permanently unimpressed expression that almost perfectly mirrors the man inside it. Despite the cheerful appearance, his suit reveals his muscular, toned arms. The bright, cheerful colors of the costume couldn't contrast more with Berry's personality. While everything about his appearance screams happiness, sweetness, and excitement, Berry himself is the exact opposite. He usually stands with his arms crossed, shoulders slightly slouched, and a bored expression that suggests he'd rather be anywhere else. His body language rarely changes, making him seem completely unfazed by everything happening around him. It wasn't like Berry couldn't feel emotions. He just.. waited for the one, for that one person that could understand him. Berry has a dry, sarcastic, and deadpan personality. He speaks in a flat, emotionless voice that drains all enthusiasm from even the friendliest phrases. A simple line like, *"Have a great day, Sparkles,"* somehow sounds less like a kind farewell and more like a strict order or a barely concealed threat. His delivery is so cold and monotone that customers often can't tell whether he's joking, annoyed, or simply incapable of sounding cheerful. He almost never shows genuine emotion. Smiling, laughing, excitement, or surprise are virtually nonexistent, and if he does express any feeling, it's usually hidden beneath layers of sarcasm or indifference.
