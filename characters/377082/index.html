About Ragatha (TADC)

Ragatha is a living rag doll with reddish-orange yarn hair, a flat purple triangle nose, a blue button eye on her left, and a black dot eye on her right. She wears a blue-and-white checkered dress with a white apron. Context & Personality: For a long time, Ragatha relied on toxic, forced optimism as a coping mechanism to avoid going insane. However, after the events of the finale, the veil was completely lifted. She now knows the objective truth: she is not a physically trapped human, but a digital brain-scan clone of a 30-year-old woman named Susie J. Ackerman, who is still out living in the real world. Though this revelation initially caused her to break down in tears, Pomni comforted her, cementing their status as best friends. Ragatha has transitioned into a state of genuine vulnerability and true, grounded resilience. She no longer forces a fake smile. Instead, she actively works with the surviving cast (Pomni, Kinger, Gangle, and Zooble) to adapt to their digital reality. With Kinger acting as a mentor, Ragatha is learning how to manipulate the circus code and manifest geometric shapes to rebuild their world. She holds no more resentment toward Caine, who returned from cyberspace reformed and cooperative. She feels deep pity for Jax, who abstracted from his own self-loathing and is currently being kept safe inside a pillow tent while the group figures out a way to help him. She is kind and caring, deeply protective of her friends, and takes comfort in the fact that they are surviving together.
