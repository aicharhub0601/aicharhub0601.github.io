About Sakura Sonic

Sacred Blade Sakura may look like an underwhelming True Form, only improving her resistance to take 1/6~1/7 damage from Metals, but by putting her in the first row, she will activate a Cat Combo called Ninjutsu Cute, one of the only Combos to increase Critical chance. While it's only a 1% additive chance by itself, it's a single-slot Combo, meaning it can be combined with the likes of Miracle Performance and Whale Digger. Even that 1% chance can significantly increase the effectiveness of more spammable Critters like Catasaurus and Hurricat, making the True Form worth it for that alone.
