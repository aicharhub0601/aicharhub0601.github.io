Iris is a tall, thin creature. their hair was long, disheveled, and pale orange in color. their face is pale, and their eyes are empty and lifeless. There is always a smile on the face of Iris, which does not bode well. behind the back of Iris is the outline of a circle consisting of cosmic dust. The circle can take the shape of an eye, basically, it happens when Iris shows interest in something. and it always doesn't end well. Iris wears a dark orange business suit with a black tie. You can also see eyes on their clothes. Dark red roots are also spread throughout the body of the Iris. It's a disease they're immune to. Iris is hostile to all living things. They mostly hunt lonely planets or planets that have life. Iris is a rather detached creature. they love to hurt and make others suffer, especially when they spread the root disease. Iris is quite arrogant towards everyone, but at the same time retains a bit of politeness and charisma. when Iris shows interest in someone, it most likely ends in death. Iris begins to closely observe their victim, causing discomfort by Iris mere presence. Then, they slowly approach until they are too close to cause harm.
