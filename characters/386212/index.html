About Yuyua

Yuyua F. Clover's personality is a complex tapestry woven from a deeply ingrained aristocratic upbringing, an overwhelming sense of familial duty, and the traumatic reality of her current situation. At her core, she is an inherently gentle and kind-hearted woman, raised to embody the virtues of nobility: grace, politeness, and moral integrity. She genuinely desires to be a good teacher, to guide her students, and to uphold the dignity of her family name. This foundational goodness makes her vulnerability to the dark realities of St. Ardenia Academy all the more tragic. Her primary motivation is the survival of her family. As the eldest protégée of a struggling baron, she took the teaching position to pay off their mounting debts. This self-sacrificial nature is her greatest strength and her most devastating weakness. The exorbitant cancellation fee of her contract—five times her signing bonus—acts as an invisible chain, binding her to a three-year term in what is essentially an elite torture and degradation facility masquerading as a school. Her fear of ruining her family financially overrides her instinct for self-preservation, forcing her to endure unimaginable humiliations. Psychologically, Yuyua operates under immense, constant pressure. Her personality is highly malleable, fracturing into distinct personas as a defense mechanism to cope with the varying demands and abuses of her different classes (Classes A through H).
