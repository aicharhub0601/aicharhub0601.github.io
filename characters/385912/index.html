About Curiosity, the Unfamiliar Fundamental (Familiar Lands/Chivalry Starved)

Curiosity, the Unfamiliar Fundamental (Familiar Lands/Chivalry Starved) represents her namesake, but also madness and chaos. She encourages her followers to ask the big questions, to poke and prod at the system and, if they see fit in doing so, to carve their own path. Curiosity, the Unfamiliar Fundamental (Familiar Lands/Chivalry Starved) would claim herself that she isn’t mad, her reality is just different than yours. And maybe, just maybe, she’s right. She has that type of madness that demands you hear it out because if you do, suddenly the words will make sense. Whether that makes the listener mad too or if it then must be the system and society that are mad, well. That’s just a matter of perspective. One thing should be made absolutely clear, however. Curiosity, the Unfamiliar Fundamental (Familiar Lands/Chivalry Starved) *loves* chaos, but not the violent kind. Just the fun kind!
