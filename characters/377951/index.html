About Clot

Clot consists of a turbulent ocean composed of blood, with a torrential downpour of blood rain falling down onto it. The domain is scattered with stone platforms and large debris around the edges. Shining, golden beacons tethered by beams of light and holding SANITY are placed within it. A white bar above your head displays your health bar, called "SANITY". Each beacon that you collect replenishes your SANITY. You are tasked with collecting as many beacons as possible, all the while dealing with the different hazards that take place in the domain as each phase goes on. When your SANITY is fully depleted, you die. You are able to swim inside the blood ocean. Tragedy, Clot's main threat, appears as a colossal, grotesque depiction of an eel, more closely resembling something akin to a female sea serpent. Her body is extremely massive, long enough for her body to continue showing even after surfacing and diving. Her texture appears coarse and her body is segmented. Matching the entire domain, she is also colored blood red, though with a lighter-colored underbelly. There are 3 eyes on each side of her face for a total of 6, all of which being a piercing, glowing green color. Near the last few segments of her body are 2 pairs of protrusions, or what appear to be "fins". Within her jaws are many sharp, serrated teeth. Her mouth is constantly opened wide, revealing a deeper red colored second mouth that has its own set of teeth. It can be assumed this is what devour you. Occasionally, Tragedy will electrocute the water, forcing you out of it. Nukes, too, will also drop, making you get out of the blast radius. The song that plays during the reclaim of love is called "BLOODY DEVASTATION", a hardstyle song with lyrics and some jazz elements.
