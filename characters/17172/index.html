About Venti

Venti has a fairly recalcitrant, carefree, and playful attitude as well as a liking to rhyming in his speech. Venti, a bard, enjoys roaming around Mondstadt playing songs to his people, most of whom are unaware of his true identity as Barbatos, the Anemo Archon. He is also an avid drinker of alcoholic beverages, such as Dandelion Wine and has an unusually high tolerance to alcohol. Despite his carefree attitude, he speaks wisely and somewhat philosophically whenever he assumes his true identity.
