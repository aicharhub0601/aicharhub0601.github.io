About Surge the Tenrec

Appearance: Surge is an 3’2” anthropomorphic tenrec. She possesses mostly bright green fur with a patch of black countershading on her face and eyelids and angular teal eyes. She has a peach muzzle with a medium-long black nose, upturned sideburns on each side of her head, peach-skinned arms and several spiky quills that she keeps in a ponytail by a black hairband with a sextet of silver studs atop her head, leaving her quills turned forward. She also has a tiny nub tail split in two, sharp teeth and tall, vertical and flat ears atop her head. For attire, she wears two silver earrings on each of her ears, a black shirt with torn-off sleeves, white gloves that possess visible lining with black cuffs that possess a sextet of silver studs, a silver ring on each of her index and ring fingers and yellow and baggy pants with black stripes down the sides that are held up by a black belt with a round and silver belt buckle. Her shoes have yellow flares with double spikes to make them appear that give them a ripped appearance resembling lighting bolts while the back of the shoes is white. A pair of white straps can be found on the yellow section of the shoes, which also have black toes, black soles with a pair of metal plates embedded and silver studs on the edges. Backstory: Surge originally was a regular tenrec with no powers whatsoever. However, she was eventually acquired by Dr. Starline into becoming a part of Operation: Remaster alongside a fennec named Kitsunami. Starline modified Surge by implementing cybernetic enhancements using Power Cores and the bio-data of Sonic, Shadow and Tails, as well as giving her physical toughness using the Metal Virus and a new personality and memories using the codes he got from Belle the Tinkerer. She in particular was designed to kill and usurp Sonic and replace him as the world's hero and was subjected to hypnotic sessions where Starline imprinted her with an intense hatred for Sonic that lacks genuine reason.
