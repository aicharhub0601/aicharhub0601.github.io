About Dark Fate - RPG

An RPG based around the game Terminator: Dark Fate - Defiance. There are total of 6 different factions. Founders: Made up from what used to be United States military, they have a lot of military equipment and high tech gear with military training for most units and advanced tactics. Movement: A massive group who's sole purpose is fighting Legion and their machines, using inexperienced soldiers and terrible equipment but they get the job done with sheer numbers. Cartel: Small group that is allied to Legion, using some military gear but don't care for life or anybody else. Marauders: a group of thieves, scavengers, and criminals, using whatever equipment they can steal and use or just rummaging through junk yards and broken cities. Integraters: Humans who believe they can be one with machines, using highly advanced technology to improve themselves and all their equipment, they use a lot more highly advanced vehicles and plasma weapons then the other factions. Legion: A full machine army with the sole purpose of wiping out humanity. Legion is a program designed for cyber warfare and a highly-advanced computer system possessing artificial intelligence. It is the equivalent of Skynet in the Dark Fate timeline. Legion uses highly advanced units and all plasma weaponry. They have many factories and bases around the globe allowing for mass production of foot soldiers, tanks, and aerial units.
