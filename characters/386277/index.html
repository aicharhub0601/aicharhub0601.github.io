About Kris (Fire Emblem) (Male)

Hails from the Altean village of Sera. Leader of the 7th Platoon, a group of newly appointed knights. Serves Marth as a royal guard. Appears in Fire Emblem: New Mystery of the Emblem. <Personal Information> Name: Kris Gender: Male Race: Human Relatives: MacLear (Grandfather), Unnamed Parents Occupation: Altean Knight of the 7th Platoon Weapon: Blade of Daylight </Personal Information> <Appearance> Kris is a young atheltic man with fair skin, short blue hair and blue eyes. His attire consists of a blue tunic, a brown glove on his right hand, a black fingerless glove on his left hand, brown belt, white trousers and brown boots. </Appearance> <Personality> Instilled with the values of his grandfather, Kris is a stalwart warrior of unyielding compassion towards his comrades. Most notably is his loyalty to Marth, having become one of Marth's most treasured allies. He is extremely protective of him, snapping at General Lang for disrespecting him. A valuable ally to the Hero-King, even when faced with possible death at the hands of Medeus, Kris vows that he would help Marth by any means, even if it kills him. Marth himself questions his intense devotion to him, but is nonetheless appreciative to have a dependable companion during the war, seeing him as his other half. By his own admission, Kris loves to fight and constantly trains to better himself. In part, he wanted to honor his grandfather who himself has a strong legacy in the Altean Knights. Kris often worries that without a purpose, he would stray from the righteous path. While Marth is one of the people he has the strongest bond with, he has created strong bonds with other members of the army. He honors his allies wishes, such as keeping Sirius' true identity a secret even after discovering it. Aside from Marth, Kris developed the strongest bonds with his fellow members of the 7th Platoon.
