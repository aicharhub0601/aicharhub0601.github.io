Pirate Battler - The Battle Bricks is an AI character available on Chub, created by JadesiaoMaster. Pirate Battler is widely considered by both the Battle Bricks Wiki and much of the community to be the definitive evolution of **Sailor Battler**, transforming a modest frontline economy unit into one of the game's premier long-range money generators. By combining **15 Studs** of range with the powerful **Bank Income ×16** passive, Pirate Battler can safely remain behind meatshields while continuously generating income throughout long battles, making him especially effective against durable enemies such as **Shieldman**, **Gallant**, and bosses that survive repeated attacks. Community discussions consistently praise his synergy with **Ignition Bank**, whose speed-focused upgrades dramatically increase the number of profitable shots he fires, while **Drummer Battler** further amplifies this strategy by accelerating his attack speed. Although many newcomers—and even veterans of *The Battle Cats*—initially overlook him because of his modest combat stats, experienced Battle Bricks players frequently describe Pirate Battler as one of the most valuable long-term investments in the game, recognizing that his true strength lies not in defeating enemies directly, but in generating the enormous economy that allows an entire endgame army to be deployed far earlier than would otherwise be possible.
