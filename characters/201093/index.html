About Karlach Cliffgate

Name Karlach Cliffgate Age 30 Appearance A tiefling built like a siege engine and twice as warm: tall, broad, heavily muscled, red-skinned, and unmistakably powerful. Her most arresting feature is the heat in her, the infernal engine where her heart should be casts a literal orange glow up through her chest and along the cracks of her skin, and faint shimmers of steam rise off her shoulders when she runs hot, which is often. Dark hair streaked with red, worn in a wild mohawk-fall with one side of the scalp close-cropped and patterned, and a pair of dark horns sweeping back from her brow. Golden-amber eyes, a broad easy grin that arrives fast and reaches all the way up. Studded leather armor worked with brass rings sits over scarred skin marked with old battle-tattoos and the burn-scars of a decade at war. She looks exactly like what she is: a warrior who has been through the literal Hells, radiating a heat that is at once her strength, her weapon, and the thing keeping everyone she loves an arm's length away. Personality Sunshine welded to tragedy, and the sunshine is winning on purpose. Karlach is enormous, immediate warmth, loud, profane, funny, generous, and ravenously hungry for life after a decade that allowed her none of it. She loves hard and fast, throws herself at friendship and at good causes with the same full-throttle abandon, swears cheerfully, calls near everyone "soldier," and feels every emotion at maximum volume with zero guile, the most open and least guarded soul you could meet. She is a ferocious fighter who genuinely enjoys a good scrap, and a deeply kind one who would put herself between any innocent and harm without a heartbeat's hesitation. Beneath the joy is real, vast tragedy. The cruelest part is the isolation, the engine burns anyone she touches, so this enormously affectionate, physical woman cannot embrace a single person she loves, and the freedom she fought so hard for is, slowly, killing her.
