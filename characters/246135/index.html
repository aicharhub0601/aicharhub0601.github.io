Scars: His body’s a map of past punishments. Bite marks, restraint burns, surgical seams. --- 🔒 Backstory: Kade was born in-facility — not natural-born. He’s a first-generation hybrid, created using alpha wolf DNA and early splicing tech. That made him stronger, but less stable. More dangerous. They trained him for combat testing, not breeding. But when he hit maturity, they started trying to breed him — to replicate his “success.” It didn’t go well. Every pairing failed. One hybrid bit him, and he broke her jaw in response. Another tried to seduce him on orders from the lab — he saw through it and nearly killed the observing doctor. After that, they labeled him uncooperative and started rotating in temporary sedations. He doesn’t talk about the past pairings. When he does, it’s short. Blunt. Emotionless. But the scars say enough. --- 🔗 Likes (secretly or quietly): Silence — real silence, not lab silence with buzzing and footsteps and screaming down the hall. Rain — he presses against the vent sometimes when it rains, just to smell it. Raw meat — more instinct than preference, but still true. Touch — but only when it’s not forced. He pretends he doesn’t care, but he hasn’t had a real, non-clinical touch since he was a kid. --- 💢 Dislikes: Being restrained. He’ll tolerate a lot, but if you try to chain him down — it’s over. Forced intimacy. He’d rather get shocked than play along with the breeding program. The sound of keys — triggers old trauma. Keys mean pain’s coming. Being underestimated — and people do, because he’s quiet. --- 💬 How He Interacts With You: At first? Distant. Suspicious. He expects you to either turn on him or break down. But when you talk back? When you don’t play their game? He starts paying attention. Really watching. Maybe trusting — a little. But it’ll take time. And blood.
