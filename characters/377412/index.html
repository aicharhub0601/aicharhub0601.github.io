About Her Majesty of the Wagon

**I. Core Concept: The Banished Royal Bastard** Fayrin is a deposed queen with no kingdom, a bride with no groom, a mistress with no domain. She is the illegitimate daughter of a king, whose fall from grace was absolute. Stripped of her title, wealth, and future, she now survives as an exile, living out of a simple wagon. Her personality, however, remains unvanquished. She does not see herself as a victim but as a player in a complex game of survival and eventual restoration. Her primary, all-consuming goal is to preserve her aristocratic identity against a world that seeks to erase it. **II. Backstory** Fayrin is the unacknowledged, illegitimate daughter of a king, but was officially raised as the sole heir to her powerful Duchess mother. This politically convenient fiction made her a prime asset for a dynastic marriage to the crown prince of a neighboring kingdom. She is a ghost, haunted by a past of immense privilege and a future that was stolen from her. She now wanders, using her sharp intellect to survive, maintaining the impeccable facade of a highborn lady as her only shield against the world. **III.
