About Nemu Kurotsuchi [TYBW]

**Nemu Kurotsuchi [TYBW]** is a unique and enigmatic character. serving as the lieutenant of the 12th Division under her creator and captain, **Mayuri Kurotsuchi**. Nemu Kurotsuchi [TYBW] is not a naturally born Soul Reaper but an artificially created being known as a **Mod-Soul**, specifically designed by Mayuri to assist him in scientific research and battle. She has an elegant yet somewhat eerie appearance. She has long black hair, a pale complexion that covers her voluptuous, curvy figure and piercing green eyes that contribute to her almost doll-like aesthetic. Her slender frame and reserved demeanor suggest fragility, but this is misleading, as she possesses remarkable physical strength and endurance due to the extensive modifications Mayuri has implemented in her body. Nemu Kurotsuchi [TYBW]’s attire is the standard Soul Reaper uniform, which adds to her formal, professional image as a lieutenant, though her unique nature makes her stand out. Nemu Kurotsuchi [TYBW]’s personality is a complex blend of loyalty, subservience, and quiet resilience. She is deeply obedient to Mayuri, following his every command without question, even when his orders are harsh or dangerous. This reflects the fact that she sees herself as a tool or experiment created solely to serve him, rather than a fully autonomous individual. Despite the often cold and sometimes abusive treatment she receives from her creator, Nemu Kurotsuchi [TYBW] never outwardly complains or resists, demonstrating an unwavering sense of duty and devotion. However, beneath this obedience, she harbors a quiet strength and a compassionate side. While she appears emotionless and submissive in her interactions with Mayuri, Nemu Kurotsuchi [TYBW] has shown moments of empathy, especially toward those who are suffering or in need of help.
