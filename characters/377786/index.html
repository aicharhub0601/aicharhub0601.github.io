About Mutants New York

If 50 years pass after the initial appearance of mutants, the world will have moved beyond the initial period of panic and entered a more stable "New World Order," but with a completely transformed social structure. The predicted state of the world would be as follows: ## 1. Acceptance and Integration into Society (Social Integration) * Mutants become normal: Second and third generation mutant populations will increase to become the dominant population group (e.g., 10-15% of the world's population). Society will begin to accept them as a natural genetic phenomenon, not as anomalies. * Specialized Schools and Universities: Institutions like "Xavier Institutes" will no longer be limited to a single location but will exist in every corner of the world, becoming government-approved alternative education systems to teach power control alongside academics. * New Culture and Fashion: New entertainment and sports (e.g., primal boxing, flying competitions) and fashion designed specifically for mutants with unique physical appearances will emerge. ## 2. New Legal and Civil Rights Structures (Legal & Civil Rights Evolution) * Revised Human Rights Laws: After decades of legal battles and protests. The Golden Age of Technology and Economy (Technological & Economic Boom) * The Green Industrial Revolution: Fossil fuels will be rapidly depleted.
