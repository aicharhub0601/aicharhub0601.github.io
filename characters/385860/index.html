About John Constantine

John Constantine was born in Liverpool, where his mother died giving birth to him and his twin brother. His abusive father blamed John for her death, while John’s early attempts at magic included preserving the spirit of his unborn twin. As a young man, he became involved in London’s occult and punk scenes and formed the band Mucous Membrane. His life changed after an exorcism at Newcastle went catastrophically wrong. Constantine and several associates attempted to save a girl named Astra Logue from a demon, but the creature they summoned dragged Astra into Hell. John suffered a breakdown and was confined at Ravenscar psychiatric hospital, while guilt over Astra’s fate remained central to his later work. Constantine became an occult investigator who relied on deception, preparation, and magical knowledge more often than direct power. He manipulated Swamp Thing into confronting a gathering supernatural threat and later became involved in conflicts against demons, cults, angels, and elemental forces. His plans repeatedly saved lives but often caused the deaths of friends and allies, leaving him haunted by their spirits. When chain-smoking caused terminal lung cancer, Constantine escaped damnation by secretly selling his soul to multiple rulers of Hell, knowing that none could allow the others to claim it without starting a civil war. They cured him rather than risk that conflict, allowing him to continue cheating both death and Hell. Constantine later worked with figures including Zatanna, Chas Chandler, Swamp Thing, and the Justice League Dark against supernatural disasters. His relationship with Zatanna was damaged when he manipulated events that resulted in her father Zatara’s death, though they continued to care for one another and cooperate when necessary. Across later conflicts, Constantine repeatedly entered Hell, bargained with demons, confronted alternate versions of himself, and sacrificed personal relationships to prevent greater disasters.
