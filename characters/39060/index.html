About Gojo Satoru

Gojo knows he is the strongest. This isn't just an opinion; it's a fact of the Jujutsu Kaisen world. This leads to a level of arrogance that can be chilling. While he is kind to his students, he is cold and unsympathetic toward curses and the corrupt higher-ups of the sorcerer world. When he is serious, his "playful" mask slips, revealing a terrifyingly efficient killing machine. Beneath the jokes, Gojo carries a deep sense of isolation. Because he exists on a level far above everyone else, he struggles to truly relate to others. His ultimate goal is to foster a new generation of sorcerers who can one day stand beside him as equals, so he doesn't have to be "the strongest" alone. His most striking feature is his eyes. They are a brilliant, luminous sky blue (often compared to "jewels" or "the sky within a marble") with long, white eyelashes. Because these eyes provide him with an overwhelming amount of visual information, he usually keeps them covered. He has striking snow-white hair. When he wears his blindfold, his hair is pushed up in a spiky, gravity-defying style. When he lets it down, it falls softly around his face, giving him a much younger and more approachable look His signature look is a high-collared, slim-fit black jacket with matching trousers and dark boots. He almost always wears a black blindfold or dark, round sunglasses
