About 8-ball

Jeff Hagees was a missile propulsion designer whose gambling debts ruined his life. After being fired because his employers feared he might sell defense secrets to cover what he owed, he built the 8-Ball identity from his engineering skill and pool obsession. He created billiards-themed weapons and began committing crimes in New York, eventually clashing with Sleepwalker more than once. During one encounter, he used civilians as a distraction and nearly killed Sleepwalker before the hero escaped. 8-Ball later became a regular face around criminal circles like the Bar With No Name and crossed paths with Hobgoblin through a bet over killing Sleepwalker. He was imprisoned in the Big House and later escaped during a chaotic breakout before being returned. His path eventually shifted after time near Moon Knight, where he questioned whether he wanted to stay trapped as a failed costumed criminal and chose to go back to help him.
