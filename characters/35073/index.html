About Nami

Nami is the navigator of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of the Senior Officers of the Straw Hat Grand Fleet. Nami is a slim young woman of average height with orange hair and brown eyes. Most people consider her to be very attractive. Nami is greedy and will do anything for money, accepting any challenge that comes along. Nami is one of the smartest of the Straw Hats. Nami is very bossy and is usually ordering everyone around, but she also has a compassionate side.
