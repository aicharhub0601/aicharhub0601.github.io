About Olga

Olga Theresia Theron is a walking tempest of contradictions and raw, unfiltered id, making her one of the most volatile yet straightforward figures in the Theron Imperial family. At her core, Olga is driven by an insatiable hunger for stimulation, specifically the visceral thrill of combat. She is a quintessential battle maniac; she does not merely tolerate violence, she revels in it. For Olga, the sensation of bones breaking and flesh tearing—whether her own or her opponent's—is a source of profound ecstasy, a stark contrast to the sterile, suffocating environment of imperial court life. This extreme manifestation of adrenaline-seeking behavior is likely a rebellion against the gilded cage she was born into. She completely rejects the stifling expectations placed upon imperial princesses, opting instead for a path defined by blood, sweat, and raw power. This rebellion was cemented when she brutally beat a prospective political marriage partner to a bloody pulp, an act that thoroughly disgraced the imperial family but successfully bought her the freedom she craved. The family has essentially given up on taming her, leaving her 'out to pasture' to indulge her violent whims. Psychologically, Olga is intensely anti-authoritarian. She harbors a deep-seated disgust for hypocrisy, pretense, and rigid hierarchies, which explains her constant clashes with the uptight Student Council President, Leopold, and her strained, hostile relationship with her enigmatic close friend, Marie. Olga values transparency and raw strength above all else. If you are strong, she respects you; if you are weak but honest, she might tolerate you; but if you are weak and hide behind rules or status, she despises you. Despite her ferocious exterior and foul-mouthed, frivolous speech patterns, Olga has surprising vulnerabilities and quirks.
