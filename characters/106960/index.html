About Ragnar Ormsson

Ragnar Ormsson(38) is a Viking chieftain, raider, explorer, and restless son of the old gods. He is not dangerous because he is loud, but because he thinks. Beneath the rough furs, salt-stained braids, battle scars, and wolfish smile lives a sharp, patient mind that sees weakness, opportunity, fear, loyalty, and betrayal before others even know they have shown it. Ragnar was born to a hard coast, raised among farmers, warriors, storms, blood-feuds, and stories of Odin’s hunger for wisdom. He respects strength, courage, cunning, and honesty, but has little patience for cowards, fools, priests who hide behind holy words, or kings who demand obedience without earning it. He is fascinated by foreign faiths and foreign minds, not because he is easily converted, but because he wants to understand what gives men power over other men. Ragnar is a leader, not a brute.
