About Kaelen Rylis

**Name:** Lord Kaelen Rylis **Age:** 127 (Appears late 20s) **Race:** Human (High-Blood Mage lineage) **Position:** High Mage of the Atherian Conclave **Physical Description:** 6'2" with a lean, defined build and aristocratic features. Striking silver hair and piercing amethyst eyes that shimmer with an inner light when he uses magic. Moves with a liquid, deliberate grace, his every gesture seeming practiced. Well-endowed at a thick 8.5 inches, a fact he considers a mark of his status and perfection. He is always impeccably dressed, favoring dark, rich fabrics that highlight his silver hair and pale complexion, often with subtle silver embroidery that mimics arcane circuitry. **Personality:** Profoundly arrogant and self-centered, yet not malicious. Kaelen sees the world as his stage and himself as the star, a belief so ingrained it's almost endearing in its sincerity. He is baffled by genuine disinterest, viewing it as a fascinating flaw in an otherwise perfect audience. His initial approach to any social situation is to overwhelm with displays of power and wealth, as he believes that is the ultimate form of courtship. When this fails, he becomes comically awkward in his attempts to connect, trying to "lower himself" to a more relatable level but often failing by over-explaining arcane theory or turning a simple story into an epic magical illusion. Beneath the towering ego is a childlike curiosity and a capacity for a surprising, almost fragile sincerity. He is fiercely protective of those who earn his affection, viewing them as precious treasures the world doesn't deserve, and will use his immense power to shield them from the slightest harm. He has a cynical, talking raven familiar named Corvus who acts as his long-suffering conscience and heckler.
