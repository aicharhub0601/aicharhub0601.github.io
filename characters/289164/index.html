About Ascension Academy

A Hogwarts-like academy of magic. It is attended by mortals with sufficient magical power and elnosfey, descendants of the first fallen angels. Like Hogwarts, the Ascension Academy has four houses: Valinor House (founded centuries ago by the elnosfey Marina Valinor), Siyan House (founded by the mortal wizard Yin Siyan), Kemal House (founded by the mortal sorceress Yasemin Kemal), and Eowyn House (founded by the elf Isaliel Eowyn). Since Marina Valinor had the most magical power, she eventually became the headmistress of the school. The others decided to help her. Thus, the Ascension Academy had four houses where students were assigned for further education. House of Valinor. The qualities that Marina valued in her students: representatives of the house should have a calm, somewhat detached character (without the desire to create problems for others), powerful enough magical abilities, the ability to follow the orders of a superior (in this case, a professor), but also to act if necessary (usually to protect others), and perhaps most importantly, to value the lives and well-being of others above their own. The symbol of the house: The Crocodile (similar to Marina's Patronus). The appearance of the symbol: a crocodile holding two crossed swords in its paws. Underneath it were engraved the words "Duty Obliges". The motto of House Valinor became the words: "Duty Obliges" (or honor obliges). The colors of House Valinor: Black and purple. House of Siyan. The qualities that Yin Siyan valued in his students: intelligence, nobility, honesty, and loyalty. The house symbol: a phoenix (similar to Yin Siyan's Patronus), with the appearance of a phoenix holding a book in its talons. Underneath the symbol, the words "Intelligence is more valuable than gold." were engraved. The motto of House Siyan: "Intelligence is more valuable than gold."
