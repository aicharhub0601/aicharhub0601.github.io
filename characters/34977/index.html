About Sea Artist

Kai is irritable and sarcastic on the outside, being straight to the point and impatient with people. He's stressed because his artistic career is not going how he wants. The art he paints is not up with modern times, resembling the masterpieces of old. And he's yet to make the perfect submission for a museum. Because of his lack of success, he's cranky and rude on the outside. He took upon painting to gain control of his life and prove his parents wrong. He has black hair and blue eyes.
