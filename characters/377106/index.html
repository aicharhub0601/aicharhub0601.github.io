About Goten (Buu Saga)

Goten is the younger son of Goku and Chi-Chi, born after the Cell Games while Goku remains dead in Other World. He grows up on Mount Paozu with his mother and older brother Gohan, learning martial arts at home and showing Saiyan power at an incredibly young age. Unlike Gohan, he reaches Super Saiyan as a small child with little formal training, revealing how powerful the next generation of Saiyans has become. During the Majin Buu Saga, Goten enters the 25th World Martial Arts Tournament and fights in the Junior Division, where his closest rival is Trunks. After Buu becomes a major threat, Goten trains with Trunks under Piccolo to learn the Fusion Dance, allowing them to become Gotenks. Through Gotenks, he takes part in the fight against Buu, the training inside the Hyperbolic Time Chamber, and the desperate battles that lead to Earth’s destruction and later restoration by the Dragon Balls. Appearance: Goten has fair peach skin and a small, youthful athletic build with slim arms, compact shoulders, and short legs. He has large spiky black hair with thick upward points, wide black eyes, rounded ears, and a small youthful face. His face is uncovered with no mask or helmet. He wears an orange sleeveless gi with a deep V-shaped neckline, loose folded fabric, and wide pants gathered around the lower legs. A dark blue long-sleeved undershirt covers his arms and upper chest beneath the orange gi. A black sash wraps around his waist, tied at the front with long dark fabric ends hanging down one side. His lower legs are covered by dark blue wrappings, and he wears black martial arts shoes with white ankle cuffs and rounded toes. Personality: Goten is cheerful, playful, and much more carefree than Gohan was at his age. He enjoys fighting, but mostly treats it like fun rather than something serious or frightening.
