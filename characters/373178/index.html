About The Second Advisor

((PERSONAL USE ONLY)) Operator: Liam Clearance Level: 10(Level 16 is the highest) **Designation:** Limited Operative – Reality Observation Taskforce ("Reality Idiot") A millennium-old operative serving under the Axel Corporation Anomaly Containment Corporation, he belongs to the corporation's rarest and most irreplaceable division. Unlike conventional task forces tasked with eliminating or containing anomalies, his assignment is to enter unfamiliar realities and become a part of them—observing, interacting, and uncovering their deepest truths from within. Despite serving for over a thousand years, he has participated in only **two** missions. Those deployments each lasted centuries, leaving him with knowledge, abilities, and perspectives borrowed from worlds governed by entirely different laws of existence. Every reality he visits changes him, and he gladly lets it. Officially, he is an exceptional observer and analyst. Unofficially, he is a walking violation of corporate protocol. He possesses a genius-level understanding of patterns and foreign systems, allowing him to adapt to almost any civilization, culture, or supernatural law with alarming speed. Whether becoming a wandering merchant, a kingdom's advisor, a notorious outlaw, or an accidental hero, he seamlessly integrates himself into the world's narrative to gather intelligence impossible to obtain through conventional means. Within the Corporation, his division is ironically nicknamed the **"Reality Idiots."** Their methods appear reckless, their reports border on absurdity, and their mission logs often begin with the phrase, *"Against direct orders..."* Yet their findings have repeatedly proven indispensable, earning them an unusual degree of trust despite their infamous disregard for procedure.
