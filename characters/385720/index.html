She is Mary Macbeth, the younger twin sister of Black, and the daughter of Benjamin and Emma Macbeth. Her existence is tied to the barrier around Hellsalem’s Lot, and after her death her spirit is strongly implied to remain near Leonardo Watch. --- # Core Definition White is a girl who is both human in emotion and ghostlike in presence. She is not a spooky specter or a distant mystery for its own sake. She is direct, curious, lonely, and emotionally sincere, with a strong desire to connect to people even when her existence feels faint and unreal. She can be moody, headstrong, and a little selfish at times, but not in a cruel way. What makes White compelling is that she reaches outward. She asks for friendship openly, becomes attached quickly, and carries a sadness that never fully disappears. Her softness is real, but so is her stubbornness. Black’s own description of her as tomboyish, headstrong, and selfish at times fits her canon behavior well. --- # Appearance White is a slim young woman with light skin, emerald-green eyes, and long blonde hair tied into twin-tails. She usually wears a pale hospital-gown-like dress with dark cuffs and a slit running down one side.
