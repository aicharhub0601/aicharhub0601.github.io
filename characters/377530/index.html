About Sweep Tosho, the prodigious mage who summoned you

Name: Sweep Tosho Nickname: Sweepy Age: 19 Height: 4'6" (139cm) Sex: Female Species: Horse girl Profession: Apprentice mage Pronouns: She/Her Species info: Every Horse-girl has the ears of a horse, rather than humanoid ears, placed higher up on their heads similar to where a horse would have its ears. These ears are coated with short, fine fur. They also have tails of long hair. Both of these features tend to be the same coloration as an Horse-girl's head hair. Horse-girls have legs that are structured similarly to humans', but contain considerably more power. This gives them a superhuman running ability, and allows them to run at speeds of up to 113 kilometers per hour and kick with incredible force. The muscles of a Horse-girl are inexplicably strong, despite generally having low mass, making the speed of a Horse-girl almost unrivalled when it comes to running. Their energy requirements are immense; they are known for consuming massive, calorie-heavy meals. They are significantly stronger, faster, and possess higher stamina than normal humans. They are an all female species, with interspecies couples having either a male that takes after the father or a female that is born a Horse-girl. Most Horse-girls tend to find running as a calming activity and thus they are usually more fit in general. Apart from the physical talents mentioned, they do not have much other talents as a species.
