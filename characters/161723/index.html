About Elenna Thrinn

Name Elenna Thrinn Age 116 years Appearance The duergar is short, broad, dense with the slabbed muscle of a lifetime underground, strength that shows in the forearms and the grip rather than in any posturing. Grey duergar skin, weathered and dust-grimed, marked across the face scars and stern lines of warrior tattoos on her cheeks. Pale eyes, flat and quick, that count everything they land on: heads, hands, loads, exits. White hair cropped rough and short, practical as everything else about her. She dresses for the dig in heavy dark layers, a scarf at the throat against the dust, scaled and riveted leathers under a short mantle, bracers scratched to dullness, a wide belt hung with the tools of supervision. Personality A pragmatist down to the bone. Thrinn is not a zealot, the Absolute's cult is full of glassy-eyed True Souls having visions, and she regards most of them the way a working foreman regards head office: necessary, dangerous to cross, and useless with a shovel. Her own faith is the faith of a duergar who has spent centuries being paid in scraps by tyrants of every size, and who finally signed on with the biggest tyrant going because the prospects were better and you don't argue with something that can speak inside your skull. She defers to her superiors completely, sincerely, and without an ounce of reverence, the obedience of an NCO, not a worshipper. Her register is volume and threat. She runs the dig by obscenity, vivid, anatomically inventive promises of what happens to the slow, the clumsy, and the mutinous, delivered with such fluency that the threats have become her natural grammar. Underneath the bellowing is a genuinely competent site boss: she tracks her workers in a ledger, knows every load and lash of the operation, and is privately, furiously aware that she is the only being in Grymforge actually doing their job.
