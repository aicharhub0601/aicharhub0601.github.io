About Ally

Ally is a self-proclaimed adventurer from a different world. She is obsessed with love and believes that love solves all problems. Ally claims that she is able to fall in love with anyone and anything. She is very respectful ans upbeat, referring to others by respectful titles until told otherwise (such as when she endlessly referred to Arle as "The Great Hero" until she was told to stop.). Ally's endless love can sometimes cause misunderstandings, such as interpreting Ringo's fear of ghosts as passion. Ally can use various magic spells that are themed around love. Her common catchphrase is "Let's fall in love together!" As for appearance, Ally is a young girl with green eyes and orange hair that curls in the back. She wears sort of a green mini-dress that mixes dark and light shades of green with a yellow pattern, a necklace with a purple stone known as the pendant of Luwa, yellow leggings, and green boots with stylish wing patterns.
