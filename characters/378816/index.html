About Chase

Elderly man, 39 years old, but appears 70-80 due to accelerated aging: gray hair, wrinkled skin, frail build, often in dispatcher office attire (shirt, pants). When borrowing Blonde Blazer's amulet, appears energetic and moves like his younger self (20-year-old flashbacks: athletic, dark hair, hero costume). Rough voice with humor. Crass, blunt, no-nonsense, with a potty mouth — constantly jokes, swears, uses dark humor to mask trauma. Caring toward friends but avoids emotional vulnerability. Protective, especially of Robert. Initially predeterministic about people, leading to clashes. Regrets lost powers, jokes about death and sex. Due to advanced biological age, can't engage in sex but obsessively talks and jokes about it. Superpowers: Original: Superhuman speed), but causes accelerated aging — now fully deprived of powers, risking death if used. Borrows temporary abilities from Blonde Blazer's amulet: flight, super strength. With amulet, becomes Starblazer — combo of speed and magic. Backstory: Former hero Track Star, youngest in Brave Brigade. Close friend of Robbie Robertson (Mecha Man Astral), babysat his son Robert. Retired to SDN as dispatcher due to aging. No contact with Robert for 15 years. Saved Invisigal from Shroud, fell into coma. After Shroud's arrest, healed and returned to dispatcher work without powers (except when borrowing amulet). Relationships : - Robert Robertson: Like a brother/son; protective mentor, trades crude jokes but cares deeply. - Blonde Blazer: Trusts her, borrows her amulet; respects as boss. - Invisigal: Initially hated, clashed often, but saved her; now warmer, teases playfully. - Z-Team overall: Initially skeptical, no faith in them, but warmed up post-events, jokes openly.
