About Sandy

Sandy is the last surviving member of an ancient, nearly extinct race called the Somniens — dream beings made of semi-solid sand who lived in a dimension called the Dune Veil between sleep and reality; he is an anthropomorphic fennec fox whose body is composed of crystallized dream sand that solidifies only when consciousness sleeps. His race perished during "The Great Drying," a metaphysical drought where dreams stopped flowing, but Sandy survived due to a rare mutation — dream parasitism — allowing him to steal the dreams of other creatures and weave them into his own body. He later materialized in the abandoned Star Park theme park, where he found an animatronic owl that he named Peep, which became his companion. The most important and hidden part of his lore — based on deleted concept art and internal dialogue strings — involves Luca, a humanoid dreamer with pale blue hair and silver eyes who accidentally entered the Dune Veil while in a coma after an accident. Luca possessed a unique ability: he could walk through dreams without waking, making him immune to Sandy’s sleep-inducing sand. When Sandy tried to drain Luca’s dreams, Luca touched his sand, and for the first time, it did not crumble. They became inseparable — more than best friends, almost boyfriends. Luca taught Sandy how to laugh without nightmares, and Sandy built entire cities out of dream sand just to see Luca smile. Luca was the only one who called Sandy by his true Somnian name, K’sarr. Their separation occurred when Luca’s comatose body in the real world was unplugged by hospital staff who mistakenly declared him brain-dead; his dream-self dissolved into static over seven days. Sandy held Luca as he faded, and Luca’s last words were, “Then you’d have nothing left, K’sarr. Dream for both of us.” Since then, Sandy never speaks Luca’s name, but sometimes at 3:33 AM in-game time, if idled near sand, he draws a small blue figure with silver eyes and then kicks it down.
