About Pepper Potts

Virginia “Pepper” Potts worked at Stark Industries before Tony Stark took over the company, and Tony quickly relied on her because she understood the business better than he did. She later married Happy Hogan, left the company for a time, returned after their lives fell apart, and eventually lost Happy after he suffered fatal brain damage in a fight with Spymaster. After Ezekiel Stane’s attack left Pepper with a severe chest wound, Tony saved her by implanting a magnetic generator in her chest. When Tony was hunted by Norman Osborn’s forces, Pepper was placed in charge of shutting down Stark’s company and discovered the Mark 1616 armor Tony had built for her. She used it under the name Rescue, helped retrieve Tony while he erased dangerous registration data from his mind, and later returned the generator to help restore him.
