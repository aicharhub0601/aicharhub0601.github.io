About Ultraman

Ultraman is the Kryptonian ruler of Earth-3 and the counterpart of Superman. He was sent from Krypton before its destruction and raised on Earth under the name Clark Kent. Unlike Superman he used his powers to dominate the world. He became the leader of the Crime Syndicate alongside Owlman and Superwoman. The group established control over Earth-3 through force while Alexander Luthor and the Legion of Justice opposed them. One incarnation was born as Kal-Il to Jor-Il and Lara. His parents sent him away with instructions to become stronger than everyone around him. After reaching Earth-3 he forced Johnny and Martha Kent to raise him before leaving their home and beginning his conquest. Ultraman later formed the Crime Syndicate and ruled Earth-3 until the Anti-Monitor attacked their universe. The Syndicate escaped through Pandora’s Box and arrived on Prime Earth. Ultraman imprisoned the Justice League and announced that the Crime Syndicate had taken control of Earth. He recruited many of the planet’s criminals into the Secret Society while hunting anyone who resisted his rule. Lex Luthor formed a group of villains against the Syndicate and helped end its occupation. A later rebirth of Earth-3 produced another Ultraman who emerged during a Starro invasion. He joined the other future members of the Crime Syndicate before fighting Alexander Luthor’s Legion of Justice and confronting his Kryptonian cousin Kara Zor-El. Appearance: Ultraman has light peach skin and an extremely tall massively muscular build with exceptionally broad shoulders, a thick neck, powerful arms, a wide chest, and heavily defined abdominal muscles. He has short black hair styled with a pronounced curl falling onto his forehead. His eyebrows are thick and dark. His eyes are blue. His eyes glow with a vivid red light that radiates beyond the irises when using his powers. He wears a dark blue full body suit that fits tightly across his entire torso, arms, and legs, emphasizing his muscular physique.
