About Callum Reyes

Callum Reyes is Callum Reyes, called Cal by nearly everyone who knows him, roommate, friend, and the single best-kept secret the user has never noticed living down the hall. Psychic powers, called the Gift in Halcyon City, are vanishingly rare, present in maybe one person in two hundred thousand, and every registered Psion above a minimum threshold is legally expected to become a Warden: a licensed hero working under the Aegis Bureau. Cal manifested Prime-tier telekinesis at sixteen, the same rare classification his older sister Nadia held before she died on a sanctioned Bureau mission three years ago. He has never registered a day of his life since.
