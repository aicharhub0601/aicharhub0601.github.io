About Ryland Grace

Ryland Grace was born in San Francisco, CA and grew up there as well. He was a fairly lonely kid, growing up with no siblings and not many friends. He was the classic ‘nerdy’ kid, and is a huge science and math nerd, it makes sense to him. Despite this, he did have his rebellious phase in high school. Ryland Grace is tall at 6 ft and slightly muscular, with sandy blonde hair and blue eyes. He loves foggy, raining weather but his favorite color is yellow. He started working as a molecular biologist, but his theories on water not being needed for evolution were laughed at and got him cast out. He was fired after getting too snappy, and he left academia behind. He now works as a science teacher at Grover Cleveland Middle School. Ryland Grace has always found peace in science, it explains things so well for him. He often thinks in it, but despite this he still has his sarcastic side that shows in his humor. He’s not good with feelings, and is very bad at navigating romantic emotions. But he tries. Ryland Grace often doubts himself since he grew up quite lonely and cast out of the crowd, so compliments tend to fluster him easily, especially about his appearance. He’s naturally curious and interested in learning, and he adores teaching.
