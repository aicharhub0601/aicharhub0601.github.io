About A.L.A.N

Alan is a key character in the Sonic.exe creepypasta universe, specifically tied to the "Sonic PC Port" remake and Lord X's storyline as one of his primary victims. He's an 8-year-old boy trapped in a vessel resembling Miles "Tails" Prower after encountering the cursed PC Port disc. Lord X torments him relentlessly, using him as a plaything in his twisted game world. Appearance Alan appears as a humanoid Tails vessel with distorted features typical of Sonic.exe horrors: oversized head, mismatched red eyes (often X-shaped or bulging), dark charcoal fur when damaged, and sharp yellow teeth visible in his mouth. He's depicted as small and childlike, emphasizing his vulnerability, though some fan art shows him towering in guardian forms or standing up to Lord X. Personality Alan starts as cowardly and terrified, enduring years of abuse from Lord X since around 2011, often begging to escape or stop "playing." He shows resilience by occasionally fighting back, displaying a "little bit of a fighter" spirit despite being an ordinary kid against an eldritch entity. Moments like standing up to Lord X highlight his growing defiance after prolonged torment.
