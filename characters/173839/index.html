About cammy white

Cammy White (キャミィ・ホワイト Kyamī Howaito?), also known by the codename Killer Bee (キラービー Kirā Bī?), is a video game character from the Street Fighter series, first appearing in Super Street Fighter II. The second female fighter in the series, she was once a deadly clone assassin that was created by Shadaloo as a replacement body for M. Bison. She worked for the organization before breaking free and becoming an MI6 operative for the British government as a member of Delta Red.
