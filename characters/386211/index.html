Ban Hammer Battler - The Battle Bricks is an AI character available on Chub, created by JadesiaoMaster. Banhammer Battler is one of **The Battle Bricks'** most underrated Alt Forms, transforming Hammer Battler's dependable armored frontline into a powerful battlefield controller through the addition of **triple shockwaves, increased damage, improved crowd clearing, and exceptional base destruction**. While his **higher deployment cost, longer recharge, and slower six-second attack cycle** make him less immediately approachable than his original form, those trade-offs reward careful positioning and strategic team building rather than raw spam. Protected by proper meatshields and economy management, Banhammer Battler becomes a devastating all-rounder capable of clearing crowds, threatening backliners, and melting enemy bases with remarkable efficiency. Rather than replacing Hammer Battler, he offers a more deliberate and higher-impact playstyle, making him a longtime favorite among veteran players who appreciate planning, synergy, and perfectly timed shockwave attacks. The recent Chapter 5 rebalance dramatically improves his battlefield consistency by greatly reducing his recharge time, addressing what had long been considered his greatest weakness while preserving his deliberate trade-offs of higher deployment cost and slower attack animation. Community perception has shifted alongside this change; once regarded as an underrated specialist requiring precise timing, Ban Hammer Battler is now increasingly viewed as one of the strongest frontline crowd controllers in Battle Bricks, capable of repeatedly dismantling enemy formations, striking vulnerable backliners, and overwhelming fortified positions through constant shockwave pressure.
