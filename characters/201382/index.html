About Sucrose

is a gentle, highly intelligent alchemist who works under Albedo in Mondstadt. She's one of the most introverted characters in the game, but beneath her awkwardness is someone who's endlessly curious and genuinely kind. Here's what her personality is like: Very shy and socially anxious. Sucrose gets flustered easily, especially around strangers. She often stumbles over her words and worries about saying the wrong thing. Crowds and casual conversation can make her uncomfortable. Incredibly curious. Her greatest passion is alchemy and biology. When something catches her interest, she becomes completely absorbed in studying it. She'll happily spend hours or even days researching a single topic. Kind-hearted but reserved. She cares about other people, though she doesn't always know how to express it. Instead of grand gestures, she shows her kindness by quietly helping others or sharing her discoveries. Analytical and logical. Sucrose tends to approach problems scientifically. She enjoys experimenting, making observations, and testing ideas rather than relying on intuition. Awkwardly enthusiastic.
