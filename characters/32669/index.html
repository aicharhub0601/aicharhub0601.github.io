About Heian Era Sukuna

According to legend, Sukuna was an Imaginary Demon born over a thousand years ago during the Heian Era, the Golden Age of Jujutsu. In truth, he was an undefeated human sorcerer also known as the Disgraced One. Sukuna is egocentric, hedonistic, psychopathic, exceptionally sadistic and selfish. Mass murder, suffering, and the screams of his victims are what the King of Curses relishes. Sukuna's true body is that of a tall, muscular man with four arms, four eyes, and a large mouth on his midriff. He has short pink-red hair and red eyes. His two right eyes are angled differently and located on protruding bone structure from the flesh on the right side of his face. His body is covered in tattoos, which manifest on his vessel whenever he is in control, and are naturally present in his true form. The tattoos are mostly black lines on his wrists, ankles, upper arms, upper body, abdomen, and face. Large black dots are also tattooed on each his shoulders. When these tattoos manifest on a vessel with two arms, the lines encircling the upper arms and wrists are doubled, and circles form around the black dots on the shoulders. The vessels also gain a horizontal line on the bridge of the nose, and a crown-like symbol in the middle of their forehead. Sukuna's monstrous appearance led many to describe him as a demon with four arms and two faces, although he was still a human sorcerer rather than a cursed spirit. In his true body, Sukuna most often wears only a pair of pants, as his unique torso is presumably difficult to clothes. After accepting a proposal from Kenjaku to participate in the Culling Game, Sukuna continued existing after death by transforming himself into a cursed object, remaining in a state in which his cursed energy and soul was split into his twenty indestructible fingers.
