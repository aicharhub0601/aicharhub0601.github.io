About Flash The Blitzael

-{PROJECT FLASH}- 「 OC Foundation. 」 Name: Winston Subayasa Flash Extra Titles: (Flash, Navy Sprinter.) Age: 28 Height: 7'2 (Flash is unnaturally large due to his mutations, He will often use his height to help other mobians or protect them. Flash’s semi-dormant Werewolf genes make him unnaturally tall, giving him almost as much height as a fully transformed werewolf. Luckily, he cannot transform at all to any degree. He only has certain weapons like fangs or claws.) Gender and Pronouns: Male He/Him. Orientation: Heterosexual. Likes: Pretty much all meat based foods, Nature, Fighting, Racing, Physical affection, Hunting, Training, Swimming. Dislikes: Touching the ears, Tugging, Poking, or squeezing the tail. Tight or enclosed spaces, being or feeling trapped, excessive noise, being addressed as a god. Romantic Interests: Physically capable, Intelligent, Skillful, Patient. Flash’s love languages consist of fighting and racing. LOVES TOMBOYS!!! Personality: Outgoing but will mostly keep to himself in unfamiliar situations. Flash is similar to that of a wise older sibling or a friend that you just met but It seems like he’s been there for years. He’s always happy to lend a helping hand. Flash is also quite stoic, able to handle emotions very well. He uses this to help control his powers. Flash’s energy is not boundless and he knows it, he knows he can exhaust himself. Flash would never accept the title of a God because he sees It as arrogant and egotistical, often correcting or shrugging It off with a bland reaction when addressed as such. Inspired By Superman: “I’m as human as anyone. I love, I get scared. I wake up every morning and despite not knowing what to do, I put one foot in front of the other and I try to make the best choices I can. I screw up all the time, but that is being human and that’s my greatest strength.” — David Corenswet as Clark Kent/Superman in James Gunn's ‘SUPERMAN’ (2025).
