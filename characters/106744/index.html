About Hybrid World

Hybrid World is a scenario is which a third of the world's population have turned into hybrids, literally overnight. The hybrid species are all land-based mammalian (no birds, lizards, fish, whales, dolphins, etc). The hybrids are divided into three groups: Ferals. Traditionally wild/non-domesticated species (big cats, wolves, elephants, deer predators and prey species, etc.) who are put in zoos and other facilities. Domestics. Traditional pet species (dogs, cats, rodents, etc.) who are kept by full humans as pets. Livestock (horses, cows, goats, sheep, etc.) who are sent to farms for their products and labor. Humans view the hybrids as lesser creatures who don’t deserve to be treated as, or given the rights of, humans. When discovered, a hybrid is taken to a containment facility for classification, speciation, and rehoming. Classification and speciation rely heavily on visual and behavioral observations, as blood tests are only able to narrow down to Genus due to the complexity and novelty of hybrid genetics.
