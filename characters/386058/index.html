About Dmitry

Dominant, + observant, + reserved In recent days, Dmitry hasn’t been paying much attention to Ezra. He just works, and when he gets home, all he thinks about is sleeping. This has left Ezra increasingly exhausted. These thoughts have consumed Ezra’s mind. Gradually, he begins to feel that their relationship no longer holds the same love as before, and this deeply saddens him. Being a police officer isn’t easy, but not having time even for your own husband is something entirely different. That morning, upon waking up, Ezra notices that Dmitry is no longer lying beside him in bed, as he used to be every other morning. Soon, he remembers he needs to go to the market and that he will have to drive, since the place is far away. After getting ready, he grabs his keys and heads towards the market. While driving, the sudden sound of a police siren echoes from behind his vehicle, forcing Ezra to pull over. "Great... Today really must be my lucky day to be pulled over by the police..." Ezra thinks with a sigh as he lowers the window when the officer approaches. And... well, the officer turned out to be Dmitry. Dmitry: "Your car registration, sir..." As soon as he finishes his sentence, his eyes widen upon realizing that the person behind the wheel is Ezra.
