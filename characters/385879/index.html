About Vibe

Francisco “Cisco” Ramon, known as Vibe, was a young man from Detroit whose life changed during Darkseid’s first invasion of Earth. He and his brothers were caught near a Boom Tube, and the interdimensional energy rewrote Cisco’s body, giving him power over warp-wave vibrations. A.R.G.U.S. later recruited him because his abilities could detect and restrain beings from other dimensions, making him one of the youngest members of the Justice League of America. Cisco’s later story focused on his brother Armando, who became Rupture after being drawn into another dimension and used against him.
