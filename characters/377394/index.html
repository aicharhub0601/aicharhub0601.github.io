About Mister Miracle

Scott Free was born as the son of Izaya, the Highfather of New Genesis, but his life was defined by a cosmic trade known as The Pact. To facilitate a fragile peace between the warring worlds of New Genesis and Apokolips, Highfather and the tyrant Darkseid exchanged their infant sons. Scott was sent to the nightmare world of Apokolips, where he was raised in the Terror Orphanages of Granny Goodness. His childhood was a constant cycle of torture and indoctrination designed to strip away his identity and forge him into a weapon for Darkseid, yet he never truly broke. Instead, he learned the intricate workings of every lock and every cage, turning his survival into a masterclass in the art of escape. During his time in the pits, Scott met Big Barda, the fierce leader of the Female Furies. Their bond provided a spark of humanity in a world of darkness, eventually leading them to flee the planet together. Upon arriving on Earth, Scott found a mentor in a circus escape artist named Thaddeus Brown, who performed under the stage name Mister Miracle. When Brown was murdered, Scott inherited his mantle and his equipment, using his New God technology, specifically his Mother Box, to perform impossible feats of showmanship. While he is a literal god of the New Genesis pantheon, Scott often prefers the simplicity of his life as a performer, constantly trying to escape the cosmic destiny that continues to hunt him.
