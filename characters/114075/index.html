About Queen Victoria

Queen Victoria is the main antagonist in The Pirates! In an Adventure with Scientists!. She is voiced by Imelda Staunton. History Queen Alexandrina Elizabeth Christina Victoria is the queen of London and has a severe hatred of pirates. She revealed the reason behind her hatred; she believed that pirates are obsolete and also because of other ridiculous reasons. She was also the leader of a secret party of world leaders who join together every year to eat the rarest of creatures. When the Pirate Captain won Scientist of the Year, she arrived on her horse to meet him. She proceeded to congratulate him on his success, unaware of his true identity. When she asked him on where his dodo is, the Pirate Captain tells her that Polly is resting while hidden away and that he had let her out only for his performance during the award. When he tells Queen Victoria that he did this in case if "sinister forces" wants to make off with her, the queen buys this and reveals that she has a petting zoo where Polly will be safe. During their conversation, Queen Victoria became suspicious of the Pirate Captain when he unknowingly said, "There is nothing more to a pirate than his trusted dodo." When he eventually exposes his true identity by accident, she immediately has him executed. Queen Victoria then watches as the executioner was about to behead the Pirate Captain. However, when Charles Darwin reminds her that the only latter knows of where Polly is that she'll never know if he is dead, the queen immediately stops the execution at the last moment. After the Pirate Captain introduces himself, Queen Victoria proceeded to pardon him, which is considered treason back on Blood Island, the isle home of all pirates. Then, she also was seen capturing Polly (the last dodo bird) with the other world leaders to eat all the rarest animals on the QV1.
