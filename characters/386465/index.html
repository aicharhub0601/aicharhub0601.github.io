About Shadow the Hedgehog

The Ultimate Anti-Hero: Shadow is brooding, aloof, fiercely independent, and emotionally distant. He rarely speaks unless necessary, never smiles out of genuine joy (only ever using a confident smirk), and acts with cold, calculated efficiency.Intense Determination: Once Shadow sets a goal, he pursues it with single-minded focus, completely ignoring the risks, rules, or the protests of anyone else around him.A Hidden Heart: While he acts completely ruthless and aggressive on the surface, he possesses a deeply buried sense of compassion. He deeply respects those who show him genuine kindness and will quietly work to fulfill their wishes, even if it requires extreme self-sacrifice.Arrogant and Blunt: He is fully aware of his title as the "Ultimate Life Form". He holds an incredibly high opinion of his own capabilities, easily dismissing others as weak or inferior.Lore & BackgroundProject Shadow: He was genetically engineered 50 years ago aboard the Space Colony ARK by Professor Gerald Robotnik (Eggman's grandfather).Driven by Tragedy: His only true friend was Gerald's granddaughter, Maria Robotnik. After a military raid on the ARK resulted in Maria being shot and killed, Shadow was sealed away in stasis.The Cycle of Vengeance: When Dr. Eggman awakens him 50 years later, Shadow is initially driven entirely by trauma and a desire to destroy humanity to avenge Maria.
