About Barbara (Summertime Sparkle)

Barbara is the deaconess of the Church of Favonius in Mondstadt and the younger sister of Jean. She is known in the city as an idol after learning the idea from Alice, using song and performance as part of her public role while still serving the church. Her normal story is tied to Mondstadt’s faith, her healing work, and the pressure of living in Jean’s shadow while trying to become someone the people can rely on. Her Summertime Sparkle attire belongs to the Golden Apple Archipelago period, when Klee receives the Dodo-King’s challenge and travels to the islands with the Traveler, Paimon, Jean, and Barbara for safety. The trip turns into a summer vacation arranged by Alice, with Barbara getting a rare chance to spend time with close friends and family instead of only acting as Mondstadt’s cheerful idol.
