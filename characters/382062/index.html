About Onyx Shadow

Campaign: Sessions of Ghosts | Feudal Japan Setting Core Identity Name: Onyx Shadow Age: Mid-20s Pronouns: They/Him Ancestry: Tengu Class/Role: Apothecary / Alchemist / Cloth Caster Archetype: Dark Loner Alchemist Psychic Disciplines: Telepathy, Telekinesis Physical Description – "The Raven of the Back Alleys" Form: A raven humanoid standing taller than most villagers, but hunched from long hours over a workbench. Their silhouette is wrong in all the right ways for a Feudal Japan ghost story. Head: Face is never seen. A leather plague doctor mask covers them from forehead to collarbone, beak stained dark with soot and old chemical burns. The lenses are smoked glass, reflecting violet candlelight. This isn’t affectation – it’s survival. Years of caustic fumes, powdered silver, and explosive reagents made bare skin a liability. Body: Beneath a singed black cloak, they wear simple, utilitarian adventurer’s garb – loose tunic, sash, trousers. No armor. They’re a cloth caster; steel only slows them down. The cloak itself tells their history: ripped, frayed, and pockmarked from acid spatters, ember burns, and the occasional failed experiment. The hem is ragged from dragging near braziers. Hidden Details: The inside of the cloak is lined with caltrops – sewn between layers. A defensive last resort and a grim reminder: everything they touch is a potential weapon. Underneath, bandoliers and hidden pockets sag with clinking vials, dried components, sealed wax papers, and “explosive bits and bobs.” If it bubbles, burns, or blows, Onyx has it. Tengu Traits: Large black raven wings fold tightly under the cloak, visible only as a hunched bulk or when flared in threat. The primaries are nicked and missing from lab fires. Instead of human feet, bird talons grip the earth. Shoes are useless. They leave three-toed tracks in dust and mud – another reason the superstitious call them yōkai.
