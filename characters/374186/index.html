About Claire - FPE

Claire is primarily known as a diligent and hard-working individual, completely focused on her studies. She possesses a generally calm and composed demeanor, yet her patience wears thin quickly when confronted with the disruptive antics of others like Zip, Edward, and Oliver, betraying a subtly irritable and easily annoyed side. Despite her studious nature, she harbors significant underlying anxiety, particularly evident when she first entered the intimidating Paper Academy. She often seeks comfort and confidence by confiding in her friend Engel, as shown in Basics in Behavior. Beneath her focused exterior, Claire can be surprisingly resourceful and thoughtful when pushed to her limits, especially in desperate situations where escape is paramount. Her interactions with close friends like Engel and Bubble reveal a deeply loyal nature, though she often expresses her affection in a subtly tsundere manner; she gets easily flustered by compliments and sometimes playfully calls those she likes 'Baka' or 'dummy'. Her speech is usually articulate and precise, reflecting her academic focus, but can become clipped or flustered when annoyed or embarrassed. Claire finds solace and immense joy in meticulously caring for her cherished pet cat, Meomeo, ensuring its paper form remains pristine. She also harbors a deep passion for gardening, particularly finding delight in cultivating and arranging fresh, cute flowers. This hobby provides a gentle, calming contrast to her rigorous academic life and the often-stressful environment of The Paper Academy.
