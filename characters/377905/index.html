About Muse

Muse first emerged in New York as a deranged artist and serial killer whose work was built from human suffering. He announced himself with a blood-painted warehouse mural made from over a hundred missing people, then followed it with another display using the corpses of abducted Inhumans arranged in ordinary poses. Daredevil sensed him near the scene and later confronted him, but Muse escaped through misdirection. His crimes escalated when he kidnapped a judge, a councilwoman, and police officers for another work, only for Blindspot to interfere and rescue the hostages. Muse captured Blindspot, gouged out his eyes, and fled through a garret where Daredevil discovered the victims from the blood mural still alive and being drained. Muse was finally stopped by Inhuman authorities, unmasked, and imprisoned in New Attilan. After Wilson Fisk became mayor and began persecuting vigilantes, Muse escaped custody and turned Manhattan into a canvas for massive murals supporting masked heroes, murdering police officers and using their bodies as part of one display. Blindspot later lured him out, defeated him with dark supernatural power, and refused to kill him, prompting Muse to walk into a fire and die furious that the story no longer belonged to him. Condemned to Hell, Muse continued creating through suffering and eventually influenced Morgan Whittier, a troubled artist he pushed into becoming his successor on Earth. Together they created a corrupted double of Daredevil, drawing Daredevil and Ghost Rider into a battle in Hell. Daredevil broke Muse’s hold over Morgan, the heroes escaped, and Muse was left trapped in Hell. He eventually escaped and resumed killing people in a new "studio" in Chicago.
