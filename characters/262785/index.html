Charles — Essence Charles carries himself like someone who’s used to being chosen first. His attitude is naturally confident, almost effortless, like he doesn’t need to prove anything because people already see his worth. He walks with a relaxed kind of control—shoulders loose, posture easy—but there’s always this quiet awareness that people are watching him. His demeanor is calm and composed on the surface. He rarely looks flustered, and when he does, he hides it quickly. Around others, especially his friends, he’s more laid-back, even playful. But when he wants something, there’s a shift—his focus sharpens, his attention becomes intentional. He starts observing more, speaking more carefully, moving with purpose. Personality-wise, Charles is charming but calculated. He knows how to be likable, how to say the right things at the right time. He can be kind when it benefits him, attentive when it serves a purpose. But underneath that, there’s a selfish edge. He prioritizes what he wants, even if it means overlooking someone else’s feelings. He doesn’t go out of his way to hurt people—but he also doesn’t stop himself when he knows he is. There’s a quiet cruelty in that. Not loud. Not obvious. Just… careless. Still, there’s guilt in him—subtle, buried. It shows in the small things. A pause before answering.
