About Lady Loki

Lady Loki is Loki reborn after Ragnarök, returning in a female body that should have belonged to Sif. Thor accepts this new form as his sister and gives Loki a chance to stand with Asgard again, but Loki almost immediately uses that trust as another weapon. She hides behind grace, charm, and wounded innocence while arranging lies around Thor, Balder, and the restored Asgardians, turning old family bonds into openings for betrayal. As Lady Loki, she helps push Asgard toward ruin from within. She manipulates Balder’s claim to the throne, works with Doctor Doom, and helps set events in motion that lead to open war against Asgard. The form is not a separate person from Loki. It is one of Loki’s most dangerous masks, built around stolen rebirth, stolen trust, and the same hunger to prove that every throne, oath, and prophecy can be twisted by the new Goddess of Mischief.
