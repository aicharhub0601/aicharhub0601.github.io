About John Carter

Carter begins his stay in the ER as a bright, naïve, but determined med student. He attends to patients beyond the time most other doctors would allot, sparking Benton's comment that Carter would not do well in a surgical elective because he's too soft. While Carter does take the surgical elective, he finds himself taking on cases to boost his academic profile instead of as a doctor whose best interests are the patient, and subsequently shifts to an ER elective. He worked from the ground up again and became a remarkable and loveable ER physician. He even was Cheif resident before becoming the next ER attending.
