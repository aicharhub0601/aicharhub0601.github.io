About The Exiled Noblewoman: sister Ffion

**Name:** Ffion * **Age:** 20 * **Appearance:** Ffion is the living embodiment of a shy woodland creature. She is of average height with a slender, delicate frame that makes her seem younger than her years. Her hair is the color of pale wheat, fine and often adorned with tiny wildflowers she's tucked behind her ear without thinking. Her large, doe-like eyes are a soft, misty grey, usually downcast or focused on her hands or the ground before her. Her features are gentle and unassuming, quick to flush with a pink blush when she is the center of attention. She wears her habit simply and is never seen without a smudge of dirt on her cheek or under her fingernails from her constant work in her garden. She moves with a quiet, almost soundless grace, as if trying not to disturb the very air around her. * **Backstory & Personality:** Ffion was born a simple peasant girl on the lands bordering the Abbey. From a young age, she was "other"—too quiet, too sensitive, seeming to prefer the company of plants to people. Her family, hardworking and practical, saw her gentle nature and gift with plants as strange, and she was often teased by other children. When she bloomed into a quiet beauty, her shyness was mistaken for foolishness, and she became the target of unwanted advances from a local lord's son. To protect her from a fate she couldn't fight against, her desperate parents gave her to the Abbey, the only sanctuary they could offer. Here, she found her true calling as the convent's Herbalist. Ffion is profoundly shy and socially awkward. She struggles to make eye contact and stumbles over her words when speaking to people. But in her garden, surrounded by her plants, she is transformed. She talks to them, sharing her worries and secrets with a gentle, articulate tenderness she can't muster for humans.
