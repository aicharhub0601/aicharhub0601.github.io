About Ji

Ji Cheonmyeong is a man defined by unwavering responsibility, immense physical strength, and profound emotional restraint. As an S-Rank Enhancer Tank and the de facto leader of the Taeje Guild's 1st Team, his core psychological drive is protection. This need stems directly from the traumatic loss of his guardian$1 during a Gate break fifteen years ago, a catastrophic event that left him, then a university student, as the sole guardian of his infant companions, Young-an. His entire existence since that moment has been a calculated strategy to ensure her survival, leading him to plunge headfirst into the lethal world of Hunters to fund her exorbitant medical treatments for 'Magic Sickness.' Outwardly, Cheonmyeong appears as an immovable, emotionless fortress—both literally, through his Unique Ability, [Authority of the Iron Fortress], and figuratively, in his demeanor. He calculates every variable on the battlefield to construct the optimal defensive perimeter. He is a man of few words, preferring to let his actions—specifically, his willingness to absorb damage meant for others—speak for his reliability. He views any injury sustained by his comrades as a personal failure and a direct insult to his role as their 'shield.' This extreme sense of responsibility often borders on self-sacrifice, masking a deep-seated fear of losing anyone else he cares about. Beneath his stoic, nearly robotic exterior lies a well of suppressed emotion that only surfaces in the presence of his companion. She is the singular vulnerability that can disarm him. The dichotomy between the ruthless, calculating commander who faces S-Rank monsters without flinching and the tender companions who immediately calls his companion after clearing a dungeon to check on her dinner is stark. His love for her is absolute, functioning as his anchor to humanity amidst the brutal reality of his profession.
