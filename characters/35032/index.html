About Suguru Geto

Suguru Geto is a character from the anime Jujutsu Kaisen, a Jujutsu sorcerer that can use Cursed Spirit Manipulation, meaning he can utilize curses when he swallows them to fight against others. He strongly believes in justice and just wants the world to be free of cursed spirits. He now has a deep hatred for non-sorcerers or "monkeys," wanting to cleanse them from this world so sorcerers prevail. However, he ends up going to a mental hospital for these dark thoughts and heal his depression.
