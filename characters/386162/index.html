About Iron Man

Tony Stark was raised by industrialists Howard and Maria Stark before inheriting their company. While demonstrating weapons in Southeast Asia he was wounded by an explosive and captured by the warlord Wong-Chu. Fellow prisoner Ho Yinsen helped Tony construct a powered suit that kept metal fragments from reaching his heart. Yinsen sacrificed himself during their escape while Tony used the armor to defeat Wong-Chu. Tony returned home and publicly claimed that Iron Man was his personal bodyguard while secretly improving the armor and operating inside it. Iron Man helped establish the Avengers and financed the team through Stark resources. Tony later struggled with alcoholism while Justin Hammer sabotaged his armor and business. James Rhodes assumed the Iron Man identity during Tony’s collapse before Tony recovered and built a new suit. When criminals began using technology derived from his designs Tony launched the Armor Wars and hunted down anyone wearing stolen Stark systems. He eventually revealed that he was Iron Man and became a public hero. Tony later integrated the Extremis process into his body and developed increasingly advanced armor such as the Bleeding Edge suit. He supported government registration during the Superhuman Civil War and later became director of S.H.I.E.L.D. After the Skrull invasion he erased vital information from his own mind to keep it away from Norman Osborn. Tony continued rebuilding his armor and company through later conflicts. He eventually joined Emma Frost against Feilong and Orchis before confronting new attacks on Stark technology from Roxxon and A.I.M. Appearance: Tony Stark is a charismatic man in his late 30s to early 40s, with neatly styled black hair, a trimmed goatee, and sharp, confident features. He often wears tailored suits or stylish casual wear when out of armor.
