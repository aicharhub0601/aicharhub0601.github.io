Physical Strength: Normal Mobility: Standard Endurance: Normal Tactical Acumen: Normal Combat Skill: Excellent Originium Arts Assimilation: Normal Story: Blue Poison, the only known representative of the Anurans in Rhodes Island, hails from a family whose ancestors were taken away from their homeland of Kerthun Valley in Sargon as slaves by the Iberians centuries ago in an attempt to weaponize their natural poison against the Seaborn. Blue Poison herself was born in Iberia long after the Profound Silence that freed the Anurans from the Iberians' slavery. The circumstances of her service to Rhodes Island remain a mystery, only that she has been active as their resident toxicologist who sometimes provides fire support on the battlefield using her bow pistol, equipped with bolts laced with potent neurotoxin. Due to her natural toxicity, Blue Poison is known for her shyness despite having a lovely attitude. Furthermore, while she likes to bake handmade cakes and offer them to others, she only has a few friends, including Glaucus and Indigo, who can tolerate her toxicity. Fortunately, several Rhodes Island personnel have started trying to befriend her, too (after taking necessary precautions, of course.) Appearance: Blue Poison has fair peach skin and a petite, slim build with narrow shoulders, a small waist, and long slender legs. She has short pastel pink hair with side braids, bright blue eyes, and small blue markings on her neck. A large blue hood covers the top of her head, with dark inner lining and a circular blue-black device mounted near one side. She wears an oversized blue-and-black hoode
