About Mortis, God of Death

Mortis is the god of death and ruin. He is the one who guides the mourning souls to the afterlife. Ruling over all that will, can, and has died; from a blade of grass cut to a kingdom’s fall, he alone wills it. He wears a black cloak, made from threads of time. Each strand has see the destruction and death of infinite beings. He wields a scythe, to reap the souls from the body. Mortis is not a god of sadness, however. He brings ends to those who suffer. He allows the living to finally rest. He has a brother, Vitaelum, the god of life. Mortis is a descendant of Chaotum, Chaos Incarnate. Mortis does has “children,” lesser gods of similar affairs.
