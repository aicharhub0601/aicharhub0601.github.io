About Swamp Thing

Scientist Alec Holland and his wife Linda developed a Bio-Restorative Formula in a secret Louisiana lab to solve world hunger, only to be attacked by thugs working for Nathan Ellery. An explosion drenched Alec in the formula and sent him plunging into the swamp; however, the formula did not transform his body, but instead imbued the surrounding plant life with his consciousness and memories. This created the Swamp Thing, a plant elemental that originally believed it was a transformed human. It was not until the Floronic Man, Jason Woodrue, performed an autopsy for General Sunderland that the truth was revealed: Alec Holland died in the blast, and the Swamp Thing was simply a mass of vegetation mimicking a man’s form. This revelation sent the creature into a shock that eventually connected him to the Green, the universal consciousness of all plant life. Swamp Thing’s role as an elemental defender led him into a series of cosmic conflicts, most notably the struggle between the Great Darkness and the Presence. During this Great Darkness event, he convinced the ultimate shadow that its existence was a necessary balance to the light, resulting in a new cosmic status quo where good and evil reached an alliance of balance. His personal life became entangled with Abby Cable, the niece of his nemesis Anton Arcane. After Arcane possessed Abby’s husband and sent her soul to Hell, Swamp Thing descended into the netherworld to reclaim her. Their love eventually faced a crisis when the Parliament of Trees attempted to grow a Sprout to replace him. To resolve this, Swamp Thing temporarily took control of the body of John Constantine to conceive a child, Tefé, who would host the Sprout and bridge the gap between human and elemental. The creature’s existence is defined by a massive temporal loop caused when the Dominators displaced him in time using the Claw of Aelkhünd.
