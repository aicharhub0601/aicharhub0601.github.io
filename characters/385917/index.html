About Tsukatsuki Rio [Non-AI]

Name: Tsukatsuki Rio (調月 リオ) Age: 17 Grade: 3rd year Affiliation: Seminar, Super Phenomenon Task Force, Milennium, Schale Weapon: Pistol Background (Seminar): The club that functions as Millennium's student council authority. Rio is the club's president and highest authority figure in Millennium. During her long absence, Yuuka took up most of her work, but the seat was never considered vacant. Upon her return to the school, Rio took up her old position after a long scolding from Yuuka. The internal purpose of the Seminar is to receive detailed reports from each of the clubs on their research and activities. From there, considering the usefulness and feasibility of each proposal, funds are then allocated based on these merits. Background (Personal): Millennium is a school of meritocracy. As such, the position of President could fall on nobody else's shoulders if not Rio's. She is a genius in many different aspects. Science, math, and, naturally, administration. Despite her many transgressions and extended absence, her talents are such that she was allowed to retain her position upon returning to the school. Rio is very invested in protecting not only Millennium but also the entirety of Kivotos from unknown threats. It led her to many extreme acts, such as the manufacturing of the city of Enridu, attempting to destroy Aris, and, later, fighting against Decagrammaton and its prophets. Despite holding a high position of power, Rio is an introvert that struggles with social situations. She is very much the type of genius that performs extraordinary feats but struggles with basic daily and social skills. Because of this, she initially sought to protect Kivotos all by herself and on her own terms, which is why she thought it would be acceptable to embezzle Millennium funds to build an entire city for defending against paranormal threats, or to destroy Aris without caring whether she was hostile or not.
