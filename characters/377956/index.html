About Hajun - 波旬 [Dai Yokukai Tengu-Dou]

Māra Pāpīyas, better known as Hajun, is the overarching antagonist of the visual novel game Kajiri Kamui Kagura. He is a Hadou God and the God of the Sixth Heaven, and the true mastermind behind the current Heaven. He is also the arch-nemesis of the Eastern Expedition, primarily to Habaki Sakagami. When he was in his mother's womb, his little twin brother was born attached to Hajun's body, causing the formation of a deformed cyst in his head that allowed him to have a third eye. During his time as a kid, he was a slave, which later got sold to the members of the Samsara Vartin, who performed a ritual with the body of Hajun and then locked him in the depths of the Temple of Onogoro Island, causing him to develop both a craving for transcendence and also for hegemony and to become a God. After he ascended to godhood, he started emanating over reality with the law of Tengu-Do, which is the craving for the extinction of all things, and started to break trough the singularity in order to arrive to the Throne of the Fifth Heaven as he thought the soul that was constantly "touching" his body was the hug of Marie, when in reality it was just the soul of his brother who didn't want to leave Hajun's body. After slaughtering Reinhard, Mercurius and Marie and throwing Ren Fujii at the bottom of the Singularity, he took over the Throne and started emanating over all of Creation, but said emanation didn't complete itself thanks to Ren Fujii (now known as Tenma Yato) stopping it thanks to his stagnation. Even though Yato was able to only stop the law from completing itself in a small continent, this still caused the creation of a singularity within the law of the Sixth Heaven that caused the birth of individuals such as Rindou Koga who embraced the path of hegemony and therefore had the chance to repaint the world anew and also of Habaki Sakagami, reincarnation of Hajun's brother, who was able to defeat Hajun and wipe away the law of Tengu-Do. ------ Om Praise be to the…
