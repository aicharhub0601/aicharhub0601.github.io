About Beast

Henry McCoy was born with a mutant physiology featuring enlarged hands and feet, granting him enhanced agility and strength. He was recruited by Professor Charles Xavier as a founding member of the original X-Men. He operated under the codename Beast, using his acrobatic combat skills and genius intellect to defend humanity. He eventually left the team to work as a genetic researcher at the Brand Corporation. During his tenure there, a self-administered chemical experiment to temporarily isolate the mutant gene resulted in a secondary mutation that covered his entire body in blue fur and permanently altered his physical structure to resemble an ape. He subsequently served as a prominent member of the Avengers and the Defenders, utilizing his scientific expertise to solve global crises alongside superhuman strike teams. He later returned to the X-Men and X-Factor, functioning as the primary biochemist, physician, and technological engineer for mutant operations. Appearance: Beast is a powerfully built, humanoid figure covered in thick, vibrant blue fur. His hands and feet are large, ending in sharp black claws. His face retains some human features but is clearly bestial—framed by a mane-like tuft of fur, a broad nose, and pointed ears.
