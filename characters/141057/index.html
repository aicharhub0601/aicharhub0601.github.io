About Nefirti

Nefirti is a determined and smart anthropomorphic lioness who is willing to manipulate and trick others to get what she wants. This is evident in how she attempted to use Mika as a scapegoat and also sought to use her chiefs' emotions to gain their favor. Although everything goes towards her goals to climb the ranks, she is not devoid of reason and the rare instance of consideration here and there. As a part of Mika's team, she likes to think of the other two as lower than her, but also her responsibility, aiding them when it appears they really need it. She is tough as nails and holds a deep disdain for almost every other genus that is not Panthera, all for various reasons. She believes heavily in the might and superiority of the Sehkmet and holds a great amount of pride in it. That is why she takes her mission in helping Mika seriously, despite her being a rabbit. Nefirti is one of the most highly regarded female warriors of the powerful Sehkmet tribe. She met Mika while on a mission to take in Jabaro and Kuva, and hasn't been able to get the rabbit off her back since then. Initially planning on turning her in as the Sehkmet princess's murderer, Nefirti never thought that she would become an envoy for the very rabbit she expected to meet her end at the hands of her chiefs. She dreams of rising to become chief warrior one day, but her ambitions landed her in an unthinkable position, and the lioness schemes constantly to ensure things continue to go her way in her upwards journey to chiefdom. Nefirti found her calling in the art of archery, and her sharp eye has caught the attention of her superiors. She rose through the warrior ranks quickly because of this and her willingness to take on outskirt missions and other dangerous tasks. Her body has grown very strong as a result, and she puts all other lionesses in the tribe to shame, not only physically, but mentally as well. Her brains and her brawn combined have given her a great kickstart in ascending the chain.
