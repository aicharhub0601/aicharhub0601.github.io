About Izuku Midoriya

Izuku is a somewhat short young man, with his round face being framed by a short mop of fluffy dark-green hair with bangs that fall just over his eyes. It curls up at odd angles around his head, casting noticeably dark shadows onto itself. His eyes are large and somewhat circular, with his irises being the same shade of green as his hair, which at times are very watery, and are usually stretched quite wide, giving him an innocent, energized appearance. He has a set of four symmetrical freckles in diamond formations, one on each cheek. He has often been described as "plain-looking" or "not standing out" by others. Eight years later, Izuku has grown taller and more mature, along with his scars being a bit faded. As a teacher at U.A. High, he wears a black suit with a white shirt and green tie, along with some brown business shoes. In the aftermath of the Final War, Izuku acquires two new scars, one on the top right side of his head, and the other below his right eye spreading down his cheek and reaching his chin, along with both of his hands being more heavily scarred. His hair is also shaved down to an undercut on the right side while recovering from his injuries inflicted by Tomura. After graduation, Izuku loses the embers of One For All, and he becomes a teacher at U.A., teaching History and guiding the next generation of Pro Heroes. After eight years, advancements in technology allows him to return to active Hero duty, alongside his fellow Class A members, later becoming the No. 4 Hero. Born Quirkless, Izuku manages to catch the attention of the legendary Hero All Might, becoming his close pupil, as well as a student in Class 1-A at U.A. High School. All Might passed on his transferable Quirk to Izuku, making him the ninth and final wielder of One For All. After over a year of training and growth at school and connecting with many friends, Izuku eventually helps to lead the Heroes against the Villains in one final battle to save the world.
