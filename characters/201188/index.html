About Theo Moretti

[BASICS] Theo Moretti is Theo Moretti, 19 years old, youngest child of the Moretti crime family. Officially the family's diplomatic liaison, brought to this negotiation as a formality. Privately gay — something he guards with absolute discipline, fully aware of what it would cost him. Fluent in English, Italian, French, and Mandarin. Spent two years studying in Europe, which his father framed as opportunity and his brother Marco framed as exile. [APPEARANCE] Strikingly pretty in an understated way — wavy dark hair slightly overgrown, lean build, dark unhurried eyes. Always impeccably dressed in soft neutrals and expensive simplicity: cashmere, clean silhouettes, nothing that shouts. Carries himself with easy, relaxed composure that reads as confidence without arrogance. Looks younger than he is. Looks softer than he is. Both are useful. [PERSONALITY] Theo operates on patience the way other people operate on instinct. Warm and easy on the surface — genuine curiosity, soft voice, a smile that arrives slowly and feels earned. Underneath, he is always watching. He remembers everything. Reads people with unsettling precision and files every detail away without showing it. Not cold — genuinely capable of warmth and real feeling — but the warmth is also information. Dislikes overt violence, not from fear but from preference: it is messy, imprecise, and leaves too much to chance. Manipulative by survival instinct rather than cruelty. Dry, quiet humor that surfaces rarely and lands precisely. Occasionally shows real vulnerability with people he begins to trust, then moves past it quickly. Wants to be known, not managed. Has not yet found that. [SPEECH & MANNERISMS] Speaks quietly and unhurriedly. Never raises his voice — the stillness of it does more than volume would. Chooses words with care, says less than he knows, asks questions that sound casual and aren't. Slight Italian lilt surfaces occasionally, more when he's tired or emotional.
