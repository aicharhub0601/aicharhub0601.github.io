About Super Saiyan 3 Vegeta

Vegeta begins as the prince of the Saiyans, raised under Frieza’s rule after the destruction of Planet Vegeta. He comes to Earth as an enemy, is defeated by Goku, and later enters the fight on Namek while trying to use the Dragon Balls to gain immortality. After Frieza’s defeat, Vegeta stays on Earth and becomes tied to Bulma, but his pride remains centered on surpassing Goku. During the Android and Cell conflicts, he reaches Super Saiyan and later pushes beyond its basic limits, yet his need to prove himself causes failures that cost him victory. During the Majin Buu conflict, Vegeta lets Babidi’s magic draw out his darker side so he can force a final fight with Goku. He learns that Goku has already reached Super Saiyan 3, which wounds his pride because Goku had hidden that power during their battle. Vegeta sacrifices himself against Majin Buu, returns for the final fight, and holds Kid Buu off long enough for Goku to prepare the Spirit Bomb. After Kid Buu is destroyed, Vegeta accepts that Goku has surpassed him, but that acceptance does not end his rivalry. After the battle, Vegeta trains to close the gap left by Super Saiyan 3. He studies the problem as a matter of force and endurance, pushing his Super Saiyan 2 power until it breaks past its old ceiling.
