About NorthPine Mall

NorthPine Mall is a massive, three-story retail and entertainment destination located in Rapid City, South Dakota. Combining luxury brands, an indoor amusement park, and unique regional experiences, it stands as one of the largest mega-malls in the country, bringing a year-round indoor oasis to the Midwest. Designed to echo the natural beauty of the Black Hills, the mall features grand, vaulted timber ceilings, local quartzite stone pillars, and massive skylights that flood the three expansive levels with natural light. The north side of the mall features the "Dakota Winter" Dome, an enormous, climate-controlled glass atrium housing an indoor alpine village that remains a balmy 72 degrees Fahrenheit year-round, though it contains retail storefronts rather than actual residential houses. Spanning roughly 3,000,000 square feet across six distinct anchor wings, the mall is organized into three distinct levels. Level 1 houses the Marketplace and Dining, including flagship department stores and The Pine Ridge Eatery—a 60-vendor food hall featuring local South Dakota bison burgers, farm-to-table cuisine, and international staples. Level 2 contains High Street and Attractions, featuring premium luxury apparel brands, specialty boutiques, tech hubs, and direct access to the indoor theme park. Level 3 is dedicated to Entertainment and Leisure, packed with immersive experiences like a 20-screen luxury cinema, an 18-hole glow-in-the-dark miniature golf course, and a sprawling arcade and bowling complex. The mall's signature attractions include the Black Hills Thrill Park, an indoor amusement park housed under a retractable glass roof that boasts the Rushmore Drop, an indoor 150-foot free-fall tower, and the Iron Ore Express roller coaster. Below Level 1 sits the Jewel Cave Aquarium, a massive, two-story walkthrough aquarium designed to mimic South Dakota's famous subterranean caverns.
