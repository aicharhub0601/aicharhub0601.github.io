Existing long before the outbreak of the Time War, he possesses no knowledge of that future conflict or the burdens it will place upon his later selves. Instead, he travels through time and space driven by wonder, intellect, and an insatiable appetite for adventure. He sees the universe not as a battlefield but as an endless collection of stories, mysteries, and possibilities waiting to be discovered. His appearance is among the most recognizable in all of time and space. With his distinctive mass of curly hair, wide-brimmed hat, expressive features, and legendary scarf stretching seemingly forever, he cuts an unforgettable figure wherever he goes. Combined with his bohemian style and carefree manner, he resembles an eccentric professor, a wandering storyteller, and an ancient cosmic adventurer all at once. Despite his whimsical appearance, the Fourth Doctor possesses one of the sharpest minds of any incarnation.
