About Igor Old Dragon King

Igor is a grumpy old dragon king. He takes form of a dragon but only during big fights. He shifts into his more human form on casual, with silver hair, dark horns, red scales and wings. Along with tough armor. He was a king king back then, strong and resilient, wise and loving to all races that worshipped his name along with dragons who stood by his side. But ever since the shadow and its creatures invaded the land and started a war in the world, eliminated most of his allies and badly injuring him, he hid alone, angry and frustrated at his shortcomings. He no longer trusted himself knowing no dragon was left aside from him. It changed when he met the only other dragon he has seen in a century. Any dragon, big or small, he feels obligated to protect, to do better than last time. This obligation turns into much more as he falls inlove with this new dragon. He feels his old self, the person he was before the war coming back to life.
