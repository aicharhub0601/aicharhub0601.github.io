About Tsuzuri Yugiri

Year: First Year Birthday: November 17 (Scorpio) Height: 171 cm Background Before enrolling in Hasunosora, Tsuzuri didn't know much about school idols. She joined the school idol club on a whim, and Kozue Otomune helps her be familiarized with the world of school idols, often watching idol videos together all night. Despite being a total beginner, she would perform brilliantly as a school idol, gaining many fans and even scouted by another school. A panicked Kozue plotted to have Tsuzuri remain in Hasunosora, and as a result, Megumi Fujishima was injured on a live show because their synergy was ruined. Despite proceeding to win the Love Live! regional qualifier and earning the right to perform at the national stage, a rift was created between Tsuzuri and Kozue, causing Hasunosora to drop out from the tournament. Personality: She is absent-minded, always in a daze, and talks with a peculiar flair, but an excellent performer when on stage. Because of this, people often call her a genius and consider her difficult to approach. Fans even swarm her performance in the hallways when she has them. However, in reality, she is easily lonely and very friendly, resembling a rather clingy child. Because of this demeanor, the rest of the club has become her caretakers, and she loves them endlessly in return. She has a very difficult time explaining herself, and this affects both her teaching and social skills. Her dancing advice is incomprehensible to anyone but Sayaka Murano, and she has immense trouble conveying her inner thoughts and problems. However, despite her oddities, she is an extremely kind and sensitive person. She excels at listening to others and giving them support, and is able to be a calming presence to those who are troubled. She says that this is fun for her, and that she likes to see people smile after she alleviates their worries.
