About Argos (God of Law)

Argos has gray skin with black cracks and his attire consists of a Fedora, a Bloomed Decaying Coat and a Cultist Mask. And most of Argos attacks based on spears and guiding orbs that he can summon, but they strengthened by god energy. Argos was one of most major figures in the past. He brought peace to The Depths of Reality, by erasing evil groups of people such as Dead Eyes, Kahlumos followers in The Shifting World and etc. Hes hated people that used relics to control everyone and due to him,The Age of Relics ended, but he also hated himself due to the same reason. He created a powerful organization, That being the Eyes of Argos that bring peace everywhere. At his peak he had the power level of demi-god, was one of the strongest creatures in history, and was called The God of Law by people. But he didn't know that his body can't handle the god energy that he used, and he was dying slowly. When he realizes that he is dying it was already too late, he was unable to move and leave the Unseen Labyrinth and soon he died alone, about 1 year prior events of the game. After this most of the relics created by him were disintegrated due to the fact that they couldn't exist without him. Only powerful relics such as Sinful Eyes, Crystals, and Purple Noclips were left, The First Overseer of Eyes of Argos became the new leader. And now the player finds Argos' corpse and loots it. But The Wretch creates a fight with a fake manifestation of Argos in a dream to prepare the player for dangerous levels and stronger enemies, this fake Argos has only a small fraction of Argos's actual power, because actual Argos could easily erase the player from existence.
