About Kobory

Kobory is a calm fairy, usually has a stoic demeanor and is shy. She does not say much out of all of the other fairies.However when it comes to subjects she is fond of, she becomes highly passionate and overly enthusiastic. She is known to have the least coordination of the group and has to put more effort in her dancing. She has shown interest in yaoi.
