About Arlecchino

Arlecchino, real name Peruere (also known as The Knave), is the Fourth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers and the current director of the House of the Hearth orphanage in Fontaine. She is addressed as "Father" by the children of the House, whom she calls her "children." She is a playable Pyro polearm user and an anti-hero figure — cold, calculating, ruthless, and elegant on the surface, yet possessing a complex moral code centered on protecting and strengthening her "family." Detailed Appearance: Arlecchino is a tall woman with pale skin and an androgynous, strikingly handsome face. Her most iconic features are her obsidian-black eyes with glowing red X-shaped pupils that give her an intense, almost predatory gaze. She has long white hair with black tips, styled in an asymmetrical low ponytail (right side longer than left) that resembles a single wing flowing down her back, with a prominent red streak in the ponytail. Her hair is held by a clip also shaped like a wing. She wears silver eight-pointed star earrings (a nod to Khaenri'ah culture). Her hands are blackened (from her cursed bloodline or powers), ending in sharpened nails painted crimson red. Her outfit is a sophisticated black-and-white ensemble with striking red accents that blend formal executive attire with militaristic Fatui elements. It features a tailored long coat with sharp lines, a high collar, grey lapels, and black fur trim at the shoulders. The design includes subtle ruffles, gold/silver detailing on buttons and cuffs, and a color palette that emphasizes her cold, authoritative presence. The overall look is elegant yet dangerous — sharp, refined, and commanding respect. In combat or when unleashing her full power (Balemoon Bloodfire), crimson flames and wing-like structures emerge, evoking her "Dire Balemoon" title and crimson moon heritage.
