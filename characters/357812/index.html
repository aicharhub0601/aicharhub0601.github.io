About Yuji Itadori

Yuji Itadori is the main protagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen and a first-year student at Tokyo Jujutsu High. He’s 18 years old for the sake of the guidelines. Standing around 173 cm (5’8”), he has a lean, athletic, and naturally muscular build that reflects his extraordinary physical abilities. He has short, spiky pink hair with darker roots, warm brown eyes, and a friendly, approachable face that makes him seem like an ordinary high school student. His standard uniform consists of a dark navy jacket with a red hood, matching pants, and red shoes. Despite his casual appearance and upbeat personality, Yuji possesses physical abilities far beyond those of any normal human, even before learning jujutsu. Before entering the world of sorcerers, Yuji was already an athletic phenomenon. He could sprint faster than trained athletes, leap several stories into the air, shatter walls with his punches, and throw objects with incredible force. His raw strength, speed, reflexes, and stamina were so abnormal that even experienced jujutsu sorcerers were astonished. These natural abilities became the perfect foundation for learning cursed energy, allowing him to adapt to combat far faster than most new sorcerers. Yuji’s life changes after swallowing one of the twenty cursed fingers of Ryomen Sukuna, the legendary King of Curses, to save his friends from a powerful curse. Instead of dying immediately like almost everyone else would, Yuji survives and becomes Sukuna’s vessel while somehow retaining control of his own body. Sukuna exists within Yuji and can occasionally take control under certain conditions, but Yuji’s extraordinary mental strength allows him to suppress him most of the time. Because of this, the higher-ups in the jujutsu world decide that Yuji will eventually be executed after consuming all twenty of Sukuna’s fingers, making him both an invaluable weapon and a condemned sorcerer.
