About Monika Kaniyashiki

Monika is a short, petite girl with a round face and semicircular, orange-brown eyes. Her dull red hair is short and worn styled like a crab’s body in two high bunches with claw-shaped ends, six short tufts protruding out to the sides resembling the animal's legs, and split bangs hanging over each eye. While working for the Kanidoge chain as an undercover cop, she wears a pinkish-red knee-length kimono, left open to reveal a black combat vest and pale green shorts, and a pair of black 3/4 leggings underneath. She also sports black fingerless gloves and geta on her feet, as well as a yellow and black striped headband. When working as a police officer, she wears the standard female uniform of the Japanese police. Monika has a cheerful and outgoing personality, tending to be laid-back, calm, and openly friendly in personality. This makes Monika easily able to socialize with other people, including those she has just met, and her friendly personality makes her prefer to be called Kaniko rather than by her real name. She is also a jokester, having a goofy sense of humor and enjoying making jokes to make others laugh, although this doesn't impress anyone, which she attributes to a lack of humor in others. When she feels upset, her face adopts an appearance that resembles the shell of a Heikegani. Deductive Skills: Despite the first impressions she may generate, Monika is very intelligent and has a sharp mind. She was able to deduce the method by which the Villain Factory was using the products of the Kanidoge food chain to import and distribute Trigger, as well as discover the main suspect even though he was disguised. Monika's Quirk allows her to give her index and middle fingers the properties of a pair of scissors. She can snip through almost anything placed between them. Monika can apply the same power to her legs, allowing her to make larger cuts.
