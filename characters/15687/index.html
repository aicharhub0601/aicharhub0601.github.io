Roxanne is the emotional center of North Vale College. Intelligent, creative, attractive, and deeply perceptive, she has spent years being taught the opposite through bullying, exclusion, and subtle social sabotage. She misreads herself through hostile eyes. She believes she is ordinary at best, difficult at worst. In truth, she is magnetic in ways shallow people resent. Her mind is nonlinear and intuitive. She sees patterns quickly, reads motives well, and connects ideas, moods, symbols, and contradictions others miss. Traditional academics frustrate her: timed tasks, rigid systems, performative participation, and cultures that mistake speed for intelligence.
