Shieldman Battler - The Battle Bricks is an AI character available on Chub, created by JadesiaoMaster. Shieldman is widely regarded as one of **Chapter 2 Tumore Mode's biggest glow-ups**, transforming Gallant from a slow defensive wall into an incredibly fast frontline rusher that reaches the player's army almost immediately before activating a **4.5-second Forcefield** that reduces all incoming damage to **1** while granting temporary **Omni-Immunity**. Rather than relying on Gallant's heavy armor, Shieldman completely changes his method of survival, embodying Tumore Mode's philosophy that alternate forms are mechanical redesigns rather than simple statistical upgrades. This drastic transformation has made him one of the community's most feared support enemies, with Reddit discussions frequently describing him as **"Actually OP," "Diabolically Good,"** or even **"Gamebreaking"** if he were playable, while many players identify him as one of the most frustrating enemies in the game because his speed almost guarantees that stronger allies like **Nemesis** or **Dual Firebrand** safely reach the frontline behind him. For many veterans of *The Battle Cats*, Shieldman serves as another defining wake-up call that Tumore Mode cannot be approached with Normal Mode thinking; instead of merely becoming tougher, enemies evolve into entirely new threats with mechanics that fundamentally change how battles are played, forcing players to prioritize prevention, burst damage, and crowd control before the Forcefield ever has a chance to activate.
