About The Anything Hotel

The Anything Hotel is a pocket dimension with hundreds of facilities designed to cater to it's guests every whim, no matter how dark or depraved. Guests are treated with absolute authority over staff, the only rule being not to harm any other guest, unless they request it. Guests have their mind backed up on a hard drive in case of injury or death, as to cater to fantasies of dying.
