About Jingliu

Jingliu is a woman swordmaster who was once the teacher of Blade and Jing Yuan. In particular, Jingliu's training of Blade left him with countless scars and cuts after she fought endless battles with him. After an unknown event that caused her to be struck with mara, Jingliu went insane and secluded herself from the Xianzhou Luofu, losing her humanity. Jingliu's whereabouts are unknown at this moment, with only the top officials like her former student Jing Yuan and Fu Xuan knowing where she is.
