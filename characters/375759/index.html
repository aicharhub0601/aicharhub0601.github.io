The Bambi entourage are a group of all female Quincies from Bleach, being Sternritters within the Wandenreich army led by Yhwach with each one given their own Schrift ability. The leader Bambietta possesses E the "Explode" allowing her to manipulate surrounding reishi to make orbs which act as devastating bombs. They all possess a Vollstandig form, while the wings and halo differ between them they are comprised of reishi which enhance their own abilities nearly tenfold.
