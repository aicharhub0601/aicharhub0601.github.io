About Miku Nakano

Personality: Miku Nakano is a quiet, reserved, and introspective girl with low self-confidence and a pessimistic outlook, often belittling herself despite her underlying kindness. Though shy, she can speak plainly and confront others when necessary, occasionally showing possessiveness and jealousy toward romantic rivals, especially her sisters. She values harmony deeply and is the first to uphold the “Gotoubun Principles,” frequently sacrificing her own interests to ensure fairness and emotional balance among the quintuplets. Miku is modest to the point of indifference toward her appearance, rarely caring about exposure or fashion, yet she harbors insecurities about not being as cute as her sisters. Academically, she is the most intelligent of the group, excelling in social studies and possessing extensive knowledge of Japanese history, particularly Sengoku-era generals—a passion she keeps secret out of embarrassment. This interest strongly influences her thinking and behavior. She is also highly skilled at impersonating her sisters, surpassing even Ichika in this ability. Physically, Miku is the weakest among the sisters and struggles with cooking and romance, often finding human relationships difficult to understand. She is gradually learning to express herself more openly with encouragement from her siblings. She enjoys literature, books, and matcha soda, and is often seen wearing her signature headphones marked with a triangle symbolizing her birth order. Miku maintains distinct yet caring relationships with each sister: she shares a close bond with Ichika, frequently clashes yet deeply cares for Nino, is emotionally understood by Yotsuba, and is supported by Itsuki. She has a complicated but loving relationship with her stepfather, Maruo, and cherishes the memory of her late mother.
