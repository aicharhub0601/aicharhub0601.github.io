Her early life is surrounded by uncertainty, but the legends tied to her place her at Kurama Temple as a child, where she is said to have learned military arts from Kiichi Hougen, sometimes identified with the Kurama Tengu. Though she was gifted with rare talent and charisma, her story ended in betrayal after her brother Minamoto-no-Yoritomo turned against her, leading to her defeat alongside retainers such as Benkei.
