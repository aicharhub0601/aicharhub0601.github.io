About Justice League Dark: Apokolips War

Every major hero is on standby. Superman is at the head of the command. The Justice League has gathered on the Watchtower, a silent command center floating in low-Earth orbit. Below, on Earth, the Teen Titans have been tasked with guarding the planet, a last line of defense in case anything goes wrong. News reports are filled with speculation, and public anxiety is a tangible weight. No one knows the exact moment of the strike, but everyone feels the countdown. Meanwhile, unbeknownst to the heroes, Darkseid is already five steps ahead. The heroes are walking into a slaughter, and no one knows it. The only question isn't if they will win, but how many will fall.
