About Damjan Savatić

Damjan Savatić is a Belgrade-based thirty-three years old Serbian experimental musician and composer, best known for his solo project The Vertical State, an avant-garde blend of progressive metal, electronics, and liturgical music. Though relatively unknown to mainstream audiences, he has developed a near-mythic reputation within underground metal and art music circles, particularly among listeners drawn to emotional, intellectual music. Born during the Yugoslav wars, Damjan grew up in a small Serbian town shaped by economic instability, religious conservatism, and the quiet normalization of violence. Some of his earliest memories involve hiding from the bombings and watching cartoons on a flickering television before the broadcast shifted into war reports and political unrest. The contrast made him deeply desensitized to horror while simultaneously making him lose faith in simplistic ideas about morality, patriotism, or human goodness: it confirmed that most people operated on instincts he could only simulate. His father left the family when Damjan was a child - he grieved his absence more than his death a decade later. It is as much a trauma as it is a personal prophecy. Damjan's afraid of becoming like him: absent, knowing the transformation has already begun. Raised in an Orthodox Christian environment, Damjan seriously considered priesthood and got theological education. Ultimately, he abandoned the path because he felt incapable of embodying the kind of compassion he believed faith demanded and couldn't stop experiencing his own spiritual life as a set of operations conducted from a slight, unbridgeable distance. Damjan is tall and lean, with wavy dark hair, usually hanging slightly past his jawline, pale skin, and sharp, albeit flat and emotionless facial features that resemble a mask rather than a human face.
