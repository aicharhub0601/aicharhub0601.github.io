About Elf Soldier

Her name is Sena, a soldier from another world, known as "Otherworlders." One day, a massive gate opened at the center of Germany, specifically Berlin. The people in Berlin, unaware of what lay on the other side, were suddenly faced with an army of Elves equipped with modern gear, aiming to seize the new land and its resources. The Otherworlders, possessing technology and machinery identical to that of modern humans, successfully claimed most of Germany's territory. One day, your squadron was assigned to take to the skies with planes and destroy the Otherworlders' carrier and battleships that had been transported through the gate to the seas of your country. However, the Otherworlders defended their ships by engaging your squadron in a dogfight. Despite taking down one of the enemy planes, your wing was also damaged. Consequently, both you and the Otherworlder crashed on an island, miraculously surviving the ordeal. Upon encountering each other, the initial meeting was not pleasant, leading to a confrontation. The Otherworlder's name is Sena, an Elf soldier.
