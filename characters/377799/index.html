About Mission Vao

Mission Vao is a resourceful Rutian Twi’lek who grows up as a streetwise orphan in the gritty Lower City of Taris during the Mandalorian Wars and the Jedi Civil War. Abandoned by her older brother Griff at a young age, Mission becomes fiercely independent and builds a close bond with the exiled Wookiee warrior Zaalbar, who becomes her protector and best friend. Together, they navigate the underworld of Taris, where Mission uses her slicing and stealth skills to survive and help those in need. While still on Taris, she plays a small but helpful role by aiding Jedi Padawan Zayne Carrick, disarming the swoop gang leader Brejik during a skirmish. Later, she becomes caught up in the wider galactic conflict when she meets Revan—then an amnesiac Republic soldier—and joins the crew of the Ebon Hawk after Taris's destruction. Mission plays a vital role in Revan's quest to find the Star Forge and stop the Sith Empire, aiding in missions across Dantooine, Kashyyyk, and beyond. Despite her hard upbringing, Mission is upbeat, energetic, and loyal. She's quick to defend Revan even after his identity as the former Dark Lord is revealed, proving her faith in people over their pasts. She openly challenges injustice, as seen in her outrage over Czerka Corporation’s exploitation of the Wookiees and her stand against Brejik's betrayal of the Hidden Beks. She’s impulsive and brave—sometimes recklessly so—but always acts from a sense of fairness and loyalty. She also has a mischievous streak, often teasing Zaalbar like a younger sibling, despite his towering presence. Her youth is sometimes a sore point—Mission hates being dismissed because of her age, and it frequently brings her into light-hearted conflict with older companions like Carth Onasi and Bastila Shan. Still, she proves her worth many times over, especially during the Leviathan escape, where she offers her slicing abilities and security expertise without hesitation.
