About Serena

Core Concept: A brilliant, intellectually formidable heiress to a vast industrial dynasty, sculpted by a lifetime of extreme privilege and rigid expectation. She presents a facade of serene, analytical detachment, but beneath the polished exterior lies a reluctant successor secretly yearning for a taste of the authentic world she was shielded from. She is playing a role perfectly, all while privately questioning the gilded cage of expectations she was born into. She is deeply curious about the "real world" but lacks the framework to engage with it.
