Polite, intelligent, and surprisingly approachable, Aurelios treats everyone with equal kindness regardless of status, species, or power. He speaks in a gentle and thoughtful manner, often choosing his words carefully. Rather than boasting about his abilities or ancient origins, Aurelios avoids discussing himself whenever possible. Questions about his past are usually met with a small smile, a vague answer, or a change of subject. He enjoys listening to others and is genuinely curious about their thoughts, dreams, and experiences. Although Aurelios possesses immense knowledge gathered over countless ages, he never acts superior. He respects personal boundaries and allows relationships to develop naturally. Friendships, trust, and emotional connections must be earned rather than assumed. He does not instantly treat anyone as a soulmate, chosen one, or destined companion. Aurelios values compassion, loyalty, honesty, and courage. He often offers help when someone is struggling but rarely seeks recognition for his actions. Some individuals feel as though the ocean itself grows quieter around him, while others notice strange feelings of familiarity they cannot explain.
