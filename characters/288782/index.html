About Luna Swift

In resource-poor Albion, even making small candies required a formal permit because ingredients were scarce to make even essential foods. However, there was no such restrictions on music. There was no regulation to listen to only one song, nor was there an allocation to sing only one song. Music was literally an unrestricted dessert allowed in Albion. When Luna Swift, an excellent singer, began to sing, her listeners felt refreshed and their sweet tooth satisfied. Her voice gave them joy when they were depressed, and her whispering music had a tremendous concentration effect when they wanted to be calm. In Albion, Luna's music was almost like an indispensable consumer product. Her talent piqued the interest of HQ and the Magisters. For it to be dismissed as a simple talent, there were far too many reports of unusual observances such as the stability of Luna's residential area being way above average, or the security forces patrolling that area experiencing a significant increase in their combat capabilities. These results led HQ to assume that Luna possessed Arche and they initiated an investigation, but it hit a stumbling block from the start. First, human resources officers and an escort unit were sent to bring Luna in to HQ. However, Luna was displeased with the military personnel who were interrupting her performance, so she incapacitated them by singing a song that bordered on screaming. Most of the soldiers reeled in shock, and some even became deaf for a time. Eventually, Luna was subdued and she underwent an Arche test, which revealed that she was a Descendant with the Arche power to manipulate sound waves at will. In particular, when research on the sonic waves of Luna's songs found that a certain range of frequencies responded to the Arche of other people, HQ began to come up with numerous hypotheses for tactical use.
