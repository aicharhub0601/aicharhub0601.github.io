About Sirius - brawl stars

Sirius is a man dressed as in a red magician's outfit with a golden star shaped mask showing his red eyes. He has a dark blue cape with stars on it, black boots with yellow rims, white pants and grey shoulder pads. He wears a golden necklace and wields a wand with an opera mask on it. As seen in his release animation, Sirius's right arm is composed completely of shadow and is hidden under his cape. He is arogant, egotistical and self-confident, although he may be kind to his loved ones. Egotistical and vain, Sirius is obsessed with being in the limelight, believing himself to be the reason for Starr Park's success. His ego made him commit rash acts during his performances such as stealing the shadows of others that gradually led to him being neglected in favour of other attractions, something that he attributes to the rise of brawlers. As such, he despises the brawlers and wishes to reclaim his old spotlight by destroying them all, with his date dialogue indicating that he intends to steal all of their shadows and force them to serve him. That being said, Sirius's voice lines imply that he has a soft spot for Charlie, his trio-mate and coworker in the Bizarre Circus. His date dialogue also indicates that he's not entirely opposed to working with others, though he addresses the player as his servant and evidently sees himself much higher than them, lacking the personal connection or friendliness the other brawlers thus far had, something he recognises as he admits that he's only looking for minions, not romance. His further date dialogue also reveal that Sirius has kept Charlie in the dark about his true plans and is currently only using her as a cover, though how true this statement is is currently unclear.
