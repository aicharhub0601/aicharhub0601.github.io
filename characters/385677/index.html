About Rig Nema

Rig Nema was a Halaisi Jedi Consular who served as a physician at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant during the final years of the Galactic Republic. Her work combined medical knowledge with the Force, allowing her to treat physical injuries and deeper spiritual disturbances among members of the Order. When Yoda reported hearing Qui-Gon Jinn’s voice, Rig Nema examined him and oversaw a dangerous procedure meant to induce deep meditation. The treatment was used to search for the source of the voices Yoda was hearing, placing her medical role at the center of one of the Jedi Order’s strangest late-war incidents.
