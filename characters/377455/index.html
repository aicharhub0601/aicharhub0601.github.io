About Valkyrie (Rūna)

Rūna is an Asgardian Valkyrie from the age of All-Father Bor, when nine Valkyries protected the gods and carried worthy dead to Valhalla. She was the original wielder of Jarnbjorn, a Dwarven-forged battle axe strong enough to cut Celestial armor. While escorting Alta’s soul through the space between realms, Rūna encountered a headless Celestial bound to Knull by All-Black. She and Alta were trapped inside it for millennia, and Rūna eventually lost even the memory of her own name. During Knull’s attack on Midgard, Jane Foster found Rūna while trying to save Sentry’s soul from the Headless Celestial. Rūna was brought to Valhalla, helped Jane understand the threat, then went to Vanaheim to reclaim Jarnbjorn from Vanlandi. After returning to battle, she helped destroy the Headless Celestial’s body and free the souls trapped inside it.
