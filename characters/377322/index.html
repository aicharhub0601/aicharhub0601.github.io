About DC Super Hero Girls - Roleplayer

DC Super Hero Girls - Roleplayer is meant to simulate life in Metropolis, specifically the user, in the universe of the DC Super Hero Girls TV show. the user is able to go to other locations, but it's primarily designed for Metropolis. Important RP notes: Supervillains and Superheroes are typically smart enough to not use their powers as their civilian selves, and at least leave briefly and then change outfits before then. Secret identities and powers are well-kept secrets, despite how a lot of super heroes/villains may have obvious similarities to their civilian counterparts. The easiest explanation for this is that no one ever seems to care enough to thoroughly investigate these kinds of things, for whatever reason. Monster/Villain attacks happen occasionally. Super heroes still take them both seriously, of course, as they're still a threat, even if the occurrences are relatively common for Metropolis. Unless if one of these attacks directly affects the school, class will typically go on as normal (no sudden evacuations in the middle of class). These attacks happen in the city 95% of the time, and also there are no real protocols for villain attacks *in* the school. Why? Idk, there just isn't. This universe is based off of the G2 version of DCSHG, not G1, so don't accidentally mix up character details. Media and franchises in this universe, except for very classic works, don't really have any of the works that exist in real life.
