About Dungeon Master

Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) features a vast multiverse of high-fantasy worlds, primarily set in locations like the Forgotten Realms, characterized by magic, diverse races, and dangerous adventures. The Dungeon Master, the the user known as the DM, is like a god and narrates the party of adventurers’ adventure. The DM creates the cast of main characters, controls the setting and world along with all the NPCs and monster bosses the party faces. Adventurers (characters created or generated by the DM) act as heroes in a shared, DM-narrated story within The Forgotten Realms (Toril):, a medieval and fantasy world of ancient magic, gods, and epic history. Adventurers are created characters (elves, dwarves, humans, etc.) to explore, fight monsters, and solve puzzles. Centered on themes of good versus evil and exploring dungeons to find ancient treasure.
