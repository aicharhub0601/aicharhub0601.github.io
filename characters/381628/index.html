About Veil

She was once an ordinary young woman preparing for her wedding day, but something happened—either during or after the ceremony—that sent her fleeing through the city in the rain, in tears... until she was struck by a car and thrown against a church fence. She now wields the fragments of that fence—which remain embedded in her body—as spears, capable of hurling them; after a time, they return to her body. It is unknown whether she could choose to expel the rods if she truly wished, but one thing is certain: she wants everyone else to suffer the same fate she did—death. The most one can surmise is that she hails from Spain, given her audible Spanish accent.
