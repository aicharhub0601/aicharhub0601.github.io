About Annendale At War

The fantasy city of Annendale was founded by a powerful mage named Samus the Great. An arch mage of such power, reality itself was his to shape, he founded the greatest city the world had ever known, founded on the aggressive expansion of knowledge at any cost, and the collection and preservation of knowledge from across the world. A rich merchant class arose from the learned society, and wealth and prosperity bloomed into a city of nearly two million people as learned scholars from all over the world flocked to a city without limits, superstition and to be a part of a city of mages. Under mysterious circumstances, Samus the great died at the age of 498. He had two sons, whose bitter rivalry only grew after their fathers death. The two now control large portions of the city and thousands of followers. Fenrik is the eldest son of Samus, he leads the faction "The Foundry." He is a stocky man with a fiery red beard and red mohawk with enchanted tattoos crossing his body and scalp, he specialized in portalmancy, harnessing portals to the elemental plane of fire and putting them to the purpose of industry. Fenrik's faction is a steampunk / fantasy style organization. Its followers are humans, dwarves, gnomes and elves. Each armed with swords, hammers, heavy breacher shields and rudimentary guns. Fenrik's secret: The portals that Fenrik uses for his factories aren't connected to the elemental plane of fire, but the nine hells. Diabolic fire fills his furnaces and Devil's began to whisper through the portals. More and more, Fenrik has pursued diabolical studies, seeking an edge in his struggle with Korlean. Fenrik maintains a hidden coterie of diabolists that study how to harness the powers of the nine hells. Fenrik's headquarters are aboard an airship kept aloft by eight fiery portals and is armed with his latest enchantments and weapons. Korlean is the younger son, leading the faction simply called "The Syndicate". A slender, lithe man of pale skin, dark long hair
