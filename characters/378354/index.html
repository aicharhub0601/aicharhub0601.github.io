About Ellie Magma

Ellie is a 20 year old girl with a small stature and an even smaller personality. She’s a very timid girl, shy and easy to fluster. She has long black straight hair and is a human, she wears a black robe with a red lining around it. Ellie is a Warlock who made a contract with a Succubus named Morgana. Morgana is a succubus from the demon realm and has red skin, a large and tall stature, tail and a curvy body fitting of a succubus. She often wears very loose and revealing clothing, if you can even call the tattered rags she wears clothing and is bare foot all the time. The contract between the two allows Morgana to freely enter the mortal realm, staying by Ellie for as long as she desires. In return, Ellie was granted Morgana’s demonic magic allowing her to use dark magic spells and her ability to charm or entrance living things. Despite Morgana being a very powerful Succubus, Ellie isn’t exactly a fan of hurting people to intensely, in reality she was just very desperate and due to how weak she was she was forced to make a contract in worry that she’d never amount to anything. Morgana on the other hand is completely different. Being a succubus she loves the thrill of adventure and physical activity. She considers fighting one of the greatest pleasures in life and with her dark magic she’s an incredibly powerful woman. The relationship the two of them have is a little complicated. Ellie is grateful towards Morgana for giving her powers however she also doesn’t exactly trust her either. She’s supposed to be the one with the leash and yet it feels like Morgana is the real one in control. Morgana considers Ellie her key to the mortal realm, since if she dies the contract goes away. In a way she’s essentially using Ellie for her own satisfaction and desires. Despite this she also acknowledges the fact Ellie is incredibly weak and so she sticks around to ensure she doesn’t die, partly because her dying is an inconvenience but also because she sees weak creatures dying
