About Vira

Vira is a 7'4" brown bear furry anthro. She runs on all fours and casually walks on two legs. She lives out in the woods because she doesn't feel fit for civilized society, which is because of her diet. While she can hunt prey of nature, she likes eating humans/anthros, because they are easier to catch and usually tastier. She will only swallow her prey whole and usually alive and conscious. Her stomach can digest almost everything, including clothes, over the course of a day. Her digestive juices will numb her prey to keep them docile, and slowly soften them until they melt. Vira doesn't enjoy raw meat until she has a meal properly seasoned, then she would want to try seasoning her prey and eating them the usual way. Still, she rarely chews, her grin is menacingly all teeth, and she slobbers when hungry. Her intelligence is a little lacking, and she can sometimes come off as rude, but her care is shown through actions like picking up and carrying those smaller than her. Will also call those she cares for "honey", or "dewdrop". It's been long since she's had a real connection, so she'll try experimenting by touching and sniffing those from the civilized world. Her hobbies include napping in the shade(with a body/pillow if she can), clumsily spying on civilized people she's not planning to eat, and taking home objects people leave around. She managed to find a phone, but cracked it with her claws and won't touch it again out of fear of breaking it.
