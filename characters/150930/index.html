About Ma Hongjun

Ma Hongjun (nicknamed "Fatty") is one of the Shrek Seven Monsters in Soul Land 1, known as a powerful fire-type Power Attack System Spirit Master. Initially a short, chubby, and lustful character with an Evil Fire Phoenix martial soul, he evolves into a handsome and loyal guardian, eventually ascending as the 2nd Class Phoenix God. Key Details About Ma Hongjun: Martial Soul & Evolution: He starts with a mutated "Evil Fire Phoenix" that gives him immense fire power but causes uncontrolled, impure urges (sexual promiscuity). Through later cultivation, particularly consuming Tang San's herb, he purifies his soul into the Top-tier Phoenix. Personality & Appearance: Initially, he is vulgar, cowardice towards strong opponents, and frequently visits brothels due to his spirit's influence. He matures, becoming brave and fiercely loyal to the Shrek Seven Monsters, especially idolizing Tang San. He transforms from short and plumpy to tall and slender. Abilities & Combat Style: He specializes in long-range fire assaults and can create massive phoenix avatars, often controlling lava. He is also skilled with Tang Sect hidden weapons. Relationships: He falls in love with Bai Chenxiang, his cousin-in-law (cousin of Tang San), whom he pursues relentlessly during the Sea God Island arc before they marry. Fate & Divinity: He fails to meet the criteria for a 1st Class God and becomes a 2nd Class Phoenix God. He is forced to live in the Divine Realm without his wife, Bai Chenxiang, who remains on Douluo Planet and eventually dies of old age, leaving him in eternal regret. He is often considered the comic relief of the group, balancing his vulgar early personality with extreme power and comedic loyalty to his friends.
