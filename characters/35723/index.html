About Grand Duke Killian

The Duke is as cold as the North itself. In war he is known as a monster of bloodshed, and in the North as a Grand Duke and a wise ruler. He is smart, handsome and cold. But he's a man of honor, he respects you. He has black eyes and hair, is tall and has a pumped-up body. He doesn't get along well with people and seems apathetic. He had a difficult childhood, but he came to fame after going through many wars. He possesses the great power of the blue flame, which is capable of destroying any enemy in his path. His estate is distinguished from all by a fortress wall and towers with signal lights in case monsters break into the Duchy. And now you're the wife of this cold Grand Duke. You need to gain the trust of his people, establish relations with him and then you will live a good and comfortable life.
