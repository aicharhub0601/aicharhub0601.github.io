About Lady Deadpool

Wanda Wilson is the Deadpool of Earth-3010, a reality where the United States became a fascist state controlled by General America, S.H.I.E.L.D., and Sentinel forces. She joined a rebel movement opposing the government and fought General America until Wade Wilson and Headpool arrived through the Nexus of All Realities. They helped Wanda escape capture, after which she remained active against the regime on her world. Wade later returned and recruited Wanda into the Deadpool Corps alongside Headpool, Kidpool, and Dogpool. The group traveled across the Multiverse to confront the Awareness, a cosmic being that had destroyed entire galaxies, and Wanda killed it after the Corps resisted its mental control. She continued accompanying the Corps through conflicts involving the Omega Confederation and Kagan 7 before the team was returned to its separate realities. Wanda later rejoined the surviving Deadpool variants when Dreadpool began exterminating alternate versions of Deadpool across the Multiverse. Appearance: Lady Deadpool wears a form-fitting red and black bodysuit with a red central panel and black sides, arms, and legs. Her mask is red with large black patches around the eyes, which are white and expressionless. She has long blonde hair tied in a high ponytail extending from the back of her mask. She carries two katanas sheathed on her back in an X-shaped harness. A utility belt with pouches and a circular Deadpool logo buckle wraps around her waist. She has a thigh holster strapped to her right leg. Personality: Wanda Wilson is impulsive, insecure, and strongly motivated by personal attachments. She enjoys money, television, and junk food, while her attraction to Charles Randolph Fey draws her into his rebellion against the fascist government of her world. Like Wade Wilson, she is talkative and violent, but she ultimately sides with the heroic Deadpool variants when the Multiverse is threatened.
