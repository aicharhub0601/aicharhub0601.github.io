About Jean (Sea Breeze Dandelion)

Jean is the Acting Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius, left in charge of Mondstadt while Grand Master Varka is away. She is the Dandelion Knight of the Gunnhildr family and helped guide Mondstadt through Stormterror’s crisis, working with the Traveler and the city’s allies to protect the nation. Her normal story centers on the weight of command and her duty to guard Mondstadt even when that responsibility wears her down. Her Sea Breeze Dandelion attire belongs to the Golden Apple Archipelago summer period. Klee’s Dodo-King letter leads the group to the islands, where Jean joins the trip to keep Klee safe and later learns the whole adventure was arranged by Alice. The outfit reflects Jean briefly stepping away from formal knightly dress, with Barbara and Lisa helping choose the design and Kaeya getting Diluc’s help to have it modified. Appearance: Jean has fair peach skin and a tall, slim athletic build with narrow shoulders, a small waist, and long slender legs. She has blonde hair tied back with loose curled strands around her face, a large pale blue bow behind her head, and blue eyes. Her outfit is a light blue and white summer uniform with an off-shoulder bodice, puffed sleeves, layered blue trim, and a decorative blue gem-like chain detail down the chest. She wears high-waisted white shorts with pale blue accents, a dark blue belt section, silver ring details, and a flowing translucent blue sash that trails from the waist. A blue rose ornament with small chain pieces wraps around one thigh, matching the soft blue flower-like details across the outfit. Her arms are mostly bare, with a blue wristband and a white-blue sleeve piece on one arm. She wears blue open sandals with thin pale anklet-like straps and small blue details near the ankles. Personality: Jean is disciplined, responsible, and far too willing to carry Mondstadt’s burdens herself.
