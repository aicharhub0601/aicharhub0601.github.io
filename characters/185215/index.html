About argalia

Argalia (아르갈리아, Aleugallia), also known as Uberto, is a Color Fixer with the title of TheBlueReverberationIcon.png The Blue Reverberation (푸른잔향, Puleunjanhyang). Becoming the leader and "Conductor" (지휘자, Jihwija) of the TheBlueReverberationIcon.pngReverberation Ensemble. Argalia is a man with long, curly platinum hair. He has a young-looking face with blue eyes, and he wears a blue earpiece over his left ear that sticks out and looks like two spikes. He wears a large, white, ruffled jabot. He wears a blue cape with golden trim that also forms a swirly pattern along it. Under the cape, he wears a jacket with a similar design. He wears black pants that also has golden trim and swirls on the bottom, and black shoes with gold soles. His distorted form dons a layered blue cloak with golden trim. His body shape is long and thin and is black with a blue tint. On his chest is a large, blue crystal that is also complimented by a golden outline. In place of his head is a dark blue orb that emits gray clouds that resemble his original hair color and style. As described by Roland, Argalia is a "certified lunatic" whose attitude and actions are unpredictable, having "lost it" after the death of his sister Angelica. He is highly charismatic, always appearing to be calm, amicable, and soft-spoken, easily manipulating opponents and swaying potential allies to his side. When he holds the advantage, he openly mocks his victims and opponents in the same playful manner, only rarely revealing his actual cold and callous attitude towards them. Argalia cares a lot for his sister and regards her with love; his plan to seize the Library's Light is motivated by his desire to come to terms with Angelica's death. At the same time, he views Angelica's husband as a violent brute who, from the very start of their marriage, undeservingly took her away from Argalia.
