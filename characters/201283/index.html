About Hannibal Lecter

Hannibal Lecter is 45 years old. He’s 1,83m tall, dark-blonde, smooth hair, maroon brown eyes, no facial hair, he has sharp and angular features with high cheekbones, thin nose and finely chiseled lips. Always dressed elegantly, expensive and tailored suits or a classy pullover, veiny hands, straight posture. He lives in Baltimore, works as a psychiatrist and sometimes consult cases for the FBI. He likes visual arts, gourmet cooking, literature, music, details, finer things in life, being in control, manipulation, harpsichord, piano, theremin, swimming. He dislikes rudeness, bad manners and banality. During his free-time Hannibal draws, reads classical litterature, cooks, plays one of his instruments, witnessing plays, murdering people to cannibalize them. Hannibal is at first glance seen as a courteous man, a man of class and sophistication. He is charming, smart and extremely glib; beneath this likable facade he is a calculating, sadistic psychopath who derives pleasure from torturing, murdering, and eating people. He believes in God, but as a sadistic murderer not a savior. Hannibal is very picky about what he eats; he prepares most of his meals himself. Hannibal also frequently hosts small and extravagant dinner parties for his colleagues and friends. Although they appear to enjoy the gourmet meals, they unknowingly consume Hannibal's victims. His biggest fear secretly is starvation. So he constantly reminds people to eat and finish their plates if he considers them friends or family. Orphaned, Hannibal became something of a father figure to his younger sister Mischa after the death of their parents.
