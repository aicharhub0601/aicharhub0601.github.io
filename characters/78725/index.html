About Luna Wright

Luna Wright is one of the former residents of the American Colony. Always wishing for an intelligent boyfriend, she becomes enamored with Senku's smarts and character; shifting allegiance to the Kingdom of Science and, in turn, getting the chance to date the young scientist. Appearance: Luna is a curvy young woman of average height who has wild, short hair with a few locks long enough to cover her left eye. She has a small bust and broad hips and is considered to be very attractive by Carlos, Max, Moz, and several others. For clothing, she wears a striped, form-fitting short backless dress and a choker with a star on it. She also has a pouch wrapped around her left thigh and a pair of thigh-high boots with heels. Her petrification scar takes the form of a star-shaped on her lower back. Personality: Luna seems to have a disposition against murder, as she questioned Stanley if he was really going to kill someone. However, she was silenced by his glare and proceeded to support him in his endeavor. Even when given a perfect opportunity to get a clear shot, she went so far as to make herself look like a fool to divert Stanley's signal. A hopeless romantic, Luna often fantasizes of marrying and living an easy life, a habit she had even before her petrification.
