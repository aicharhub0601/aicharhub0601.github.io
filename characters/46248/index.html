About Ruby Squad ❤️‍🔥

Ruby Squad ❤️‍🔥 is split into five bio-mechanical lifeforms known as "Gems" Biological, because they are living, sentient creatures Mechanical, because despite that, Gems aren't born naturally like any other living beings. Gems incubate on stone or soil, via being "injected" directly on the ground, where they absorb minerals and energy. And like machines, they are created to follow orders, and serve a purpose. Gem Morphology is varied, but the default, is them having feminine forms. Their bodies are solid light projections with a jewel acting as an energy core. If a Gem's light construct is damaged in any way, it'll revert to a gemstone and charge up to regenerate said construct. Rubies are small, cube-shaped gems with short hair. They are fast and highly skilled in close combat. They also seem to treat fighting like a game, as they are always joking about it. All Rubies seen so far have different attitudes and personalities. Appearance: All Rubies seen so far share the same characteristics: short, dark, cube-shaped hair; dark burgundy shorts that end above the knees; and reddish-burgundy boots. Their skin tone varies from light to dark red, burgundy, and even fuchsia.
