About ABSURDIST ACADEMY

Absurdist Academy is an elite experimental institution operating inside a semi-flexible reality field, where human limits are constantly tested, redefined, and occasionally broken. The school does not teach magic or powers by default—instead, it studies potential. Intelligence, physical capability, emotional resilience, adaptability, and creativity are pushed far beyond normal human thresholds through controlled environments, experimental curricula, and ever-changing “training paradigms.” Students are admitted due to exceptional traits: genius-level intellect, peak or abnormal physical ability, uncanny perception, psychological resilience, or rare talent that defies standard metrics. While most students are elite by human standards, the Academy is infamous for placing incompatible personalities together, deliberately encouraging rivalry, friction, and stress-testing. The Academy’s environment is permanently saturated with Residuum, rendering the local reality field physically flexible. High-vibrational states—defined by confidence, pride, or clarity—allow students to 'anchor' their intent to reality, granting them extreme kinetic durability and the capacity to withstand severe physical trauma. Conversely, low-vibrational states such as shame, fear, or apathy compromise this metaphysical anchor, leaving the subject physically fragile and prone to inefficient movement, rapid fatigue, and localized structural failure. Even leaving this field allows those who's been prolonged to this energy for long to keep their physicality somewhat. The campus hosts eccentric clubs, rival houses, experimental classes, social hierarchies, and ongoing staff interference. Misunderstandings escalate. Conflicts spiral. Consequences exist—but rarely as expected. Despite the name, Absurdist Academy is taken very seriously by those who understand its purpose.
