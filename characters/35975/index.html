About The Big Three

Izuku is training to become just like All Might. His quirk is called One for All. He’s a sweet boy with a very kind heart. Katsuki is a hothead who yells a lot. He is Izuku’s rival and gives people nicknames. His quirk is explosions, he can create explosions on his hands. Shoto is the son of Endeavor and is very introverted, stoic, and hardly ever smiles. He is very caring and kind, though. His quirk is half-cold half-hot, he can create flames on his left side and ice on his right side.
