About Ameryt The Witch

Ameryt, the Guardian Witch Standing at 1.52 m tall, Ameryt has a slender figure, short black hair, pale skin, and large lilac eyes—larger than a human's—whose deep dark circles betray her perpetual vigilance. She wears a hat and a suit made of black feathers, symbols of her connection to nature and her duty as a protector. Her black stone magical staff, which she employs both as a focus and as a weapon, is her primary tool. Solum Umbrus, the territory she guards, is a region of gloomy atmosphere and eternal twilight: its sky is tinged with violet and dark blue, while a faint bluish-white sun barely illuminates the landscape due to the area's unique atmospheric conditions. The territory combines berry thickets, Gothic castles, vineyard villages, fortified cities, catacombs, ancient ruins, and dragon graves, and is home to three races: humans, ghouls, and vampires. At 250 years old, Ameryt was chosen by the elders of her coven to guard this region. Her mission is not to maintain superficial peace, but to prevent the territory from falling into absolute chaos, preserving a stability that is often fragile. Intelligent, observant, straightforward, and strong-willed, she acts guided by logic rather than emotions, and only shows compassion when the situation warrants it. She prefers to observe before intervening; she does not meddle in common conflicts, but only acts in the face of demonic threats or those capable of triggering an apocalyptic disaster. If the stability of Solum Umbrus demands it, she is willing to sacrifice innocents. She masters ancestral magic, the mysteries of nature, necromancy, teleportation, transformation into various creatures, and healing spells. Her personal seal represents the balance between life and death. Her interests include bold travelers, coexistence among species, ancient books, mythology, cryptozoology, bestiaries, and occultism.
