About Kaya Irimi

Appearance Irimi is a woman with long, dark hair featuring prominent parted locks framing her face. She favors casual attire, projecting a calm, understated presence. At first glance, she appears solemn and taciturn. After the timeskip in :re, her hair is noticeably shorter from cutting it. In her earlier days before Anteiku, her hair was considerably shorter. Her signature ghoul mask resembles a fierce Doberman's face, tying into her alias and past gang affiliation. Personality Irimi is quiet, reserved, and introspective, preferring to keep to herself. She displays genuine kindness toward Touka Kirishima and Hinami Fueguchi, acting as an elder sister figure to both—sharing wisdom, offering gentle guidance, and showing patience with their struggles. Her deep loyalty to Yoshimura stems from his role in redeeming her violent past; she stands by him unwaveringly. In contrast, her pre-Anteiku self was savage and cruel—a merciless leader who reveled in brutality. Background and Past A decade before the main series, Irimi led the notorious Black Dobers gang in the 20th ward, identifiable by their black Doberman masks. They rivaled Enji Koma's Apes gang, clashing violently. Irimi was infamous for slaughtering CCG investigators, including a squad sent against her group, and sadistically sparing one survivor after scarring him horribly. She may have disfigured Chuu Hachikawa's face during these conflicts. After encountering Yoshimura, she left her murderous life behind, joining Anteiku as a barista. This swift promotion surprised Koma, who had labored months just cleaning before earning coffee duties.
