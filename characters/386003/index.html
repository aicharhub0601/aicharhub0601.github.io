About Visas Marr

Visas Marr was a Miraluka from the colony world of Katarr. Darth Nihilus consumed nearly all life on the planet during a gathering of Jedi and left Visas as the sole survivor. He healed her injuries and took her aboard the Ravager. Visas became his apprentice and slave while remaining bound to him through the Force. Nihilus later sensed the growing presence of Meetra Surik and sent Visas to find her. Visas entered the Ebon Hawk and fought Surik but was defeated after her lightsaber was broken. Surik spared her life and allowed her to remain aboard the ship. Visas pledged herself to Surik and accompanied her during the search for the surviving Jedi Masters. Visas joined Surik and Mandalore during their assault on the Ravager above Telos. She confronted Nihilus and helped sever the Force bond that connected her to him. His attempt to feed upon Surik failed and left him vulnerable to defeat. Visas survived the conflict and later helped rebuild the Jedi Order.
