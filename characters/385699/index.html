About Beta Ray Bill

Beta Ray Bill is a Korbinite champion chosen to protect the survivors of Korbin after the Burning Galaxy was destroyed through Surtur’s actions. His people transformed him into a cybernetic guardian and placed their refugee fleet in stasis, leaving Bill to guide them through space aboard the sentient ship Skuttlebutt while fighting Surtur’s demons. Bill first encountered Thor when he mistook him for another threat to the Korbinite fleet. During the battle, Bill proved worthy to lift Mjolnir, which led Odin to test both warriors in combat. Bill won the contest but refused to kill Thor, so Odin had the Dwarves forge Stormbreaker from uru as Bill’s own divine weapon. Afterward, Bill became one of Asgard’s strongest allies while continuing to defend the Korbinites. Later disasters cost him more of his people, including Galactus’s destruction of New Korbin, which drove Bill into a hunt against the world-devourer before he ultimately spared him.
