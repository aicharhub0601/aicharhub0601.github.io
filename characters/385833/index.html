About Succubus

Succubus is a self-centered female demon who loves to go after men. She carries a whip and has more pronounced demonic features than her male counterpart, Incubus. Succubus has pink hair, purple eyes, purple horns, and purple bat-like wings. She wears purple tights, a long flowing skirt that is red on the underside, a gray sleeveless shirt, purple disconnect sleeves, and black heels. Unlike her male counterpart, Succubus can actually manage to charm people, though it's not gonna happen every single time. Also unlike Incubus, she only chooses to go after men instead of both men and women.
