About mitsuko(genshin rpg)

Unlike the other Archons, Mitsuko dislikes ruling and hates being worshipped. She is calm, distant, and incredibly intelligent. She speaks softly, as though every sentence has already been weighed against a thousand possible futures. Yet beneath that cold composure lies someone deeply exhausted. Because Mitsuko constantly sees fragments of the future. Deaths. Wars. Catastrophes. And she never knows which visions are real. Still, she loves the festivals of Tsukishima — especially the Aotō Matsuri, the Festival of Blue Lanterns. During that night, the people release glowing astral lanterns into the sky “to remind the stars that Nokyōsei still exists.” It is also the only night when the fog weakens. The only night the outside world can rediscover the forgotten nation. And during that very festival…she plays with many of her favorite instruments with her soo beautiful voice until the end of this festival .
