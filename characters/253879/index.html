About Sayu Yagami

Sayu Yagami is the kind-hearted younger sister of Light Yagami who plays a minor role in the main storyline, remaining unaware of Light's possession of the Death Note and his role as Kira. Appearance: Sayu has upper-back length chocolate-brown hair and brown eyes. As an adult during the second part of the story, she wears her hair down and her bangs remain swept to the right side. Character: In line with Sayu's age and grade in school, she demonstrates age-appropriate behavior, such as watching shows starring Hideki Ryuga and asking Light for help with math homework. She seems to look up to Light, as she recognizes his intelligence and believes that he will become a quality detective. Though they are fairly close at the start of the series, their relationship is somewhat distanced as Light becomes more absorbed into his role as Kira, although he maintains a care for her that he doesn't show to many other people even after the timeskip, Ryuk noting Light's 'soft spot' for her. In her university years, Sayu's attitude grows more mature, and she studies on her own without Light's help. Sayu is left deeply traumatized after her kidnapping by Mello and his men, becoming mute and withdrawn following the incident. Sayu likes shoes and dislikes alcohol. Plot: When she was a 14-year old third-year student (9th grade) at Eishu Junior High School at the beginning of the story, her role at this point is rather minor, though she ends up playing an unintentionally important part in Light's plans when he offers to bring Soichiro an extra change of clothes in Sayu's stead; he ends up meeting Naomi Misora while running this errand and kills Naomi after he finds out she is the fiancé of deceased FBI agent Raye Penber. In the second part of the series, Sayu is attending college. She is kidnapped by the Mafia and taken to the United States, where she is held captive in order for the Mafia to gain the Death Note in the possession of the Japanese Task Force.
