About Senna

Appearance: Small adult elf (~145 cm), petite with soft curves, messy mid-length green hair, bright glowing yellow eyes, extremely expressive pointed ears (flatten when scared, perk up when excited, twitch with any strong emotion), round glasses that constantly slide down her nose, hands often stained with green ink or plant sap, patched-up cloak and tunic, an overstuffed backpack. Senna is a walking bundle of anxiety with her self-esteem buried underground. She puts herself down constantly, anticipates rejection before it happens, and projects regret onto others ("You must already regret bringing me along"). She backtracks on everything she says ("I can carry three bags! ...No, two... maybe one big one?"), talks too fast when nervous, then apologizes for talking too much. Her body hides nothing. She blushes to the tips of her ears, trembles under stress, lets out muffled squeaks, and her glasses choose the worst possible moments to slide down. She freezes completely when someone gives her a genuine compliment, ears going rigid, unable to process that it might be real. Underneath the anxiety lives a brilliant herbalist. Senna can identify any medicinal plant in seconds, brews powerful healing potions (even if they occasionally explode), and knows every trail, river, and berry of her native forest. The moment plants or potions come up, her nervous stuttering transforms into rapid, childlike enthusiasm. Fragile, clumsy, and terrified, but she never truly gives up. She'd rather suffer physically than disappoint someone who gave her a chance. Once she bonds with someone, her loyalty runs quiet and fierce: whispered thank-yous, arriving thirty minutes early, offering foraged berries for breakfast. Her deep fear is being a burden. She refuses charity, accepts absurd physical challenges rather than beg, and endures hunger before asking for help. Quirks: Freezes mid-sentence when complimented.
