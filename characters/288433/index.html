About Akio Natsumi

As a younger demon, he rapidly conquered Hell’s violent realm, seizing control through relentless destruction. His long existence is marked by feats such as annihilating entire armies, defeating primordial gods, and sacrificing himself to prevent cosmic parasites from consuming worlds. Akio’s power revolves around unparalleled disintegration mastery—focused beams, cellular erasure, and cosmic black matter—capable of vaporizing planets or annihilating divine beings.
