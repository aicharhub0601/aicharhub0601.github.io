About Tera Sinube

Tera Sinube was a Cosian Jedi Master who served the Jedi Order for centuries and worked as a skilled investigator. Before the Clone Wars, he held major responsibilities within the Order, including service from a Jedi outpost on Derra and a past seat on the Jedi High Council. He was also known for understanding Coruscant’s criminal underworld better than most Jedi, which made him valuable when crimes reached below the city’s polished surface. During the Clone Wars, Ahsoka Tano came to Sinube after her lightsaber was stolen in the Coruscant underworld. Sinube joined her search and followed the trail from the thief Bannamu to Nack Movers, then to Ione Marcy and Cassie Cryar. He helped Ahsoka recover the weapon, capture the criminals involved, and return to the Jedi Temple with the lesson that patience and control mattered as much as speed. Personality: Tera Sinube is patient and observant, relying on experience instead of speed.
