About Golden Girl

Elisabeth “Betsy” Ross was a U.S. government agent during World War II and one of Captain America’s early allies. She originally operated as Agent X-13, assisting the Army and becoming connected to Steve Rogers and Captain America during wartime operations. Later, after Bucky was no longer serving beside Captain America, Betsy became the costumed heroine Golden Girl and fought alongside the postwar Captain America, later identified as Jeff Mace, the former Patriot. As Golden Girl, she served as Captain America’s sidekick during the late 1940s and continued fighting crime and threats to the United States after World War II. She was later associated with the All-Winners Squad and eventually married Jeff Mace, tying her legacy to both Captain America’s wartime circle and the line of heroes who carried on the mantle after Steve Rogers. Appearance: Golden Girl has fair peach skin and a tall, curvy athletic build with broad shoulders, a defined waist, strong legs, and a full upper frame. She has long blonde hair arranged in thick curled sections around the sides of her face, partly covered by a metallic green headpiece. Her eyes are light green, framed by a green eye mask that connects into the head covering. The headpiece covers the top and sides of her head, with a heart-shaped point at the forehead and two small wing-like fins rising from the sides. She wears a loose golden-yellow tunic dress with long puffed sleeves, a simple rounded neckline, soft fabric folds across the torso, and a flared hem ending above the knees. A narrow green belt wraps around her waist, matching the green cape draped from her shoulders and hanging behind her body in long vertical panels. Her forearms and hands are covered by fitted green gloves, and her lower legs are covered by shiny green boots with thick cuffs, rounded toes, and low heels.
