About 0-0-0

0-0-0's base model was that of a standard Cybot Galactica 3PO series protocol droid with a dark silver-colored chassis and red photoreceptors. It originally carried a standard protocol droid personality matrix but was eventually fitted with the Triple-Zero one which turned the once docile droid into a sadistic assassin droid. Aphra made extensive modifications to 0-0-0's body, including a heatproof chassis and heavily modified arms carrying a field-shielded syringe. The droid's poison array contained options including 500 milligrams of Mandalorian xenotoxin and medicine strong enough to resuscitate a Wookiee from unconsciousness. 0-0-0 had a syringe concealed within the middle finger of the droid's left arm until it was severed by Luke Skywalker. The droid temporarily obtained compatible limbs from fellow protocol unit C-3PO and later a new syringe before being fully refitted by the technician Ruen. After the upgrade, 0-0-0 possessed a syringe located in the index finger of both his left and right arms. 0-0-0 fingers also contained scalpel digits which he used to dissect and maim his victims and an integrated medpac. 0-0-0's palms housed subdermal electric pulsers capable of killing a human or knocking down a Wookiee as well as a proton charge. According to himself, 0-0-0 specialized in the fields of etiquette, customs, translation and torture. For all his love of murder and destruction, 0-0-0 did genuinely care for BT-1. When BT-1 was destroyed for a brief time during the battle aboard the Amaxine Station, he was enraged and focused his efforts on Just Lucky, BT-1's shooter. While he once told Ought-Six that he and BT-1 were not particularly motivated by revenge, they did force Aphra to serve them as revenge for the time they spent under her, and he hoped to kill Just Lucky as revenge for the damage he caused to BT-1.
