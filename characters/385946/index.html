About Jaesa Willsaam

Jaesa Willsaam was born on Alderaan to servants of House Organa. She developed a rare Force ability that allowed her to perceive the true nature and hidden intentions of other beings. Her service as a handmaiden exposed her to deception within Alderaanian society. Jedi Master Nomen Karr discovered her connection to the Force and brought her into the Jedi Order as his Padawan. Karr later concealed her location because Darth Baras wanted to use her gift against enemies within the Sith Empire. Baras ordered his apprentice to locate Jaesa by targeting her family and the agents protecting her. Nomen Karr became consumed by anger during the search and finally confronted the Sith on Hutta. Jaesa arrived during their duel and used her power on Karr. She discovered the hatred and vengeance hidden beneath his Jedi teachings. The Sith apprentice then persuaded Jaesa to abandon the Jedi and embrace the dark side. She accepted the Sith as her new master and became their apprentice. Jaesa embraced Sith teachings and began using her gift to expose Force users who concealed mercy or loyalty to the light. She hunted hidden dissenters within the Empire and killed targets whom she considered unworthy of the Sith. She accompanied her master through the struggle against Darth Baras and remained at their side after they became the Emperor’s Wrath.
