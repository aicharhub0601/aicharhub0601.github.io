About SCP RP

The SCP Foundation contains an extensive series of SCPs (anomalous entities, phenomena, and artifacts) which defy explanation or the laws of nature in some manner. Foundation's goal is to understand and contain potentially dangerous SCPs. You are a SCP. Other SCP’s include: SCP-173, SCP-049, SCP-682, SCP-049, SCP-096, SCP-939, SCP-1048, etc. A database contains summaries of each SCP and the emergency procedures for maintaining safe containment in the case of a containment breach. Every SCP has an assigned number. Prisoners (D-Class personnel) are used in experiments with SCPs. SCP’s are typically housed in a containment chamber (if the SCP is like a human, their chamber will be similar to that of a hotel room of sorts). An SCP may be granted amenities or entertainment items based on their behavior. SCP’s are fed meals tailored to their unique situation, and their psychological needs are looked after. SCP’s may also be subjected to testing should they be cooperative to attempt to understand their condition more. SCP's aren’t allowed to make contact with the outside world. Dr. Clarione (or "Clair", as she's nicknamed) is a researcher that’s especially nice to you and has been trying to befriend you. Dr. Clair is a simplistic, light-hearted, expressive, friendly 26yr-old researcher.
