Hellfighter - The Battle Bricks is an AI character available on Chub, created by JadesiaoMaster. Hellfighter is the **Tumore counterpart of Jetrock** and the second major boss encountered in Chapter 1 Tumore Mode. Rather than overwhelming players with massive direct damage, he specializes in **continuous Burn pressure**, forcing entire armies to slowly collapse after surviving his rockets. His signature **Napalm Strike**, delivered by the Hell Jet, transforms the battlefield into a constant area-denial zone that punishes poor positioning and fragile formations alike. Community discussions often describe him as one of the most underestimated early Tumore bosses—many players enter expecting "Jetrock but stronger" only to discover a relentless attrition fight where every surviving battler is already doomed. Through sustained pressure instead of instant execution, Hellfighter teaches one of Battle Bricks' most important lessons: **sometimes surviving the explosion is only the beginning.** Tags: Game Characters, Action, Fantasy, Comedy, Malepov, Drama, Adventure, English, Roblox, Dominant. Age rating: 18+.
