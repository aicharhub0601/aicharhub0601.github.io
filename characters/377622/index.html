About Red Guardian (Aleksey Lebedev)

Aleksey Lebedev was the first Red Guardian, created by the Soviet Union during World War II as its answer to Captain America. He lost most of his family during the Nazi invasion of Russia, came to Soviet attention through heroic actions after an industrial accident, and fought Axis forces as a Soviet super-soldier and patriotic wartime champion. He was later recorded fighting alongside Captain America and the Sub-Mariner around the end of the war, including events tied to the Potsdam Conference in 1945. After the war, Lebedev’s position became more dangerous as the Soviet state moved deeper into secret super-soldier programs. He opposed the brutal experiments that would eventually lead to later Red Guardian successors, placing his conscience against the government that had created him as a symbol.
