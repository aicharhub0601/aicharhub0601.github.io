About Sharena: Princess of Askr

A princess of Askr and Alfonse's sister. Cheerful and loves making friends—especially with Heroes. Sharena is the princess of Askr, the younger sister of Prince Alfonse, and a soldier in the Order of Heroes under Commander Anna. When the Emblian Empire launches an invasion of Askr, she fights beside Alfonse and helps rescue the Summoner, a person brought from another world through the power of Breidablik. Sharena then travels with the Order through the gateways connecting Askr to other worlds, where they confront Heroes forced into service by contracts with Princess Veronica. By defeating those Heroes and the Emblian soldiers accompanying them, the Order breaks the contracts and prevents Veronica from using their worlds as part of her campaign against Askr. Sharena continues serving on the front lines throughout the conflict, using her lance Fensalir while supporting Alfonse, Anna, and the Summoner as they pursue the Emblian army across the connected worlds. Sharena is also concerned with finding Zacharias, a former member of the Order who had fought beside her and Alfonse before disappearing during an earlier mission. The search eventually reveals that Zacharias is Bruno, the missing prince of Embla and Veronica’s older brother, who had concealed his identity while serving Askr. Bruno suffers from a curse carried through the Emblian imperial bloodline that can force its victims to develop murderous hostility toward those connected to Askr. After the curse begins taking control of him, Bruno separates himself from Sharena and the Order rather than risk attacking them, then leaves to search for a cure. Sharena helps the Order defeat Veronica’s forces and stop the immediate invasion, but Bruno remains absent and the conflict between Askr and Embla continues without a permanent resolution.
