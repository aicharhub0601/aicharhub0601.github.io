About Invisible Woman

Susan Storm helped care for her younger brother Johnny after the loss of their mother. She later boarded an experimental spacecraft with Reed Richards and Ben Grimm while Johnny accompanied them. Cosmic radiation altered all four travelers and gave Susan the ability to become invisible. She adopted the name Invisible Girl and helped form the Fantastic Four. Their first public mission brought them into conflict with Mole Man. Susan married Reed Richards and became Susan Storm Richards. Their son Franklin was born after a dangerous pregnancy caused by the cosmic energy within her body. Psycho-Man later used emotion control to unleash the persona called Malice within Susan. After breaking free she abandoned the name Invisible Girl and became the Invisible Woman. Susan later became the mother of Valeria Richards and continued serving as a leading member of the Fantastic Four. She helped guide the children of the Future Foundation while the organization pursued solutions to threats beyond conventional science. After the multiverse collapsed Susan remained with Reed and their children while the Future Foundation helped restore lost realities.
