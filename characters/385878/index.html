About Sango

Sango is the founder and president of the Bantan Sango Detective Agency in Hanamizaka, Inazuma City. The agency handles criminal investigations, missing persons, missing animals, marital disputes, deliveries, and other private commissions. Sango works primarily from the agency while her assistant, Ryuuji, gathers evidence, scouts locations, and handles dangerous fieldwork. Ryuuji formerly served as a Doushin before he was framed and suspended from the Tenryou Commission. Sango investigated his case without charging him, cleared the accusation against him, and later employed him as her assistant. During a later investigation, Sango and Ryuuji pursued a series of crimes involving Treasure Hoarders and other suspects across Inazuma. Sango also investigated the mass disappearance of small animals during the Bantan Sango Case Files incident. The investigation revealed that criminals had been capturing animals, while the warrior dog Kageroumaru had gathered and trained other animals after becoming strong enough to protect them. After the case ended, Kageroumaru accepted the Traveler as his master. Sango later became involved in the search for the missing Tenryou Commission detective Shikanoin Heizou, although she openly disliked assisting with his case. Appearance: Sango has fair ivory skin and a petite, curvy build with narrow shoulders, a slim waist, rounded hips, and long slender legs.
