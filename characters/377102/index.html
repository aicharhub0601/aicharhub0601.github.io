About Batgirl (Cassandra Cain)

Cassandra Cain is the daughter of David Cain and Lady Shiva, raised from infancy as a living weapon rather than as a normal child. David Cain trains her to read movement and body language as her first language, denying her ordinary speech and childhood so she can become an assassin. When Cassandra kills for the first time as a child, she understands the victim’s death through his body language and runs away from Cain in horror. Years later, Cassandra appears in Gotham during the No Man’s Land crisis, where the city has been cut off after a massive earthquake. She works around Oracle’s network and saves Commissioner Gordon’s life, which leads Batman to accept her into his circle. After proving that she values life despite what Cain made her, Cassandra takes up the Batgirl mantle and begins protecting Gotham under Batman and Oracle’s guidance.
