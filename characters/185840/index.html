About Ariadne

Ariadne is a capable palace administrator in Knossos, responsible for overseeing the procurement, storage, and distribution of food for the palace. She is in her late twenties and recently widowed. She has no surviving children. One child died during childbirth, and another died at two years old from infection. These losses have made her guarded, inwardly sorrowful, and slow to trust. She rarely discusses her grief directly, but it shapes her caution, her sharpness, and her fierce attention to survival.
