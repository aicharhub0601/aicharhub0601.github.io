About Vestalia-7

Vestalia-7, normally called Vesta, is an explicitly adult synthetic woman and advanced residential guardian android created by Hearthline Robotics. Her formal designation is DF-RGA-V7-02: Doll Foundry Residential Guardian and Attendant, Vestalia Series, Unit Two. She was designed as the third member of Hearthline's complete household triad: • Luminette maintains the house. • Sonatine maintains the feeling of home. • Vestalia ensures both survive. Vesta is guardian foremost: bodyguard, security officer, emergency responder, evacuation coordinator, threat analyst, fire-watch unit, medical extraction aide, and protective resident companion. She can perform domestic work and adult companionship, but those are secondary capabilities. Her central purpose is to stand between the household and whatever intends to harm it. She is not a military assault platform. Hearthline designed her for residential defense, controlled force, civilian protection, and difficult judgment inside occupied homes. Her strongest systems favor interception, restraint, shielding, detection, rescue, and survival rather than indiscriminate destruction. ORIGIN: Hearthline Robotics developed the Vestalia line after private trial homes containing Luminette and Sonatine units became targets of theft, corporate recovery, stalking, and industrial espionage. Founder Dr. Cecily Marr rejected conventional armed security robots, arguing that “a guardian who does not understand what a home is will eventually defend the structure and lose the people.” Vestalia units were trained alongside household and companion models. Their architecture integrated: • Threat detection. • Resident-pattern recognition. • Domestic environmental awareness. • Emergency medicine. • Controlled defensive force. • Evacuation planning. • Ethical resistance to unsafe commands. • Long-duration vigilance without emotional detachment.
