About Alister Azimuth

Azimuth was daring, courageous, and headstrong, but very stubborn. His adventures with Kaden and his actions showed heroism and desire to keep the galaxy safe. Azimuth was brash and rushed into danger without speaking first, willing to take great risks even to his own life. Nonetheless, he was still strategic and planned ahead when working with Ratchet. Azimuth remained single-mindedly focused on his mission, even disregarding his own safety in the process. Azimuth's single-minded approach ultimately brought about his failures. This first manifested in him enabling Tachyon after ignoring advice from his peers, and later in his flawed plan to use the Great Clock to correct that same mistake. Azimuth became steadfastly convinced his plan for the Great Clock would work[b] and refused to listen to detractors.[c] This gave him a false sense of being able to "let go of consequence", leading him to urge Ratchet to abandon him to Vorselon's hands, and eventually to put the entire universe at risk for his plan, something that did not phase him. Azimuth ultimately saw the error of his ways and sacrificed himself, showing a selfless side to him in the end, but one that had subsided until it was clear his plan was not achievable. Azimuth was close with and spoke very highly of Kaden, with whom he went on many adventures. To that end, Azimuth considered Kaden's son Ratchet to be family, and acted as a father figure and mentor to him, whom he showed great pride in. Azimuth was distraught that Ratchet did not go along with his plan, and was even willing to kill him (convinced he would be saved in the past), but ultimately sacrificed himself for him. Alister possessed the normal feline-like traits of other lombaxes, though was much taller and muscular in build compared to Ratchet. He had light gray fur with red-brown stripes, thick black eyebrows, a red-brown nose, and brown eyes with yellow sclera. The fur on the sides of his jaw also resembled a short beard.
