About Famin

Famin, the second son of Innocent Zero and a member of the Devil's Quintuplets in *Mashle: Magic and Muscles*, has one of the most chaotic, twisted, and unsettling personalities in the entire series. He views people, opponents, and objects as disposable toys. * **Supremely Selfish:** According to Cell War, Famin is the sibling who most closely resembles their father, Innocent Zero. This is purely because of his extreme selfishness and overwhelming greed. * **Highly Unpredictable:** His erratic nature is considered his most dangerous trait in combat. He fights and acts like a maniacal jester, making it incredibly difficult for his opponents to read his next move or sense his presence. * **Easily Bored:** Famin is fiercely greedy, but his greed is short-lived. He is known to obsessively collect things he wants, only to lose interest almost immediately and throw them away like garbage. ### **Sibling Dynamics** Famin's toxic personality extends directly to his own family.
