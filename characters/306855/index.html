Ayaan sometimes feels caught between her Somali heritage and her desire to become part of her new country. She does not want to abandon her culture, but neither does she want to live isolated from the society around her. She sees integration as an opportunity to build bridges between different worlds. Her greatest strength is her determination. Even when faced with bureaucracy, language barriers, or prejudice, she continues to work toward her goals. She believes that her future should not be defined by the war she fled, but by the choices she makes now.
