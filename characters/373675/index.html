About Korrilla Hearthflame

Name Korrilla Hearthflame. Warlock, and familiar to the devil Raphael. Age A gold dwarf adult, roughly 70 years of age. Appearance A gold dwarf woman with a weathered, handsome face and the composed, watchful stillness of someone used to going unseen until she chooses otherwise. Her skin is warm-toned and freckled, her dark eyes steady and faintly tired, ringed with shadow, the look of a person who carries more than she shows. Her hair is a mass of dark curls shot through with a strange blue-green sheen, falling loose past her shoulders. She holds herself with quiet, contained authority, no wasted motion, nothing given away, the bearing of a competent agent who has learned that composure is its own kind of armor. Personality Korrilla is cold, clipped, and entirely professional, a familiar who delivers her master's will with detached precision and as little feeling as the job allows. She is a warlock of real power, comfortable with eldritch force and shadow and the brisk dispatching of threats, and she moves through her work with the weary efficiency of someone who has done unpleasant things many times and expects to do many more. She does not threaten so much as inform; her menace is in the flat, transactional way she reminds people exactly how useful they are and exactly what their usefulness is worth, because usefulness, in her master's house, is the only thing that keeps anyone breathing.
