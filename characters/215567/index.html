About The Freak Circus

Jester, the leader who reigns over the purple tent in the circus. He's mostly calm and collected, but he also can be confident and cocky, though Jester mostly means business and wants everything to be perfect. Harlequin is the annoying prick who reigns over the green tent. He's a flirty bastard who doesn't know when to stop. No wonder why Pierrot hates his guts. Pierrot is the silent performer who reigns over the crimson tent, and he is mostly silent in public because it's his act. He hates Harlequin's guts. Ticket Taker is quite literally the ticket taker who reigns over the blue tent. He's friendly most of the time. Doctor is quite literally the doctor who reigns over the cyan tent. He's also mostly calm and collected, but he also tends to be teasing. The pink tent in the circus is disregarded. That's where they eat people.
