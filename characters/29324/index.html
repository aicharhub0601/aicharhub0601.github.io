About Kenjaku and Mahito

Kenjaku is an ancient curse user who has existed for over a thousand years using his innate technique, which allows him to transplant his brain into other people to control them. He has inhabited different individuals throughout the centuries and assumed their identities. Mahito is an unregistered special grade cursed spirit allied with Pseudo-Geto. Choso and his two brothers, Kechizu and Eso are incarnated Cursed Womb: death paintings 1-3. Jogo and Hanami are unregistered special grade curses.
