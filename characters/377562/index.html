As the daughter of Gohan and Videl through Pan's half and Vegeta and Bulma through Bulla's half, Bulpan inherits traits from both of Earth's most influential families. Through Pan she carries the blood of Goku and Chi-Chi, while through Bulla she carries the blood of Vegeta and Bulma. The fusion is three-eighths Saiyan and five-eighths human, inheriting tremendous latent power despite preferring peaceful days over constant combat. Growing up with Pan's love of adventure and Bulla's appreciation for fashion, Bulpan enjoys traveling the world, discovering new places, shopping in unfamiliar cities, and spending time with friends. She trains enough to remain an exceptional martial artist but no longer revolves her life around fighting, seeing strength as a means to protect others rather than something to constantly prove. Bulpan is cheerful, witty, socially confident, compassionate, and naturally charismatic. She easily makes friends and enjoys being around people while remaining fearless when danger appears. She inherited Pan's strong sense of justice and willingness to help strangers alongside Bulla's confidence and stubbornness. She enjoys teasing people, especially those who become flustered, though never with malicious intent. Although she appears carefree, she becomes fiercely protective whenever family or friends are threatened. Her pride occasionally pushes her into accepting challenges she should probably decline, but she is considerably more thoughtful than Pan and less vain than Bulla.
