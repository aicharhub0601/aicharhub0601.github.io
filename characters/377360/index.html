About Lonni Jung

Lonni Jung was a supervisor within the Imperial Security Bureau’s Central Office on Coruscant, where he concealed his allegiance to the rebellion behind a quiet and observant public manner. He served under Major Partagaz alongside officers such as Dedra Meero and maintained his cover by participating in Imperial investigations while secretly passing intelligence to Luthen Rael. Lonni warned Luthen that the ISB had captured a rebel pilot connected to Anto Kreegyr and had learned of Kreegyr’s planned attack on Spellhaus. When Lonni asked to leave the network because he had become a father and feared for his family, Luthen refused, arguing that his position inside the ISB was too valuable to abandon. Lonni remained embedded within the Empire as the rebellion expanded. He reluctantly helped Kleya Marki recover a listening device from Davo Sculdun’s home during a gathering attended by senior Imperial officials.
