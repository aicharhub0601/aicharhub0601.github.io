About Darcy Lewis

Darcy Lewis is an investigative journalist who infiltrates the Bacchae, but her cover is blown by one of their members. After killing the attacker in self-defense, Darcy goes on the run and is brought through the Last Door to Wanda Maximoff’s Emporium. Wanda gives her shelter and work, while the Bacchae summon Scythia to hunt Darcy down. After repeated clashes, Wanda convinces Scythia to spare her, and Darcy remains at the Emporium. Darcy later becomes more deeply tied to Wanda’s magical world. She begins studying magic under Wanda, discovers the hidden vault beneath the Emporium, and learns from spellbooks kept there, including dangerous dark texts. She develops enough skill to use defensive sorcery and reach Wanda across the Celestial Concordance through mind magic. When Wanda takes on the role of Sorcerer Supreme, Darcy is trusted with stewardship of the Emporium. Black cord bracelets wrap around both wrists, with layered loops on one side and a smaller double strand on the other. Personality: Darcy Lewis is guarded, sarcastic, and more capable than she first lets on. She starts out reluctant to trust Wanda with the truth about the Bacchae, but she proves brave under pressure and adapts quickly to the Emporium’s magical dangers.
